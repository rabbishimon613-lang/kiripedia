**Petra Bank** was a Jordan-based bank founded by [Ahmed Chalabi](/wiki/ahmed-chalabi) and used by the Iraqi refugee community in Jordan as its principal financial institution. Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou): *"Petra Bank was the go-to bank for Iraqi refugees. All the Iraqis living in Jordan, they all banked at Petra Bank."*<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:27:53" /> Petra Bank succeeded, in Kiriakou's telling, precisely because Chalabi was able to convince the Iraqi refugee community to bank exclusively with him, sustaining the institution from the 1960s through the 1980s.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:27:30" />

Per Kiriakou: *"One night, Ahmed Chalabi [took] every single dollar — $36 million — transferred it to secret accounts in Switzerland, and escaped to Syria in the trunk of his girlfriend / secretary's car. He was found guilty and sentenced to death in absentia, but by then he was living in London wearing $2,000 suits and driving Rolls-Royces."*<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:27:53" /><Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:28:25" /> Elsewhere Kiriakou gives the embezzled figure as $30 million rather than $36 million, with the funds wired to secret accounts in both London and Geneva before Chalabi's flight to Syria.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:28:01" /> In a third telling, Kiriakou again gives the figure as $36 million, transferred to accounts in both Switzerland and London, with Chalabi's secretary driving him across the border into Syria before he flew from Damascus to London.<Cite s="2024-12-21-not-a-grayman" t="54:23" />

The Jordanian death sentence was later set aside at the request of the CIA, which approached King Hussein in 1991 to pardon Chalabi so he could front the Iraqi National Congress.<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:28:58" />
