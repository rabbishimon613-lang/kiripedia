**Max Blumenthal** is an American independent investigative journalist whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes as a personal friend and one of the most capable analysts working.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="11:50" />

## Kiriakou's assessment

*"Max Blumenthal is a friend of mine and I'll tell you what, he's one of the most brilliant people I've ever encountered. He is able to put these pieces of a puzzle together in a way that almost no one else I know is able to do."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="11:50" /> Kiriakou routinely places him among the small set of commentators whose reading of the Israeli–Iranian confrontation he treats as the conventional wisdom worth taking seriously, alongside [Trita Parsi](/wiki/trita-parsi) and Larry Johnson.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="43:12" />

## The Venezuelan embassy case

Kiriakou recounts that Blumenthal was accused of assaulting a woman outside the Venezuelan embassy in Washington and had the charges dropped roughly a month before Kiriakou discussed it in February 2020. On Kiriakou's account the video showed the reverse of the allegation — *"it was Max who was assaulted, he never raised his hand to anybody, a woman or otherwise"* — and the police did nothing.<Cite s="2020-02-17-the-bitter-truth-abe-abdelhadi" t="19:21" />

## Under the same pressure

Kiriakou groups Blumenthal with himself and with outlets such as MintPress News when describing official and institutional pressure on independent journalists: *"nobody's safe … ask Max Blumenthal, he'll tell you all about it, because he's under the same kind of pressure … they're coming after all of us."*<Cite s="2022-05-06-scott-horton-ep-5709" t="16:45" />
