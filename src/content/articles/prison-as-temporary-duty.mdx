In the interval between sentencing and surrender, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) and his wife adopted a frame for the coming thirty months drawn from his [CIA](/wiki/cia) career: *"we''re treating this like temporary duty overseas. It was not unusual for me to go overseas for many months at a time, sometimes as long as two years at a time."*<Cite s="2015-01-09-terrorpedia-video-center-aggregator-channel-likely-re-upload" t="56:50" />

The practical position at the time was uncertain. He knew only that he would have to report *"sometime in the next four to six weeks,"* and not where: *"I''ll have to report to a prison somewhere — I don''t know where."* The preparations he lists are administrative rather than emotional — a power of attorney, arrangements for child care — and he describes the whole of it as *"just overwhelming,"* crediting his wife, whom he called *"very strong and very tough and very supportive,"* with holding the household together. He was the father of five.<Cite s="2015-01-09-terrorpedia-video-center-aggregator-channel-likely-re-upload" t="56:18" /><Cite s="2015-01-09-terrorpedia-video-center-aggregator-channel-likely-re-upload" t="56:50" />
