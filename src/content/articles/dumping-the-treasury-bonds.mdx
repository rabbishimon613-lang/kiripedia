**Dumping the treasury bonds** is the second Chinese economic lever [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) named in his February 2026 assessment of what an American attack on Iran would cost, alongside [the rare earth calculation](/wiki/the-rare-earth-calculation).

## The holding

*"You made a second point that I think bears repeating,"* Kiriakou told his interviewer. *"The Chinese are, depending on what numbers you're looking at, the second or third largest holder of long term government treasury bonds."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-health-ranger-report-war-with-iran-interview-with-john-kiriakou" t="1:37:56" />

## The lever

The mechanism requires no restriction, licence or announcement — only a sale. *"If the Chinese really want to put the pain to us, they can just dump those bonds and our entire economy would crash."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-health-ranger-report-war-with-iran-interview-with-john-kiriakou" t="1:37:56" />

## The question attached

Kiriakou used the two levers together as a cost estimate rather than a prediction, and turned them into a question about the war itself: *"So, you know, is it really worth it? Do we really want to do this? Besides the fact that you're probably not going to get your Nobel Peace Prize for waging a war of choice."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-health-ranger-report-war-with-iran-interview-with-john-kiriakou" t="1:37:56" />

He accepted the framing that these are costs to America and not to [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu), who *"doesn't care if he throws America under the bus"* so long as Israel gets chaos in the Middle East — adding only the observation that saying so publicly gets a person called an anti-Semite, as had happened to him weeks earlier. *"The word is lost all meaning now."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-health-ranger-report-war-with-iran-interview-with-john-kiriakou" t="1:38:27" /><Cite s="2026-02-11-health-ranger-report-war-with-iran-interview-with-john-kiriakou" t="1:39:00" />
