**Russia-Ukraine war origins**, per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), trace to 2014 rather than the 2022 invasion. The United States repeatedly promised Russia that NATO would not expand to its borders and that Ukraine would not join — then did the opposite.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="25:34" /> He notes Russia itself twice asked to join NATO and was refused under Presidents Clinton and Obama.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="26:05" /> After the 2014 removal of a democratically elected pro-Russian Ukrainian leader and his replacement with a pro-Western one, Russia seized Crimea *"for their own protection."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="26:37" /> Kiriakou says Russia is now winning and has no reason to accept talks brokered by [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump); Ukraine will end as a *"rump state,"* possibly landlocked, and never in NATO — because a weak Russia serves Western interests.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="27:08" /><Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="28:09" /> In the same conversation, Kiriakou contrasts Russia's military threat with what he frames as the more serious long-term rival: [China as an economic threat](/wiki/china-economic-threat), which he argues is buying influence worldwide while the U.S. spends heavily on weapons.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="30:13" />

## The Nuland recording (Reason2Resist)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) points to the leaked recording of Victoria Nuland telling U.S. Ambassador Geoffrey Pyatt who the next prime minister of Ukraine would be — before he became exactly that — as evidence of U.S. involvement in the 2014 events he places at the [war's origin](/wiki/russia-ukraine-war-origins). He uses it to argue that aligning with the Russian government on a fact does not make one a propagandist.<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="25:02" />
