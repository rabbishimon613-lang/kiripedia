**Russia-Ukraine war origins**, per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), trace to 2014 rather than the 2022 invasion. The United States repeatedly promised Russia that NATO would not expand to its borders and that Ukraine would not join — then did the opposite.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="25:34" /> He notes Russia itself twice asked to join NATO and was refused under Presidents Clinton and Obama.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="26:05" /> He tells the fuller version: at the end of the Clinton administration Putin, newly in power, floated Russia joining NATO outright; Clinton demurred but agreed at least not to admit countries on Russia's border. Latvia, Lithuania, Estonia, and Poland joined anyway.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:23:38" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:24:40" /> Years later, at a summit with Obama, Putin raised it again and warned that Ukraine joining NATO "would be a serious problem"; Obama said it wouldn't happen.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:25:11" /> After the 2014 removal of a democratically elected pro-Russian Ukrainian leader — which Kiriakou says came at Washington's urging — and his replacement with a pro-Western one, Russia seized Crimea *"for their own protection,"* and talk of Ukraine joining both NATO and the EU resumed under Biden.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="26:37" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:25:43" /> Kiriakou frames this pattern as consistent with how U.S. power works abroad generally — informal control and influence rather than a traditional territorial empire like the British — and argues that sustaining a defense budget bigger than the next eight countries combined requires having an enemy to justify it.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:26:47" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:31:31" />

Kiriakou says that from the war's earliest days, observers on all sides agreed the conflict was untenable for Ukraine and the West, and that any peace would require Ukraine to cede the [Donbass](/wiki/russia-ukraine-war-origins) and Crimea while being barred from NATO but allowed to join the EU — roughly the shape the war has taken by 2025.<Cite s="2025-03-22-break-it-down-ukraine-foreign-policy-intelligence" t="18:12" /> Western sanctions initially surprised Russia but ultimately failed to bite, he says, because its economy is closely tied to China, India, and Iran.<Cite s="2025-03-22-break-it-down-ukraine-foreign-policy-intelligence" t="18:45" /> Kiriakou says Russia is now winning and has no reason to accept talks brokered by [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump); Ukraine will end as a *"rump state,"* possibly landlocked, and never in NATO — because a weak Russia serves Western interests.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="27:08" /><Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="28:09" /> Rather than NATO, he predicts Ukraine will get fast-track EU membership after ceding territory, comparing its likely trajectory to Cyprus — not in NATO, but in the EU, with a strong economy despite a banking crisis 15 years ago.<Cite s="2026-06-28-deep-focus-global-power-struggles" t="37:04" />

Kiriakou is skeptical of NATO's continued relevance generally, relaying his attorney's question of why Montenegro — which no plausible power would invade — is even in an alliance originally designed to counter a Soviet Union that no longer exists.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:29:25" />

## The Nuland recording (Reason2Resist)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) points to the leaked recording of Victoria Nuland telling U.S. Ambassador Geoffrey Pyatt who the next prime minister of Ukraine would be — before he became exactly that — as evidence of U.S. involvement in the 2014 events he places at the [war's origin](/wiki/russia-ukraine-war-origins). He uses it to argue that aligning with the Russian government on a fact does not make one a propagandist.<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="25:02" />
