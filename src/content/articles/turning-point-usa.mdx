**Turning Point USA** is the conservative youth organization founded by [Charlie Kirk](/wiki/charlie-kirk). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) treats it primarily as an electoral instrument: it was Kirk, he says, who brought young people voting for the very first time over not just to the Trump campaign but to MAGA as a movement, and [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) said he would not have won a second term without him.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="15:39" />

Asked whether the movement can continue without its founder, Kiriakou agrees that Kirk was its centre and does not think it can be done at the same scale. The organization retains recognizable figures — he names Ben Shapiro and Benny Johnson among *"a handful of other people"* — but his verdict is flat: *"They're not Charlie Kirk. So, this is going to hurt. This is going to do damage."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="15:07" />

Kiriakou grounds the judgment in what he takes Kirk to have been rather than what the organization does. Kirk was *"one of those unique figures who had a generational impact on American politics,"* an authority Kiriakou compares to Martin Luther King and Robert F. Kennedy Sr. — a personal asset the institution cannot inherit.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="15:39" /><Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="16:09" />
