The **Family Jewels** is the name for the CIA's internal record of its own abuses, which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says the [Church Committee](/wiki/church-committee) and [Pike Committee](/wiki/pike-committee) demanded in full in the mid-1970s.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:03:30" /> The first things to come up, he says, were [MK-Ultra](/wiki/mk-ultra) and associated assassination programs: the agency was not supposed to be assassinating anyone, yet it had killed thousands. Congress moved to put the brakes on. It was during this same reckoning that [Richard Helms](/wiki/richard-helms), as CIA Director, ordered the destruction of the MK-Ultra documents.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:04:01" />
