**Elon Musk** is the entrepreneur whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts meeting by chance in early 2009 at the Hay-Adams Hotel bar in Washington, D.C., after an introduction from a mutual friend — at a time when Musk was worth about $1 billion following the sale of PayPal.<Cite s="2025-03-22-break-it-down-ukraine-foreign-policy-intelligence" t="23:30" /> Kiriakou uses that memory to question how Musk's net worth grew from $1 billion in 2009 to $446 billion by 2024-2025, arguing the gain came "on the backs of the American people."<Cite s="2025-03-22-break-it-down-ukraine-foreign-policy-intelligence" t="26:06" /> He also cites a Wall Street Journal report that Musk lost $40 billion in wealth after [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) first announced tariffs, with Jeff Bezos separately losing about $20 billion amid the same stock market drop.<Cite s="2025-03-22-break-it-down-ukraine-foreign-policy-intelligence" t="28:14" />
