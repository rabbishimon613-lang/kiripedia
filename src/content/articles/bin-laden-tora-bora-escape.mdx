**Bin Laden's Tora Bora escape** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of how Osama bin Laden slipped out of the U.S. cordon at [Tora Bora](/wiki/tora-bora) in late 2001.

## The infiltrated translator

Per [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), in October 2001 the [CIA](/wiki/cia) believed it had bin Laden and the al-Qaeda leadership cornered at Tora Bora amid extensive bombing. What the U.S. did not know was that the translator for the commander of Central Command was an al-Qaeda operative who had infiltrated the U.S. military.<Cite s="2025-10-24-pakistan-nuclear-secrets-cia-lies" t="0:00" /><Cite s="2025-10-24-pakistan-nuclear-secrets-cia-lies" t="13:00" />

## The dawn pause

Bin Laden, communicating through that translator, asked for a pause: *"Can you just give us until dawn? We want to evacuate the women and children."* The translator convinced General Tommy Franks to grant the request.<Cite s="2025-10-24-pakistan-nuclear-secrets-cia-lies" t="0:30" /><Cite s="2025-10-24-pakistan-nuclear-secrets-cia-lies" t="13:00" />

## The escape

Under cover of darkness, *"bin Laden dressed as a woman and he escaped under the cover of darkness in the back of a pickup truck into Pakistan."* When the sun came up at dawn, there was no one at Tora Bora to surrender — they had all escaped, forcing the U.S. to move the fight into Pakistan proper.<Cite s="2025-10-24-pakistan-nuclear-secrets-cia-lies" t="0:30" /><Cite s="2025-10-24-pakistan-nuclear-secrets-cia-lies" t="14:00" /><Cite s="2025-10-24-pakistan-nuclear-secrets-cia-lies" t="14:30" />
