[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) gave a series of speeches around Ireland — visiting Belfast, Cork, Dublin, and Waterford before continuing on to Scotland — during which he met with a number of European intelligence and law-enforcement officials.<Cite s="2026-05-20-covert-operations-insight" t="11:03" />

## Anti-money-laundering and counterterrorism strength

Kiriakou says Irish intelligence and the Garda are first-class specifically at anti-money-laundering investigations, and separately notes they are also strong at counterterrorism. He traces the counterterrorism skill to the 1970s, when large numbers of Irish militants — mostly linked to Northern Irish and international communist groups aligned with organizations such as the PFLP, DFLP, and the Abu Nidal Organization — traveled to Libya and Lebanon to be trained by [Carlos the Jackal](/wiki/carlos-the-jackal). In Kiriakou's account, the Irish government's later skill at recruiting spies to penetrate those same groups grew directly out of that period, and the service has maintained that expertise since.<Cite s="2026-05-20-covert-operations-insight" t="11:35" />
