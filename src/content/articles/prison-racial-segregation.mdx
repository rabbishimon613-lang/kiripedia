**Prison racial segregation** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of how rigidly American prisons divide by race and ethnicity — a shock, he says, *"like walking into 1950s American South."* One half of the cafeteria was entirely Black; the other was subdivided into graduated tables of Aryans, Italians, Native Americans and, at the end, *"the rats"* and child offenders, with Hispanics seated among them.<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:03:30" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:03:58" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:04:32" /> Kiriakou argues this does lasting harm: inmates are hardened toward violence, taught to hate other groups for years, then released with no new skills — over 50% re-offend — making society less safe, since nothing prepares them for the outside.<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:02:27" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:03:00" />
