**Khalid Sheikh Mohammed (KSM)** was the mastermind of the September 11, 2001 attacks. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was the CIA officer present at the March 2002 capture of [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) in Pakistan — and identifies Abu Zubaydah's subsequent disclosure to FBI interrogator [Ali Soufan](/wiki/ali-soufan) as the moment the U.S. learned KSM's true identity.<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:08:18" /><Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:09:52" />

## "Mukhtar" → KSM

Per Kiriakou, the CIA had been tracking a high-value al-Qaeda operative under the kunya **Mukhtar** since at least 1996, when Filipino police entering an apartment in Manila uncovered plans and photographs for what became known as the [Bojinka plot](/wiki/bojinka-plot) — a plan to hijack as many as 14 747s out of Manila Airport and fly them into 14 buildings up and down the U.S. west coast. *"We knew he was al-Qaeda, we knew his kunya was Mukhtar, and we knew that he was serious dangerous. We had no idea who he was."*<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:08:48" /><Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:09:21" />

Six years later, in Soufan's interrogation of Abu Zubaydah at the black site: *"Abu Zubaydah actually laughed and said, 'You don't know who Mukhtar is?' And Ali said, 'No.' And he said, 'He's Khalid Sheikh Mohammed.' We had never heard that name before."*<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:09:21" />

## Background and stated radicalization

Per Kiriakou's relay of the CIA name-traces that followed: *"They see that he was educated in North Carolina and he lived with an American family as an exchange student."* On KSM's self-stated radicalization: *"He said later that what radicalized him was news video of an Israeli soldier with his boot on the neck of a Palestinian woman, and he just decided then, 'I'm going to kill every Jew I can and every American who supports the Jews.'"*<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:09:52" /><Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:10:25" />

## Identification via Abu Zubaydah

The [CIA](/wiki/cia) knew KSM only by the alias Mukhtar prior to [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah)'s cooperation with FBI agent [Ali Soufan](/wiki/ali-soufan). According to [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), when Soufan asked Abu Zubaydah about Mukhtar, Abu Zubaydah laughed and responded: *"You don't know who Mukhtar is? It's Khaled Shik Muhammad."*<Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="43:51" /> Kiriakou described this as the first time the CIA had ever encountered the name Khalid Sheikh Mohammed. Once they had the name, they were able to target him. Approximately one year later, he was captured in Rawalpindi, Pakistan.<Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="45:22" />

KSM had previously been responsible for planning the [Bojinka plot](/wiki/bojinka-plot), the 1996 Manila-based plot to hijack fourteen 747s and crash them into buildings along the West Coast of the United States. Kiriakou described him as *"the bona fide number three in al-Qaeda and the head of al-Qaeda operations."*<Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="44:52" />

## Waterboarding count and false confession

[Khalid Sheikh Mohammed](/wiki/khalid-sheikh-mohammed) was [waterboarded](/wiki/waterboarding) one hundred and eighty-seven times, according to [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou).<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="26:00" />

Among the confessions KSM produced under waterboarding was a claim that he had personally beheaded journalist Daniel Pearl. Kiriakou cited this as a textbook example of [learned helplessness](/wiki/learned-helplessness) producing false intelligence: KSM's arm hair, visible in the execution video, does not match the arm hair of the man who actually beheaded Pearl. Kiriakou's summary of KSM's implied logic: *"My arm's that hairy."* The confession was false, and waterboarding generated it.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="26:30" />

## First named by Abu Zubaydah

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says the CIA first learned the name [Khalid Sheikh Mohammed](/wiki/khalid-sheikh-mohammed) from [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) during [Ali Soufan](/wiki/ali-soufan)'s rapport-based interrogation. They had known only of a dangerous planner using the nom de guerre *"Mukhtar"* but had no idea who he really was until Abu Zubaydah supplied the name.<Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="08:25" />
