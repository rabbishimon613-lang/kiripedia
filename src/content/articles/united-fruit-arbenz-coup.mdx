**United Fruit and the Arbenz coup** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the 1954 CIA overthrow of Guatemala's elected president. He traces it to a Boston industrialist who, while building a railway from Guatemala City to the coast, discovered his workers eating an unknown fruit — the banana — and founded what became the United Fruit Company (today Chiquita).<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="26:27" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="27:28" /> When President Jacobo Árbenz moved in 1953 to nationalize the banana crop, three United Fruit board members turned out to be Secretary of State John Foster Dulles, CIA director [Allen Dulles](/wiki/allen-dulles), and the brother of Eisenhower's secretary. The CIA overthrew Árbenz and installed a military dictatorship that brutalized Guatemalans for half a century — and Chiquita still owns the bananas.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="28:00" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="28:32" />
