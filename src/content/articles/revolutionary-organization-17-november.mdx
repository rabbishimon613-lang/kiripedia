**Revolutionary Organization 17 November** (also *17 November*, *17N*, or *N17*) was a Greek far-left urban guerrilla group active between 1975 and 2002. It was the deadliest terrorist organization in the history of Greece, responsible for 28 confirmed murders including the [CIA](/wiki/cia) Athens station chief [Richard Welch](/wiki/richard-welch), two American defense attachés, a U.S. Air Force technical sergeant, and the British defense attaché [Stephen Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders).<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:29:29" />

During the late 1990s the group's continued operation caused Athens to be rated "critical threat" — the highest classification — for terrorism against American personnel. The U.S. embassy in Athens spent more on security during this period than its counterpart in Beirut.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:30:01" />

## Membership and identification

17 November was extraordinarily successful at concealing the identities of its members. Despite a quarter-century of operations and an extensive U.S.–Greek intelligence effort, no member was identified prior to the group's accidental exposure in 2002. Western intelligence services maintained a list of approximately 500 suspects; the group ultimately turned out to have only about a dozen members.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:30:33" />

## Methods and weapons

17 November hits typically used two operatives on a motorcycle — a driver and a shooter on the back — who would approach the target's vehicle, place two rounds in the victim's head, and depart. No witness ever produced a usable identification of either rider.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:36:52" />

Every confirmed 17 November hit was carried out with the same .45 caliber pistol, which Western intelligence came to refer to as *the Welch .45* after its use in the group's first murder. See [The Welch .45](/wiki/welch-45).<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:53:38" />

In some operations the group also used long guns; the round that killed [Stephen Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders) in March 2000 was armor-piercing and severed Saunders's right hand from his body, leaving it on the floor of his vehicle.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:40:05" />

## Funding and weapons sourcing

Western intelligence suspected during the 1990s that 17 November received weapons from Carlos the Jackal and from Libya. The Carlos connection turned out to apply not to 17 November but to a separate Greek group, Popular Revolutionary Struggle; the Libyan connection was partly accurate as a source for other Greek groups. 17 November itself was funded and armed entirely domestically.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:31:04" />

## Operations

### December 23, 1975 — Richard Welch

The group's first attack was the assassination of [Richard Welch](/wiki/richard-welch), CIA station chief in Athens, in front of his home in the suburb of Psychiko. The hit was carried out by two gunmen on foot with two more operatives in a getaway car (a male driver and a female lookout). One gunman shouted *"Richard Welch, get out of the car,"* announced *"Richard Welch, you have been convicted of crimes against the Greek people and you have been sentenced to death,"* and shot Welch three times in the chest in front of his wife.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:53:38" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:54:09" /> See [Richard Welch](/wiki/richard-welch) for the full account.

### Other American victims

In addition to Welch, 17 November killed two American defense attachés in Athens, permanently disabled the embassy's DEA representative, and attempted to kill a State Department officer.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:32:39" /> One victim was an Air Force technical sergeant, killed while doing his laundry in the basement laundry room of his apartment building.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:29:29" />

### March 2000 — Stephen Saunders

The British defense attaché [Stephen Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders) was assassinated by 17 November in March 2000 in heavy traffic on Kifissias Avenue in Athens. The original target for that morning's operation had been [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), the U.S. embassy's lead [CIA](/wiki/cia) officer working the 17 November problem. In a manifesto mailed to a Greek left-wing newspaper after the attack, the group explicitly stated they had identified *"the big spy"* (Kiriakou) but elected to kill the *"war criminal Saunders"* instead because Kiriakou's armored vehicle and arms made the hit too risky.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:43:14" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:43:45" />

The naming of Kiriakou in the manifesto triggered the emergency exfiltration of his family from Athens within hours; embassy staff packed and shipped their household possessions in their absence. See [Stephen Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders) for the full account.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:44:46" />

## The crimper peripheral

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s sole confirmed recruitment of a 17 November–adjacent individual was of a peripheral figure — not a group member — who had nonetheless personally built and planted several of the bombs Western intelligence had attributed to 17 November on the basis of a recurring forensic signature: a distinctive **crimp** in the way the bomber twisted the wires inside each device. *"He had a very unique way of connecting the wires and twisting them. So we could tell — in fact, we had a name for him based on the style of the crimp."*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="17:47" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="18:20" />

On recruitment in approximately the late 1990s the asset volunteered, unprompted, that he had personally built and planted the bombs that detonated at a Citibank branch, a McDonald's, and an Inter-American Insurance Company office in Athens — closing six years of forensic guesswork. He demonstrated the bomb-construction technique in front of Kiriakou at a subsequent meeting using supplies he had brought along.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="18:20" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="18:50" />

The asset was, in 17 November's organizational typology, *"one of the many many peripheral figures that … had kind of brushed up against 17 November but never were asked to join the group."* He had then migrated to a separate anarchist circle — *"what they wanted to do was just blow shit up for no reason other than to shout 'yay anarchy.'"*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="19:21" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="23:31" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="24:02" />

His on-the-spot recruitment vulnerability was financial — he had impregnated his girlfriend and could not afford his daughter's care. Kiriakou paid him regularly, and on one occasion bought $5,000 of the asset's wife's jewelry at the station chief's authorization (*"call it an early Christmas bonus"*) when the asset turned up at a 2:00 a.m. meeting in a desperate state with a paper bag of gold pieces.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="19:53" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="21:27" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="21:57" />

Kiriakou attempted to contact the asset on a post-CIA Greece visit and learned from his son that the asset had died of lung cancer two years earlier.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="24:33" />

## Dissolution

The group was rolled up accidentally in 2002. A 17 November operative was carrying a bomb concealed in a paper bag, intending to place it under the car of a Greek shipowner in Piraeus, when the device detonated prematurely. The blast severed both of the operative's hands and destroyed his right eye. Believing himself to be dying, he confessed to his role, named every other member of the group, and disclosed the locations of the safe house, the weapons cache, and the bombs. He survived. Greek authorities used the confession to arrest the entire membership; the group has not operated since.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:42:10" />

## Related groups

Several other Greek far-left organizations operated alongside 17 November during the same period, including:

- Popular Revolutionary Struggle — recipient of weapons from Carlos the Jackal
- Revolutionary Nuclei
- *Sons of the Anarchic* (full name uncertain)<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:30:33" />

Most of these were primarily anarchist; 17 November and Popular Revolutionary Struggle were the only groups Western intelligence regarded as full terrorist organizations during this period.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:31:04" />

## Manifesto language

In the manifesto mailed to a Greek left-wing newspaper after the assassination of [Stephen Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders), 17 November wrote in Greek: *'Eda meton megalo kataskopos'* — 'we saw the big spy' — and explained that because [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was armed and driving an armored car, they had elected to 'carry out the revolutionary sentence on the war criminal Saunders' instead.<Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-cia-marriages-relationships" t="6:43" />

## The Athens posting (Mark Bouris, March 2026)

The Athens position was advertised as requiring either Greek or Arabic language fluency, covering two Greek groups — [17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november) and Popular Revolutionary Struggle — as well as several Arab organizations including the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine (PFLP) General Command, the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine (DFLP), the Abu Nidal organization, and Libyan networks. [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) noted he was the only [CIA](/wiki/cia) officer fluent in both Greek and Arabic, and received the posting on that basis.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="15:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="15:30" />

Shortly after his arrival, the Arab side of his target list effectively dissolved. The PFLP, DFLP, and Abu Nidal organization had all aged into irrelevance — old men who had retired and moved to Damascus. The focus shifted entirely to the Greek groups.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="15:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="16:00" />

## Stephen Saunders — the BMW joke

At a party at the French defence attaché's residence, [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) had just taken delivery of a [CIA](/wiki/cia)-provided Level IV armoured BMW 540 — the first of its model in Greece. The armour load was such that he could not obtain insurance in Greece and had to insure the vehicle through a German company. [Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders), standing with Kiriakou, the French attaché, and the American attaché, made fun of the car: *"You Americans, you're so paranoid about security. This is an EU country. It's a NATO country. What are you so afraid of?"*<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="17:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="17:30" />

Kiriakou's reply: *"You Brits live in a dream world if you think that because they have pretty beaches and palm trees that they're not going to kill you if they have the chance. Believe me, if they have the chance, they're going to kill you."*<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="17:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="18:00" />

## The assassination and the manifesto

[17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november) assassinated [Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders) in broad daylight in April 2000, on the main road between their residential neighbourhood and the British and American embassies. [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was approximately a quarter of a mile behind Saunders when it happened. A taxi driver pulled Saunders from the vehicle and rushed him to hospital. He died twenty minutes after arrival.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="18:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="18:30" />

The group had a practice of leaving a manifesto at the scene of each attack, either dropped in place, deposited in a rubbish bin with a call to a leftist newspaper, or occasionally mailed. They did not leave a manifesto when they killed Saunders. Four months passed. Then [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) arrived at work one morning and his supervisor told him: *"They dropped a manifesto. They talk about you."*<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="18:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="19:00" />

The manifesto said in Greek: *"Είδαμε τον μεγαλοκατάσκοπο"* — *"We saw the big spy."* It explained that the group had identified Kiriakou, knew he was armed and driving an armoured car, and had therefore elected to *"carry out the revolutionary sentence on the war criminal Saunders"* instead.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="19:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="19:30" />

## Evacuation

Kiriakou's supervisor told him he had to leave immediately. Kiriakou's sons were six and three years old; they had been dropped at school that morning. He was put in an armoured car and driven to the airport. Armoured cars were dispatched to collect his two sons and his wife separately. At the airport, [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) told his wife: *"I am so sorry."* She said: *"I want a divorce. I'm not doing this anymore."* They flew separately — she to Cleveland, he to Washington.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="19:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="20:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="20:30" />

He continued working the [17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november) case from [CIA](/wiki/cia) headquarters. The group was destroyed eighteen months later, in April 2002, when one of the bomb-makers was transporting a device intended for the car of a Greek shipping magnate. It detonated prematurely, blowing off both his hands and his right eye. As he was bleeding to death, he confessed to a policeman, named all of his comrades, and gave the location of their weapons cache. Police saved his life. The entire organization was arrested. The founder, the lead assassin, and the three lead bomb-makers received sentences totalling 1,765 years.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="20:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="21:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="21:30" />

## Favorite CIA work — Marxist ideology and 17 November (Feb 2026)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) identified his work against [Revolutionary Organization 17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november) as the most fascinating and most important thing he did in his [CIA](/wiki/cia) career — more so than his Pakistan counterterrorism work. His reasoning: the Athens assignment required immersing himself in Marxist and communist ideology, particularly Eurocommunism, to an extent he had never previously engaged with. This opened what he described as a whole new world and way of thinking. He also had to reconcile that ideology with the group's use of it to justify political assassination — describing this as *"cloak and dagger"* in the fullest sense.<Cite s="2026-02-10-deep-focus-favorite-cia-work-nato-espionage-privacy" t="0:30" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deep-focus-favorite-cia-work-nato-espionage-privacy" t="1:00" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deep-focus-favorite-cia-work-nato-espionage-privacy" t="1:30" />

He also described a second operation he considered unforgettable: he was asked to facilitate the hypnosis of a witness to an assassination. The hypnotist was the president of the American Society of Hypnotists and had fifty years of experience. The witness did not speak English, requiring [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) to serve as the translator and to count backwards softly in Greek to bring him under. The subject held his arm raised for four hours while under hypnosis. When the hypnotist snapped his fingers to bring him partway back, the arm dropped and the subject — apparently disoriented and overwhelmed — immediately ran to the bathroom and vomited. The hypnotist told Kiriakou: *"I've been doing this for fifty years. I've read about that in the literature, but I've never seen anybody vomit when they came out of hypnosis. Never saw anything like it in my life."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deep-focus-favorite-cia-work-nato-espionage-privacy" t="2:00" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deep-focus-favorite-cia-work-nato-espionage-privacy" t="2:30" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deep-focus-favorite-cia-work-nato-espionage-privacy" t="3:00" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deep-focus-favorite-cia-work-nato-espionage-privacy" t="3:30" />

## Greece — most difficult recruitment environment

[Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described Greece as a more difficult posting than the Middle East, despite his personal love of the country. The challenge: people he needed to befriend in order to recruit were genuinely physically dangerous, and his Greek American background worked against him. Left-wing Greek militants considered Greek Americans to be *"the worst part of both Greece and America"* — right-wing extremists and traitors — which generated hostility that he described as considerably more intense than anything he encountered in Arab countries.<Cite s="2026-02-10-deep-focus-favorite-cia-work-nato-espionage-privacy" t="23:30" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deep-focus-favorite-cia-work-nato-espionage-privacy" t="24:00" />

## The mastermind's release (May 2026)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) disclosed — as current news at the time of recording — that Alexandros Yotopoulos, the mastermind of [17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november) and architect of all its assassinations, had been released from prison three days before the episode recorded. He described his reaction as genuine distress, to the point of losing sleep.<Cite s="2026-05-26-inside-cia-lizzie-israel-deep-state" t="4:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-inside-cia-lizzie-israel-deep-state" t="5:00" />

The mechanism: Greece is the only country in the European Union with a statute of limitations on murder — twenty years. The five principal 17 November members had been sentenced to 1,765 years, which in practice meant twenty years under Greek law. When that period ended in 2022, the American Embassy protested and the releases were initially blocked — but the delay did not hold. Separately, one of the shooters in the [Richard Welch](/wiki/richard-welch) assassination, Yiannis Serifis, had already been released on the same statute-of-limitations grounds.<Cite s="2026-05-26-inside-cia-lizzie-israel-deep-state" t="4:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-inside-cia-lizzie-israel-deep-state" t="9:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-inside-cia-lizzie-israel-deep-state" t="10:00" />

## *"Make like Lafargue"* — the manifesto suicide reference

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described a phrase in one of [17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november)'s manifestos that stumped the entire [CIA](/wiki/cia) station: *"Make like Lafargue."* Nobody understood it. He triggered a meeting with a communist source and asked for an explanation. The source was dismissive: *"I thought you were smart. Lafargue was married to Marx's daughter."* Kiriakou still didn't follow. The source explained: Paul Lafargue was a Marxist who fell in love with Karl Marx's daughter, brought Marxism to France, and founded the French Communist Party. After thirty years, both Lafargue and Marx's daughter concluded that Marxism was hollow and had nothing behind it. Together, they went to the roof of their apartment building and jumped — committing suicide hand in hand. The manifesto was telling the German ambassador to do the same.<Cite s="2026-04-07-deep-focus-mideast-revenge-cia-territories" t="07:00" /><Cite s="2026-04-07-deep-focus-mideast-revenge-cia-territories" t="07:30" /><Cite s="2026-04-07-deep-focus-mideast-revenge-cia-territories" t="08:00" /><Cite s="2026-04-07-deep-focus-mideast-revenge-cia-territories" t="08:30" /><Cite s="2026-04-07-deep-focus-mideast-revenge-cia-territories" t="09:00" />
