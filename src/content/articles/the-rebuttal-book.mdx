**The rebuttal book** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s name for the volume with which a group of retired senior [CIA](/wiki/cia) officers answered the [Senate torture report](/wiki/senate-torture-report). He describes it as the agency leadership closing ranks in public after the report's release: *"a big group of retired senior CIA leaders, George Tenet, Jim Pavitt, big important people, Jose Rodriguez and Cofer Black. They all attached their names to a rebuttal. A book that was published saying torture worked, torture saved lives, we would do it again if we could, we didn't make any mistakes."*<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="28:48" />

His own verdict on it is one word — *"outrageous"* — and the detail he fixes on is not the argument but the vocabulary. Asked whether the signatories at least conceded that the programme had been torture, he answers that they did not: *"They called it enhanced interrogation techniques."*<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="28:48" /><Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="29:18" />

He sets the book alongside what its signatories were paid elsewhere. [Tenet](/wiki/george-tenet), he says, *"got 6 million for his book"* and [Rodriguez](/wiki/jose-rodriguez) *"got 4 million for his book,"* and the group generally moved on to seats on the boards of American defence contractors and to work as television commentators — against which he sets the fact that *"none of the other people involved with the torture program, none served any time."*<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="1:09:22" /><Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="1:09:53" />
