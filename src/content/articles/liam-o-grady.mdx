**Liam O'Grady** is a federal judge in [the Eastern District of Virginia](/wiki/eastern-district-of-virginia) who sentenced [Daniel Hale](/wiki/daniel-hale), and one of the few figures in that court [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes without hostility.

## The sentence

*"When Daniel Hale was sentenced in the notorious district court for the Eastern District of Virginia, Judge Liam O'Grady shocked everybody by giving him a sentence of 45 months in prison."*<Cite s="2023-03-05-enema-of-the-state-enema-of-the-state" t="16:24" /> In a district Kiriakou characterises by its conviction rate and its severity, the surprise ran the other way.

## The recommendation that was ignored

*"Judge O'Grady recommended that Daniel go to a low security prison where he could be treated for depression and addiction issues. The US [Bureau of Prisons](/wiki/bureau-of-prisons) ignored that recommendation, and instead sent him to the [communications management unit](/wiki/communications-management-unit) of the supermax prison at Marion, Illinois."*<Cite s="2023-03-05-enema-of-the-state-enema-of-the-state" t="16:24" />

Kiriakou's assessment of the destination is unambiguous: *"this is one of the two most secure and most dreaded prisons in the entire US prison system,"* where Hale was *"incarcerated with some of the most dangerous people in the world, including terrorists and Mafia leaders."*<Cite s="2023-03-05-enema-of-the-state-enema-of-the-state" t="16:55" />

The episode is his standing example of how little a sentencing judge controls once the sentence is passed.
