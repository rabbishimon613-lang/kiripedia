**Iranian sleeper cells** are a threat [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) treats as real but widely misplaced. He confirms the basic fact — the IRGC and the Ministry of Intelligence and Security *"have sleeper cells all over the world"* — and then narrows the map considerably.<Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="03:40" />

## Where they are

His doubt about the domestic threat is explicit: *"I doubt that they have any in the United States, or Canada, or the UK."*<Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="03:40" /><Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="04:11" />

Where he does place them: *"They absolutely have them in places like Pakistan, Afghanistan, Thailand, Indonesia, Argentina even."*<Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="04:11" />

## The lone wolf

The category he considers *"just as dangerous"* is the attacker with no organisation behind him. His example is a man who, waving an Iranian flag, opened fire on bar patrons in Austin, Texas, killing four people and wounding seven before being shot and killed.<Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="04:11" /><Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="04:42" />

## Consistently poor operatives

On the quality of Iranian clandestine work, Kiriakou is dismissive and consistent with his assessment elsewhere of Iranian intelligence: *"It's all good. It's never been good. And it's because they don't get good training."* Russia and China help *"a little bit,"* but not enough to change the picture.<Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="05:12" />

## The target that would actually matter

The vulnerability he identifies in the Gulf is not military. If Iran were to attack an American military installation in Abu Dhabi or Dubai, *"nobody's going to really care."*<Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="05:44" />

What would register is a landmark — he names the Burj Al Arab, the sail-shaped hotel, as the kind of target whose destruction would carry a wholly different weight.<Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="05:44" />
