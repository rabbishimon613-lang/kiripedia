**Robert Mueller** was the Director of the FBI during the years immediately following September 11, 2001. Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou): on July 31, 2002 — the day CIA Director George Tenet went to the White House and successfully asked President George W. Bush to remove the FBI from the CIA black site holding [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) — Mueller withdrew the entire FBI presence from the country.<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:11:28" /><Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:11:59" />

Kiriakou: *"Robert Mueller, who was the FBI director at the time, decided to withdraw all FBI personnel — not just from the secret site but from the country that the secret site was in. He's like, 'We know what these CIA guys are going to do; they're going to go completely nuts; we don't want any part of it.' Everybody withdrew within 48 hours. The CIA began to torture Abu Zubaydah."*<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:12:30" /><Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:13:02" />

## Pulling the FBI out of the country

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says FBI director [Robert Mueller](/wiki/robert-mueller), knowing what the CIA was about to do to [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah), not only pulled [Ali Soufan](/wiki/ali-soufan) but withdrew *all* FBI personnel from the country holding the secret prison — not wanting his people even present while the CIA tortured.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:29:13" />

## The 2007–2008 declination letter

Kiriakou says Mueller played a further, separate role in his life: during an FBI investigation of Kiriakou running from December 2007 to December 2008, the Bureau sent his attorney [Plato Cacheris](/wiki/plato-cacheris) an unusual declination letter, concluding Kiriakou had committed no crime and closing the case — a step Kiriakou says the FBI rarely takes. Kiriakou believes Mueller, who he says felt strongly the CIA's torture program was itself a crime, personally instructed the counterintelligence division to send it.<Cite s="2026-04-20-dead-drop-s1e24-moment-of-truth" t="21:10" /><Cite s="2026-04-20-dead-drop-s1e24-moment-of-truth" t="21:42" />
