[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described **Aldrich Ames** as an example of catastrophic institutional failure at the [CIA](/wiki/cia). Ames was not a low-level employee; he was a GS-15 who had been promoted to chief of counterintelligence for the Soviet branch — meaning he had the actual names of every CIA source who had penetrated the Kremlin or the KGB.<Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="12:30" />

His initial motivation was mundane: a $50,000 credit-card debt. In his first meeting with Russian intelligence, he provided the identities of two CIA agents in exchange for the money needed to pay it off. Both agents were executed immediately. He continued spying, ultimately causing the deaths of twelve people and accumulating $2 million.<Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="9:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="10:00" />

His lifestyle made the betrayal obvious to anyone paying attention: a $2 million house in Arlington, Virginia; a Jaguar; an in-ground pool with a gazebo he had constructed — on a $70,000 annual salary. He was also known by everyone in the office to be drunk throughout the workday.<Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="10:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="10:30" />

A report was nonetheless sent to a 27-year-old security officer, who sat on the file for eighteen months while Ames continued to spy. The officer eventually drove to Ames's house, looked at it, said *"Wow, that's a really nice house,"* and left.<Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="10:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="11:00" />

It was [Sandy Grimes](/wiki/sandy-grimes), a [CIA](/wiki/cia) analyst, who broke the case. She noticed that every time Ames traveled to Mexico City, he made large bank deposits within days of returning — $100,000 or $150,000 at a time. Pre-9/11, there was no anti-money-laundering reporting requirement for deposits under $10,000; deposits over that amount did not automatically trigger investigation at the time. Grimes drew the connection and identified Ames as a spy.<Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="11:30" />

In the post-arrest review — known in the [CIA](/wiki/cia) as a *"hot wash"* — Grimes submitted a paper to the director recommending that 27 people be reprimanded up the chain of command. Ames had been a known alcoholic for twenty years; rather than being managed out, he had been promoted repeatedly to become someone else's problem, until he was given the most sensitive possible position. Director John Deutch chose instead to place *"a very strongly worded letter"* in the files of seven of the twenty-seven. Grimes quit. The year was 1993.<Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="12:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="13:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-02-carlos-watson-conversations-cia-deep-state" t="13:30" />
