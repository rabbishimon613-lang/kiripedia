Kiriakou had spent the session arguing that the persecution of national security whistleblowers is bipartisan — that Joe Biden was just as bad as Donald Trump and Trump just as bad as Barack Obama — when he asked to add one last thing.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="1:04:23" />

## Who Hale was

There was, he said, a very important whistleblower out there right then by the name of Daniel Hale. Hale is the drone whistleblower, who went public with the fact that the United States was killing civilians — women, children and the elderly — in its drone programme.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="1:04:23" />

He had been scooped up by the Justice Department the previous week and was incarcerated at a federal holding facility in Alexandria, Virginia — which is to say, Kiriakou noted, the Eastern District of Virginia, the district in which he had himself been charged and in which no national security defendant has ever won.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="1:04:23" /><Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="1:04:59" />

## The condition he described

Kiriakou's description of Hale's state was blunt and, by the standards of a public event, unusually specific. The poor guy, he said, was terrified. He was on suicide watch. He was going through some dark days.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="1:04:59" />

## The ask

What he asked the audience for was small and concrete. He had posted Hale's address on Facebook and on Twitter. If you have five minutes, he said, send him a letter, send him a postcard — just tell him that he is not forgotten.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="1:04:59" />

The request draws directly on Kiriakou's own experience of incarceration. He has said that so many people offered him a helping hand when he was at his lowest, and reached out to him proactively, that helping other whistleblowers afterwards was the least he could do.<Cite s="2026-05-05-whistleblower-of-the-week-john-kiriakou-cia-whistleblower-exposed-tortu" t="30:38" />

What he was asking for, in other words, was the thing he says worked on him: not advocacy, not legal help, but evidence delivered to a cell that somebody outside knew the man's name.
