**Russiagate** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s term for the recurring claim that Russia covertly interfered in U.S. elections, which he rejects as *"just a lie."* The Mueller report, he notes, found no Russian interference in 2016, though the government keeps repeating the charge under a *"take our word for it, it's classified"* posture.<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="04:10" /> The DNC emails, he says, were authentic primary-source documents leaked in person — NSA technical director [Bill Binney](/wiki/bill-binney) showed the upload speed made a remote hack impossible, and it was [Craig Murray](/wiki/craig-murray) who couriered the thumb drive to WikiLeaks.<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="05:11" /><Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="05:45" /> As for the fabled Facebook operation, Russia spent roughly $50,000 — half after election day, in already-red or already-blue states, mostly cat emojis and jokes — against billions in campaign spending.<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="08:21" /><Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="09:22" /> In a separate telling he puts the same $50,000 into YouTube ads rather than Facebook — again none of it political, again about half spent after the election was already over, and the ads themselves nothing more than memes of *"kittens and puppies and flowers."*<Cite s="2026-06-16-julian-dorey-daily-mkultra-twin-experiment" t="50:29" /> Kiriakou's blunter view: Hillary Clinton *"was probably the only Democrat in America who couldn't beat Donald Trump."*<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="09:54" />
