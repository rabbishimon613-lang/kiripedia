**CIA and FBI embedded at social media firms** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s claim, drawn from the Twitter Files that Matt Taibbi exposed, that active-duty CIA and FBI officers were placed — often undercover — inside Twitter, Facebook and other social-media platforms.<Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="31:48" /> He asks why active-duty officers would hold such positions and answers that there can be only one reason: *"Because they're spying on Americans."*<Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="32:19" />
