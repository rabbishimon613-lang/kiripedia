**Vladimir Putin** is the President of Russia. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s treatment of Putin runs consistently against the Washington narrative: a rational, competent leader whose overtures to the West were rebuffed, not a madman bent on empire.

## The NATO overtures

Kiriakou recounts that when Putin first became president, at the tail end of [Bill Clinton](/wiki/bill-clinton)'s presidency, he floated the idea of Russia joining NATO.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:23:38" /> Years later, at a summit with [Barack Obama](/wiki/barack-obama), Putin raised it again: *"I spoke with Clinton years ago about Russia joining NATO. It's something that we would like to explore."* Obama said no — and Putin answered that the Americans had lied to him about other countries joining NATO before.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:25:11" /><Cite s="2025-02-28-epstein-bin-laden-china-israel" t="1:15:26" /> Kiriakou pairs this with Putin's standing complaint about NATO's eastward march: told that the non-expansion pledge had been made to the Soviet Union, which no longer existed, Putin's reply was — then there is no more Soviet Union, so can I join?<Cite s="2025-03-29-lionel-nation" t="23:04" />

Why refuse a Russia asking to be friends? Kiriakou's answer is cynical by his own admission: the policy is really determined by defense contractors — the United States lives in a permanent wartime economy.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:30:59" />

## Against the madman narrative

Kiriakou has never believed Putin wanted to conquer Ukraine or Eastern Europe.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:23:38" /> He dismisses portrayals of Putin issuing nonsensical orders and pacing hysterically: the meetings Putin runs are *"very competent and very on point."*<Cite s="2022-12-27-the-roundtable-gonzalo-l-the-roundtable-gonzalo-l" t="43:56" /> He likewise rejects the reflex of reducing Putin to *"nothing more than a menace."*<Cite s="2022-07-24-scheerpost-scheer-intelligence-kiriakou-on-biden-s-grove" t="42:43" /> And he points out the historical whiplash: Putin is the anti-communist that the U.S. government itself backed in the Gorbachev and Yeltsin years.<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="49:05" />

On the 2023 Prigozhin mutiny, Kiriakou mocked the New York Times' banner analysis of how Putin was "almost overthrown" — a split in the Russian military is a loss, he notes, not a victory.<Cite s="2023-08-09-london-real" t="14:12" />

## Putin and Trump

Kiriakou notes Putin said he preferred Trump to [Biden](/wiki/joe-biden) because [Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) was so unpredictable — you never knew when he would propose something genuinely great.<Cite s="2025-02-28-epstein-bin-laden-china-israel" t="50:16" /> That unpredictability cuts both ways: Kiriakou has described Trump expecting Putin to come to the table on Ukraine, and Putin declining.<Cite s="2025-05-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:39" />

## Odds and ends

Kiriakou recounts that [Ray McGovern](/wiki/ray-mcgovern) traveled to the Kremlin with Oliver Stone, and Putin said: *"Oh yeah, let me meet with Oliver and Ray."*<Cite s="2025-05-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:34" /> He also tells the story of the journalist barred from Russia for reporting on the 1999 apartment-building bombings — in Kiriakou's telling, one of only two people in the Western world reporting that story, the other being FSB insider Alexander Litvinenko, later poisoned in Britain.<Cite s="2025-02-28-epstein-bin-laden-china-israel" t="2:00:53" /> And he cites Putin's needling question about American jihadi-backing in Chechnya: *"You guys were backing these jihadis — why were you doing that when you're supposed to be fighting this war on terror?"*<Cite s="2024-04-12-american-exception-the-oily-war-of-drugs-john-kiriakou-dcc29" t="45:32" />
