**Allen Dulles** was the long-serving CIA director, in office until January 1961, whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) calls a *"monster."*<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="28:32" /> A board member of the [United Fruit Company](/wiki/united-fruit-arbenz-coup), Dulles ran the CIA that overthrew Guatemala's Árbenz, and his bust once greeted visitors at CIA headquarters.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="28:56" /> By the time Kiriakou joined, Dulles was *"kind of forgotten,"* his era of *"kicking asses and taking names"* from 1952 to 1960 long past. But Kiriakou distinguishes Dulles from counterintelligence chief [James Angleton](/wiki/james-jesus-angleton): Dulles *"knew he had been doing some filthy stuff,"* reportedly saying on his deathbed he would *"see my boys back in hell."*<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="29:03" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="37:57" />

Kiriakou confirms the account that Dulles went to former President Truman's home to try to talk him out of pursuing his public complaints against the CIA — made in a Washington Post op-ed shortly after the Kennedy assassination that the agency demanded be pulled from the paper's afternoon edition — calling Dulles *"a very dark figure in American history"* and noting both Dulles brothers were dark figures.<Cite s="2026-01-01-fair-observer" t="11:00" />
