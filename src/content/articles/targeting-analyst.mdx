A **targeting analyst** is, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, a specialized CIA analyst assigned almost exclusively to the Directorate of Operations and its Counterterrorism Center, distinct from an intelligence analyst who reads all-source reporting and writes papers.<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="17:56" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="18:26" /> The targeting analyst instead pours through millions of pieces of data, *"much of it metadata,"* to physically locate a person — for only two reasons, to capture or to kill.<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="18:57" /> Kiriakou flew a targeting-analyst friend to Islamabad, who over several weeks built a butcher-paper *"spiderweb"* around [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah-capture)'s phone number — concentric layers of every contact — narrowing the search to 14 sites.<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="17:25" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="19:28" />
