**Signature strikes** are the drone-targeting doctrine, exposed by [Daniel Hale](/wiki/daniel-hale)'s leak in [The Drone Papers](/wiki/the-drone-papers), under which the United States kills people based on patterns of behavior or association rather than confirmed identity.<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="12:37" /> [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says the *"signature of a terrorist"* could be as little as doing *"jumping jacks"* or carrying *"a bag of something over their shoulder."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="19:21" /> He says operators are explicit that *"any male over the age of 12 is a legitimate target"* on the theory that *"any male over the age of 12 can pick up an AK-47 and shoot an American soldier"* — *"not that they have the gun, but that they could pick up a gun."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="19:52" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="20:25" /> Kiriakou compares the practice to *"the Gestapo rounding up all the people that they want to murder in the center of town."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="19:52" /> He adds that when officers radioed asking permission to launch, superiors told them it was *"just a formality — you're supposed to say launch."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="20:57" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="21:29" />
