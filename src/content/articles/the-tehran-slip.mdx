The **Tehran slip** is John Kiriakou's account of a remark made in a secure video teleconference of the national security principals a day or two before the March 2003 invasion of Iraq.

## Kiriakou's position in the room

After the capture of Abu Zubaydah, Kiriakou was made executive assistant to the CIA's deputy director for operations, and Iraq became his portfolio. In that role he was the note-taker at Director George Tenet's meetings with the principals. He has described Tenet sitting at the head of the table with himself directly behind him. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="40:36" />

He attaches to this a rule about the CIA's institutional position: the agency, in his description, is a support agency, so the director's job in such a meeting is to answer a question if one is asked and otherwise to sit silently, because the CIA does not make policy. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="41:06" />

## The briefing

On the video screens, by Kiriakou's account, were Dick Cheney, Condoleezza Rice, Colin Powell, the head of the National Security Agency and a general whose name he says he cannot recall. The general was asked for a briefing and described the disposition of divisions and armour along the border, the movements planned from the south and the west, and the ships offshore. He then said that if all went as planned they could be in Tehran by August. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="41:37" /> <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="42:08" />

Kiriakou has described writing it down — *"Tehran, Tehran"* — when Tenet reached forward, turned off his microphone, turned round and asked him whether the man had said Tehran or Baghdad. Kiriakou answered that he had said Tehran. Tenet then asked him whether these people had lost their minds, turned the microphone back on, and sat there. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="42:38" />

## Afterwards

Kiriakou has said he went back to the deputy director's office and asked whether he had known they were going to attack Iran. The deputy director answered that they had been talking about it for a few months, and that he could not imagine it actually happening. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="43:11" /> Kiriakou's own view is that the only reason it did not happen was that American forces became bogged down in southern Iraq. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="43:11" />
