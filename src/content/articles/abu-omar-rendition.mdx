The **Abu Omar rendition** was the CIA's kidnapping of Abu Omar, a cleric in Milan, Italy, who was sent to Egypt to undergo torture — an instance of [extraordinary rendition](/wiki/extraordinary-rendition).<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="03:11" /> [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says a journalist writing a book on the case in the summer of 2008 asked him to introduce him to the officers involved; Kiriakou said he had not worked with the *"kidnappers."* But when the journalist named an officer mentioned in Kiriakou's own book, Kiriakou confirmed the man's last name — and *"that's what they hung me on."* The espionage charges came from his torture interviews, but the conviction rested on this confirmation.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="03:43" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="04:13" />
