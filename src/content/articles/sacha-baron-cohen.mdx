**Sacha Baron Cohen** is a British comedian and actor. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes him as *"a comedic genius — and I don't use the word genius lightly. I've only worked with a handful of people in my life that I would consider to be geniuses. He's a genius."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="42:34" />

## The *Bruno* advisory role

Kiriakou was brought in as a Middle East script advisor for *Bruno* by a mutual friend who worked as a fixer and connector. Baron Cohen wanted to film portions of the movie in the Middle East — specifically, to recruit retired terrorists to react on camera to explicit photographs — and needed expert guidance on navigating regional intelligence services.<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="41:01" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="44:37" />

Kiriakou proposed filming in Libya (which was *"begging us to film movies there"* under Gaddafi), Morocco (rejected because its architecture would be immediately recognizable), and Syria (rejected after Baron Cohen went to the Syrian consulate in Newport Beach without authorization). On Syria, Kiriakou had advised working through the Syrian ambassador. Baron Cohen went to the Newport Beach consulate instead; *"as soon as he walked through the door, the guy came from behind the glass partition and said, 'I know who you are. I know what you do. You are not welcome in Syria.'"* Baron Cohen called Kiriakou at midnight: *"John, I think I fucked up, mate."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="41:33" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="43:36" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="44:06" />

## The Jordan incident

Kiriakou repeatedly warned Baron Cohen that proceeding to Jordan without informing the Jordanian Intelligence Service would backfire, describing it as *"one of the best services in the world."* Baron Cohen refused: *"Can't break cover. Can't break character. Don't want to tell the Jordanians."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="42:34" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="43:05" />

Baron Cohen flew to Amman a day ahead of Kiriakou. At the airport, a man was waiting with a sign reading *"Sacha Baron Cohen."* A limousine drove them to the Royal Palace, where King Abdullah II greeted him personally: *"Sacha Baron Cohen, I'm your biggest fan. Borat? I thought I was going to pee my pants."* The director of the Jordanian Intelligence Service was standing beside the king, who told Baron Cohen: *"Anything you need, you call him. Welcome to Jordan."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="46:11" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="46:43" />

Kiriakou's verdict: *"I told you before you even landed, they knew what you were doing. They're that good."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="47:14" />

## *The Spy*

Baron Cohen later played [Eli Cohen](/wiki/eli-cohen) — the [Mossad](/wiki/mossad) deep-cover agent executed in Damascus in 1966 — in the Netflix series *The Spy*. Kiriakou called it *"a very, very serious role"* and *"fantastic."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="56:00" />

## The *Brüno* fixer in Newport Beach (Morgan v2)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described a series of events surrounding the production of [Sacha Baron Cohen](/wiki/sacha-baron-cohen)'s film *Brüno* (2009) in which a friend of his served as the fixer arranging access to Middle Eastern governments.

Kiriakou's friend — a British national he described as a fixer — was hired by the *Brüno* production to arrange filming access in the Arab world. The fixer secured permission to film in Libya, Morocco, and Jordan. He also attempted Syria.<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="55:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="55:30" />

The fixer traveled to the Syrian consulate in Newport Beach, California to apply for a filming permit. Kiriakou had specifically warned him not to do this. The fixer went anyway.<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="55:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="56:00" />

Syrian intelligence identified the fixer as a known associate of Israeli intelligence. He was detained at the consulate and held for several hours before being released. He called Kiriakou afterward and said: *"I fucked up, mate."* Kiriakou's response: *"I told you."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="56:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="56:30" />

## The Jordanian king at the airport

In Jordan, the fixer encountered King Abdullah II at an airport. [Baron Cohen](/wiki/sacha-baron-cohen) — in character as Brüno — approached the king and said: *"I'm your biggest fan. I loved you in *Borat*."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="56:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="57:00" />

Kiriakou described the king's reaction as unamused. The production was asked to leave.<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="57:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="57:30" />

Kiriakou's summary of the entire sequence: *"I told you."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="57:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="58:00" />
