Discussing in December 2025 how badly Americans understand the countries they sanction, John Kiriakou described being argued out of his own priors by evidence he could see.

## The videos

He has a very close friend, he says, who goes to Iran with regularity, once or twice a year. She would always come back and tell him how great things are there and how smoothly things are operating — and he would think, okay, yeah, sure. Then she started taking videos of just everyday normal life, and showing him other people's videos on YouTube. And she's right, he concluded. <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="02:07" />

## What he concluded

There is no US involvement inside Iran, he says, and there has not been since 1979: no embassy there, no diplomatic presence, no business presence, nothing. And they lack for nothing. They want for nothing. They have great relations with China and with Russia; they get all the spare parts they need; they have healthy trade relationships with all of the region's powers and larger economies. <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="02:07" /> <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="02:38" />

## The comparison

The point of the observation is the contrast that follows it. Then you look at something like Iraq, he says, which is right next door and has a very heavy American presence — and we've wrecked that country. When his interviewer added that wrecking it was the plan, Kiriakou did not dispute it. <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="03:09" />

## The rule he draws from it

The passage introduces one of the sharpest formulations Kiriakou endorses about American foreign policy, offered to him by his interviewer and immediately affirmed: that wherever the United States goes it creates instability, and that the second rule is that it refuses to accept rule number one. Kiriakou's answer was that this is exactly right, and that the United States does not accept responsibility. <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="03:40" />
