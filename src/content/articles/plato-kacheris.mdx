**Plato Kacheris** was [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s lead defence attorney, described by him as a *"legendary legal figure in Washington."*<Cite s="2019-10-31-useful-idiots-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-on-impeachmen" t="56:49" />

His significance in Kiriakou's account is a piece of reading. On the day of his arraignment the government offered forty-five years — *"take a plea, you do 45 years and you might live to meet your grandchildren"* — an offer Kiriakou, by his own description in a state of shock, answered with *"I'm not doing 45 minutes. I didn't do anything wrong."*<Cite s="2019-10-31-useful-idiots-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-on-impeachmen" t="56:17" /> Months later the offers began to fall: ten years on one Monday, eight on the Wednesday, five by the Friday.<Cite s="2019-10-31-useful-idiots-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-on-impeachmen" t="56:49" />

Kacheris told him this was not how it works. *"I've been practicing law in Washington for 53 years and I've never seen them come down in time. If you reject a plea, they always go up in time"* — ten becomes twelve, twelve becomes fifteen.<Cite s="2019-10-31-useful-idiots-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-on-impeachmen" t="56:49" /><Cite s="2019-10-31-useful-idiots-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-on-impeachmen" t="57:20" /> Asked why the government was moving the other way, his answer was that the case would not survive a jury: *"because they have a shit case and they know it's shit, and we're going to trial."*<Cite s="2019-10-31-useful-idiots-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-on-impeachmen" t="57:20" />

Kiriakou dates that conversation to March 2012. The defence went *"all the way up to trial in October of 2012"* before a plea was finally negotiated — to a single count under the [Intelligence Identities Protection Act](/wiki/intelligence-identities-protection-act), carrying thirty months, of which he served twenty-three.<Cite s="2019-10-31-useful-idiots-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-on-impeachmen" t="57:20" /><Cite s="2019-10-31-useful-idiots-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-on-impeachmen" t="57:51" />
