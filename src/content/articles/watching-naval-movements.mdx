**Watching naval movements** is, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says, one of the very first lessons he learned at the CIA: *"if you want to determine American military intentions, watch naval movements."* Troops can be flown anywhere, but sending an entire aircraft carrier strike group *"means business,"* and sending two *"means war."*<Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="24:20" /> He used the rule — together with a tip from a friend at the White House — to publicly predict, days in advance, that the U.S. would [strike Iran](/wiki/iran-strike-factions-2026): two carrier groups had been sent.<Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="22:46" /><Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="23:17" />
