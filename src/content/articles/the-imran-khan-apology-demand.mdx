**The Imran Khan apology demand** followed an interview [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) gave to an Indian outlet in late October 2025, which he described on air as having set off *"all hell"* across South Asia.<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="17:42" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="18:13" />

## The demand

*"I have more death threats than I can shake a stick at,"* he said. *"And then [Imran Khan](/wiki/imran-khan)'s party issued a formal statement demanding that I apologise to Imran Khan, I apologise to the party, and I apologise to the Pakistani people. And they want it in writing by the end of the day today."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="18:13" />

## The reply

Kiriakou prefaced his answer with an apology of a different kind, to his audience: *"please forgive me, but I told them that I wipe my ass with their apology, and that's as good as they're getting."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="18:13" />

*"And then the death threats came in."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="18:13" />

The host's response was not to argue the point: *"I can't say I'm surprised, but I would definitely have someone else — someone who you don't like — start your car in the morning."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="18:44" />
