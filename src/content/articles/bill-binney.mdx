**Bill Binney** is the former technical director of the NSA — its fourth-ranking official and, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says, one of the country's leading technical thinkers. Binney stated on the record that the Russians were not behind the DNC email leak: analyzing the speed at which the documents were uploaded, he concluded it was *"impossible"* to have taken them remotely, and that they could only have been copied in person onto a thumb drive.<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="05:11" /><Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="05:45" /> Kiriakou uses Binney's analysis as a pillar of his case against [Russiagate](/wiki/russiagate).

Kiriakou also cites Binney's own treatment as a whistleblower as emblematic of how the government responds to national-security dissent: Binney, he says, was pulled naked out of a shower by the FBI and handcuffed behind his back with one leg raised — treatment Kiriakou calls absurd given that Binney posed no plausible danger to the American people.<Cite s="2017-06-06-disruption-network-lab" t="1:10:15" />

## Havana syndrome

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) names Binney — whom he describes as a renowned NSA whistleblower, the agency's former number four, and "a great hero of transparency" — as one of the documented cases of [Havana syndrome](/wiki/havana-syndrome), with brain damage on record.<Cite s="2026-07-27-national-security-files-john-kiriakou-middle-east-on-the-brink-of-a-m" t="53:33" /> He cites Binney alongside the former Moscow station chief Mark Polymeropoulos as evidence that whatever is causing the injuries is being directed at people with access to very high-level classified information, and against the [CIA](/wiki/cia)'s own finding that the cause was crickets.<Cite s="2026-07-27-national-security-files-john-kiriakou-middle-east-on-the-brink-of-a-m" t="53:01" /><Cite s="2026-07-27-national-security-files-john-kiriakou-middle-east-on-the-brink-of-a-m" t="54:05" />
