**John McCain** was a long-serving U.S. Senator from Arizona, the Republican presidential nominee in 2008, and — in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account — the most prominent legislative advocate of his post-prison rehabilitation.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="1:05:07" />

## Senate-floor defense of Kiriakou

In December 2014, six weeks before Kiriakou's release from [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto), the U.S. Senate Select Committee on Intelligence released the executive summary of its report on CIA detention and interrogation — the document subsequently known as the [Senate Torture Report](/wiki/senate-torture-report). McCain, on the floor of the Senate, made what Kiriakou identifies as the single most consequential public defense of his whistleblowing:

> If it weren't for John Kiriakou, the American people would never have had any idea what the CIA was doing in their name.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="1:05:07" />

## The post-prison welcome and the pension amendment

On Kiriakou's release, one of his first received calls was from McCain's chief of staff, who relayed McCain's *"welcome home"* and asked what could be done to be helpful. The CIA had, following Kiriakou's felony conviction, stripped him of his federal pension — *"They drove me into bankruptcy and took my pension."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="1:05:38" /><Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="1:06:10" />

McCain's proposed remedy was a tightly drafted amendment to the National Defense Authorization Act for 2016, designed exclusively to apply to Kiriakou:

> Every American convicted of violating the Intelligence Identities Protection Act between October 1st and October 31st, 2012 shall hereby have his pension reinstated.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="1:06:40" />

(Kiriakou is the only American falling within that window.)

McCain intended to insert the amendment into the NDAA via his seat on the conference committee. He was then diagnosed with the brain tumor that ultimately killed him, was removed from the committee, and the amendment was withdrawn. Kiriakou's pension has not been restored.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="1:07:10" />

## Working relationship through the Kerry years

McCain and Kiriakou had developed a personal connection during Kiriakou's 2009–2011 service as senior investigator on the Senate Foreign Relations Committee under [John Kerry](/wiki/john-kerry). Per Kiriakou, McCain would *"go out of his way to shake my hand and say hi"* — to the visible jealousy of Kerry, who once needled them: *"Why don't you two get a room or something?"* Kiriakou's reply, in his recollection: *"We have this connection over torture. McCain takes me seriously and I take him seriously."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="1:05:38" /><Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="1:06:10" />

## The pension amendment

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says [John McCain](/wiki/john-mccain), whose Senate office sat next to [John Kerry](/wiki/john-kerry)'s, made a point of greeting him warmly *"because he was tortured and I put my whole career on the line to call out torture."* After Kiriakou's release, McCain's staff worked with his attorney on an amendment to the National Defense Authorization Act to restore the pension [Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) had stripped — narrowly written to cover anyone convicted under the [Intelligence Identities Protection Act](/wiki/intelligence-identities-protection-act) in October 2012, meaning only Kiriakou. McCain developed brain cancer and died before it could be inserted.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:13:04" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:13:35" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:14:05" />

## 'An American hero' (News Beat)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says that when the [Senate torture report](/wiki/senate-torture-report) was released in December 2014, [John McCain](/wiki/john-mccain) rose on the Senate floor and called him an American hero — saying the American people would never have known what was done in their name had Kiriakou not told them.<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="23:31" />
