**CIA and the UFO question** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s consistent framing that unidentified craft and alien technology are a Pentagon matter, not a CIA one. On his first day he asked his boss *"where are the aliens?"*; the boss laughed that it was everybody's first question, but *"that's all at the Pentagon."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="53:47" /> Kiriakou adds that nearly every one of his former fighter-pilot friends — flying F-16s, F-18s and F-35s — *"has seen something,"* sometimes a ship emerging from the ocean, *"completely inexplicable."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="54:20" /> The CIA, he stresses, recruits spies and steals secrets; it is *"not beaming waves at your head."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="55:21" />

## Kiriakou's own sighting (Jay Dyer)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts that at 17, driving through Pennsylvania Amish country with his father, they watched a silent orange trapezoid *"in the shape of a saucer"* hover 1,500 feet up, then shoot off *"at a fantastic speed that defied the laws of physics."* His father refused to report it — *"people are going to think we're insane."*<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:15:53" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:16:54" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:21:43" /> In a separate telling he places the family home in New Castle, Pennsylvania, with the restaurant they were driving to in nearby Sharon.<Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:21:43" /> Years later a cousin, an F-15 pilot, told him Air Force pilots *"see stuff like that all the time,"* sometimes *"coming up out of the water off the coast of Norfolk"* — filing a form the Pentagon never answers.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:17:57" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:24:17" />
