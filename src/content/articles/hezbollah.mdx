**Hezbollah** is a Lebanese Shia militant organization and political party. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) discusses it primarily through the lens of Israeli strategy and [Mossad](/wiki/mossad) operations.

## Israel's strategic goal — chaos, not victory

Per [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), Israel does not have any real intention of defeating Hezbollah outright. Instead, the goal is to turn Lebanon *"into a place of chaos,"* because *"chaos is good if you're Israeli."* He frames this as the same logic Israel applies to Iran — that a population fighting among itself, or preoccupied with finding food, water, and medical care, is not a population focused on fighting Israel.<Cite s="2026-03-09-dialogue-works-cia-hit-iran-retaliation-strike" t="27:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-09-dialogue-works-cia-hit-iran-retaliation-strike" t="28:00" />

## The pager operation

[Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described [Mossad](/wiki/mossad)'s simultaneous pager-detonation attack against Hezbollah — in which explosives concealed in pagers were activated at once — as decapitating the organization's leadership. He called it *"the most incredible operation I've ever seen in my life"* and said *"somebody at Mossad's getting promoted today,"* while acknowledging it *"might be"* a war crime because innocent people were killed.<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="1:26:30" />

## The Nasrallah strike

Kiriakou cited the killing of former Hezbollah leader [Hassan Nasrallah](/wiki/hassan-nasrallah) as an example of Israeli readiness to inflict mass casualties: Israel *"killed 300 people in southern Lebanon when they went after Hassan Nasrallah ... just blew up the entire complex. It decapitated Hezbollah. It was also a war crime."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="3:00:00" />
