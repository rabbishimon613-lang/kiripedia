**Manuel Noriega** was the Panamanian dictator and one of the most notorious drug traffickers in the Western Hemisphere. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) raises him to rebut a televised denial by former CIA officer Jack Devine that the agency ever worked with drug traffickers: Noriega, he notes, *"was a paid CIA source for decades."*<Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="23:25" /> Kiriakou frames it as part of the CIA's need to address its [drug-facilitation](/wiki/cia-drug-facilitation) history *"truthfully."*
