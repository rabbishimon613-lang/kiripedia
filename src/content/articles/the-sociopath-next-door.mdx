The Sociopath Next Door is a book Kiriakou has cited for the claim that 85% of people incarcerated at any level of the carceral system, whether federal, state, or local, have a diagnosable mental illness. <Cite s="2026-02-18-barracks-media-inc-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-inside-the-cia-s-tortu" t="48:20" /> He raised the book while discussing how prison is its own form of trauma and how that shared trauma builds bonds between inmates, drawing on his own experience of incarceration. <Cite s="2026-02-18-barracks-media-inc-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-inside-the-cia-s-tortu" t="48:20" />
