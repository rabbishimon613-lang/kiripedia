**CIA liaison relationships** are the intelligence-sharing arrangements the [Central Intelligence Agency](/wiki/cia) maintains with foreign governments — arrangements [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says expanded sharply after the September 11, 2001 attacks, when the CIA suddenly had reason to open relationships with countries it previously had no cause to deal with, including the Central Asian states.<Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="27:41" />

## The risk officers don't weigh

Kiriakou says CIA officers do not consider the long-term risks of these relationships: *"Does anybody inside the agency ever stop to consider the long-term risks of liaison activities — that by disclosing your most sensitive operations to a foreign intelligence service, which has its own separate national interest, you're just handing over America's darkest secrets, giving those governments leverage, even potential blackmail material, over the United States itself?"* His answer: *"No, they don't consider it. They don't consider it at all."*<Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="32:22" />

## The collapse of oversight

Kiriakou attributes part of the problem to high turnover — officials rotate out every two or three years, so institutional memory of why a liaison relationship was established gets lost — compounded by the disappearance of congressional overseers willing to challenge the agency. He names Senator Frank Church, Congressman Otis Pike, Senator Daniel Patrick Moynihan, and Senator Ted Kennedy as the last overseers with the will to do so, noting they are all dead, and that no one currently in Congress has the guts to stand up to the CIA.<Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="33:25" />

## The choreography of a liaison visit

Kiriakou describes the hosting of a visiting foreign service as a standardized and constant piece of business rather than an occasion: setting up *"a day of briefings, they get a photo op with the director, you know, shaking hands, we exchange gifts, and we take them out to a fancy dinner at night."* This was done *"literally every single day, usually multiple times a day,"* and the visitors were not senior — *"they're all mid-level, like majors and lieutenant colonels."* Asked whether there was a lot of ceremony in it, he agreed there was.<Cite s="2026-07-03-comedy-overload-comedy-overload" t="0:00" />

It is that routine setting into which he places [Cofer Black](/wiki/cofer-black)'s pre-9/11 warning: Kiriakou had booked a young analyst covering al-Qaeda to fill an hour, and the director of counterterrorism came in his place, bringing the director of operations from the [bin Laden](/wiki/osama-bin-laden) unit and an appeal — *"I'm begging you, if you have any sources inside Al-Qaeda, please help us."* The delegation *"just sat there and looked at him. Nothing."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-comedy-overload-comedy-overload" t="0:30" /><Cite s="2026-07-03-comedy-overload-comedy-overload" t="1:32" />
