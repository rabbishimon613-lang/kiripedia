[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described the origin of the [Taliban](/wiki/taliban-origin-benazir) as an accidental product of a Pakistani policy decision under [Benazir Bhutto](/wiki/benazir-bhutto).

## The word *"Taliban"*

Kiriakou noted that *"Taliban"* is the plural of *"talib,"* which means *"student"* in both Pashto and Arabic — so *"Taliban"* literally means *"students."*<Cite s="2025-06-01-not-silenced-part-1" t="25:00" />

## The truck hijackings

In the early 1990s, Pakistani trucks traveling from Pakistan to Iran were being hijacked in Afghanistan, where chaos reigned and no one was in charge. [Benazir Bhutto](/wiki/benazir-bhutto), the pro-American Prime Minister of Pakistan, decided to do something about it. Her idea: take the Afghan religious students ([Taliban](/wiki/taliban-origin-benazir)) studying in Pakistani madrassas — refugees who had fled the chaos in Afghanistan — and place them in the trucks alongside the drivers to protect the cargo.<Cite s="2025-06-01-not-silenced-part-1" t="25:30" /><Cite s="2025-06-01-not-silenced-part-1" t="26:00" />

## Loss of control

The scheme worked; hijackings dropped. Bhutto then escalated: rather than having the Taliban ride back and forth, she placed them in villages all along the truck routes, gave them weapons and money so they could protect themselves and the trucks. Kiriakou's summary: *"The next thing you know, she created the Taliban, and then she lost control of it, and then it took the whole country, and then it let al-Qaeda come in, and the rest is history. It was an accident. It was poor policy planning, and we're still paying the price."*<Cite s="2025-06-01-not-silenced-part-1" t="26:00" /><Cite s="2025-06-01-not-silenced-part-1" t="26:30" /><Cite s="2025-06-01-not-silenced-part-1" t="27:00" />
