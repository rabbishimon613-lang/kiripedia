**Henry Kissinger** occupies a special seat in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s demonology — the war criminal Washington chose to canonize.

## Greece and Cyprus

For Kiriakou — a Greek-American whose politics were forged partly by the junta years — the declassified record settles it: the Greek military junta was allowed to remain in power *"because Henry Kissinger wanted it so."*<Cite s="2015-04-23-the-real-news-network-the-iraqi-pissing-match-john-kiriakou-on-rai" t="16:04" /> He tells of a cab driver in Nicosia asking out of nowhere, *"Do you know Kissinger? The butcher of Cyprus"* — Kiriakou had met him exactly once, as a brand-new CIA officer.<Cite s="2019-06-14-slow-news-day" t="21:42" />

## "The biggest war criminal alive"

Kiriakou asks what bigger war criminal walks the earth — *"the butcher of Cyprus, the butcher of Chile, we could go on"* — and is appalled that he still dictates foreign policy through acolytes like [John Bolton](/wiki/john-bolton) and admirers like [Hillary Clinton](/wiki/hillary-clinton), who cited him as friend and mentor in a debate.<Cite s="2019-06-14-slow-news-day" t="20:07" /><Cite s="2019-06-14-slow-news-day" t="20:39" /> His advice on encountering the man: *"your natural impulse if you ever meet Henry Kissinger should be to become involuntarily violent."*<Cite s="2019-06-14-slow-news-day" t="21:10" /> Kissinger belongs, he says, in the same breath as Westmoreland and Nixon — not on a foreign-policy altar both parties pray at.<Cite s="2019-06-14-slow-news-day" t="23:46" /> He also relishes the Ellsberg whiplash: Kissinger took credit for [Daniel Ellsberg](/wiki/daniel-ellsberg) as his brilliant student — then, in Nixon's service, branded him *"the most dangerous man in America."*<Cite s="2022-05-19-scheerpost" t="51:36" /><Cite s="2023-06-26-kevin-gosztola" t="31:24" />

## The concession

And yet Kiriakou grants the devil his craft. Nixon and Kissinger *"were not saints — they were killing people in the millions in Vietnam and Laos and Cambodia — but they also could be rational"*: the China opening was made for stability, and it was Kissinger's doing.<Cite s="2022-07-24-scheerpost-scheer-intelligence-kiriakou-on-biden-s-grove" t="42:10" /> When even Kissinger says he sees no aim in current U.S. foreign policy, Kiriakou takes the point: *"as much of a genocidal maniac as Kissinger was, he at least had goals... you knew what his policy was. Realpolitik was real."*<Cite s="2024-06-13-hr-news-channel-hr-news-channel" t="11:04" /><Cite s="2024-06-13-hr-news-channel-hr-news-channel" t="11:35" />
