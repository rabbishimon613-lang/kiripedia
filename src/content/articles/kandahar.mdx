**Kandahar** — city and province in southern Afghanistan — threads through [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s accounts of al-Qaeda's infrastructure, the hunt that followed 9/11, and the heroin economy.

## Al-Qaeda's Kandahar

[Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah), in Kiriakou's telling, founded and managed both of al-Qaeda's training camps in southern Afghanistan — one in Kandahar province, the other in Helmand — along with the [House of Martyrs](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) safe house in Peshawar: the logistics man who smuggled recruits from Karachi to Kandahar to start their training.<Cite s="2019-03-14-salem-access-tv-public" t="14:03" /><Cite s="2020-11-30-crossing-faiths" t="20:49" /><Cite s="2023-01-13-the-open-forum-podcast" t="18:25" /> The March 2002 raids that took Zubaydah also captured the commanders of both camps.<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="11:00" /> Among the camp's foot soldiers Kiriakou describes: a non-Arabic-speaking Australian recruit handed a gun and told to stand guard at the Kandahar base — later shipped to [Guantanamo](/wiki/guantanamo-bay) and *"tortured mercilessly."*<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="59:34" />

## The town too costly to bomb

Kiriakou cites Bill Clinton's own words on the 1990s chance to kill [bin Laden](/wiki/osama-bin-laden): *"I nearly got him and could have killed him, but I would have had to destroy a little town called Kandahar and killed 300 innocent people."*<Cite s="2021-09-12-trt-world-years-after-9-11-inside-america-with-ghida-fa" t="07:15" /> He has also always believed the Taliban's protection of bin Laden operated under the auspices of Pakistani intelligence.<Cite s="2015-05-04-the-real-news-network-the-real-news-network" t="01:48" />

## The poppy study

As chief investigator for the [Senate Foreign Relations Committee](/wiki/senate-foreign-relations-committee), Kiriakou ran a study on heroin poppies: the military flew him by helicopter first to Kandahar, then onward — almost all of the country's heroin came from Helmand and Kandahar.<Cite s="2019-06-14-slow-news-day" t="39:54" /><Cite s="2021-09-01-garland-nixon-talk-afghanistan" t="09:01" /> On a later trip his request was simple — *"I want to go to Kandahar and Lashkar Gah and I want to go into the poppy fields"* — and the answer was absolutely not.<Cite s="2024-12-10-jon-leon-guerrero-john-kiriakou-enhanced-techniques-whistleblowing" t="06:43" />
