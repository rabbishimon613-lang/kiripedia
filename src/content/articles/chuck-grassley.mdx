**Chuck Grassley** is the senior United States senator from Iowa and, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, the single member of Congress who has taken whistleblower protection seriously as a legislative project.

## The count on one finger

*"You can count the number of pro-whistleblower members of Congress on one finger, and that is Chuck Grassley, the senior senator from Iowa."*<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="21:35" /> Grassley, he says, wrote the [Whistleblower Protection Act](/wiki/whistleblower-protection-act) and the National Security Whistleblower Protection Act, and speaks every year at the National Whistleblower Center's annual luncheon. *"He's the only guy that's serious about protecting whistleblowers — and you can't keep people safe with the support of one member of Congress."*<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="21:35" />

Pressed that Grassley is not literally alone, Kiriakou conceded there are one or two junior House members who turn up at such events, but held the substantive point: *"in terms of legislative authority, only Grassley can push some of these things through the system and get them signed into law. That's why we have these whistleblower protection laws."*<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="22:06" />

## Forced into the Ukraine case

When Grassley issued a statement backing [the Ukraine call whistleblower](/wiki/ukraine-call-whistleblower) in 2019, Kiriakou read it as obligation rather than judgement: *"he had to — it's his law. The whistleblower invoked his law to protect himself, so Grassley was sort of pushed into it."*<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="22:38" /> He also noted the limit on what Grassley could know. The senator does not sit on the Intelligence Committee, so he had none of the answers to the questions Kiriakou was asking about whether the complaint was legitimate, and *"just has to sort of go with it until proven otherwise."*<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="22:38" />
