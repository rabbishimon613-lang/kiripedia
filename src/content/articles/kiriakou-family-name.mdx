The **Kiriakou name** originates, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s own account, in an uncorrected clerical error.

His great-great-grandfather was arrested in Rhodes for stealing a potato. He was starving.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="28:36" /> The family name at the time was Christodoulou. In court he was asked his name and answered "Marcos, Marcos the son of Kiriakos" — and the clerk wrote down *Marcos Kiriakou*. He never fixed it.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="28:36" /><Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="29:06" />

The consequence is a family split down two surnames: one branch remains Christodoulou and the other Kiriakou, a divergence never corrected.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="29:06" />

Kiriakou tells the story against himself. Asked in a rapid-fire round about potatoes, he answered that he is a long-time fan but can no longer eat them — an answer that prompted the host to raise the origin of the name.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="28:05" /><Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="28:36" />
