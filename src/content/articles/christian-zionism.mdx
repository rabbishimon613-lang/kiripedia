**Christian Zionism** — the evangelical doctrine that American Christians owe unconditional support to the state of Israel — is a subject [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) approaches from two directions at once: as an Orthodox Christian, and as someone who was invited to teach evangelical students and disagreed with them to their faces.

## The disappearing congregations

Kiriakou frames the issue by its consequence rather than its theology. He cites the projection that by Christmas of that year *"there will be no Christians left in Nazareth"* — a city where *"there have been Christians for more than 2,000 years, and they'll be gone."* He names Bethlehem as another traditionally Christian city on the same trajectory.<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="01:04" />

The question he puts is whether the trend can be reversed at all, or whether *"the US government is all in to help the Israeli government push religious minorities out of the Holy Land."*<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="01:34" />

## Teaching the people he disagreed with

Kiriakou was invited to teach classes at an evangelical institution by people who were not themselves evangelical but liked a position he had taken. He accepted, and does not regret it: *"I said yes. I'm glad that I did. They were lovely people."*<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="04:44" />

The disagreement, though, was foundational — *"we had fundamental disagreements on the role of evangelical Christianity in the Middle East."* His own position leaves no room for negotiation: *"I would posit that there is no role for evangelical Christianity in the Middle East, because evangelical Christianity, in my humble opinion, is an arm of Zionism."*<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="04:44" />

## The contradiction he cannot resolve

The core of Kiriakou's objection is not political but internal to the faith being claimed. *"I was never able to wrap my head around how these Christians — or these people who call themselves Christians — with a straight face can tell you that they're doing the right thing by supporting Israel as it wipes out ancient Christianity."*<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="05:17" />

His question to his interlocutor is put as a genuine one: *"How is it that they're able to justify this policy to themselves?"*<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="05:17" />

## The missionary bound for North Macedonia

The anecdote Kiriakou offers as the clearest illustration concerns the daughter of an evangelical friend who volunteered for missionary work overseas. He asked where she was going. North Macedonia. He asked why. *"Why not?"* she said.<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="11:01" />

His response was the obvious one: *"Because they're already Christian. Why aren't you going to Africa? Or go to the Middle East somewhere."* Her reaction is the point of the story — *"she looked at me like I had two heads."*<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="11:01" />

The premise he draws out of it is that in this framework an Orthodox Christian does not register as a Christian at all: *"an Orthodox Christian is not a real Christian, right? I mean, isn't that what they're led to believe these days?"*<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="11:01" />

## Forcing God's hand

The mechanism Kiriakou identifies in the doctrine is one of leverage over providence — that adherents *"believe they can force God's hand,"* for instance by encouraging Jews to move to Israel, on the reasoning that the Messiah cannot return until all Jews have returned there.<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="14:38" />

The question he keeps returning to is the plain civic one underneath the theology, and he notes that a few people are beginning to ask it: *"Why are we supposed to do whatever Israel says? What does that have to do with American foreign policy?"*<Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="00:33" /><Cite s="2026-07-14-jay-dyer-what-is-christian-zlonism-really-w-cia" t="14:07" />
