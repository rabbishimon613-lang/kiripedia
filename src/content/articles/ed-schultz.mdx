**Ed Schultz** was an American broadcaster who worked at MSNBC and then at [RT](/wiki/kiriakou-russian-media), where he became a colleague of John Kiriakou's. Kiriakou describes him as a friend.<Cite s="2025-11-19-dmz-america-podcast-dmz-america-podcast" t="43:24" />

## The Sanders instruction

Kiriakou uses Schultz as his standing example of how editorial control operated at a mainstream cable network. Schultz was told in no uncertain terms that he could not discuss Bernie Sanders during the Democratic primary — a major candidate in a live primary — and he never forgave MSNBC for it.<Cite s="2025-11-19-dmz-america-podcast-dmz-america-podcast" t="43:24" />

## The label after death

Schultz appears again in Kiriakou's account of the 2022 labelling wave. Describing how he checked his own Twitter profile daily to see whether he had been marked as an official mouthpiece of the Russian government, Kiriakou noted the two cases that showed how mechanical the process was: George Galloway, tagged although he was no longer connected to RT at all, and Ed Schultz, tagged *"even though he's been dead for four years."*<Cite s="2022-04-21-lee-camp-unredacted-toni-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-joins-lee-cam" t="3:09" />

## Labelled after his death

Kiriakou uses Schultz''s account as his standing example of how mechanically the platforms applied their [state-affiliated media labels](/wiki/twitter-state-affiliated-media-labels). Describing his own daily check for the tag in May 2022, he added: *"poor Ed Schultz has been dead for how many years, and they put a tag on him saying that he''s a mouthpiece of the Russian government."*<Cite s="2022-05-05-panquake-lee-camp-full-interview-kiriakou-sean-o-brien-talk-pan" t="05:14" />
