**Iraq WMD intelligence** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of how the case for Iraqi weapons of mass destruction was manufactured. The consensus of every effect analyst across the intelligence community — CIA, DIA, the Department of Energy — was that Iraq had no WMD at the time, though it may have had them once.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="10:58" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="11:30" /> But the White House had already decided to invade and overthrow Saddam Hussein, and sought analysis to support the policy — *"exactly the opposite of what you're supposed to do."* When [George Tenet](/wiki/george-tenet) told President Bush it was a *"slam dunk,"* Kiriakou says, there was no intelligence analysis behind the conclusion — *"none."*<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="12:02" /> The staged image of [Colin Powell](/wiki/colin-powell) at the UN, flanked by Tenet and John Negroponte, was meant to imply total certainty; *"the entire policy was based on a lie."*<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="12:33" /> Kiriakou recalls a senior National Security Council official, in a briefing on the coming invasion, predicting Iraqis would *"throw flowers at us"* as U.S. troops crossed the border — a prediction Kiriakou says showed the official had never been to the Middle East or read any history, since Iraqis would rather live under Saddam Hussein than be occupied by the American army.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:24:19" /> He also recalls that when CIA officers demanded access to a highly-touted WMD source, the Pentagon eventually admitted the source was [Ahmed Chalabi](/wiki/ahmed-chalabi), whose information proved entirely false — *"not a single word is true, not a single word we went to war for this."*<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:38:25" />
