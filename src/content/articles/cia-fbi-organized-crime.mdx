**CIA and FBI ties to organized crime** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s survey of documented partnerships between U.S. intelligence and the mob. During World War II the FBI worked with the Genovese family to secure the ports of Brooklyn and Newark; the CIA repeatedly used the Santo Trafficante family of Tampa in its plots to assassinate Fidel Castro; and in the 1980s it worked with the Pablo Escobar cartel against communist separatists in Colombia — while the DEA fought the same cartel.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="11:49" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="12:20" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="12:09" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="12:40" /> He also cites the FBI recruiting Colombo-family hitman Greg Scarpa to find the bodies of murdered civil-rights workers — whom Kiriakou also refers to as "freedom riders" — in Mississippi; Scarpa kidnapped a Klansman and shot him in the knees to make him talk, and *"like magic"* the FBI found the bodies.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="12:50" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="13:13" />
