Joe Weisberg is a former CIA counterterrorism officer whom Kiriakou describes as a colleague and a brilliant writer. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="1:22:40" /> Kiriakou says Weisberg walked up to him at the office one day and announced he was quitting that morning, explaining he wasn't married, had no children, and was going to Hollywood to find his fortune; Weisberg went on to create the television series The Americans. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="1:22:40" /> Kiriakou says the show succeeded because it was realistic, pointing to a former neighbor of his in Arlington, Virginia, an elementary school substitute teacher who was arrested as a Russian sleeper agent for the SVR after living for years as an ordinary American waiting to be activated. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="1:23:12" />
