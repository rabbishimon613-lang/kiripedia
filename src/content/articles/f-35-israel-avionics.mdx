The **F-35 avionics dispute with Israel** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of how the U.S. deliberately degrades the avionics on exported F-35 fighters — designating Israel's the F-35I and the United Arab Emirates' the F-35E — so that a downed aircraft cannot yield its full capabilities to the Iranians, Russians or Chinese.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:17:13" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:17:45" /><Cite s="2025-09-25-joe-mkhitaryan" t="1:23:27" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight" t="31:19" /><Cite s="2026-03-10-former-congressman-matt-ga" t="43:44" /> The Emirates accepted the degraded model without objection; Israel demanded the undegraded version — *"we want the F-35 ... we want the same one you have"* — was refused, and, Kiriakou says, instead worked through Northrop Grumman, Lockheed Martin, Boeing and other contractors to steal the upgrade. *"The Israelis are not our friends, period,"* he concludes.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:18:16" /><Cite s="2025-10-12-danny-haiphong" t="44:13" /><Cite s="2026-03-10-former-congressman-matt-ga" t="43:44" /> Kiriakou says Israel's attempt to steal the avionics by infiltrating Lockheed Martin and its subcontractors is the reason Israel is excluded from the [Five Eyes](/wiki/five-eyes) intelligence alliance.<Cite s="2025-10-12-danny-haiphong" t="44:44" />

He uses the episode as his standing answer to the argument for closer military integration with Israel. In that telling the sequence is symmetrical and the contrast is the point: Israel came first, was offered the F-35I with slightly degraded avionics for the same stated reason, and answered *"No, we want your F-35. We don't want any I with degraded avionics."* The Emiratis, offered the identical arrangement as the F-35E, *"gladly took"* it. Israel then *"sent their spies all over the defense sector to steal the avionics so that when they finally got the F-35I, they could upgrade them on their own."*<Cite s="2026-07-10-kiriakou-takes-ana-cenk-behind-the-curtain" t="52:18" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-kiriakou-takes-ana-cenk-behind-the-curtain" t="52:48" /> The rhetorical question he closes on is about placement rather than aircraft: *"that's the country that we want side by side with our people in the Pentagon and the CIA and, god forbid, NSA or DIA?"* — at a moment when, he notes, the DIA director was warning every employee that Mossad was unusually aggressive against them.<Cite s="2026-07-10-kiriakou-takes-ana-cenk-behind-the-curtain" t="53:20" />
