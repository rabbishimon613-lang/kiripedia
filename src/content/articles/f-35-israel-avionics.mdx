The **F-35 avionics dispute with Israel** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of how the U.S. deliberately degrades the avionics on exported F-35 fighters — designating Israel's the F-35I and the United Arab Emirates' the F-35E — so that a downed aircraft cannot yield its full capabilities to the Iranians, Russians or Chinese.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:17:13" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:17:45" /> The Emirates accepted the degraded model; Israel demanded the undegraded version, was refused, and — Kiriakou says — instead worked through Northrop Grumman, Lockheed Martin, Boeing and other contractors to steal the upgrade. *"The Israelis are not our friends, period,"* he concludes.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:18:16" />
