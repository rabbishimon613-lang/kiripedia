Donald Trump's 2016 election victory was partly due to public fatigue with perpetual wars, as the majority of Americans opposed the war. <Cite s="2026-03-11-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-what-s-really-happening-in-the-ir" t="10:55:00" /> In the same conversation, Kiriakou describes [Trump's ceasefire efforts with Iran](/wiki/trump-iran-ceasefire-efforts), reaching out behind the scenes twice in ten days only to be rebuffed. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he believes Trump genuinely thought he was doing the right thing and saving lives when he ordered the Iran strikes, and now realizes he has been *"duped"* — falling for the same nuclear-weapons bluff from [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu) that [Barack Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) had called years earlier.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="45:38" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="46:09" />

## 'The most blindly pro-Israel president' (SaltCube)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) calls [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) the most *"blindly, knee-jerk"* pro-Israel president ever — the one who moved the U.S. embassy from Tel Aviv to Jerusalem, closed the Palestinian office in Washington and expelled its diplomats, and claimed he could solve the Arab-Israeli conflict *"in a day"* by giving Israel everything it wanted.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="47:43" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="48:18" /> Kiriakou says Trump himself once described the Israeli goal in Gaza as pushing out all Palestinians to develop the property — that it would be *"better than Monte Carlo"* — which Kiriakou calls the very definition of ethnic cleansing.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="1:20:07" /> He also blames Trump, not just Bill Clinton, for the embassy's eventual move, noting [Sheldon Adelson](/wiki/sheldon-adelson) personally paid for it out of pocket.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="1:14:27" />

## The Putin exception

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) notes that Trump has criticized nearly every world leader and ally — including the Canadian and British prime ministers — but has never once criticized [Vladimir Putin](/wiki/russia-ukraine-war-origins), a pattern Kiriakou calls *"strange"* and reads as evidence Trump *"admires autocracy"* and *"loves Xi Jinping"* too.<Cite s="2026-06-10-zeteo-abolish-the-cia" t="47:23" /> Kiriakou also says Trump uses his personal cell phone and answers calls from unrecognized international numbers on the chance one might be a foreign leader — and agrees, drawing on his own CIA experience with such interception, that a dozen or more countries are likely listening in.<Cite s="2026-06-10-zeteo-abolish-the-cia" t="47:54" />

## War powers, war crimes, and naming things

Kiriakou says Trump posted on Truth Social telling U.S. allies unable to get Gulf oil to buy from the United States instead, to "learn to fight for themselves," and to take the Strait of Hormuz by force now that Iran has been "decimated." He calls this several distinct war crimes, since seizing another nation's natural resources is explicitly barred under the law of war.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="48:22" /><Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="49:26" /> He notes the contradiction of Trump, in the same period, calling NATO weak and useless while also expecting NATO allies to fight for the Strait — and calls false Trump's claim that NATO has never helped the U.S., pointing out that the alliance's only invocation of Article 5 came after September 12, 2001, when NATO agreed to help fight al-Qaeda in Afghanistan.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="50:29" /><Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="50:59" /> Kiriakou has separately questioned the alliance's own consistency, noting NATO troops have been deployed to non-member, non-European countries — Afghanistan, Syria, Iraq, and the Sahel — contrary to its founding charter.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:29:56" />

Kiriakou also points to Trump's announcement renaming the Department of Defense to the "Department of War" as an example of Trump exceeding his actual authority — only Congress can rename a federal department, he notes — contrasting it with Trump's rebranding of the Gulf of Mexico to the "Gulf of America" on Google Maps, which is within a president's real power.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="1:47:39" /><Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="48:22" />

## Diplomacy by FedEx

[Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he laughed out loud reading, in the unsealed Mar-a-Lago search affidavit, that Trump had a former lawyer FedEx correspondence to Kim Jong Un — specifically so Trump could track the package's progress on his phone — rather than using the normal diplomatic-pouch process delivered by an ambassador.<Cite s="2022-08-27-kevin-gosztola-mar-a-lago-affidavit-unsealed" t="16:08" />
