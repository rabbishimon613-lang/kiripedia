## Duped over Iran

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he believes [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) genuinely thought he was doing the right thing and saving lives, and now realizes he has been *"duped"* — falling for the same nuclear-weapons bluff from [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu) that [Barack Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) had called years earlier.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="45:38" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="46:09" />

## 'The most blindly pro-Israel president' (SaltCube)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) calls [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) the most *"blindly, knee-jerk"* pro-Israel president ever — the one who moved the U.S. embassy from Tel Aviv to Jerusalem, closed the Palestinian office in Washington and expelled its diplomats, and claimed he could solve the Arab-Israeli conflict *"in a day"* by giving Israel everything it wanted.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="47:43" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="48:18" />
