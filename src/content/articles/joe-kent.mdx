Joe Kent is a former director of the National Counterterrorism Center whose public departure from government Kiriakou has discussed. Kiriakou says that as soon as Kent went public, he called a producer he knew to obtain Kent's contact information on behalf of Veteran Intelligence Professionals for Sanity, a group of which Kiriakou is a member, in order to give Kent the group's Sam Adams Award for Integrity in Intelligence. <Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="26:26" /> Kiriakou says the producer sent him Kent's information and the award was given, even though Kiriakou says he has never personally spoken to Kent. <Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="26:26" />
