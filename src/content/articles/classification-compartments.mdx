**Classification compartments** are the U.S. secrecy tiers above *top secret* that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) explains through his own clearances. Every CIA hire receives a TS/SCI/TK/gamma clearance — top secret, special intelligence, talent-keyhole and gamma — and access to any given cable depends on which compartments a person holds.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="55:54" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="56:26" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="56:58" /> As executive assistant to the Deputy Director for Operations, Kiriakou added six more compartments; in one morning briefing the Deputy Director stopped him — *"You and I are the only ones cleared for that"* — and the associate deputy directors, lacking the need to know, were excluded from the cable.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="57:29" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="58:00" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="58:31" />
