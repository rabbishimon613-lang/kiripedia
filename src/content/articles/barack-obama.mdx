**Barack Obama** was the 44th President of the United States. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says that from the Reagan era onward, every Israeli prime minister who visited the White House pressed the president to *"please bomb Iran"* — and every president refused, until the present one.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="44:38" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="45:08" /> Kiriakou recounts that Obama himself said Netanyahu had asked him to bomb Iran and threatened to use nuclear weapons if he did not; Obama, in Kiriakou's telling, replied *"Go ahead. Go ahead. Do it,"* and Netanyahu backed off because the threat was a bluff. Kiriakou says Netanyahu ran the same bluff on [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump), who believed it.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="45:38" />
