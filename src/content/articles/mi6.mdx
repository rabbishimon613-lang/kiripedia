**MI6**, formally the **Secret Intelligence Service** (SIS), is the foreign-intelligence service of the United Kingdom. It is the [CIA](/wiki/cia)'s closest liaison partner under the [Five Eyes](/wiki/five-eyes) framework — the U.S.–U.K.–Canada–Australia–New Zealand intelligence-sharing arrangement in which the five services have, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s words, *"literally cart blanche to do anything, to see anything, to go anywhere. We don't spy on each other."*<Cite s="2026-01-16-julian-dorey-french-intelligence" t="7:15" />

## *"We created the CIA"*

[Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounted a conversation with a retired MI6 officer during a series of speaking engagements across the United Kingdom. The MI6 officer told him: *"You know, we always loved you guys. In fact, we created the [CIA](/wiki/cia)."* Kiriakou's response: *"Oh, I know. I've written about it. It was you guys, you know, your station in New York um helped us create the OSS."* President Truman, after World War II, specifically asked MI6 to help set up an American equivalent.<Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="1:08:02" />

The officer continued: *"We always saw you as our little brother and then after the empire fell apart, you became our big brother and then after 9/11, you became strangers because your policy was just simply to kill everybody and that can't be a policy."*<Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="1:08:34" />

## MI5 vs MI6 vs Scotland Yard — Kiriakou's worldview shift

While working a case in London against the middleman between Carlos the Jackal and the Greek terrorist group [17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november), [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) reached out to MI6 counterparts. They told him: *"We'll find him, but this is an MI5 issue. You'll work with MI5. Just let us know what what you find."* Working with MI5 over the course of six months, Kiriakou learned that MI5 does not have arrest powers.<Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="1:59:40" /><Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="2:00:12" />

He asked his British counterpart for clarification: *"I always thought MI6 was the British [CIA](/wiki/cia). MI5 was the British FBI."* The reply: *"No, Scotland Yard is the British FBI."* When Kiriakou asked what MI5 was, the answer was: *"We are domestic intelligence. We spy on Britons."* Kiriakou said: *"I don't think I like that at all"* and *"this just turns my worldview on its head."* The British officer's response: *"Yeah, we're even worse than you guys are."*<Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="2:00:42" /><Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="2:01:15" />

## Five Eyes — what the relationship actually means

Per [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), the [Five Eyes](/wiki/five-eyes) relationship is operationally unique: officers from MI6 and the other Five Eyes services sit physically next to [CIA](/wiki/cia) and NSA officers at headquarters. The five services share everything and do not spy on one another. No other intelligence-sharing relationship in the world approaches this level of access — including with France, which is a NATO ally and EU member but is treated as a foreign service.<Cite s="2026-01-16-julian-dorey-french-intelligence" t="7:15" /><Cite s="2026-01-16-julian-dorey-french-intelligence" t="7:45" />
