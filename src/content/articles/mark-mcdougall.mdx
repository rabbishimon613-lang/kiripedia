**Mark McDougall** is a U.S. criminal defense attorney and was a member of [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s defense team in the 2012–2013 Espionage Act prosecution under the [Plato Cacheris](/wiki/plato-cacheris)-led firm. Kiriakou identifies him as *"one of the best attorneys I've ever encountered in my life, and the one that I liked and respected the most."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="58:26" />

## The decisive argument

On the morning of the final plea-deal decision — when Cacheris, Bob Trout, and McDougall came to Kiriakou's house to argue him into accepting — McDougall delivered the line Kiriakou cites as the one that broke through:

> You know what your problem is? Your problem is you think this is about justice — and it's not about justice. It's about mitigating damage. Take the deal.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="58:26" />

Kiriakou took the deal: thirty months, of which he served twenty-three at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto).<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="58:58" />

Attorney Mark McDougall advised John Kiriakou to accept a plea deal, emphasizing that the case was about mitigating damage rather than justice, and warned that a conviction at trial could result in a 12-to-18-year sentence. <Cite s="2026-05-25-john-kiriakou-podcast-cia-john-kiriakou-new-posdcast-2" t="40:39:00" />

## 'It's about mitigating damage' (Tegan Broadwater Pt 2)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says friends independently described his attorney [Mark McDougall](/wiki/mark-mcdougall) as *"the meanest man in Washington"* — and that McDougall got in his face to tell him, *"You think this is about justice, and it's not. It's about mitigating damage. Take the deal."* It was McDougall who told him the case could be *"a blip in your life or the defining event — make it the blip."*<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="24:57" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="25:28" />
