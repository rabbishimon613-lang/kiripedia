**Cofer Black's warning to the liaison class** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s first-hand account of the CIA counterterrorism chief's state of mind in the weeks before 11 September 2001.

Kiriakou was running a class for visiting foreign liaison officers, and [Cofer Black](/wiki/cofer-black) — then meeting the president every day or every other day — made time to come and address it. Black described al-Qaeda members saying goodbye to their families in tears, telling them *"I'll see you in paradise"*, and set out the code words the agency was hearing for a massive attack: the honey salesman is coming with vast quantities of honey; there is going to be a great football match; there is going to be an enormous wedding. *"These are all code words for a massive attack. We just don't know when and where."* He then begged the assembled officers: if you have any sources inside al-Qaeda, please help us.<Cite s="2026-02-02-dead-drop-s1e13-hostages" t="18:57" />

Kiriakou's description of the response is one sentence: *"they just sat there and looked at him."* Black shook their hands and walked out.<Cite s="2026-02-02-dead-drop-s1e13-hostages" t="18:57" /><Cite s="2026-02-02-dead-drop-s1e13-hostages" t="19:30" />

## The private question

Shaken by the briefing, Kiriakou went to Black's office afterwards to thank him for coming — and asked the question directly: he did not work on al-Qaeda, so had Black said all that for the visitors' benefit, or was he serious? *"Oh, I was deadly serious. Something terrible is going to happen."*<Cite s="2026-02-02-dead-drop-s1e13-hostages" t="19:30" /><Cite s="2026-02-02-dead-drop-s1e13-hostages" t="20:00" />

What Kiriakou did next was leave: a few weeks later he flew to the Middle East for six weeks, stopping in Rome on the way back.<Cite s="2026-02-02-dead-drop-s1e13-hostages" t="20:00" />

## Afterwards

Standing in the [Counterterrorism Center](/wiki/counterterrorism-center) bullpen on the morning of 11 September, watching the towers on a dozen televisions among some 150 silent colleagues, the thought Kiriakou reports having is not about the attack itself: *"this is what Cofer was talking about. This was the attack that he was talking about."*<Cite s="2026-02-02-dead-drop-s1e13-hostages" t="23:08" />
