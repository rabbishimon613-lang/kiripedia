A **walk-in** is, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, someone who literally walks into an embassy and offers information to an intelligence officer. About 95% are *"lunatics or liars"*; others are *"intelligence peddlers"* who sell a small nugget to the CIA, then the British, French, Russians and Chinese in turn; and some are hostile *"probes"* casing the embassy's defenses. Only about 1% are *"the real McCoy."*<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="25:15" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="25:45" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="26:18" /> During the [Abu Zubaydah hunt](/wiki/abu-zubaydah-capture), a friendly service claimed a *"walk-in"* had reported al-Qaeda fighters in a big yellow house; when told the walk-in was *"not available,"* Kiriakou knew there was no walk-in — it was a phone intercept the ally would not admit to.<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="26:48" />
