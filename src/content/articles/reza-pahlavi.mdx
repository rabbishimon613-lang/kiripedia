**Reza Pahlavi** is the exiled Iranian crown prince, son of the last Shah, whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) dismisses as an Israeli-backed fantasy candidate to rule Iran. Pahlavi has not lived in Iran since he was 16 or 17, lives comfortably — by one Kiriakou account in Potomac, Maryland, by another in McLean, Virginia — and draws his support mainly from wealthy Iranian exiles in Beverly Hills.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="17:35" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="18:05" /> The Israeli plan, Kiriakou says, is for the U.S. to install him and take 49% of Iran's oil in return — *"a fantasy"* echoing the [1953 coup](/wiki/united-fruit-arbenz-coup). Pahlavi, he says, *"wouldn't survive the walk from the plane to the terminal,"* and *"literally nobody in Iran"* wants the Pahlavis back.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="19:07" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="19:39" />

Kiriakou says Pahlavi told podcaster Patrick Bet-David directly that he does not actually want to return to Iran, having lived in the US since 1979: *"My kids are Americans. I have my life. It's in McLean, Virginia."*<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="45:48" /> He adds a further, unflattering personal detail — that Pahlavi's wife is cheating on him with her French personal trainer, which he says is documented in the French press.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="45:48" /> Kiriakou says he has received threats after calling Pahlavi "a clown" on a podcast, and repeats the assessment that Pahlavi would not survive the walk from the plane to the terminal if he returned to Iran.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="46:20" /> Mocking calls to reinstall Pahlavi as Iran's leader, Kiriakou compares him to Ahmed Chalabi, the Iraqi exile figure used to help justify the Iraq War — "except not as intelligent."<Cite s="2026-03-01-deprogram-w-ted-rall" t="47:50" /> He says he has faced criticism for his opposition to restoring the Iranian "so-called royal family."<Cite s="2026-06-16-the-third-way-orthodox" t="39:22" />

## Why he is hated in Iran

Kiriakou traces the hostility to Pahlavi's father, the Shah: he created SAVAK, the Iranian intelligence service notorious for mutilating dissidents, and — with CIA and MI6 help — overthrew Mohammad Mosaddegh, Iran's only democratically elected prime minister, in [1953](/wiki/united-fruit-arbenz-coup).<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="46:51" /> He says the U.S. worked with the Shah, Mohammad Reza Pahlavi, to crush all opposition — religious and communist alike — through escalating harassment, jailing, and exile, until the 1978 revolution culminated in the Shah's February 1979 overthrow and the November 1979 storming of the U.S. embassy.<Cite s="2021-09-15-garland-nixon" t="36:09" /> He notes the Shah and the royal family fled Iran for Switzerland in February 1979 as the revolution unfolded, taking with them gold bars, gems, jewels and Picassos.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="47:52" />

Responding to a *Washington Post* op-ed in which Reza Pahlavi called for Iran's "return to democracy," Kiriakou notes Iran was never a democracy under his father, whose SAVAK intelligence service tortured and executed tens of thousands, and calls Pahlavi financed by the Israelis.<Cite s="2026-01-09-unfiltered-with-s-a-m" t="34:19" />
