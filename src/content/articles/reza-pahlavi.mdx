**Reza Pahlavi** is the exiled Iranian crown prince, son of the last Shah, whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) dismisses as an Israeli-backed fantasy candidate to rule Iran. Pahlavi has not lived in Iran since he was 16 or 17, lives comfortably in Potomac, Maryland, and draws his support mainly from wealthy Iranian exiles in Beverly Hills.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="17:35" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="18:05" /> The Israeli plan, Kiriakou says, is for the U.S. to install him and take 49% of Iran's oil in return — *"a fantasy"* echoing the [1953 coup](/wiki/united-fruit-arbenz-coup). Pahlavi, he says, *"wouldn't survive the walk from the plane to the terminal,"* and *"literally nobody in Iran"* wants the Pahlavis back.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="19:07" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="19:39" />
