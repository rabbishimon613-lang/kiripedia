**George Habash** was the co-founder of the [Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine](/wiki/pflp). Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), Habash and [Ahmed Khatib](/wiki/ahmed-khatib) — the medical doctor whom Iraq installed as occupation governor of Kuwait on August 2, 1990 — were college roommates at the American University of Beirut, where they together founded the PFLP.<Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="4:40" /><Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="5:10" /><Cite s="2024-12-21-not-a-grayman" t="15:14" /><Cite s="2025-10-27-dead-drop-mini-episode-1-sadam-s-shrink" t="5:15" /><Cite s="2025-10-27-dead-drop-mini-episode-1-sadam-s-shrink" t="5:45" />

Kiriakou gives a fuller account of the roommate himself. Ahmed Khatib was Kuwaiti, and had written about him earlier as simply an interesting character: his mother had been a slave of the Kuwaiti royal family, a status not abolished in Kuwait until the 1950s, leaving Khatib with deep resentment toward the royal family. He was also Black, a minority in Kuwait, and faced discrimination on that basis — but he was bright enough that the Kuwaiti royal family nonetheless gave him a scholarship to study medicine at the American University of Beirut, where he roomed with Habash. Khatib returned to Kuwait afterward, declared his support for communism, and remained a thorn in the royal family's side from then on — a resentment that culminated in Iraq naming him interim occupation governor of Kuwait after the 1990 invasion.<Cite s="2025-10-27-dead-drop-mini-episode-1-sadam-s-shrink" t="5:15" /><Cite s="2025-10-27-dead-drop-mini-episode-1-sadam-s-shrink" t="5:45" />
