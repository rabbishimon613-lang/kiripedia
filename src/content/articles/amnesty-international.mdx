**Amnesty International** plays two opposite roles in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s story: witness for his defense, and bystander at his prosecution.

## The 2006 report

Amnesty's report that the CIA was torturing prisoners — following [Human Rights Watch](/wiki/human-rights-watch)'s in 2005 and preceding the International Committee of the Red Cross's in 2007 — is a fixed point in Kiriakou's account of his 2007 [ABC interview](/wiki/brian-ross): the torture was already public knowledge, so his confirmation *"added nothing, technically, to the debate."* What he added was being the first current or former CIA officer to say it.<Cite s="2015-05-06-the-real-news-network-why-i-was-targeted-by-the-cia-rai-9-10" t="09:25" /><Cite s="2017-05-31-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="36:46" /><Cite s="2015-07-25-splendour-in-the-grass" t="13:04" /> The FBI's first investigation reached the same conclusion — the information was out there, no crime committed — before Obama had the case reopened.<Cite s="2016-07-11-adventures-in-the-free-sta" t="24:49" /> Kiriakou also cites Amnesty, alongside Human Rights Watch and the Red Cross, on the drone war: reports that some 90% of those killed were innocent civilians who happened to be near the strike site.<Cite s="2016-04-26-adelya-f-hako-beyond-the-horizon-s1-e9-john-kiriakou" t="10:14" />

## The silence

Asked at a Berlin event why he never mentions Amnesty International, Kiriakou's answer was unsparing: *"I begged Amnesty International to release even a one-sentence statement about my case."* He wrote to headquarters in London, and to Zeke Johnson, director of Amnesty International USA — *"who was kind enough to send me a subscription to The New Yorker magazine, along with a letter saying I'm sorry, but they don't want to have anything to do with your case."* After about a year, he got tired of begging. *"I got zero help from Amnesty."*<Cite s="2017-06-06-disruption-network-lab-locked-up-for-exposing-crimes-kiriakou-annie" t="1:30:03" /><Cite s="2017-06-06-disruption-network-lab-locked-up-for-exposing-crimes-kiriakou-annie" t="1:30:35" />
