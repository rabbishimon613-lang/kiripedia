The **Office of the Director of National Intelligence** is the coordinating layer placed above the American intelligence agencies in 2004. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s position is that it should not exist.

## Why it was created

He gives the origin as a reaction to a single man's failures: it was created *"because [George Tenet](/wiki/george-tenet), who is the DCI, director of central intelligence, essentially the director of the entire intelligence community, fell on his face with 9/11 and the Iraq war and they needed to have an adult in the room. And so they just created this ODNI thinking if we put one more layer of of oversight, it's going to make everything better. And all it did was make everything more confusing and fatter."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="32:13" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="32:46" />

## The growth

Kiriakou says he agrees with one thing [Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) has said about it — that it is bloated, inefficient and full of fat — and gives the numbers: *"When when I was still in the [CIA](/wiki/cia) and they created ODNI, they had 16 employees. 16 employees. Now they have about 24,000 employees. Why? What are they doing?"* He allows for the transfer of the President's Daily Brief from the CIA — *"okay, there's there's two dozen people right there"* — but not for the rest, and asks in particular why CIA operations against Iran, Iraq, Russia or China are run from the ODNI when *"it's CIA case officers collecting the information, CIA analysts analyzing the information, CIA budgets that are financing the information."* His answer: *"It it was just a power grab."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="30:09" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="30:41" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="31:13" />

## Authority it does not have

The structural objection is that the director of national intelligence does not direct national intelligence. Almost all of the agencies technically under the ODNI *"are actually DoD agencies"* — the NSA and the DIA belong to the Defense Department, as do army, navy, air force, marine and coast guard intelligence, all of them nominally ODNI components while falling under the Pentagon. *"So what does the DNI do with all of these other organizations? It controls only the budget. Nothing but the budget. So why bother why bother having it in the first place?"*<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="31:13" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="31:43" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="32:13" />
