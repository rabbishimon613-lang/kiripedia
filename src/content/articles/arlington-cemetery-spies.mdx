On the *Walk With History* podcast, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) — author of *Remains of the Day: A Definitive Guide to Washington D.C.'s Historic Cemeteries* — walked through Arlington National Cemetery and discussed the graves of figures connected to American intelligence history, several of whom he knew or whose stories he witnessed firsthand.<Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="1:00" />

## The creation of the CIA

[Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounted how the [CIA](/wiki/cia) came to exist independently. When Truman pushed the [National Security Act of 1947](/wiki/national-security-act), FBI Director J. Edgar Hoover opposed creating a separate agency, arguing that if one were created he should run both it and the FBI. Truman called Hoover into the Oval Office and told him the bill would make the CIA a division of the FBI. That was a lie. Hoover believed it and withdrew his objections; the CIA was created independently, and [William Donovan](/wiki/william-donovan) became its natural first leader.<Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="2:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="3:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="3:30" />

## The figures buried there

Kiriakou's tour covered OSS founder [William "Wild Bill" Donovan](/wiki/william-donovan); deputy CIA director and secret Nixon envoy [Vernon Walters](/wiki/vernon-walters); codebreakers [William and Elizabeth Friedman](/wiki/william-friedman), whose work led to the founding of the NSA; slain Athens station chief [Richard Welch](/wiki/richard-welch); [Beirut](/wiki/beirut/) station chief [William Buckley](/wiki/bill-buckley), tortured to death on videotape; U-2 pilot [Francis Gary Powers](/wiki/francis-gary-powers); and [Mike Spann](/wiki/mike-spann), the first [CIA](/wiki/cia) officer killed after the September 11 attacks.<Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="4:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="13:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="16:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="18:00" />
