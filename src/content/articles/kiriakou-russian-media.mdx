**Kiriakou's Russian media work** is his daily radio show on Sputnik, which he has hosted for seven and a half years. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he initially declined, then agreed on the condition — written into his contract — that he could *"say anything I want and criticize anyone I want, including Vladimir Putin."*<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="15:12" /><Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="15:43" /> He has never been asked to say or not say anything: on the morning of the invasion he opened by condemning it and refused to use the state term *"special military operation,"* calling it the *"Russian invasion of Ukraine."*<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="16:14" /><Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="16:45" /> He notes the Washington Post accused him of *"weakening our democracy"* over his weekly criminal-justice segment — for airing stories like a Pennsylvania judge jailing children for a private-prison bribe.<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="17:17" /><Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="17:48" />
