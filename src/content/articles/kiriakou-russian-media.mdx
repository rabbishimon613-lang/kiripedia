**Kiriakou's Russian media work** is his daily radio show on Sputnik, a Russian state-owned media company, which he has hosted for seven and a half years (elsewhere he says seven years, and elsewhere dates the run 2017 to 2024). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he initially declined, then agreed on the condition — written into his contract — that he could *"say anything I want and criticize anyone I want, including Vladimir Putin."*<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="15:12" /><Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="15:43" /><Cite t="45:20" /> He says only the Russians offered him work at all after his release from prison, contrasting this with being shunned by the CIA and the broader U.S. media landscape; he has said he took the job purely for financial reasons after being turned down for work as a convicted felon by Uber, Lyft, Safeway, Target, and even as a baggage handler at Reagan National Airport.<Cite
