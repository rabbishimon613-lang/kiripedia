**Panquake** is a social platform developed by Talk Liberation, an Iceland-registered company. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has served as its brand ambassador since [Suzie Dawson](/wiki/suzie-dawson) approached him in approximately March 2020, and describes the people behind it as having *"devoted their lives to transparency, to equality, and to making sure that everybody has a platform that they can trust that's not going to sell their data, rat them out to some intelligence service."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="10:37" /><Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="19:35" />

## Design principles

Kiriakou characterises the platform as open source with *"no secrets, no back doors"*, carrying no advertising and taking money from neither governments nor billionaires.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="15:52" /> He describes it as blockchain-based — specifically a layer-one blockchain — rather than merely open source.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="30:41" /> Monetisation runs on subscription rather than advertising, which he contrasts with a Facebook feed where *"you got to go down 25 stories before you finally get to somebody that you actually know"*, and with an X account that serves him [Elon Musk](/wiki/elon-musk)'s posts although he does not follow him and every attempt to unfollow is refused.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="47:30" /><Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="48:32" />

A detail Kiriakou says stuck with him from Dawson's first approach is that the servers are powered by geothermal steam from Icelandic geysers: *"not only is it open source, open code and no back doors and no capitalist intervention — it's green on top of it all."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="19:35" />

Being distributed across the world is, in his account, a jurisdictional design choice as much as a practical one — a company based in Iceland with staff in every time zone *"doesn't have to deal directly with the American government or the British government or any of the Five Eyes."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="41:18" /> The team is spread widely enough that meetings require some participants to wake in the middle of the night, and Kiriakou says the first time he sat with his colleagues in person was at an international conference in Hong Kong roughly a year before this interview.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="22:13" />

## PanquakeMe and LinkMe

Talk Liberation ships several separate products under one umbrella.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="38:12" /> The first Kiriakou describes is PanquakeMe, a link cleaner: a pasted URL is returned as a shortened link that can be read without cookies being deposited, without data collection and without the reader's information being sold onward, and which also archives the content.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="23:51" /><Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="28:01" /> Kiriakou frames the need for it in terms of the ordinary chain of resale — clicking through to a news article causes cookies to follow the reader, and the publisher sells that data on to companies that resell it to advertisers *"and really anybody who wants to pay the money for it including the FBI, the CIA and the NSA."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="23:18" /> See [purchased data as a warrant workaround](/wiki/purchased-data-warrant-workaround).

A second product, LinkMe, offers a comparable data-protection outcome aimed at businesses, marketers and journalists managing several platforms at once.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="37:38" />

## Free-speech consistency

Asked whether Benjamin Netanyahu would be permitted an account, Kiriakou answered that he would: *"if we're serious about this we've got to be consistent."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="49:04" /> He drew the boundary at criminal content — child pornography, drug sales — rather than at political speech, and framed the alternative as the pattern by which activists writing about Gaza lose access to existing platforms.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="46:30" />

He has also engaged rather than dismissed the criticism that the platform has taken too long, saying he understands that people are *"hungry for something to fight"* the incumbents.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="21:09" />
