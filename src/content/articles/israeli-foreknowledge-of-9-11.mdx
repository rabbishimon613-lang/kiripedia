**Israeli foreknowledge of 9/11** is a position [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) states as his own belief and labels as speculation, arrived at by working backwards from an arrest pattern he considers otherwise unexplained.

## The arrests

Dozens of Israeli citizens were arrested by NYPD officers on 11 September 2001 and in the days that followed — mostly for photographing sensitive sites, including the World Trade Center, or for comments they made about the attacks — and held on suspicion of something national-security related. The Israelis then came to the United States, said the men were theirs, and asked for them to be released.<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="13:33" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="14:03" />

Kiriakou is careful that the release itself is unremarkable. Normal practice when an American officer is caught in a friendly country is public denial and a private request from the station chief to the head of service; the person may be made to cool his heels for a week or two, but he is let go.<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="10:58" /> "Yes, that actually is normal."<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="10:58" />

## The inference

What he does not consider normal is the presence. His stated belief is that Israel had infiltrated al-Qaeda, knew the attack was coming, and did not say so — and that the men were in position to document what was about to happen. The motive he assigns is that Israel knew an attacked America would overreact: a war lasting twenty years, two million Muslims killed, roughly six trillion dollars spent. "Just like they knew chaos in Iraq was good for Israel, chaos in Libya was good for Israel, chaos in Syria was good for Israel, and now chaos in Iran is good for Israel."<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="11:30" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="12:00" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="12:31" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="14:03" />

## What the CIA was asking for

The context Kiriakou supplies is his own. On 6 July 2001 he was entertaining a group of Middle Eastern intelligence officers when [Cofer Black](/wiki/cofer-black), director of the Counterterrorism Center, came into the meeting and minced no words: something terrible was going to happen, on a massive scale; they did not know what, where or when. Black ended by saying, "I'm begging you" — if you have any sources inside al-Qaeda, please help us.<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="14:33" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="15:04" />

The agency had no sources inside al-Qaeda of its own, which is why it needed liaison. The other Arab countries did not have them either; the Gulf states did not; the Pakistanis either did not or would not say. "And the Israelis just sat back and waited for it all to happen and then came and said, 'We are one with you, America.'"<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="15:04" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="15:35" />
