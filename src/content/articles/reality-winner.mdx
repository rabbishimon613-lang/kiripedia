**Reality Winner** was a translator at the National Security Agency who believed the Russian government had helped the Trump campaign committee win the 2016 election.<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="21:47" /> She sent a one-page classified analysis of Russian involvement in that election to [The Intercept](/wiki/the-intercept), whose website invited exactly this — *"Hey, if you have classified information, here's our encryption key. Send it to us."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="21:47" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="22:19" /> It reached [Matthew Cole](/wiki/matthew-cole).<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="22:19" />

## How she was burned

In Kiriakou's account Cole scanned the page and emailed it to the NSA to ask whether it was a genuine NSA document.<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="22:19" /> The failure he identifies is technical rather than moral: an experienced national security reporter should have known that intelligence agencies guard against leaks by embedding the printing employee's identification number in the document itself, *"in the dot of an I or in a period at the end of the sentence."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="22:50" /> The agency therefore needed only to enlarge the image. Winner was arrested four hours after Cole published the story, and served five years and three months in prison.<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="22:50" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="23:20" /> Kiriakou's verdict on the reporter is unqualified — *"he's so stupid"* — and on the outcome, that she went to prison *"because of Matthew Cole."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="22:50" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="23:20" />

He places her alongside [Terry Albury](/wiki/terry-albury) as the second of Cole's burned sources, and notes that he has since come to know both: *"I'm proud to say that Reality and Terry are friends of mine now."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="23:20" />
