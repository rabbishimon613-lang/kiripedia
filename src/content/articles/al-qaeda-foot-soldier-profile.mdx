Asked what Americans would understand differently about the Middle East if they had seen what he had seen, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) answers with the biographies of the captured, and calls the finding revelatory.

He prefaces it with his own standing: a bachelor's degree in Middle Eastern studies, a master's in legislative affairs focused on Middle East foreign policy, Arabic, years living in the region, and an affection for its history, literature, poetry, food and culture — *"I love all of it."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-epstein-was-an-access-agent-for-mossad" t="26:33" />

## The same story, over and over

*"What was revelatory to me was when we started capturing al-Qaeda fighters — not the leaders, but these kids, 18, 19, 20 years old. They all essentially told the same story, or variations on the same story."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-epstein-was-an-access-agent-for-mossad" t="26:33" />

The common elements: an isolated village in whatever country they came from — Yemen, Tunisia, Saudi Arabia — mostly illiterate, no job skills, and *"very poor relationships with their fathers, which struck me as very interesting. And I'll let the psychologists sort that out. I don't know what it means."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-epstein-was-an-access-agent-for-mossad" t="27:04" />

## The thing that actually moved them

The element Kiriakou treats as decisive is neither theology nor politics but marriage. *"They were of an age where they wanted to get married. And what father is going to want his daughter to marry a kid who can't read, can't write, can't work, and has no prospects for the future? I wouldn't want my daughter to marry a guy like that."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-epstein-was-an-access-agent-for-mossad" t="27:35" />

Into that gap came a recruiter with a price list. *"The local imam went up to them and said, 'Why do you want to stay in this village? You don't want to stay in this village. You should go to Afghanistan and make jihad against the Americans, and we'll pay you $300 a month. And if you're martyred, we'll give your parents a $500 martyrdom bonus.'"*<Cite s="2025-12-15-epstein-was-an-access-agent-for-mossad" t="27:35" />

Their answer, as Kiriakou reports it, was not ideological either: *"Okay. Have nothing better going on."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-epstein-was-an-access-agent-for-mossad" t="28:05" />

## What follows from it

The policy conclusion he draws is economic rather than military: *"what we really need to do in the Middle East is spend money on economic development, on education, and on infrastructure. And it doesn't have to be our money. It can be Emirati money or Qatari money or Saudi money."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-epstein-was-an-access-agent-for-mossad" t="28:05" />

The mechanism is occupation of a life rather than persuasion: *"what they really need is economic development so that people are busy building careers and growing families, rather than looking for a place to go make jihad against the Americans."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-epstein-was-an-access-agent-for-mossad" t="28:36" />
