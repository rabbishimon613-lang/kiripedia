*"Admit nothing, deny everything, make counter-accusations"* was a slogan printed on coffee mugs sold inside [CIA](/wiki/cia) headquarters during [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s service there. *"It was kind of a joke, but it was kind of not a joke — and they were by far the most popular coffee mugs in the whole place. Everybody had them on their desks."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="28:46" />

Kiriakou raised it in 2019 while discussing merchandise as a fundraising route for [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange)'s legal defence, proposing that the same slogan could be printed and sold to pay for *"the best lawyers in Washington"* — *"there would be nothing better than to have that actually work."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="28:46" />
