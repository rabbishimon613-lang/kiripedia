**Truman's CIA op-ed** is the 1963 Washington Post piece in which former President Harry Truman — who had signed the CIA into existence under the National Security Act of 1947, insisting on bans against domestic law enforcement and spying — repudiated it. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says the op-ed declared the CIA *"a damn fool mistake"* that should be disbanded, and that it appeared in the newspaper's morning edition but was gone from the evening one.<Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="32:19" /><Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="32:49" /> Kiriakou adds that Truman came to believe the CIA was involved in the [Kennedy assassination](/wiki/jfk-assassination), and that even Eisenhower, one of its greatest supporters, concluded it was *"out of control"* — before congressional oversight committees existed in 1976.<Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="33:21" /><Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="33:52" />
