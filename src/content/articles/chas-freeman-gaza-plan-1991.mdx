The **1991 Saudi plan for Gaza** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of an infrastructure proposal made during his first overseas posting, which he raises whenever a version of it resurfaces.

In 1991 Kiriakou was a new [CIA](/wiki/cia) officer on his first overseas tour, in Saudi Arabia, working to an ambassador he describes as having become legendary — Chas Freeman, later ambassador to China and an assistant secretary of defense, *"brilliant"* in Kiriakou's assessment.<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="29:03" /> Freeman put a question to the Saudis: what if they were asked to build a port, an airport, a water desalination plant and an electrical grid in Gaza? *"And they said, yeah, we would do it."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="29:03" /> The proposal was then taken to the Israelis, *"and the Israelis said, absolutely never."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="29:35" />

Kiriakou raises the episode because the same architecture reappeared in the Israeli press more than three decades later: Israel would technically continue to own Gaza while day-to-day administration passed to Saudi Arabia, which would also invest in an electrical grid, desalination, and a port, with Gaza disarmed and no Palestinian military. He calls it *"a great idea"* — and notes he has said so on podcasts more than once — before adding the date: *"That was 1991."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="28:31" /><Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="29:03" />

## Why he thinks the question recurs

The reason Kiriakou keeps returning to the plan is his reading of the conditions it was meant to relieve. Before [October 7](/wiki/october-7-attack), he says, Gaza had *"but 5 hours of water every day"* before Israel shut it off, and a few hours of electricity before Israel shut that off too.<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="29:35" /> His conclusion is stated as a prediction rather than a judgement: *"The situation's untenable because another October 7th is going to happen if there's not a sort of a pressure release valve."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="29:35" /> He closes the thought with a line he attributes to Martin Luther King: *"Never underestimate a man's desire to be free."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="30:07" />

On the ceasefire that had just been reached at the time of the interview, Kiriakou is careful about the word: *"It's not a peace deal. It's just a ceasefire. It doesn't address a single one of the core issues. Not a single one."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="26:27" /> He says he is rooting for it to hold, and commends the populations on both sides for trying, while withholding any confidence in either leadership — and states that a durable settlement requires an independent Palestine, *"because the current situation's untenable."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="26:57" /><Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="28:01" />
