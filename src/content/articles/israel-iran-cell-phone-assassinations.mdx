**Israel's cell-phone assassinations in Iran** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of how Israel destroyed Iran's military leadership. It killed all 12 of Iran's top generals by targeting their cell phones — and gathered the intelligence by recruiting some of the two million undocumented Afghan refugees living destitute in Iran, paying $50 to $100 a month to learn when each general left his apartment and which way he went.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="12:26" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="12:56" /> When Iran realized what was happening and forbade its generals to carry phones, Israel targeted the *bodyguards'* phones instead, killing the next wave of leaders.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="13:27" /> Kiriakou says Israel similarly eliminates Iranian nuclear scientists — recruiting them or, if they refuse, killing them by drone or by assassins abroad.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="13:59" />

Kiriakou has repeated and expanded on this account elsewhere. He says Mossad has effectively blanket coverage of Iran, intercepting all Iranian communications — including military and leadership traffic — and has infiltrated the country on the ground using Farsi-native Iranian Jewish emigres who moved to the West and were sent back.<Cite s="2026-01-09-unfiltered-with-s-a-m" t="29:43" /> He gives a more specific mechanism for the phone-tracking recruitment: because Iran, itself strained by sanctions, offers Afghan refugees no state benefits — no free food, housing, or medical care — Israel approached them directly, paying $300 to $500 a month for a general's or scientist's home address, departure time, and daily route.<Cite s="2026-01-09-unfiltered-with-s-a-m" t="30:43" /> In another telling, Kiriakou describes Israel successfully recruiting desperate, deported Afghan illegal aliens inside Iran for around $50 a tip to identify the same locations.<Cite s="2025-10-16-truth-hurts-show" t="23:55" /> He also says Israel uses Iranian-born Jews who speak fluent, accentless Farsi to identify the specific apartment blocks where Iranian nuclear scientists live, before striking with a rocket — killing everyone in the building if necessary.<Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="58:47" /> Kiriakou adds that Israeli hackers separately broke into Tehran's traffic cameras, letting them watch generals and scientists driving through the city and know exactly where they would be at what time — *"and that's when you rocket them. It's good tradecraft is what it is."*<Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="05:51" /> Separately, Kiriakou says Israeli intelligence can break encryption faster than the United States, and that the FBI will sometimes contract encryption-breaking work out to Israel, which retains the intercepted data.<Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="37:44" />

## Coercion by phone

In a June 2026 account, Kiriakou described a further Israeli tactic — psychological coercion by phone, the same method he says the United States used against Iraqi officers in 2003. Citing *Washington Post* reporting of a recording in which a Farsi-speaking Mossad officer telephoned an Iranian general, he said the Israelis first slipped notes under generals' apartment doors in the middle of the night — *"we're going to kill you tomorrow... you can come work for us or we're going to kill you"* — then called their wives, warning that the whole apartment building might be destroyed, and finally called the generals themselves, demanding each record a video renouncing the Iranian government and backing Israel. When they refused, he says, the Israelis *"legitimately went out and killed every one of these guys, more than half a dozen of them."*<Cite s="2026-06-06-mossad-s-deadly-tactic-in-iran" t="01:35" /><Cite s="2026-06-06-mossad-s-deadly-tactic-in-iran" t="02:07" /> He added that the Israelis would level an entire apartment block to be sure of killing one target — *"they'll kill hundreds just to get that one person"* — as he says they did in Beirut and southern Lebanon.<Cite s="2026-06-06-mossad-s-deadly-tactic-in-iran" t="02:37" />

Kiriakou is careful to distinguish these recruited refugees from Iran's resident Jewish community: the conventional wisdom that Israel simply recruits Iranian Jews is, he says, false — Iranian Jews, more numerous than in any Middle Eastern country outside Israel, are loyal to the Iranian government, many having volunteered for the military to fight Israel. The exploitable population is instead the millions of destitute Afghan and Pakistani Baloch refugees who, having crossed Iran's vast unguarded border since 1979, are eligible for neither Iranian citizenship nor state aid.<Cite s="2026-06-06-mossad-s-deadly-tactic-in-iran" t="03:41" /><Cite s="2026-06-06-mossad-s-deadly-tactic-in-iran" t="04:45" />
