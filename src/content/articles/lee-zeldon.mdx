**Lee Zeldin** — rendered "Lee Zeldon" in the source transcript — is discussed by [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) as the sitting EPA administrator reportedly considered by [Trump](/wiki/trump) as a possible replacement for Attorney General [Pam Bondi](/wiki/pam-bondi), per a Semaphore report that Trump had told Bondi her time as attorney general was "nearing an end."<Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="01:02" />

Kiriakou later notes that earlier reports floated Zeldin as Bondi's likely replacement, but that Deputy Attorney General [Todd Blanche](/wiki/todd-blanch) was seen as making "a lot more sense" for the role, both because of his job title and his reputation as well-liked within the department.<Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="1:24:45" />
