**Jim Jordan** is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Ohio, whom Kiriakou calls powerful and identifies as the chairman of a major committee while declining to be certain which: *"the Judiciary Committee or the Homeland Security Committee in the House. Something like one of the very big committees."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="35:55" />

His view is explicitly divided — *"I sort of have a love-hate thing going with Jim Jordan"* — and the first criticism is one of effectiveness: *"He talks a tough game. But he doesn't really get a whole lot of things done."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="35:55" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="36:27" />

What decides the grade is older. Before Congress, Jordan was the wrestling coach at Ohio State University, and *"wrestlers repeatedly went to him and said, 'Coach, we're being sexually abused by the team doctor.' And he did nothing to help them. Nothing."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="36:27" /> Kiriakou converts that into the operational judgement the segment calls for: as a case officer *"he probably couldn't be trusted to live within the confines of the rules,"* and the grade is a D.<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="36:59" />
