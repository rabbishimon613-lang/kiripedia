**Site 13** was one of the fourteen possible locations a [targeting analyst](/wiki/targeting-analyst) narrowed the hunt for [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) down to, and the one that mattered. It was the nearest target to the CIA safe house in Faisalabad, and became known afterwards simply as the Abu Zubaydah house.<Cite s="2026-02-23-dead-drop-s1e16-captured" t="35:31" />

## From the roof

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) watched from the roof of the Faisalabad safe house with his colleague. At half past one everyone else left; at two he counted down — *"three, two, one, here we go"* — and immediately heard a sound in the distance: *boink, boink, boink*, metal on metal. He said at once that it was not good.<Cite s="2026-02-23-dead-drop-s1e16-captured" t="35:31" /><Cite s="2025-01-19-eyes-wide-open-i-conor-ryan-ex-cia-spy-john-kiriakou-hunting-terroris" t="52:29" /> Then shots. He called Site 13 on the walkie-talkie and got only crackling — the walkie-talkies never worked — and reached the team leader by mobile telephone, who could only scream *shots fired, shots fired* back at him.<Cite s="2026-02-23-dead-drop-s1e16-captured" t="36:03" /><Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="1:38:26" />

## What had happened

The *boink* had been the battering ram failing against a steel-reinforced door, which cost the assault team the seconds that mattered. Upstairs were three men: Abu Zubaydah, a Syrian bomb-maker and a Syrian bodyguard. Downstairs were about twenty low-level fighters who knew only that somebody important was above them — they had worked it out because whoever was upstairs had a tea boy who went to the market each day to buy and cook their food, while the men on the ground floor fended for themselves.<Cite s="2026-02-23-dead-drop-s1e16-captured" t="37:04" />

The three upstairs ran to the roof and tried to jump to the neighbouring house. Pakistani police on the ground picked them off with an AK-47: the bomb-maker jumped first and was dead before he hit the ground; Abu Zubaydah jumped second and was shot in the thigh, the groin and the stomach; the bodyguard jumped last and was shot through the leg.<Cite s="2026-02-23-dead-drop-s1e16-captured" t="38:09" />

## The scene

Kiriakou and his colleague drove over to find what he calls utter chaos: three men lying in the street, one clearly dead, one who looked as though he would be dead within minutes, and one screaming with an enormous gunshot wound through the centre of his femur.<Cite s="2026-02-23-dead-drop-s1e16-captured" t="36:33" /><Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="1:38:57" /> A Pakistani officer told him they had got his man, they had got [Mr. Fish](/wiki/mr-fish), and pointed at the one who looked nearly dead — a man who resembled the four-year-old passport photograph not at all. Kiriakou's standing instruction from headquarters had been to take him alive and fire no shots.<Cite s="2026-02-23-dead-drop-s1e16-captured" t="37:04" /> He was identified by a photograph of his ear and taken to [Faisalabad Hospital](/wiki/faisalabad-hospital).<Cite s="2026-02-23-dead-drop-s1e16-captured" t="40:23" />
