**Jerry Falwell Jr.** was an evangelical leader and president of Liberty University. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts that Falwell called him shortly after he blew the whistle on the [CIA torture program](/wiki/cia-torture-program-whistleblowing) and offered him a post lecturing at the university's Jesse Helms School of Government.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="52:22" /> Kiriakou says he warned Falwell they probably disagreed on 99% of the issues; Falwell answered, *"we want you because torture is not Christian."* Kiriakou took the job and says he loved every second of it, even as they argued over the role of evangelical Christians in the Middle East.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="52:52" />
