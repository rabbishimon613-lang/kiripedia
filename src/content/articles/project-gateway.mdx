**Project Gateway** is, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, the CIA's astral-projection and remote-viewing effort — a component of the broader [MK-Ultra](/wiki/mk-ultra) program that ran from 1952 to 1975, distinct from the later [Project Stargate](/wiki/project-stargate), which Kiriakou dismissed as *"so out there."*<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:57:47" /> He says it grew out of a fabricated source who told the CIA the Russians were experimenting with ESP, astral projection and mind reading, prompting a panic to catch up. In fact, Kiriakou says, the Russians were doing no such thing — though the Chinese were, unknown to the agency at the time.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:58:18" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:58:49" />
