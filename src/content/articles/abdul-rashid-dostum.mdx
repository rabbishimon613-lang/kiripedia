**Abdul Rashid Dostum** is an Afghan warlord and Northern Alliance commander. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) characterizes him as *"the greatest traitor in the history of Afghanistan"* — a man who switched sides repeatedly: *"he's with the Northern Alliance, then he's with the Taliban, then he's with the Northern Alliance again, he's with the Taliban again."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="3:39:30" />

## The Dashti-Leyli massacre

Per [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), after roughly 2,000 Taliban soldiers surrendered, Dostum was placed in charge of the prisoners. The [CIA](/wiki/cia) advised loading them onto trucks and holding them in the desert. Dostum's forces crammed the prisoners into shipping containers with no food, water, or air holes; one of the few survivors said that when the containers were opened, the bodies fell out *"like sardines from a can."* Almost all of the 2,000 suffocated. This is the [Dashti-Leyli massacre](/wiki/dasht-i-leili-massacre).<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="3:39:43" /><Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="3:40:00" />

Kiriakou's assessment: *"We always believed Dostum did it on purpose because he was that kind of a psychopath."* He notes that Barack Obama promised during the 2008 campaign to order a National Security Council investigation of the massacre, but that *"never happened because the agency got to him right away."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="3:40:30" /><Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="3:41:00" />

## Ordering the containers

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) names General [Abdul Rashid Dostum](/wiki/abdul-rashid-dostum) — *"anti-Taliban, then pro-Taliban… then pro-Taliban again"* — as the man who ordered the surrendered prisoners into the airless trucks of the [Dasht-i-Leili massacre](/wiki/dasht-i-leili-massacre), and says the CIA was *"certain that he did it specifically to murder them."*<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:03:07" />
