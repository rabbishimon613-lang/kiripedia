**Iranian suicide drones** are the weapon [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) told viewers to watch on the first day of the 2026 war, breaking into the news reads to say so: *"Another thing we should be we should be looking for, Ted, um is uh is the continuing use of drones."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:42" />

## Why they matter

The advantage he identifies is industrial rather than technical. *"One thing that the Iranians are very good at is they build their own suicide drones right there in Iran using Iranian parts, and they can build lots of them."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:42" />

Domestic production from domestic components is what makes them immune to interdiction — a supply that cannot be blockaded, in a war where Kiriakou expected everything arriving from Russia or China to face exactly that problem.

He grounds the claim in the previous autumn: *"Remember all these drones that they sent against against Israel back in October, these were all homemade drones."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:42" />

## What "suicide drone" means

Kiriakou defines the term rather than assuming it. *"When I call them suicide drones, it means that they have explosives strapped to them, and they are remotely flown directly into a target where they explode. They're not meant to launch a missile and then return to their base. So, they're they they commit suicide, um so to speak."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:42" /><Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:12" />

## Against a ship

The same logic of quantity governs the naval case. A single drone sent against an American warship, he said, will be shot down; the defence fails on volume, because *"a ship has only so many defensive missiles on it."* At five hundred, *"you can't shoot all of them down."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="58:07" /><Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="58:40" />

The October attack on Israel is, on his reading, the same lesson learned in the other direction: [six hundred drones sent, seven through](/wiki/iron-dome), and the seven were the point.
