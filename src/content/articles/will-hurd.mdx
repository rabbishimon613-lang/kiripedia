**Will Hurd** is a former CIA case officer, U.S. congressman and OpenAI board member. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he mentored Hurd — then a junior case officer with *"a gift"* for convincing people to commit espionage — before Hurd resigned to run for Congress in a court-mandated majority-Hispanic district, which he won three times.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="52:43" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="53:15" /> Hurd briefly ran for president in 2024, gave up his seat, and joined the board of OpenAI; Kiriakou offers him as an example of the intelligence and technology worlds coming together, keeping *"one foot in the game and one foot in business."*<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="53:46" /> In a separate interview Kiriakou gave a fuller account of the same mentorship, calling Hurd *"an awesome case officer, as honest as the day is long"* despite their differing politics, and recalling Hurd announcing his resignation to run for Congress; Kiriakou notes Hurd — who is half Black, half white — won three times in the same Supreme Court-mandated majority-Hispanic district before his 2024 presidential run and subsequent seat on the OpenAI board.<Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:39:57" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:40:28" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:40:58" />
