**Jonathan Pollard** is the U.S. Navy civilian intelligence analyst convicted in 1985 of spying for Israel. He is the canonical publicly proven case of Israeli espionage against the United States. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) invokes Pollard's case in his account of his first appearance on Piers Morgan's program, in which he debated a former director of the [MOSSAD](/wiki/mossad) who denied any post-1985 Israeli espionage against the United States. Kiriakou's counter: *"Jonathan Pollard was caught in 1985, and you were spying on the United States in 1998, you know, or 2004."* The former Mossad director declined to comment.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:11:32" />

## The KGB trade (Tommy G, April 2026)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described a dimension of the Pollard case that he characterized as illustrating the nature of the U.S.-Israel intelligence relationship. The Israelis, after receiving the classified Pentagon material from [Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard), traded that intelligence to the KGB in exchange for a planeload of Russian Jews being permitted to resettle as refugees in Israel.<Cite s="2026-04-20-tommy-g-israel-double-agents-directed-energy" t="18:00" /><Cite s="2026-04-20-tommy-g-israel-double-agents-directed-energy" t="18:30" />

Kiriakou offered this as context for why he believed [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) was collecting classified defense documents from figures such as [Peter Mandelson](/wiki/peter-mandelson) and Prince Andrew: *"This is exactly what the Israelis have collected through history. Why would today be any different?"*<Cite s="2026-04-20-tommy-g-israel-double-agents-directed-energy" t="18:30" />

## "The year of the spy"

Kiriakou sets the arrest in the atmosphere of the year that produced it: 1985 was the year Ronald Reagan declared *"the year of the spy."* He uses the date as the hinge of his standard rebuttal — Pollard was arrested in 1985, and five years later, on Kiriakou's first day at the [CIA](/wiki/cia), the agency's head of security was briefing new officers on undeclared [Israeli intelligence](/wiki/israeli-espionage-in-the-united-states) officers then operating across the United States. The claim that Israeli espionage stopped with Pollard therefore fails on the agency's own onboarding material.<Cite s="2025-07-24-deepstatebear-everybody-hates-israel" t="38:57" />

## 187 undeclared Israeli officers — day-one CIA briefing

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described what he was told on his very first day at the [CIA](/wiki/cia) as a new hire. In an auditorium with approximately 300 other new employees, the CIA director of security gave a briefing. He stated that at the Israeli embassy in Washington, there are two declared intelligence officers: one from [MOSSAD](/wiki/mossad), one from Shin Bet. But the FBI had identified 187 undeclared Israeli intelligence officers operating across the United States, the majority attempting to infiltrate American defense contractors.<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:01:00" /><Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:01:30" />

The reason they were not arrested: the political relationship with Israel was too close and Congress would not permit it.<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:01:30" /> Kiriakou has repeated the same 187-officer figure elsewhere, saying he stated on Piers Morgan's show that Israel spies on the United States and that Pollard "was not an anomaly."<Cite s="2026-02-13-o-keefe-media-group" t="1:16:44" />

## Pollard's motive and the Soviet trade

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) stated that [Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard) was a Navy intelligence analyst — not a [CIA](/wiki/cia) officer — who stole thousands of pages of top-secret documents and sold them to Israel. His motive was money, not ideology, not religion, not love of Israel. The Israelis then traded those top-secret documents to the Soviet Union in exchange for the release of Soviet Jews to emigrate to Israel.<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:02:00" /><Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:02:30" /> Kiriakou has repeated this account elsewhere, describing Pollard as an American Navy analyst of Jewish faith caught in 1985, whose stolen military documents Israel likewise traded to the Soviet Union for the release of Soviet Jews.<Cite s="2024-08-12-danny-jones-loyal-officer-vs-dissident-spy" t="2:28:55" /> On a separate program he called it "the top secret documents that he stole from the Pentagon, traded them to the Soviet KGB in exchange for a plane load of Jewish refugees," adding that Pollard was "essentially spying for the KGB."<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="1:58:09" /> Kiriakou has also cited Pollard as the standard example of a civilian U.S. naval intelligence asset who passed classified material to Israel, which Israel then disseminated onward.<Cite s="2026-06-08-mitha-intel-israel-spies-on-americans" t="12:36" />

## Netanyahu's repeated demands — the Tenet ultimatum

On literally every visit to the Oval Office, regardless of which president was in office, Benjamin Netanyahu demanded: *"Release Pollard."* Every president said no.<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:02:30" />

When Bill Clinton came closest to agreeing, [George Tenet](/wiki/george-tenet) delivered an ultimatum: *"If you release Pollard, I will resign, and the head of every intelligence agency in the American government will resign."* Clinton backed down. Pollard served his full thirty-year sentence.<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:02:30" /><Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:03:00" /> Kiriakou's fuller account of the pattern: *"every single time Netanyahu would come to the country, no matter who was president, he would say you got to release Pollard,"* and *"literally every head of every American intelligence agency said if you release Pollard, [I] will resign."*<Cite s="2024-08-12-danny-jones-loyal-officer-vs-dissident-spy" t="2:29:26" />

## Release, Adelson jet, subsequent statements

Upon release, Pollard flew to Israel on a private jet owned by [Sheldon Adelson](/wiki/sheldon-adelson). He was met at the airport by [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu). He got off the plane and kissed the ground. Netanyahu bestowed Israeli citizenship on him at the airport.<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:03:00" /> Kiriakou also says the Israelis "gave him citizenship, put him on a plane, and now he lives in Jerusalem," and that he had heard separately Pollard flew back to Israel "on Adelson's jet."<Cite s="2024-08-12-danny-jones-loyal-officer-vs-dissident-spy" t="2:29:58" /><Cite s="2024-08-12-danny-jones-loyal-officer-vs-dissident-spy" t="2:28:55" />

Pollard subsequently gave an interview to Israeli media in which he: (1) urged Israel to use nuclear weapons against the United States if the United States did not comply with Israeli demands; and (2) urged all American Jews holding security clearances to spy for Israel.<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:03:00" /><Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:03:30" /> In a similar retelling, Kiriakou says Pollard told an Israeli journalist that American Jews should take up arms against the American government, and that as of Kiriakou's account, Pollard is running for the Knesset.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="1:58:40" /><Cite s="2026-05-31-lazaros-sideras" t="1:22:09" /> Kiriakou adds that Trump later pardoned Pollard's Israeli Mossad handler, who — unlike Pollard — had never been charged with or imprisoned for any crime.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="1:59:12" />

Kiriakou noted that following these statements, the U.S. ambassador met with Pollard at the American embassy. His stated reaction to the entire sequence: *"I'm befuddled by the whole situation."*<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:03:30" /> In a separate telling, Kiriakou says U.S. Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee welcomed Pollard into the embassy as a hero and personally ushered him in to meet with him — prompting Kiriakou to tweet that morning that Huckabee was an embarrassment to the United States and should resign immediately.<Cite s="2026-07-05-the-bad-news-program" t="18:43" /><Cite s="2026-07-05-the-bad-news-program" t="18:11" /><Cite s="2026-07-05-the-bad-news-program" t="17:41" /><Cite s="2026-07-05-the-bad-news-program" t="17:09" />

## Which service ran him — and the Knesset platform

Kiriakou is precise about who Pollard actually worked for, correcting a common assumption in the course of a conversation about Israeli espionage: *"he wasn't even Mossad. He was military intelligence"* — a service he places *"very much below Mossad."* The handler, he says, was Rafi Eitan, the longtime Mossad officer known for the operation that seized Adolf Eichmann.<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="1:46:06" /><Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="1:46:37" />

He also specifies the platform Pollard is standing on. Pollard is running for the Knesset, Kiriakou says, *"on a platform of we should be able to use nukes"* — an escalation he treats as insult added to injury, coming after *"a parade and a hero's welcome and an Israeli passport."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="1:45:05" /><Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="1:45:36" /> On the release campaign he broadens the pattern beyond Netanyahu alone: *"every single Israeli prime minister lobbied for [him] with every president"*, and every one refused — *"he's doing every day of the 30 years. And he did every single day."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="1:45:36" /> Asked whether the sequence angers him, Kiriakou's answer was short: *"Yeah, very much so."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="1:45:36" />

## The 30-year sentence and embassy welcome

Jonathan Pollard received a 30-year sentence and was welcomed at the American embassy in Jerusalem. This reception was seen as controversial and sparked criticism. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="01:29:55" />

## Jason Jones retelling — the KGB trade and the Adelson jet

Retelling the case, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says [Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard) gave Israel top-secret U.S. information on Soviet military movements, which Israel then passed to the KGB in exchange for planeloads of Russian Jews permitted to emigrate — damage that Secretary of Defense [Caspar Weinberger](/wiki/caspar-weinberger) called *"grave."*<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="41:02" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="41:35" /> Pollard served every day of his thirty-year sentence, then flew to Israel on [Sheldon Adelson](/wiki/sheldon-adelson)'s jet, was met by [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu), kissed the ground, received citizenship, and urged American Jews to take up arms against the United States — after which, Kiriakou says, an American ambassador welcomed him to the embassy *"like some kind of conquering hero."*<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="42:05" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="42:36" />

## "It didn't end with Pollard"

The Pollard case features in the security briefing [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) received in his first week at the [CIA](/wiki/cia) in January 1990 — not as a closed matter but as the counter-example. Against two officially declared Israeli intelligence representatives at the embassy in Washington, one from [Mossad](/wiki/mossad) and one from Shin Bet, the [FBI](/wiki/fbi) had identified 187 Israeli spies spread across America, mostly infiltrating defence contractors to steal secrets. "It didn't end with the arrest of Jonathan Pollard," the director of security told the class, before ranking Israel a critical counterintelligence threat alongside Russia, China, Cuba and North Korea.<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="21:21" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="21:52" />

## Money, not ideology

Kiriakou is consistent that Pollard's motive was payment. He describes him as a Navy intelligence officer who stole thousands and thousands of pages of top secret — he stresses top secret, not secret — documents and sold them to Israel for money, *"not for ideology or because he was Jewish or loved Israel."*<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="1:00:36" />

He generalises the point from counterintelligence practice. Officials in the field, he says, will tell you that for 85 per cent of cases *"it's the money. This is just a cash deal."* A handful act for ideological reasons; a handful for revenge — passed over for promotion, hating a boss, angry at the government. *"But for the most part, it's… a cash transaction."*<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="1:05:52" />

## The onward sale to Moscow

Kiriakou raises the Pollard case in 2026 as part of the record he says American policy has chosen to disregard while writing [permanent intelligence sharing](/wiki/ndaa-2027-israel-fusion) into law. Israel, he notes, is *"the same country that sold our talks top secret information to the Soviet Union through John that they that they acquired through Jonathan Pollard in order to bring a plane load of Russian Jews into Israel. Um and that's okay. We just have chosen not to pay attention to that."*<Cite s="2026-06-13-ddgeopolitics-ddgeopolitics" t="08:19" />

He places it alongside the Defense Intelligence Agency's warning to its own staff about stepped-up Israeli espionage — see [Israel's counterintelligence threat ranking](/wiki/israel-counterintelligence-threat-ranking).

## The general's date, and the 1990 briefing

On a televised panel, an Israeli general offered Pollard's arrest as the end point of Israeli espionage against the United States: *"when Pollard got caught, that's when the prime minister ordered us to stop spying on the United States."* Kiriakou dates that to 1985 and rebuts it with his own experience five years later — a briefing from the CIA's director of security stating *"that Israel was one of the most active countries engaged in espionage against the United States in the whole world."* The general's response was *"no comment."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="23:33" /><Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="24:03" />

In the same conversation Kiriakou restates the substance of the damage: Pollard gave the Israelis top secret military and targeting information the United States had collected against the Soviet Union; the Israelis passed it to the Soviets *"in exchange for a couple of planeloads of Russian Jews"*; and the Soviets used it to shut down American sources. *"People were executed for that."* It is, he notes, what the courts call grave damage to the national security.<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="25:05" /><Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="25:36" />

## Retold: the embassy meeting and the resignation call

Returning to the episode in December 2025, Kiriakou described his own reaction first: on reading that [Mike Huckabee](/wiki/mike-huckabee) had received Pollard for a private meeting at the American embassy, *"I thought when I first read the report that it was a joke, that somebody was just playing a joke. But unfortunately it wasn't a joke."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="9:00" />

His grounds for calling publicly for the ambassador's removal are Pollard's post-release conduct as he characterises it: a traitor who *"has called for the use of Israeli nuclear weapons against the United States"* and *"has called on every American Jew with a security clearance to spy for Israel against the United States"* — and who was nevertheless *"feted at the American embassy and treated like a VIP visitor."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="9:00" />
