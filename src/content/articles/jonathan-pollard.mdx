**Jonathan Pollard** is the U.S. Navy civilian intelligence analyst convicted in 1985 of spying for Israel. He is the canonical publicly proven case of Israeli espionage against the United States. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) invokes Pollard's case in his account of his first appearance on Piers Morgan's program, in which he debated a former director of the [Mossad](/wiki/mossad) who denied any post-1985 Israeli espionage against the United States. Kiriakou's counter: *"Jonathan Pollard was caught in 1985, and you were spying on the United States in 1998, you know, or 2004."* The former Mossad director declined to comment.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:11:32" />

## The KGB trade (Tommy G, April 2026)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described a dimension of the Pollard case that he characterized as illustrating the nature of the U.S.-Israel intelligence relationship. The Israelis, after receiving the classified Pentagon material from [Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard), traded that intelligence to the KGB in exchange for a planeload of Russian Jews being permitted to resettle as refugees in Israel.<Cite s="2026-04-20-tommy-g-israel-double-agents-directed-energy" t="18:00" /><Cite s="2026-04-20-tommy-g-israel-double-agents-directed-energy" t="18:30" />

Kiriakou offered this as context for why he believed [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) was collecting classified defense documents from figures such as [Peter Mandelson](/wiki/peter-mandelson) and Prince Andrew: *"This is exactly what the Israelis have collected through history. Why would today be any different?"*<Cite s="2026-04-20-tommy-g-israel-double-agents-directed-energy" t="18:30" />
