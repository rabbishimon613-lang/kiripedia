**Hamas** is the Palestinian movement that carried out the [October 7 attack](/wiki/october-7-attack). Asked whether it is a terrorist organization, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) answers that *"an argument could be made that it's a freedom-fighting organization… dedicated to the liberation of its people."*<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="23:28" /> He frames the attack as the product of decades of subjugation, pushed *"over the edge"* by the election of the hardest right-wing government in Israeli history under [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu).<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="22:55" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="23:59" />
