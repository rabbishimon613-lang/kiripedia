**Hamas** is the Palestinian movement that carried out the [October 7 attack](/wiki/october-7-attack). Asked whether it is a terrorist organization, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) answers that *"an argument could be made that it's a freedom-fighting organization… dedicated to the liberation of its people."*<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="23:28" /> He frames the attack as the product of decades of subjugation, pushed *"over the edge"* by the election of the hardest right-wing government in Israeli history under [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu).<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="22:55" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="23:59" /> Elsewhere, Kiriakou calls Hamas a *"bloodthirsty terrorist organization,"* alongside Palestinian Islamic Jihad, saying it formed around 1990-91 and has ruled [Gaza](/wiki/gaza-administrative-history) since Israel's 2005 withdrawal.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:53:01" /> He also notes that Hamas is officially labeled a terrorist organization by only 13 of the more than 100 member states of the United Nations.<Cite s="2024-08-12-danny-jones-loyal-officer-vs-dissident-spy" t="2:26:48" />

On motive, Kiriakou says Hamas itself stated in the week after October 7 that the impending Israel-Saudi diplomatic normalization deal was what finally drove the group to launch the attack.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:54:33" />
