**Hamas** is the Palestinian movement that carried out the [October 7 attack](/wiki/october-7-attack). Asked whether it is a terrorist organization, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) answers that *"an argument could be made that it's a freedom-fighting organization… dedicated to the liberation of its people."*<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="23:28" /> He frames the attack as the product of decades of subjugation, pushed *"over the edge"* by the election of the hardest right-wing government in Israeli history under [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu).<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="22:55" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="23:59" /> Elsewhere, Kiriakou calls Hamas a *"bloodthirsty terrorist organization,"* alongside Palestinian Islamic Jihad, saying it formed around 1990-91 and has ruled Gaza since Israel's 2005 withdrawal.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:53:01" /> He also notes that Hamas is officially labeled a terrorist organization by only 13 of the more than 100 member states of the United Nations.<Cite s="2024-08-12-danny-jones-loyal-officer-vs-dissident-spy" t="2:26:48" />

On motive, Kiriakou says Hamas itself stated in the week after October 7 that the impending Israel-Saudi diplomatic normalization deal was what finally drove the group to launch the attack.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:54:33" /> Asked directly, in a separate interview, whether he considers Iran's regional allies destabilizing, Kiriakou again resists the label: *"With the exception of targeting civilians on October 7th, I think that Hamas was created to liberate the Palestinian people from Israeli oppression"* — arguing that terrorism is often *"in the eye of the beholder."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-deprogram-w-ted-rall" t="1:01:50" />

## No longer a fighting force

By mid-2026 Kiriakou's assessment was that Israel's stated war aim had been overtaken by events: Israel *"say that their goal is to destroy Hamas. Well, Hamas for all intents and purposes no longer exists, at least not as a fighting force."*<Cite s="2026-07-04-the-jk-report-roger-waters-on-palestine-human-rights" t="10:59" /> He couples this with a point about the organisation's capabilities before the war, asking how many planes, tanks and artillery pieces Hamas had even before [October 7](/wiki/october-7-attack), and answering himself: none.<Cite s="2026-07-04-the-jk-report-roger-waters-on-palestine-human-rights" t="10:59" /><Cite s="2026-07-04-the-jk-report-roger-waters-on-palestine-human-rights" t="11:31" /> From that he concludes that the campaign's actual objective is the removal of the Palestinian population from Gaza rather than the destruction of an army.<Cite s="2026-07-04-the-jk-report-roger-waters-on-palestine-human-rights" t="11:31" />

## "In part" — the formulation Kiriakou defends on air

Asked directly by [Piers Morgan](/wiki/piers-morgan) whether Hamas is a terrorist group, Kiriakou declined both available answers and gave a divided one: Hamas *"is essentially a freedom fighting group, but as it pertains to attacking civilians on October 7th, yes, if the point was to attack civilians, that's an act of terrorism. That's the definition of terrorism."* Pressed on whether the attack itself was a terrorist attack, he answered *"in part"* — because it *"specifically targeted a music festival with a purpose of killing or injuring civilians for a political purpose."*<Cite s="2026-06-27-deep-focus-report-john-kiriakou-on-iran-endless-wars-and-u-s-fo" t="9:18" /><Cite s="2026-06-27-deep-focus-report-john-kiriakou-on-iran-endless-wars-and-u-s-fo" t="9:49" />

He refuses the inference the question is built to produce. Asked what his problem with Israel was if he would concede that much, his answer was that a concession about Hamas licenses nothing: *"You cannot have as a policy to just kill everybody. That's not a policy. That's called genocide. There's a word for that."*<Cite s="2026-06-27-deep-focus-report-john-kiriakou-on-iran-endless-wars-and-u-s-fo" t="10:19" />

Kiriakou adds two claims about the attack's background. The first is that Israel funded Hamas and that this *"was a policy decision taken by [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu)"*; the second is that there were *"weeks full of intelligence indicating that an attack was coming."*<Cite s="2026-06-27-deep-focus-report-john-kiriakou-on-iran-endless-wars-and-u-s-fo" t="10:19" /><Cite s="2026-06-27-deep-focus-report-john-kiriakou-on-iran-endless-wars-and-u-s-fo" t="10:50" />
