Daniel Ellsberg is recognized as an early whistleblower who released the Pentagon Papers, exposing classified U.S. government assessments of the Vietnam War. <Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="31:02" />

## Baiting the Justice Department at 90 (Scott Horton, 2021)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) — who calls [Daniel Ellsberg](/wiki/daniel-ellsberg) *"my hero"* and *"the godfather to all national security whistleblowers"* — describes Ellsberg, at 90, deliberately trying to get himself indicted under the [Espionage Act](/wiki/espionage-act).<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="34:57" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="36:00" /> Ellsberg had held onto a document he liberated alongside the Pentagon Papers describing the 1958 debate over whether to nuke China over Taiwan, promoted it, and gave it to reporter Charlie Savage to publish in the New York Times — then told the Justice Department, *"indict me."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="33:25" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="34:27" /> Kiriakou says Ellsberg wants to take an Espionage Act prosecution to the Supreme Court, confident the First Amendment will finally strike the statute down.<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="36:00" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="36:31" /> Kiriakou notes, honored, that Ellsberg told him he listens to this very show while walking on his treadmill.<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="34:57" />
