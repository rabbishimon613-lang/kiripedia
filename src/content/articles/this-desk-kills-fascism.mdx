**"This desk kills fascism"** was the sign Kiriakou wrote on a page from a legal pad and taped to the desk in his cell at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto), a play on "This machine kills fascism," which the folk singer Woody Guthrie had famously written on his guitar in the 1930s.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="38:33" />

The sign was a response to an escalating attempt to deprive him of the means of writing. Roughly twelve hours after finishing one [letter from Loretto](/wiki/letter-from-loretto), Kiriakou was summoned to the Special Investigative Service office, where an officer returned an envelope containing the first 20,000 words of his book — which he had not realised was missing since a cell shakedown. The officer who had taken it claimed the manuscript had "made threats against staff"; the SIS officer said he had read it himself and believed it contained no such threats, and returned it in its entirety, though Kiriakou assumes a copy was made.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="36:58" /><Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="37:29" />

The same officer then tried to remove Kiriakou's desk from the wall and confiscate it, on the reasoning that without a desk he could not write. The attempt failed because the bolts were stripped — Kiriakou had stripped them himself, in advance, as a preemptive measure. The officer issued a work order to chip the desk off the wall, and Kiriakou went to the unit manager, who promised the desk would stay. It did.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="37:29" /><Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="38:02" />

After the letter was published, Kiriakou taped up the sign. It hung for only a couple of hours before a correctional officer tore it down, crumpled it into a ball and threw it on the floor. Kiriakou picked it up and mailed it home to himself, and keeps it as a reminder that he was not alone in what he went through.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="38:33" />

His broader characterisation of the episode is that crude, amateurish attempts to deprive him of his rights do not originate with a low-level correctional officer but come from higher up, from officials who should know better.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="38:02" />
