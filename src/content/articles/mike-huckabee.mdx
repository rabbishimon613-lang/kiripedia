**Mike Huckabee** is the United States ambassador to Israel appointed by [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump), and a figure [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has publicly demanded be removed.

## The Pollard meeting

Kiriakou's objection is to a single act: Huckabee's reception of [Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard) for a private meeting at the American embassy in Israel. He went on Twitter to say that he believed *"quite strongly"* Huckabee should be forced to resign *"solely for inviting Jonathan Pollard to a private meeting in the American embassy."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="8:29" />

The grounds are Pollard's record as Kiriakou describes it — a traitor to the United States who *"has called for the use of Israeli nuclear weapons against the United States"* and *"has called on every American Jew with a security clearance to spy for Israel against the United States"* — and who was nonetheless *"feted at the American embassy and treated like a VIP visitor."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="9:00" /> Kiriakou's first reaction on reading the report was that somebody was playing a joke: *"unfortunately it wasn't a joke."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="9:00" /> He calls the episode a serious violation *"of well, not just protocol, but common sense."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="9:32" />

## The diagnosis

Kiriakou's account of why it happened is about temperament rather than loyalty. *"Mike Huckabee was a popular governor of a small, lightly populated state. He thinks that he's still governor, and that he can make these decisions independently of the State Department or the National Security Council. He can't, and he needs to be reprimanded for not falling into line."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="8:29" />

Asked directly whether Huckabee was a traitor, he declined the word and offered a blunter one: *"no, I don't think he's a traitor against the United States. And excuse me for being blunt — I think he's an idiot, and he just didn't think that this was any big deal."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="10:02" /> Asked whether people inside the CIA wanted him gone, his guess was that *"everybody at the CIA wants him gone for this."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="9:32" />

## On the ceasefire

The same passage carries Kiriakou's position on what Huckabee represents in the region. *"I say at every opportunity, in every interview that I can get myself scheduled on, that there is no peace deal. There is not even a ceasefire — at least not a well-maintained one."* The Emiratis, Qataris, Bahrainis and even the Saudis may be satisfied, he says, but the Palestinians are not, because dozens still die every day, and Israel continues to bomb southern Lebanon and south-western Syria.<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="7:58" />
