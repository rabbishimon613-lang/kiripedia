**The TikTok fan edits** are the short-form videos that, on [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s own account, changed his life in eight weeks.

He found out about them second-hand: in Dubai, his niece called to tell him he was exploding on TikTok, and could not say why.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="03:38" /> The cause was a twenty-year-old sophomore at the University of Texas who had taken his [Diary of a CEO](/wiki/diary-of-a-ceo-january-2026) appearance, cut it into shorts, given him a high-pitched Alvin and the Chipmunks voice and laser beams shooting out of his eyes — and hit with Gen Z.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="04:09" />

Kiriakou's reading of it is not that the joke matters but that the access does: "I'm a 61-year-old man with access to Gen Z … And yes, they're all having fun with it, but they're discussing and considering very important ideas."<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="04:09" /><Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="04:42" />

The consequences arrived quickly. He is now represented by what he describes as the largest talent agency in the world, meeting Hollywood figures about films and television series; and having written nine books, he was asked to make the tenth a novel — his first fiction, already 10,000 words in.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="04:42" /><Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="05:12" /> "Life has changed completely in the last eight weeks only because this kid put me on Tik Tok. Bless him."<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="05:12" />

On [his own programme](/wiki/johns-briefing-room) he devoted a segment to the tributes, saying he cannot walk five feet without being recognised and that he has a personal policy of never refusing a selfie or an autograph. Among the items shown was a tattoo of his face on a fan's leg — "I'm not sure that you're going to want a tattoo of my pouting face 20 years from now, but listen, I'm humbled and flattered."<Cite s="2026-07-18-national-security-files-john-kiriakou-former-cia-officer-exposes-the" t="37:41" /><Cite s="2026-07-18-national-security-files-john-kiriakou-former-cia-officer-exposes-the" t="38:13" /> He also mentioned a petition to put him on *Dancing with the Stars*.<Cite s="2026-07-18-national-security-files-john-kiriakou-former-cia-officer-exposes-the" t="39:57" />
