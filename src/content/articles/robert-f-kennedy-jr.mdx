**Robert F. Kennedy Jr.** is the son of Robert F. Kennedy. He is the source of an anecdote [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) repeats about the day of the [JFK assassination](/wiki/jfk-assassination).<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:48:19" /> As Kennedy told it, on November 22, 1963 his mother pulled him from his McLean junior high after the shooting and drove him home to Hickory Hill, where he got out of the car and heard his father say to CIA director [John McCone](/wiki/john-mccone), *"Tell me your people didn't do this."* McCone — who Kiriakou notes was dining with the grieving Kennedys nightly after his own wife's death — answered, *"I don't know who did it,"* rather than denying the agency's involvement.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:48:53" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:49:24" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:49:55" /> Kiriakou's conclusion: the CIA as an institution did not kill Kennedy, but whether elements of it did is *"something that we'll never know the answer to."*<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:50:25" />

Kiriakou has separately noted that Kennedy appeared on at least one released Jeffrey Epstein flight log, explaining that his then-wife was friends with Ghislaine Maxwell and had accepted a ride on Epstein's plane to Palm Beach for Easter.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:56:42" />
