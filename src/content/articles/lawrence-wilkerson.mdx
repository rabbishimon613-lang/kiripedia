**Lawrence Wilkerson** is the retired U.S. Army colonel who served as chief of staff to Secretary of State [Colin Powell](/wiki/colin-powell). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts interviewing Wilkerson, who told him the [Iraq WMD case](/wiki/iraq-wmd-intelligence) Powell presented at the UN was knowingly *"sold"* — a confirmation Kiriakou says nearly knocked him off his chair.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="12:33" />
