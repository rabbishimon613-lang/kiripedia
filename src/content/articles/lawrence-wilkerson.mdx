**Lawrence Wilkerson** is the retired U.S. Army colonel who served as chief of staff to Secretary of State [Colin Powell](/wiki/colin-powell). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts interviewing Wilkerson on his program, who confirmed that the [Iraq WMD case](/wiki/iraq-wmd-intelligence) Powell presented at the UN was knowingly *"sold"* to the public.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="12:33" /> Kiriakou says the confirmation nearly knocked him off his chair: *"I was lucky I was sitting on the chair otherwise I almost fell off."*<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="12:33" />

## On Trump and Netanyahu

Kiriakou also cites Wilkerson as a source on the personal relationship between Donald Trump and Israeli Prime Minister [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu). Puzzling over an apparent public rift between the two, Kiriakou offers Wilkerson's read as the ordinary explanation: the two men simply dislike each other, so friction between them is nothing unusual — *"Colonel Wilkerson said they do not like each other. So them fighting is normal."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="33:16" />
