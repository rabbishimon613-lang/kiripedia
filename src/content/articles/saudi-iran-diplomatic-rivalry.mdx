**Saudi-Iran diplomatic rivalry** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the long hostility between the two regional powers and its unexpected 2023 thaw. He traces the rupture to 1979, when Iranian pilgrims infiltrated the Hajj and took over the Kaaba — more than a thousand people were killed in the takeover and the ensuing Saudi military response, after which Saudi Arabia broke diplomatic relations with Iran for years.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:56:37" /> Kiriakou also notes that Saudi Arabia's oil-rich Eastern Province has a large Shia minority that the Sunni Saudi government suppresses, executing many Shia Saudis to keep the province calm.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:57:43" />

## China brokers a surprise rapprochement

Kiriakou says China brokered the 2023 restoration of Saudi-Iranian diplomatic relations, catching the United States off guard in what Washington had long considered its own sphere of influence.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:58:14" />
