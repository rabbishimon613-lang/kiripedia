Kiriakou has voiced alarm about the pace of mass surveillance, describing it as accelerating alongside the growth of artificial intelligence and data centers, including under the Trump administration. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="42:25" /> He says the [NSA](/wiki/nsa) no longer makes any pretext of preserving Americans' civil liberties, even though its founding charter bars it from spying on Americans. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="42:57" /> According to Kiriakou, the agency intercepts every phone call, text message, and email, along with metadata on all Americans, and warehouses the data in a facility in the Utah desert and elsewhere, without needing court orders or warrants. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="42:57" /> <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="43:27" /> He says the FBI now bypasses warrants entirely by simply calling the NSA and asking for data on a target, and that the same practice extends to private companies. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="43:27" /> Kiriakou says that in his own case, investigators used a national security letter rather than a court warrant to obtain his information from his internet service provider, and that Apple complied by turning over everything it had. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="44:00" />
