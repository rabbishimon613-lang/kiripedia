Kiriakou has voiced alarm about the pace of mass surveillance, describing it as accelerating alongside the growth of artificial intelligence and data centers, including under the Trump administration. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="42:25" /> He says the [NSA](/wiki/nsa) no longer makes any pretext of preserving Americans' civil liberties — the tension he frames elsewhere as [national security vs. civil liberties](/wiki/national-security-vs-civil-liberties) — even though its founding charter bars it from spying on Americans. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="42:57" /> According to Kiriakou, the agency intercepts every phone call, text message, and email, along with metadata on all Americans, and warehouses the data in a facility in the Utah desert and elsewhere, without needing court orders or warrants. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="42:57" /> <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="43:27" /> He says the FBI now bypasses warrants entirely by simply calling the NSA and asking for data on a target, and that the same practice extends to private companies. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="43:27" /> Kiriakou says that in his own case, investigators used a national security letter rather than a court warrant to obtain his information from his internet service provider, and that Apple complied by turning over everything it had. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="44:00" />

## The data-center build-out

Asked about the scale of new AI data centers, Kiriakou's first objection is physical — they consume enormous quantities of water and are being sited in states that do not have it, Texas among them — and he cites a proposed 40,000-acre campus in Utah, two and a half times the size of Manhattan, whose power demand would exceed double the entire state's current consumption. Loudoun County, Virginia, he says, is now miles of unbroken data-center complexes.<Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:43:14" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:43:45" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:44:16" />

His inference is drawn from ownership rather than from any inside knowledge: *"you have to assume that this is intelligence related — look at the companies that are involved."* He names Palantir, Nvidia and [Arrais](/wiki/arrais-corporation) as firms that either took CIA investment as seed money or are staffed by retired senior CIA officers, and his summary of their motive is that *"they're not doing it for their health."*<Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:44:16" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:44:48" />

On the [Utah facility](/wiki/bluffdale-nsa-facility) specifically, he repeats the figure he attributes to [Edward Snowden](/wiki/edward-snowden) and [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange): storage sufficient for every phone call, email and text message from every American for the next 500 years — which leaves the question of what all the other buildings are for. His account of the legal shift is that until roughly the post-9/11 period the government had to go to a federal judge and justify the request; now a national security letter served on a provider suffices, or the name can simply be entered at a data center. *"You don't have any legal protections. They just take whatever they want. And that's legal now."*<Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:45:20" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:46:52" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:47:24" />

Asked what an individual can do, his answer is that it is close to impossible — short of going *"Eric Rudolph or Unabomber"* and owning no technology at all.<Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:46:21" />
