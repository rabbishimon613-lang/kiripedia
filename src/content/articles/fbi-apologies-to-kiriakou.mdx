**FBI apologies to Kiriakou** are the letters [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he has received from FBI agents who worked on his [prosecution](/wiki/cia-torture-program-whistleblowing) — three since his conviction. He read one aloud: an agent who worked both the headquarters and Washington field-office teams wrote to *"personally apologize sincerely"* for *"the disgraceful way that the FBI and our government treated you,"* explaining that the case was *"directed and driven by seniormost officials"* while many mid- and street-level personnel were against it — *"but nevertheless, we just followed orders."*<Cite s="2025-05-28-ironclad-hidden-terror-program" t="39:23" /><Cite s="2025-05-28-ironclad-hidden-terror-program" t="39:55" /> Kiriakou's mixed reaction: gratitude, but also *"you should have stopped following orders."*<Cite s="2025-05-28-ironclad-hidden-terror-program" t="40:26" />
