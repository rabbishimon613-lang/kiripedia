## The goats that were girls

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts a story from drone whistleblower [Daniel Hale](/wiki/daniel-hale), who operated a drone from Nevada while his superior watched from MacDill Air Force Base. Ordered to fire on a target beside two children, Hale protested; told *"Those aren't kids. Those are goats,"* and then *"Fire or be court-martialed,"* he fired and killed a 12-year-old and a 9-year-old girl, telling his girlfriend that night, *"I became a child killer today."*<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:54:07" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:54:40" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:55:12" />

## The persecution of Daniel Hale (Scott Horton, 2021)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes [Daniel Hale](/wiki/daniel-hale) as an NSA contractor and drone targeting analyst who recognized *"very early on"* that the program was carrying out *"illegal strikes on civilian targets"* across Yemen, Afghanistan and Somalia, and took his objections to Jeremy Scahill at [The Intercept](/wiki/the-intercept), even appearing in the documentary [National Bird](/wiki/national-bird).<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="02:10" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="02:40" /> On March 31, on his lawyer's advice, Hale pleaded guilty to a single count under the [Espionage Act](/wiki/espionage-act) — carrying a 10-year maximum — hoping the judge would take pity and dismiss the four other counts; because he pleaded on his own rather than in a deal, prosecutors refused to drop them, leaving him facing *"50 years in prison."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="03:43" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="04:14" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="04:45" /> Kiriakou says a court-appointed psychiatrist, fearing Hale was suicidal, had him placed in [solitary confinement](/wiki/solitary-confinement) *"for his own safety,"* which Kiriakou argues made him suicidal.<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="05:50" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="06:20" /> Sentencing was set for July 13; Kiriakou, in close personal contact, urged the [RDAP program](/wiki/rdap-program) route and shared advice from his own imprisonment.<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="06:20" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="29:47" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="31:21" /> He publicized, through Consortium News, a letter-writing campaign, noting Hale could receive mail only on unlined white paper in black ink with no photographs, at the William G. Truesdale Adult Detention Center in Alexandria, Virginia.<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="50:58" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="51:29" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="52:34" />
