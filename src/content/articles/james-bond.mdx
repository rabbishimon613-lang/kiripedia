**James Bond** is the myth that shadows every real intelligence career — including [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s.

## Wanting to be Bond

Asked whether he wanted to be James Bond as a kid, Kiriakou's answer is yes: he asked his parents for walkie-talkies at nine and kept logs of what the neighbor kids were doing.<Cite s="2019-08-08-david-gornoski-archives-david-gornoski-archives" t="02:07" /> He loved the movies — the subterfuge, the excitement<Cite s="2022-09-13-hr-news-channel-hr-news-channel" t="06:50" /> — and remembers the recruitment-era thrill of working against Soviet communism as *"a James Bond kind of thing... of course that turned out to be ridiculous."*<Cite s="2023-03-28-art-and-documentary-art-and-documentary" t="04:44" /> The pull is institutional, not just personal: *"It's in operations where everybody wants to be James Bond — and in some cases they actually achieve that,"* aspiring to the handful of legendary senior officers with many recruitments.<Cite s="2025-04-27-the-dr-phil-podcast-i-exposed-the-cia-and-went-to-prison-john-kir" t="08:24" /> Training delivered a taste: airplanes, mini-subs from the ocean depths, a shot-out windscreen that made him think *"this is awesome."*<Cite s="2015-07-25-splendour-in-the-grass" t="05:18" />

## The reality audit

Rating Hollywood counterterrorism scenes, Kiriakou is blunt: *"Literally none of that is true to life — but that's what makes you love James Bond."* Almost no officers carry weapons; a post must be rated high or critical threat, and in his whole career he was armed only in Athens and Pakistan.<Cite s="2024-12-17-insider-cia-officer-john-kiriakou-rates-9-counterterr" t="00:36" /> But one Bond staple he certifies: James always runs into the CIA officer at the embassy party — *"that happens every day in real life. We always see each other and say hello."*<Cite s="2023-03-28-art-and-documentary-art-and-documentary" t="30:32" /><Cite s="2024-11-07-julian-dorey-249" t="2:25:57" />

The franchise even reached the org chart: the CIA's venture capital arm named itself In-Q-Tel, the Q inserted *"as kind of a wink-wink nod to Q from the James Bond movies."*<Cite s="2025-02-19-katie-halper" t="1:02:10" />
