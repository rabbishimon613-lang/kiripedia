**Israeli torture of Palestinian prisoners** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of systematic abuse in Israeli detention after the [October 7 attack](/wiki/october-7-attack), corroborated by a CNN report from an Israeli whistleblower and, he says, by former DOD official Mark Fallon.<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="35:00" /><Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="36:07" /> With over 10,000 potential prisoners held, doctors have reported to the United Nations an *"inordinate"* number of unnecessary amputations — legs cut off so prisoners *"can't run,"* arms so they *"can't fire a gun."*<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="35:34" /><Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="36:07" /> An Israeli human-rights attorney who represents only Palestinians told Kiriakou that the entire prison system enacted a rule barring inmates from walking: they must crawl on hands and knees, and speaking above a whisper brings a beating with a truncheon or rebar, broken limbs, and — once infected — amputation.<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="36:39" /><Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="37:10" /> Kiriakou has also pointed to the language used in hostage-negotiation coverage as evidence of the same double standard: Israelis held by Hamas are called "hostages," while Palestinians held by Israel — many never charged with any crime, seized when the military "busts down the door of their house" — are called only "detainees." He notes that 70 percent of the Palestinian detainees released in exchanges have been children.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="1:52:00" />
