Kiriakou has told this story in more than one interview, and the tellings differ in their details but not in their shape.

He went one day to the office of something called the Iraq Operations Group to pick up a briefing book. The officer at the desk was the director of Iraq operations — the same man who, in Kiriakou's account, had earlier laid out the secrecy agreements he was made to sign before being read in on the coming invasion. These were, as Kiriakou puts it, serious, high-level people he was dealing with every day.<Cite s="2019-06-27-the-bitter-truth-with-abe-the-bitter-truth-with-abe" t="25:26" />

## The markers

The director was sitting at his desk with Crayola coloured markers, colouring something in. Kiriakou asked what he was doing. He said he was designing the new Iraqi flag.<Cite s="2019-06-27-the-bitter-truth-with-abe-the-bitter-truth-with-abe" t="25:57" />

Kiriakou asked whether he did not think the Iraqis should perhaps design the new Iraqi flag. In the 2019 telling the director looked at him and said: not necessarily.<Cite s="2019-06-27-the-bitter-truth-with-abe-the-bitter-truth-with-abe" t="25:57" /> In a 2023 telling the answer is closer to a shrug — "I don't know, I thought I'd do it" — and Kiriakou adds a detail absent from the shorter version, that the design used the colours of the Israeli flag.<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="1:27:13" />

## What he took from it

Kiriakou's reaction is the same in both accounts. He thought: we are in serious trouble here, serious trouble.<Cite s="2019-06-27-the-bitter-truth-with-abe-the-bitter-truth-with-abe" t="25:57" />

He tells the anecdote as the second of a pair. The first is the briefing in which he learned that the invasion was already decided — that the United States would overthrow Saddam Hussein, install its own man, and move its air assets out of Saudi Arabia into a new base in southern Iraq — and was told which side of the argument the CIA, the State Department and the Joint Chiefs were on, and which side loses.<Cite s="2019-06-27-the-bitter-truth-with-abe-the-bitter-truth-with-abe" t="25:26" />
