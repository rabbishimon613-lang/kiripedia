**The Intercept** is the investigative news outlet, home to Jeremy Scahill, that published [Daniel Hale](/wiki/daniel-hale)'s drone leak as [The Drone Papers](/wiki/the-drone-papers).<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="12:05" /> [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says the outlet *"has gotten a lot of people caught"* and points to [Matthew Cole](/wiki/matthew-cole) — *"the guy that got you busted works there… and he's the same guy that got Reality Winner busted."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="14:11" /> Reality Winner, interviewed on Kiriakou's own show, says she was never read her [Miranda rights](/wiki/miranda-rights) during the FBI's 2017 arrest that followed the leak. But Kiriakou — who says he is *"happy to be the first one to point the finger at the Intercept"* — insists the outlet did nothing sloppy in Hale's case: *"they didn't blow his identity,"* Hale *"decided that he was going to go public and be public about the drone program."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="14:42" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="15:12" />
