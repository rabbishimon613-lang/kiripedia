The **American Psychological Association** (**APA**) is the world's largest professional body of psychologists, and the scientific authority [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) reaches for whenever he argues that the [torture program](/wiki/enhanced-interrogation)'s quieter techniques were its worst.

## The sleep-deprivation datum

Kiriakou's standing claim, repeated across years of interviews: [waterboarding](/wiki/waterboarding) was supposed to be the ultimate technique, but several others were clearly worse — above all sleep deprivation. *"We know from the American Psychological Association, which has studied this extensively, that people begin to lose their minds at around day seven with no sleep"* — and begin to die at day nine. [Mitchell and Jessen](/wiki/mitchell-and-jessen) were authorized to keep prisoners awake past that line.<Cite s="2019-04-20-david-gornoski-on-torture-prison-ptsd" t="22:55" /><Cite s="2022-05-19-scheerpost" t="26:56" /><Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="1:50:14" /><Cite s="2017-11-23-stelios-kouloglou" t="07:14" />

What "no sleep" meant in practice, he specifies: the prisoner stripped naked, chained to an eye bolt in the ceiling so he could neither sit nor lie down nor get comfortable in any way, with industrial-strength sound preventing rest — a technique the APA has deemed a form of torture.<Cite s="2022-05-19-scheerpost" t="26:56" /><Cite s="2023-01-13-the-open-forum-podcast" t="37:16" />

## Broader use

Kiriakou also invokes the psychological literature — the American Psychiatric Association, the APA, even Psychology Today — for his running observation that psychopathy concentrates at the top: studies finding that virtually every major CEO in America shows the profile.<Cite s="2019-04-25-david-hartful-john-kiriakou-speaks-at-venezuelan-embassy-04" t="49:40" />
