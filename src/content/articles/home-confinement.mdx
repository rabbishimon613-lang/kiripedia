**Home confinement** is the stage of a federal sentence served at the prisoner's own address after release from custody. Kiriakou served three months of it after [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto), and describes it as worse than prison — *"way worse"*.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="1:02:20" /><Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="1:02:51" />

The confinement is to the interior of the house. The front yard, the back yard and the stoop are all outside the permitted boundary.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="1:02:20" /> Compliance is enforced by telephone, quite literally at any hour of the twenty-four, and the prisoner must give his name, his prisoner number and his social security number to prove he has not left.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="1:02:51" />

On top of that he was required to check in at the [halfway house](/wiki/halfway-house) once a week and to submit to drug testing twice a week — a condition he notes was imposed on a man who has never taken a drug in his life, and which he was content to let waste as much of the government's money as it wasted of his time.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="1:02:51" /><Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="1:03:23" />

The transition back to ordinary life was, in his account, very difficult, and the harder problem was the one that followed it: nobody is willing to hire a convicted felon. After about a year at a Washington criminal-justice think tank he concluded he would have to work for himself, which led to a radio show he did for seven years, then to a succession of podcasts, his own television show, syndicated columns and a ninth book.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="1:03:23" /><Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="1:03:55" /><Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="1:04:25" />
