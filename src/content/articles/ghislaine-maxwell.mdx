**Ghislaine Maxwell** is a British socialite and convicted sex offender who served as the principal associate of [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s assessment of the Epstein operation centers on her family background: *"Ghislaine Maxwell's father was a prolific Mossad spy. That's a historic fact."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="53:22" />

## The Mossad connection

Kiriakou holds that Maxwell — or her father, [Robert Maxwell](/wiki/robert-maxwell) — introduced Epstein to [Mossad](/wiki/mossad): *"It stands to reason that she would have introduced him to the Mossad or even her father would have introduced Epstein to Mossad."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="53:22" />

In Kiriakou's framing, the entire Epstein operation bears the hallmarks of a [Mossad](/wiki/mossad) access operation — using Epstein to cultivate, and where necessary compromise, high-value figures such as Bill Clinton, Prince Andrew, and Bill Gates — and the Maxwell family connection is the structural explanation for how it was assembled: *"They kept it all in the family."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="53:52" />
