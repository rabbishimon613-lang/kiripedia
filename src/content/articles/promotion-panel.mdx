The **promotion panel** is the CIA board that considers an officer for advancement. In [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account it is also where the price of his refusal to take part in the [enhanced interrogation techniques](/wiki/enhanced-interrogation) was first made explicit.

Kiriakou had been offered the training and declined it. Weeks later — *"like three weeks"* after catching the purported number three of al-Qaeda *"with these two hands"* — he was passed over for promotion.<Cite s="2022-09-13-hr-news-channel-hr-news-channel" t="13:09" /><Cite s="2018-04-20-bookia-gr" t="2:15:17" />

He went to ask why, and was told what had been said about him in the panel: that in declining to be trained in the enhanced interrogation techniques he had displayed *"a shocking lack of commitment to counterterrorism."*<Cite s="2019-03-04-a-good-place-with-ella-a-a-good-place-with-ella-a" t="1:22:39" /> He attributes the remark variously — in one telling to [Cofer Black](/wiki/cofer-black)'s successor at the Counterterrorism Center, in another to [Jose Rodriguez](/wiki/jose-rodriguez), the chief of counterterrorism.<Cite s="2024-12-21-not-a-grayman" t="2:17:49" /><Cite s="2025-01-26-neutrality-studies" t="04:43" />

The outcome was not a career ending. The [chief of station](/wiki/chief-of-station) in [Pakistan](/wiki/pakistan-station-2002) for whom Kiriakou had worked was promoted to associate deputy director of the whole CIA, made Kiriakou his executive assistant and promoted him. But the formula in the panel had already told him where he stood: *"I got the promotion, but I realised I'm on the other side of this thing."*<Cite s="2019-03-04-a-good-place-with-ella-a-a-good-place-with-ella-a" t="1:22:39" />
