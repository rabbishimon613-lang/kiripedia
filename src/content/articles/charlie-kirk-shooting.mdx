The **Charlie Kirk shooting** is the killing of activist Charlie Kirk, whose official investigation [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has publicly questioned. He points to a court filing, reported by the Daily Mail, stating the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosives was unable to match the bullet recovered at Kirk's autopsy to the rifle allegedly tied to suspect Tyler Robinson.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="37:58" /><Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="24:39" /> He also questions why the FBI became so heavily involved in the case despite no evident state-line crossing — the shooting, planning, and confession all occurred in Utah, with no state line ever crossed — noting that Kash Patel, asked about it early on, described the bureau's role only as "offering the FBI's expertise" to Utah State Police, even though Kiriakou says nothing in the case as described would make it federal absent a broader conspiracy.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="40:01" /><Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="26:12" /><Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="26:43" /><Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jason-jones-show" t="31:14" /> Kiriakou speculates the case may involve a wider conspiracy than officially acknowledged, suggesting Robinson may have been "duped or tricked or recruited," and notes that Robinson's lawyers have said nothing publicly — a silence he contrasts with his own whistleblower case, in which his side leaked to the media specifically to combat Justice Department leaks against him.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="41:04" /><Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="27:45" /><Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="28:16" />

Kiriakou also calls it suspicious that [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu) publicly denied any Israeli involvement before anyone had accused Israel, and that Israel is erecting a statue of Kirk in Tel Aviv despite his having recently come out against Israel.<Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jason-jones-show" t="30:42" />

Kiriakou connects the case to [Joe Kent](/wiki/joe-kent), who he says was quietly removed from the investigation because superiors believed he could not conduct adequate research into whether a foreign asset was involved, without Kent accusing any specific country — Kent was reportedly examining clandestinely collected information that did not neatly fit the official narrative that Tyler Robinson shot Kirk.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="35:53" /><Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="22:34" /><Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="23:36" />

In the immediate aftermath, Kiriakou said the governor of Utah was the only official who made any sense, despite having the least responsibility for the investigation, while FBI Director Kash Patel announced a suspect was in custody and then released him, twice.<Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="02:07" /><Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="02:40" /> He also faulted campus police at Utah Valley University for failing to check the roof of the closed, renovation-shuttered building the shooter used, calling the overall response amateur hour.<Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="03:12" /> He said the shooting suspect was ultimately turned in by a family member or family friend.<Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="04:14" />

Kiriakou has also weighed in on the public reaction to the killing. He noted that a Stanford University Center for the Study of Extremism poll found 34% of American college students believe using violence is justified when they disagree with someone's speech, and said some of his own friends were on social media unequivocally endorsing Kirk's killing.<Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="01:36" /><Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="05:46" /> He noted that Laura Loomer called for rounding up, arresting, and investigating all leftist organizations in America after the assassination, with Trump echoing similar language the night of the killing, and separately called Loomer's demand to ban the Democratic Party a claim no U.S. court would uphold.<Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="06:17" /><Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="08:23" /> He also said conservatives outraged over reactions to Kirk's killing were the same people who had mocked the attack on Paul Pelosi and spread a rumor it was a "lover's quarrel" between secretly gay men.<Cite s="2025-09-19-harrison-berger" t="09:59" />

## Kiriakou's position on Israeli involvement

Asked directly whether Israel was behind the killing, Kiriakou answered that he had always said no, but that there are many people he respects — *"people far more important than I could ever be"* — who say there is enough there that it should at least be investigated.<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="47:30" />

He distinguishes that from the specific claims in circulation, rejecting outright the assertion that the Egyptian Air Force was responsible.<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="48:01" />

His stated reason for wanting an inquiry is Kirk's independence rather than any particular theory: Kirk *"was kind of cutting an independent path, and there are a lot of interests out there that don't want people to take independent paths."* On that basis he says an investigation is owed to Kirk's family and, more broadly, to the American people.<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="48:01" /><Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="48:33" />

## The jurisdiction question

The objection Kiriakou says he most wants answered is not ballistic but procedural: *"I'd like to know why in the world the [FBI](/wiki/fbi) is involved in this case."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="35:39" />

He lays out the geography to make the point. The alleged shooter left his home in Utah to go to northern Utah, shot Kirk in Utah, drove back to his home in Utah and told his friends in Utah. *"There's literally no federal aspect to this to this crime at all. None."* On his reading it should be an investigation headed by the Utah State Police and the Utah Attorney General's Office — *"So, why in the world is the FBI involved? Unless they know something that we don't know. And if that's the case, they need to come out with it."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="36:10" />

He frames his own scepticism carefully. *"I've said in interviews that I am the least conspiracy-oriented person that I know. I really am."* The qualification he adds is empirical: over the last six, eight, ten years some conspiracy theories *"have actually proven to be true"* — he names the COVID lab leak, where *"you were a crazy person if you believed it,"* and [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein).<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="34:36" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="35:07" /> What he draws from that is a rule about timing rather than a theory: *"we should not make any judgments until all the facts are out."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="35:07" /> The details he says do not yet sit right include how quickly the weapon was broken down — *"it's impossible to break down that gun so quickly"* — and how the shooter got away.<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="35:07" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="35:39" />

## Why a federal agency is in a state case

Kiriakou has repeatedly said he holds no inside information on the killing and is working from the same public record as everyone else. On that record, *"it seems like Tyler Robinson was the person who killed Charlie Kirk"* — he told friends in advance that he intended to, and afterwards texted his girlfriend to say he had.<Cite s="2025-12-09-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-cracks-hegseth" t="03:45" /><Cite s="2025-12-09-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-cracks-hegseth" t="04:16" />

His standing objection is jurisdictional. *"I said from the very beginning that I just simply did not understand why the FBI was involved in the investigation. The alleged shooter is from Utah. The crime took place in Utah. The shooter began the crime in Utah, driving from the south to the north to carry out the shooting. This is a state crime. It has nothing to do with the FBI."* He took the bureau''s public confirmation that it was in a supporting role as the explanation for the silence since: *"I think that''s why we''re not getting any more information … it''s a state case, and the state just has chosen not to release any information."*<Cite s="2025-12-09-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-cracks-hegseth" t="04:48" /><Cite s="2025-12-09-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-cracks-hegseth" t="05:18" /><Cite s="2025-12-09-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-cracks-hegseth" t="05:49" />

What he does find odd is the opposite of what most commentators find odd — not that the investigation has gone quiet, but that it is continuing at all. *"I can tell you that in my own case, once I was charged with a crime, the FBI just stopped. They just stopped and they moved on to the next person. Why they are continuing to investigate when there doesn''t seem to be any evidence that others were involved is interesting to me."*<Cite s="2025-12-09-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-cracks-hegseth" t="04:16" /><Cite s="2025-12-09-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-cracks-hegseth" t="04:48" />

## Why the answer is not believed

Kiriakou declines to treat public disbelief as irrational, and grounds it historically: *"in the old days, let''s say around the days of the [Kennedy assassination](/wiki/jfk-assassination), people believed their government. People wanted to believe that the government was telling the truth. And now in 2025 people don''t believe that the government is telling the truth. They always believe that there''s more to the story — because in fact, over the years, that has proven to be the case. And so I can understand why people are just not accepting this notion that Tyler Robinson was acting alone."*<Cite s="2025-12-09-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-cracks-hegseth" t="07:55" /><Cite s="2025-12-09-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-cracks-hegseth" t="08:25" />
