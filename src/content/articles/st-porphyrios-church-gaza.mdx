The **Church of Saint Porphyrios** is the Greek Orthodox church in Gaza that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) identifies as the oldest continuously operating church in the world. He cites its bombing — on October 18, with 24 women and children sheltering inside — as an atrocity he remains angry about, part of what he describes as a broader assault on the ancient Christian communities of the Holy Land.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="49:15" />
