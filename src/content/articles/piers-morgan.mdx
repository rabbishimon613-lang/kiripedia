**Piers Morgan** is the British broadcaster whose panel show became a recurring — and combative — venue for [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) in 2025.

## The Dershowitz smackdowns

Kiriakou describes the marquee bout as *"the equivalent of a WWE Smackdown with [Alan Dershowitz](/wiki/alan-dershowitz)"* — four panelists, with Kiriakou and Scott Horton (*"absolutely brilliant... I wish my brain could work"* like his) on one side, Dershowitz and former Mossad chief Moshe Yaalon on the other.<Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="56:11" /><Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="11:28" />

The flashpoint was Israeli espionage. When Kiriakou said the Israelis spy on the United States, Yaalon denied it; Morgan pressed, and the general answered that the spying stopped when [Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard) got caught and the prime minister forbade it. Kiriakou's reply: *"General, with all due respect, that's just simply not true. I joined the CIA in 1990..."*<Cite s="2025-07-11-useful-idiots-halper-mat-they-destroyed-epstein-files" t="35:28" /><Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="11:59" /> In another exchange, Morgan asked whether Israel has nuclear weapons — the general only smiled.<Cite s="2025-06-01-austin-and-matt-11-inside-the-cia-whistleblowers-war-crimes-and-blind" t="32:01" /> And when Kiriakou said he believed [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) was an Israeli [access agent](/wiki/access-agent), the Mossad chief chuckled — a reaction Kiriakou reads as telling.<Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="1:06:37" /> He got into *"a little tiff"* with Dershowitz, who he believes lashed out from anger.<Cite s="2025-08-01-the-deep-focus-show-the-deep-focus-show" t="10:36" />

Describing a later appearance alongside Dershowitz and a retired Israeli general he names as Danny Ayalon, Kiriakou recalls arguing that Israel is *"prolific in their espionage work in the United States, especially as it relates to American defense contractors,"* until Morgan cut Dershowitz off and put the question to the general directly. The answer, Kiriakou says, was a laugh and a formula: *"My position is whatever the prime minister's position is."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="21:16" /> Morgan then asked whether Israel has nuclear weapons and received the same construction — the prime minister has not confirmed it, so the general would go with what the prime minister says. Kiriakou treats the repetition as its own answer on both counts.<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="21:46" /> *(The transcript's auto-captions render the name as "I alone"; the identification of the general as Mossad is Kiriakou's own.)*

Kiriakou also used Morgan's platform (and Fox News) to say he believes Epstein material was probably destroyed — noting that despite the Federal Records Act, the CIA destroys documents all the time.<Cite s="2025-07-15-useful-idiots-useful-idiots" t="01:01" /><Cite s="2025-07-15-useful-idiots-useful-idiots" t="08:52" /> A separate appearance covered the JFK files with Colonel Douglas Macgregor.<Cite s="2025-03-26-truthovercomfort-truthovercomfort" t="46:41" />

## Interrupting on command

Asked how one makes a point while being constantly cut off, Kiriakou admitted the show's format runs against his nature: his mother raised him not to interrupt. On Piers Morgan that has to go out the window — the producer explicitly told him not to be afraid to interrupt, and that Morgan would break it up if it got to be too much. *"I have to force myself to do it on that show."*<Cite s="2025-08-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:41" /><Cite s="2025-08-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="34:14" />
