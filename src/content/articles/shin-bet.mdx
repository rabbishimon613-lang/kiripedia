**Shin Bet** is Israel's internal security and counterintelligence service. In [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, the CIA's day-one counterintelligence briefing named it as one of two declared Israeli services operating out of the Washington embassy — one [Mossad](/wiki/mossad) officer, one Shin Bet officer.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="39:30" />

Kiriakou describes his first liaison briefing, six weeks into the job, as a joint session with a Mossad officer and a Shin Bet officer held in a rented safe house rather than at headquarters — because, he says, Israeli officers kept bringing bugged gifts to CIA conference rooms and were ultimately banned from the building.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="35:21" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="35:52" /> When he introduced himself under his true name, the Shin Bet officer pushed his glasses down, asked him to spell it, and said, *"You are Jewish?"* Kiriakou answered, *"I am not recruitable. Don't even think about trying to recruit me."* His boss later told him the Israelis pitch every officer they meet, reasoning that if they approach a thousand, one will eventually say yes.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="36:54" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="37:26" /> He tells the same story elsewhere, adding the specific gift that got Israeli officers barred from CIA headquarters in the first place: a large ceremonial CIA seal, meant for display in a conference room, that concealed a transmitter with enough batteries to last a year and a half — discovered only when the agency X-rayed it.<Cite s="2023-04-12-danny-jones" t="1:37:16" /> In that telling, briefed jointly by the Mossad and Shin Bet officers at an off-site location as the most junior analyst present and using his real name for the first time, Kiriakou again recounts being told *"You are Jewish"* and replying, *"I am not recruitable,"* reading it as an attempted recruitment approach five minutes into their first conversation.<Cite s="2023-04-12-danny-jones" t="1:38:50" />
