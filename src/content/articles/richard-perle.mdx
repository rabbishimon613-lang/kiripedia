**Richard Perle** is a prominent neoconservative — a lifelong Democrat who became a major adviser to the George W. Bush White House. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) relays a famous story that on September 12, 2001, Perle went to [Dick Cheney](/wiki/dick-cheney) and said the United States had to invade Iraq — planting the seed even though there was never any evidence Saddam Hussein had anything to do with 9/11, and Osama bin Laden in fact hated Saddam.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="20:16" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="20:49" />
