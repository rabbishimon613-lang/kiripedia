Describing his posting to Athens as the [Counterterrorism Center](/wiki/counterterrorism-center) representative, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) gives a single figure to convey the threat level.

## The comparison

"Athens was so dangerous for Americans — well, was back then — we spent more money on security in Athens than we did in Beirut. That's how dangerous it was, because of this group."<Cite s="2025-11-04-truth-hurts-show-truth-hurts-show" t="04:13" />

The group was [Revolutionary Organization 17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november). Kiriakou describes it as so deadly that it had murdered 28 people and wounded hundreds, and had never been caught — indeed, "we were never able to identify a single one of them." The assassinations were carried out in broad daylight in front of thousands of people, "and nobody saw a thing because they were so petrified of being the next victim."<Cite s="2025-11-04-truth-hurts-show-truth-hurts-show" t="00:00" />

## What the spending bought

It bought armour and routine. Kiriakou drove a fully armoured BMW 540 — the first in Greece, and uninsurable there because there were no others, so it had to be insured through a company in Germany. He was armed with a 9mm in a tear-away waist pack, a .38 snub-nose on his ankle and a knife in his back pocket. Every day he left the house at a different time by a different route, on the standing principle that a pattern tells an assassin where to stand.<Cite s="2025-11-04-truth-hurts-show-truth-hurts-show" t="01:03" /><Cite s="2025-11-04-truth-hurts-show-truth-hurts-show" t="01:33" />

## What it did not buy

The one measure the danger ruled out was diplomatic plates: they were "too dangerous. It would allow them to more easily identify us as Americans."<Cite s="2025-11-04-truth-hurts-show-truth-hurts-show" t="04:13" />

What replaced them defeated the purpose. "The Greeks in their infinite wisdom gave literally everybody in the American embassy license plates that began with YHB. Everybody. It was worse than a diplomatic plate."<Cite s="2025-11-04-truth-hurts-show-truth-hurts-show" t="04:45" />

Kiriakou still remembers his own registration a quarter-century later, and his wife's. The same convention identified the British embassy, and it is how he recognised the car in which [Stephen Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders) had just been shot while he inched past it in traffic.<Cite s="2025-11-04-truth-hurts-show-truth-hurts-show" t="04:45" />

That morning was the one on which he had overslept and skipped his two-hour surveillance detection route — the only reason he was on that road at all.<Cite s="2025-11-04-truth-hurts-show-truth-hurts-show" t="03:07" />
