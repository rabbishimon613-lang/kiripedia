**Scott Horton** is an American antiwar journalist and interviewer, and one of the very few commentators [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) singles out by name for intellectual force on Middle East questions: *"Scott Horton, who is one of the most brilliant people I've ever engaged with on these issues."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="22:58" />

The occasion for the description was a televised panel hosted by [Piers Morgan](/wiki/piers-morgan) at which Kiriakou and Horton took one side and [Alan Dershowitz](/wiki/alan-dershowitz) and a former Israeli intelligence chief the other. Kiriakou used it to state that the Israelis actively spy on the United States, and to put his own 1990 CIA counterintelligence briefing to the Israeli side of the table.<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="22:58" /><Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="23:33" /><Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="24:03" />

Horton has also interviewed Kiriakou repeatedly on his own programme, including on the pressure applied to independent journalists — the conversation in which Kiriakou grouped himself, [Max Blumenthal](/wiki/max-blumenthal) and MintPress News together as targets of the same campaign.<Cite s="2022-05-06-scott-horton-ep-5709" t="16:45" />
