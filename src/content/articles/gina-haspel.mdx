**Gina Haspel** was the Director of the Central Intelligence Agency under President Trump's first term. Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) she and Mike Pompeo were the two *"very, very bad people"* whom Trump appointed to the position in his first term.<Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="25:51" />

## "Bloody Gina"

Kiriakou: *"The other one was bloody Gina Haspel. We called her bloody Gina for a good reason. She flew out to the secret site to sit in on one of the torture sessions just because she could. Just to sit there and enjoy it and take it all in. What kind of sick person does something like that?"*<Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="25:51" /><Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="26:23" />

## *"Bloody Gina"*

When [Gina Haspel](/wiki/gina-haspel)'s appointment as [CIA](/wiki/cia) Director was announced, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) wrote an op-ed in the *Washington Post* opposing her nomination. He stated that in the hallways of the CIA, officers referred to her as *"Bloody Gina Haspel"* — because she traveled to the secret prison site specifically to observe torture sessions, in Kiriakou's description, *"just for the kick of watching the torture."*<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="38:30" /><Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="39:00" />

Kiriakou characterized her as *"1,000% pro-torture."* He also described her, in the context of the broader debate about the deep state, as *"the definition of the deep state"* — a reference to her more than thirty years at the [CIA](/wiki/cia).<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="39:00" /><Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="39:30" />
