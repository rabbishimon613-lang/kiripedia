The **Dashti-Leyli massacre** took place on November 30 and December 1, 2001, near Mazar-i-Sharif in northern Afghanistan. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described [CIA](/wiki/cia) involvement in the events and his subsequent investigation of the incident as a Senate Foreign Relations Committee investigator — an investigation that was shut down by Senator John Kerry.

## The surrender

Two thousand Taliban soldiers surrendered to Northern Alliance forces under General Abdul Rashid Dostam. Dostam called American forces and said he could not hold them. The [CIA](/wiki/cia) advised him to put the prisoners on trucks, transport them to the desert, and hold them there until they could be distributed to smaller prisons around the country.<Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="8:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="8:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="9:00" />

## The containers

The prisoners were crammed into shipping containers on the back of trucks. There was no food, no water, and critically, no air. When prisoners began beating on the inside of the containers because they could not breathe, Dostam's forces responded by shooting holes in the trucks. *"Now you have air holes,"* [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) characterized their reasoning. They killed an untold number before the trucks even moved.<Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="9:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="9:30" />

The trucks were driven to the desert — approximately a day's journey. According to one of the fourteen survivors (out of two thousand), when the containers were opened at the destination, the bodies fell out *"like sardines from a can."*<Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="9:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="10:00" />

## CIA presence on site — two men in blue jeans

Years later, while working as chief investigator on the Senate Foreign Relations Committee, [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was contacted by a human-rights activist who requested a secret meeting — an empty classroom at Johns Hopkins University, lights off. The activist had a new witness: a twenty-year-old who had been twelve at the time of the massacre and had hidden behind a rock when the containers were opened. He described seeing two men present: wearing blue jeans and black t-shirts and speaking English. Kiriakou's response: *"Who else could possibly be in the middle of the Afghan desert on December 1st, 2001, wearing black t-shirts and jeans and speaking English?"*<Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="10:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="11:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="11:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="12:00" />

## Kerry shut down the investigation

[Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) drafted a letter to the [CIA](/wiki/cia) under Kerry's signature requesting clarification on whether American personnel were present when the containers were opened. The CIA classified their response at the top-secret level — the same letter had been sent unclassified — and deposited it in a vault to which Kiriakou, holding only a secret clearance, had no access. A colleague described the response to him as: *"Go fuck yourself."*<Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="12:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="13:00" />

Kiriakou brought this to Kerry and told him it was a cover-up: he was one hundred percent certain the [CIA](/wiki/cia) was involved in the massacre, and the agency had refused to respond to a letter signed by the committee chairman. Kerry's response: *"I've been thinking about this, and I think we should probably just drop this."* His reasoning, as Kiriakou characterized it: Kerry never wanted to do anything that could jeopardize his chances of becoming Secretary of State. *"That's what he lived for."*<Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="13:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="13:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-operations-insight-cia-secrets" t="14:00" />
