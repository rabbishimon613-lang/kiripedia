The **National Security Agency (NSA)** is the U.S. signals-intelligence agency that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says came, after 9/11, to "instrument the United States of America" and treat the country "as the equivalent of a foreign country for the purposes of dragnet blanket electronic surveillance on a vast scale."<Cite s="2025-10-14-jspione-silenced-2014-the-war-on-whistleblowers-feature-documentary-" t="18:37" />

Kiriakou also states that the NSA "abysmally failed the nation" in the run-up to the September 11 attacks: information on al-Qaeda and associated movements that he says could have gone a long way toward rolling up the plot was already present in its database, but the agency's systems were so poor that no one knew it was there.<Cite s="2025-10-14-jspione-silenced-2014-the-war-on-whistleblowers-feature-documentary-" t="50:27" />
