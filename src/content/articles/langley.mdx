**Langley**, Virginia is the home of [CIA](/wiki/cia) headquarters — [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s workplace for most of his career, and his shorthand for where American power actually lives.

## Kiriakou at headquarters

Kiriakou began as a desk analyst at Langley — in the days, he jokes, when the CIA needed only one analyst for Iraq and Kuwait. He was told to start on Iraq *"because nothing ever happened there. Nothing ever changed there."*<Cite s="2015-07-25-splendour-in-the-grass" t="02:07" /> He returned to Langley between foreign tours — from Greece in 2000,<Cite s="2015-04-24-the-real-news-network-the-real-news-network" t="00:08" /> and from Pakistan in 2002, when a senior officer approached him in the headquarters cafeteria and casually asked whether he wanted to be trained in the new interrogation techniques.<Cite s="2019-04-20-david-gornoski-on-torture-prison-ptsd" t="13:00" />

## The bloat critique

Kiriakou concedes the charge that the CIA is bloated and inefficient — *"when a majority of your operational staff is actually sitting in cubicles in Langley, Virginia, rather than serving overseas recruiting spies... you have a problem."*<Cite s="2017-02-13-around-the-empire-around-the-empire" t="38:08" />

## The permanent government

Langley anchors Kiriakou's darker structural points. Agencies get caught in misdeeds, get dragged before Congress, swear they're sorry — *"then they go back to Langley and of course nothing ever, ever changes."* He was unimpressed that Trump spent inaugural week at headquarters to *"kiss their derriere."*<Cite s="2017-03-09-berlin-gegen-krieg-berlin-gegen-krieg" t="03:10" /> If Langley doesn't like a president's orders, he says, it doesn't refuse — it slow-rolls whatever comes from the White House.<Cite s="2023-01-11-real-progressives" t="07:23" /> And when Scott Horton put it to him that the real government of America is out in Langley and the white marble in Washington is just for the rubes, Kiriakou answered: *"Yeah — no, you're right. That's about the shape of it."*<Cite s="2018-02-06-scott-horton-ep-4439-torture-program-his-time-in-prison" t="14:36" />

Smaller Langley details round out his commentary: from headquarters — or anywhere — the agency can activate a phone and its camera without the owner realizing the device is on,<Cite s="2017-03-09-berlin-gegen-krieg-berlin-gegen-krieg" t="02:38" /> and a former president wanting intelligence simply calls a liaison officer at Langley, who dispatches analysts to brief him at the secret level.<Cite s="2022-08-27-kevin-gosztola-mar-a-lago-affidavit-unsealed" t="28:08" />
