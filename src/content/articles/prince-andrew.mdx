**Prince Andrew** is a British royal who appears in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) case in two distinct roles: first as an illustration of the [access agent](/wiki/access-agent) principle, and later as a criminal suspect whose alleged conduct Kiriakou reads as proof of espionage.

## The un-recruitable principal

Andrew is one of the names Kiriakou returns to when explaining why a foreign service would recruit Epstein rather than the powerful people around him. A service wanting closely held information from figures such as [Bill Clinton](/wiki/bill-clinton), Bill Gates or Andrew cannot recruit them — they do not have the traditional vulnerabilities that would allow it, and money does not motivate them — so it recruits somebody with ready access instead.<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="2:47:45" /><Cite s="2025-02-28-epstein-bin-laden-china-israel" t="02:08" /><Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="14:06" /><Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="06:41" /> Kiriakou has also noted that Andrew, unlike Epstein, actually held classified information — which is what made his access worth buying.<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="1:12:00" />

## The absence of charges

Before the 2026 proceedings, Kiriakou repeatedly raised Andrew as an example of the case's selective enforcement, asking why charges had never been filed against him and saying he should be charged with crimes against children, as should the other adults who had sex with minors on Epstein's island.<Cite s="2025-07-08-the-deep-focus-show-the-deep-focus-show" t="04:44" /> He has listed Andrew among the *"A-list people who we know for a fact were at that house"* about whom nothing was heard for years.<Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="03:42" />

## The 2026 arrest

By March 2026 Kiriakou was describing a changed situation: Andrew was no longer a prince, had been stripped of his royal residence and *"essentially just thrown out into the street"*, and had been arrested and would be prosecuted for allegedly giving Epstein classified defence ministry documents.<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="55:51" />

Kiriakou treats the nature of the material as the decisive fact. *"Why in blazes would Jeffrey Epstein want classified UK defence ministry documents unless he's spying for a foreign power?"*<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="56:23" /> He makes the same argument about [Peter Mandelson](/wiki/peter-mandelson) and British trade-negotiation positions: Epstein had no personal use for either, so the collection was for somebody else.<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="56:53" /><Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="57:27" />
