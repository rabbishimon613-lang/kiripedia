**Section 219** of the National Defense Authorization Act for 2027 — renumbered from Section 224 in an earlier attempt — would co-locate Israeli personnel with the Pentagon and allow closer, [Five Eyes](/wiki/five-eyes)-style liaison with the [CIA](/wiki/cia).<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="04:44" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="19:47" /> [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) notes that an earlier version was moved without a vote, and that opposition has been led by [Thomas Massie](/wiki/thomas-massie) and Ro Khanna.<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="19:47" />

## The argument against

Kiriakou's objection turns on who the United States normally merges militaries with: "the closest allies that we have in the world. Very specifically the British, the Canadians, the Australians, and the New Zealanders." Israel is not in NATO and is not a member of the European Union.<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="09:24" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="09:56" />

He adds a structural warning. The Israeli defence minister said publicly that Israel's next war would be against Turkey — a NATO member. Were Turkey to invoke Article 5, the United States would be obliged to go to war with Israel to defend it, which Kiriakou says will not happen; the effect would be to destroy NATO.<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="09:56" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="10:27" />

The deeper objection is the counterintelligence record: [Israeli espionage in the United States](/wiki/israeli-espionage-in-the-united-states) is ongoing, the Defense Intelligence Agency warned its own employees about intensified Israeli recruitment weeks before the debate, and the [F-35 avionics episode](/wiki/f-35-israel-avionics) demonstrated what happens when Israel is refused a technology it wants.<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="05:14" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="22:53" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-young-turks-former-cia-agent-explains-why-we-need-to-brea" t="23:23" />
