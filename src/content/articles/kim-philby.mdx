**Kim Philby** was the British MI6 officer — its deputy director — secretly recruited by the KGB in college and run as a mole for decades. In [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s telling, the cruelest twist is that Philby was counterintelligence chief [James Angleton](/wiki/james-jesus-angleton)'s best man and the godfather of his child — his *koumbaros* — even as Angleton, obsessed with moles, suspected everyone but him.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="32:10" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="32:43" /> When suspicion finally fell on Philby, he was removed and sent to Beirut; tipped off by another mole, he excused himself from a Christmas party at the British ambassador's residence, climbed out the bathroom window, and fled to the Soviet embassy. Six days later he surfaced at a Moscow press conference and later lectured new KGB officers.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="33:16" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="34:18" /> In the same conversation, Kiriakou also discussed [Operation Paperclip](/wiki/operation-paperclip), the program that brought Nazi scientists to the United States after World War II.
