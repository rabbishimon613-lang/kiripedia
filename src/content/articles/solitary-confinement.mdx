**Solitary confinement** is the prison-isolation practice that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) notes the United Nations has declared *"a form of torture."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="07:22" /> He argues its use against depressed prisoners like [Daniel Hale](/wiki/daniel-hale) — locked down *"quote unquote for his own safety"* — is self-fulfilling: *"he was not suicidal; now he is, because he's in solitary confinement."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="06:20" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="07:22" /> Kiriakou draws on his own ten days in isolation, when guards took his glasses so he *"couldn't read anything."* His cell was *"six feet by 16 feet"*; he slept on *"a steel bunk with no mattress, no pillow,"* balling up a bath towel, and coped by *"walking in circles all day long"* to reach 10,000 steps.<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="08:24" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="08:57" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="09:29" /> He notes the same isolation held [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange) for years and calls jailing the suicidal in solitary *"ridiculous."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="06:52" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="47:22" />
