**Pakistan Station (2002)** refers to [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s posting to Islamabad in January 2002 as CIA chief of counterterrorism operations, under station chief [Bob Grenier](/wiki/bob-grenier), in the immediate aftermath of the September 11 attacks and the U.S. bombing of [Tora Bora](/wiki/tora-bora).

## Arrival

Kiriakou flew from Washington via London and Kuwait to Islamabad in January 2002; his luggage was lost in transit and did not arrive with him.<Cite s="2026-02-16-dead-drop-s1e15-the-hunt-begins" t="01:35" /> On his driver's advice he avoided the Islamabad Marriott, warned it was al-Qaeda's likely bombing target; the hotel was truck-bombed roughly six weeks later, killing dozens and leaving a 30-foot crater.<Cite s="2026-02-16-dead-drop-s1e15-the-hunt-begins" t="07:48" />

## A skeleton crew of retirees

Kiriakou's counterterrorism branch in Islamabad consisted of himself and seven CIA retirees — senior former officers, including one former deputy director of the CIA for operations, who had volunteered to return to the agency as contractors after 9/11.<Cite s="2026-02-16-dead-drop-s1e15-the-hunt-begins" t="12:01" />

## First raid

Acting on a tip about an al-Qaeda safe house in Islamabad, Kiriakou's first raid captured two 19-year-old Tunisians who had fled [Tora Bora](/wiki/tora-bora) and were squatting, with no plan, in a vacant house.<Cite s="2026-02-16-dead-drop-s1e15-the-hunt-begins" t="15:44" />

## Pakistani liaison

Kiriakou liaised daily with three Pakistani intelligence contacts during raid operations: Colonel Tarek, Major Khaled, and Major General Muhammad.<Cite s="2026-02-16-dead-drop-s1e15-the-hunt-begins" t="20:52" />

## Pulling officers off the border

Kiriakou proposed pulling CIA officers off the dangerous Afghanistan-Pakistan border, where they had been catching al-Qaeda fighters one at a time as they crossed, and instead letting fighters cross uncaught into Pakistan — betting that once gathered at safe houses they would expose themselves via cell phone or email, allowing the CIA to capture them *"20 at a time"* rather than one at a time. The plan led directly into the operation that captured [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah).<Cite s="2026-02-16-dead-drop-s1e15-the-hunt-begins" t="35:29" />
