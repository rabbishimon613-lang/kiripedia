The **United Food and Commercial Workers** (**UFCW**) — one of America's largest unions — was [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s employer at the moment his life bent toward the [CIA](/wiki/cia).

## The job

Kiriakou worked at the UFCW's international headquarters on K Street in Washington while studying Middle Eastern affairs at George Washington University — a natural berth for a man with *"a long history of labor involvement in my family."*<Cite s="2015-04-17-the-real-news-network-the-real-news-network" t="01:17" /><Cite s="2019-08-08-david-gornoski-archives-david-gornoski-archives" t="04:48" />

## The assignment

In [Dr. Jerrold Post](/wiki/jerome-post)'s class on the psychology of leadership, students were assigned to shadow their bosses for a week and write a psychological profile.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="11:40" /> Kiriakou's boss frightened him a little: an old-time tough-guy union organizer whose back had been broken by scabs during a strike.<Cite s="2022-07-23-the-darkened-hour" t="04:10" /><Cite s="2024-07-30-spartan-leadership-podcast" t="08:47" /> Halfway through the week they got into a serious argument, and Kiriakou called him a racist — *"which he was."* The man balled up his fist in fury.<Cite s="2021-11-21-dcm-tv-exclusivo-parceria-lava-jato-eua-foi-movida-p" t="02:53" /><Cite s="2019-10-03-kiriakou-on-the-cia-obama-vs-trump" t="01:35" />

In a later telling Kiriakou supplies what happened next, and it is not a punch. With both men squared up — Kiriakou putting his hands up and thinking *"dang it, I went too far this time"* — the boss instead said, twice, *"my penis is bigger than yours."* Kiriakou's reply was *"you know what, you're nuts,"* and he quit and walked out on the spot, having concluded the man was dangerous. He wrote the paper immediately afterwards.<Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="17:01" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="18:33" />

The paper Kiriakou turned in concluded that his boss was a sociopath — with psychopathic and possibly violent tendencies — and potentially dangerous, with evidence to support it; Kiriakou stresses that he footnoted the whole thing, so that it read as analysis rather than as him venting.<Cite s="2015-04-17-the-real-news-network-the-real-news-network" t="01:17" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="17:01" /> His own reading of why it worked is that the analysis was concise, to the point, and backed with facts.<Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="18:33" /><Cite s="2015-04-17-the-real-news-network-the-real-news-network" t="01:17" /> Dr. Post — who, Kiriakou later learned, had founded the [CIA's political psychology division](/wiki/cia-political-psychology-division) — took notice. The union job ended; the intelligence career began.
