The **deep state** is a term [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) defends against the people most likely to share his politics. He notes that *"a lot of people, especially on the left, think that the deep state is just a right-wing trope to complain about government"* — and rejects that flatly, with an explicit statement of where he is speaking from: *"I can tell you, coming from the left, that the deep state is absolutely real."*<Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-aaron-good-on-the" t="00:30" />

## "I was a part of it"

His claim to standing is participation rather than observation: *"It's real, and it's ugly, and I know because I was a part of it when I spent 15 years at the CIA, and frankly, another couple of years on Capitol Hill on the Senate Foreign Relations Committee staff."*<Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-aaron-good-on-the" t="01:00" />

## The label doesn't matter

Kiriakou treats the terminology as a distraction from the thing being named, and offers to drop it: *"you don't have to call it the deep state. You can call it whatever you want. You can call it the state. You can call it the federal bureaucracy."*<Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-aaron-good-on-the" t="01:00" />

What he will not concede is its existence or its durability: *"the truth of the matter is that it is there. It's there to stay. It's not going anywhere."*<Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-aaron-good-on-the" t="01:00" />
