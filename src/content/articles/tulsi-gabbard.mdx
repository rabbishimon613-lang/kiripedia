## What the DNI actually controls

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) explains that as Director of National Intelligence, [Tulsi Gabbard](/wiki/tulsi-gabbard) nominally oversees all 18 U.S. intelligence agencies and holds *"everything of everything,"* serving at the pleasure of the president with no fixed term. But he notes the DNI was, until recently, really in charge only of budgets — and not even fully those, since most agencies sit inside the Pentagon or State Department.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="58:31" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="59:01" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="59:34" />
