## What the DNI actually controls

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) explains that as Director of National Intelligence, [Tulsi Gabbard](/wiki/tulsi-gabbard) nominally oversees all 18 U.S. intelligence agencies and holds *"everything of everything,"* serving at the pleasure of the president with no fixed term.
