**Human Rights Watch** (**HRW**) is the organization that got to the CIA torture story first — a fact at the center of [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of why his prosecution was retaliation, not law enforcement.

## First to report

In 2005, Human Rights Watch published a report, in real detail, saying the CIA was torturing its prisoners; [Amnesty International](/wiki/amnesty-international) followed in 2006, and the International Committee of the Red Cross in 2007.<Cite s="2015-07-25-splendour-in-the-grass" t="13:04" /><Cite s="2017-05-31-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="36:46" /> That sequence is why Kiriakou insists his 2007 [ABC interview](/wiki/brian-ross) revealed nothing new: the FBI watched the tape and concluded it was already common knowledge — *"I had added nothing, technically, to the debate. But what I had done was I had become the first CIA officer, current or former"* to confirm it.<Cite s="2015-05-06-the-real-news-network-why-i-was-targeted-by-the-cia-rai-9-10" t="09:25" /><Cite s="2016-07-11-adventures-in-the-free-sta" t="24:49" /> The leaks to HRW, Amnesty, and the Red Cross also meant someone inside was already talking — another whistleblower whose identity, he notes, has never been learned.<Cite s="2015-08-18-politics-done-right-politics-done-right" t="18:52" /><Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="42:38" /> It was those accumulating reports that convinced him he had waited long enough for somebody official to speak.<Cite s="2020-12-24-live-on-the-fly-randy-credico-kiriakou-rebecca-vincent-assange-countdown-to" t="18:11" />

Kiriakou also cites HRW's drone-war findings — that the great majority of drone-strike dead were innocent civilians near the target.<Cite s="2017-06-30-ryot-lies-and-torture-of-the-cia-with-john-kiriako" t="10:06" />

## In the chain of his arrest

HRW appears once more, fatefully, in Kiriakou's telling of his prosecution: when a classified name surfaced, the FBI went to Human Rights Watch and asked where it came from. The answer was journalist Matthew Cole; asked the same question, *"Matthew Cole said John Kiriakou gave it to me."* Two dozen FBI agents raided Kiriakou's house and charged him with five felonies.<Cite s="2023-10-08-scheerpost-scheerpost" t="10:58" />
