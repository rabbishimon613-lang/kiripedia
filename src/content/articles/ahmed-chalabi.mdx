**Ahmed Chalabi** was an Iraqi exile and the founder of the Iraqi National Congress. Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), Chalabi was *"one of those very rare Shia Muslim Kurds"* — born in Iraq in the 1940s, emigrated as a child to Jordan in 1965, educated in finance at Harvard and in London, and the founder of Petra Bank in Jordan.<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:26:52" /><Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:27:22" />

## The Petra Bank fraud

Petra Bank was the principal bank for the Iraqi refugee community in Jordan. Per Kiriakou: *"One night, Ahmed Chalabi [took] every single dollar — $36 million — transferred it to secret accounts in Switzerland, and escaped to Syria in the trunk of his girlfriend / secretary's car."* He was tried in Jordan and sentenced to death in absentia. By the early 1990s he was living in London *"wearing $2,000 suits and driving Rolls-Royces."*<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:27:53" /><Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:28:25" />

## The 1991 CIA arrangement and the burn notice

In 1991 the CIA, looking for a face for post-Saddam Iraq, persuaded King Hussein of Jordan to pardon Chalabi from his death sentence. The CIA assembled 35 Iraqi opposition groups at a hotel ballroom in Vienna and consolidated them into the Iraqi National Congress, with Chalabi as its head. Per Kiriakou: *"We allocated $6 million to get the Iraqi National Congress running, and then the $6 million disappeared. And then Chalabi was showing off his new house in Mayfair London that he just paid cash for, because he [had taken] every single dollar of the six million."*<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:28:58" /><Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:30:01" />

The CIA then issued *"a burn notice — a cable that went to every CIA officer in the world — and it says, 'There's this guy Ahmed Chalabi, he's an Iraqi, and all CIA personnel are forbidden from dealing with him.'"*<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:30:01" />

## The Cheney resurrection and the WMD fabrications

*"Dick Cheney, nine years later, decides, 'What about this Chalabi guy? I like this guy.' And we said, 'No, we issued a burn notice on him, we won't work with Chalabi.' And he said, 'Oh, but I will.' And so they set up a new division at the Pentagon, the intelligence division — there had never been one before — and they created an Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence, and his sole job was just to work with Chalabi."*<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:30:31" />

Per Kiriakou, during the run-up to the 2003 Iraq invasion the Pentagon began transmitting cables claiming reliable Iraqi WMD sourcing — running alongside the German-handled fabricator [Curveball](/wiki/curveball) as one of the war's two central fabrication pillars — *"and they're here located in the garden of Saddam's palace, which of course we can't search."* The CIA was barred on classification grounds from knowing the source's identity. *"We're like, 'We know who the source is. The source is Chalabi, and he's making the whole thing up — we've known that for a decade. You guys are being suckered.' And so he just kept with these reports. Weapons, weapons, weapons, weapons. Bush goes on TV, says they have weapons of mass destruction. We invade Iraq. We never find anything."*<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:31:02" /><Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:31:34" /><Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:32:04" />

Chalabi, never known inside Iraq itself, was briefly made Minister of Oil and died of a heart attack — in London, two or three years before Kiriakou recounted the story in a 2023 interview. None of the other Iraqi National Congress figures ever gained real power; the exception was the Kurds, particularly [Jalal Talabani](/wiki/jalal-talabani), head of the Patriotic Union of Kurdistan, who became president of Iraq.<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="2:32:04" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:38:57" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:39:27" /> The other Kurd who later became Iraq's president, Barham Salih, was Kiriakou's own main Kurdish counterpart during the Iraq War; he lived nearby in Annandale, Virginia, and their families socialized regularly, which Kiriakou says made the news of Salih's presidency feel unreal — *"it's like wow, Baram really made it, makes me feel like an underachiever."*<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:39:58" />

In a separate, near-identical retelling, Kiriakou frames the CIA's original position on the 2003 invasion in terms of "battle lines": roughly eight months before the invasion, a senior officer told him the pro-war faction was the Office of the Vice President, the Office of the Secretary of Defense, and the National Security Council, against an anti-war faction of the State Department, the CIA, and the Joint Chiefs — concluding, "we've lost." Responsibility for the invasion decision, in this telling, rested squarely on [Dick Cheney](/wiki/dick-cheney) and Donald Rumsfeld, with [Condoleezza Rice](/wiki/condoleezza-rice) a lesser factor because she was "easily pushed around."<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:25:23" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:26:25" /> Chalabi himself, in this account, is again described as a rare Shia Kurd born in Iraqi Kurdistan who fled the country before Saddam Hussein's rise, and someone the CIA and U.S. officials favored over rival exile candidates largely because he spoke fluent, unaccented English and dressed well — *"$2,000 Italian suits,"* per Kiriakou.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:26:58" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:29:34" /> Kiriakou also gives a $40 million figure for the CIA's Iraqi National Congress seed funding in this telling — spent on a London house, a Geneva house, and a Mercedes S600, with the rest unaccounted for — and confirms that [Cheney](/wiki/dick-cheney), despite the CIA's burn notice, had the undersecretary of defense for intelligence secretly reopen a channel with Chalabi, explicitly instructing him not to tell the CIA.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:32:40" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:33:44" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:35:15" />

## *"A dirty used-car salesman"*

Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) on the Dalton Fischer Podcast: *"I always hated this guy from the minute I met him. I was like, this guy is a dirty used-car salesman."*<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="1:33:28" />

## The Petra Bank Ponzi scheme

Kiriakou's framing of the [Petra Bank](/wiki/petra-bank) collapse is sharper than the standard account: it was not just embezzlement, it was a Ponzi scheme. *"He opened a bank in Jordan that was specifically meant to cater to the needs of Iraqi refugees. In fact, it was a Ponzi scheme. And he stole all their money — by one account, \$36 million worth of deposits. He escaped in the trunk of his secretary's car across the border into Syria, and then from Syria made his way to London. In Jordan they filed charges against him and found him guilty in absentia and sentenced him to 30 years in prison."*<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="1:33:59" /><Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="1:34:30" />

## The Defense-Intelligence-Report laundering channel

After the CIA issued its [Burn Notice](/wiki/petra-bank) and refused to deal with Chalabi, Dick Cheney's office routed him through a new channel. Per Kiriakou: *"They told Chalabi to start reporting through the U.S. defense attaché in Amman. So we started getting these classified cables called DIRs — Department of Defense Intelligence Reports. And they're like, 'weapons of mass destruction were found at such-and-such coordinates,' and we're like, 'oh my god, what's the source of this report?' So I'd call over the Pentagon — 'hey, I'm looking at DIR 12345, who's the source?' 'We're not at liberty to share that with you.' 'What do you mean, I'm the CIA, you got to share it with me.' 'It's a particularly sensitive source.' We're like, 'fuck — this is Chalabi.'"*<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="1:37:06" /><Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="1:37:40" />
