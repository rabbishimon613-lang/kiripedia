**Carlos the Jackal** — born Ilich Ramírez Sánchez in Venezuela — was a militant active through the 1970s and 1980s. Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), Carlos was *"the Osama bin Laden of the 70s and 80s"* — a benchmark Kiriakou uses when teaching the history of terrorism, with the recurrent observation that his students *"look at me like they've never ever heard this name before."*<Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="1:59:10" /><Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="1:59:40" />

## The Greek connection: Athens, the middleman, and London

Carlos's link to Greek terrorism was Kiriakou's own portfolio for years. Assigned to Athens, he was tasked with tracing the connection between Carlos and [Revolutionary Organization 17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november): *"What I was trying to do was figure out who the middleman was between Carlos the Jackal and the Greek terrorist group revolutionary organization 17 November. So I got a tip about a guy who was the middleman back in the day. And I found him and he's living in a housing project in London."*<Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="1:59:40" /><Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="2:00:12" /> He later described becoming, over these years, the CIA's in-house *"master of the old files on Greek terrorism"* — an obsession so consuming it cost him his first marriage, and one that eventually drew a Greek assassination attempt against him in Athens.<Cite s="2026-06-16-julian-dorey-daily-mkultra-twin-experiment" t="58:17" /><Cite s="2026-06-16-julian-dorey-daily-mkultra-twin-experiment" t="58:50" />

MI6 routed the London side of the operation to MI5 — Kiriakou's first working encounter with the British domestic service. He flew to London every few weeks for six months pursuing the lead. It was on this case that he learned MI5 has no arrest powers and is, per its charter, the British service that *"spies on Britons."*<Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="2:00:12" /><Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="2:00:42" /> Headquarters eventually told him the middleman had converted to Christianity, married an Englishwoman, and was living openly in London.<Cite s="2026-06-16-julian-dorey-daily-mkultra-twin-experiment" t="58:50" />

By Kiriakou's later analysis, the connection ran the other way from what Western intelligence originally suspected: Carlos did not arm 17 November itself, but he did supply weapons to a separate Greek group, Popular Revolutionary Struggle, and he is the identified link the CIA had spent years trying to nail down.<Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="43:28" />

## Name and nickname

Kiriakou explains the "Jackal" nickname's origin: during a Brussels police raid on an apartment used by the still-unidentified Ilich Ramírez Sánchez, a French photojournalist photographed the interior and noticed a copy of the novel *The Day of the Jackal* on a side table, left there by a Belgian associate who had been reading it. The journalist simply began calling the unnamed terrorist "the Jackal."<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="46:41" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="47:13" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="50:47" />

## The OPEC kidnapping and the training network

In 1975, Carlos and his team raided an OPEC oil ministers' meeting in Vienna, killed three people, and kidnapped every minister present, flying the hostages to Libya. The Saudis alone paid roughly a billion dollars in ransom to secure the release of the group.<Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="36:30" /><Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="37:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="53:57" />

With that financing, Carlos partnered with Muammar Gaddafi to establish terrorist training camps in the Libyan desert and in Lebanon, simultaneously training the IRA, Greece's [Revolutionary Organization 17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november) and Popular Revolutionary Struggle, the PFLP, the PFLP-GC, the DFLP, the Abu Nidal Organization, Italy's Red Brigades, and France's Action Directe — with Carlos handling both funding and weapons sourcing.<Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="37:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="54:31" /><Cite s="2026-05-31-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-new-posdcast-12-secrets-that-have" t="10:56" /> Kiriakou described him as a *"true believing communist"* who wanted to bring down the West.<Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="37:30" />

## The Khartoum capture

The operational capture of Carlos originated with a chance sighting by CIA contractor [Billy Waugh](/wiki/billy-wall) — recounted by Kiriakou and independently confirmed, he notes, by Cofer Black, then CIA station chief in Khartoum. Waugh spotted Carlos browsing a Khartoum vegetable market in the mid-1990s — the only other white man he'd ever seen in the city — and over the following month worked to confirm the sighting. To photograph Carlos without alerting him, Waugh paid two Sudanese men to stage a public fistfight; as a crowd gathered to watch, Carlos craned his neck in with everyone else, giving Waugh a clean, repeated shot to photograph him for headquarters.<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="17:51" /><Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="19:28" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="52:47" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="57:38" />

With Carlos's location confirmed, the CIA arranged for a dentist to summon him in for a supposed checkup and sedate him with gas; he woke up on a French military plane bound for Paris to face murder charges. The precise operational mechanics of the capture itself remain, per Kiriakou, *"still classified."*<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="20:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="20:31" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="56:55" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="59:12" /> He was captured in 1994 and has since been imprisoned in France, serving multiple life sentences.<Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="37:30" />

## Prison meeting, 2000

Kiriakou personally met Carlos in a French prison around 2000, roughly a 40-minute drive outside the city. He introduced himself, invoked the 25-year-old promise to Mrs. Welch to find her husband's killers, and asked Carlos only for the names of the Greeks involved in the [Richard Welch](/wiki/richard-welch) assassination. Carlos — by then in his 70s, unrepentant — replied with an obscenity and walked away, giving up nothing.<Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="52:53" />
