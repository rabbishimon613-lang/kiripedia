**Arabic and Greek** refers to the language combination John Kiriakou credits with making possible his transfer from the analytic side of the CIA to operations in the late 1990s.

## The move

Kiriakou has said the transition from analysis to operations was very unusual at the time. His hook, in his own word, was that he was literally the only person in the CIA fluent in both Arabic and Greek. A position had opened in Athens working counterterrorism — handling, as he lists them, Libyan, Iraqi and Palestinian cases. He went down to the Counterterrorism Center and got the job. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="19:01" /> <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="19:33" />

## The motive

The reason he gives for wanting the move is boredom. He has said Iraq consumed him entirely and that it became clear the United States was not serious about acting against Saddam Hussein, at least through the Clinton administration, so he decided to do something else. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="19:01" /> Asked what he told his analytic supervisor, Kiriakou has recounted saying that he was so bored he just sat and drank coffee all day. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="20:06" />

## The warning

His supervisor's reaction, in Kiriakou's telling, was to ask whether he was sure he knew what he was getting into, and then to say that they were going to train him to kill people. Kiriakou has said he laughed and told the man he watched too many films — and that two weeks later he was at the CIA's training facility being trained to kill people. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="20:06" /> <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="20:39" />

## The Greek connection

Kiriakou's Greek is a family inheritance: he has described himself as the grandson of four grandparents who emigrated to the United States from Greece, in a household where the family's debt to the country was a settled matter and public service the expected repayment. <Cite s="2025-04-16-the-dr-phil-podcast-likely-ingested-ev1ozulvly8" t="03:38" />
