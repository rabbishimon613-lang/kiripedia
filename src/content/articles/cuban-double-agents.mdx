Asked to rank, in order, the foreign intelligence services best at recruiting Americans, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) named the Israelis first, the Russians second — *"the Russians have a long view, they''re quite good at it too"* — and the Cubans third.<Cite s="2026-02-27-one-america-news-network-one-america-news-network" t="44:51" />

On the Cubans his assessment runs in both directions at once. They are, he says, *"very, very good"* — and the evidence he offers for it is the record on the other side of the ledger: *"in fact, the CIA has never recruited a Cuban who didn''t turn out to be a [double agent](/wiki/double-agent-capture)."*<Cite s="2026-02-27-one-america-news-network-one-america-news-network" t="44:51" />
