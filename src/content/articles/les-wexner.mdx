**Les Wexner** is the Ohio billionaire retail magnate. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) raises Wexner when asking where [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein)'s money came from, citing *Vanity Fair* allegations that the two were in some sort of relationship and that Wexner gave Epstein *"millions and millions of dollars"* to be his tax advisor — a characterization Kiriakou notes Wexner and his wife both deny.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="02:34" /> Kiriakou offers the unexplained money as part of his case that Epstein was working for the Israelis.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="03:05" />
