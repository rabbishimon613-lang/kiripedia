**New Castle, Pennsylvania** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s home town, and the fixed starting point of almost every account he gives of himself: he grew up in western Pennsylvania, wanted to go into public service and see the world, and left at eighteen for [George Washington University](/wiki/george-washington-university).<Cite s="2023-01-13-the-open-forum-podcast" t="01:39" /><Cite s="2019-03-04-a-good-place-with-ella-a-a-good-place-with-ella-a" t="56:01" />

He places it about an hour north of Pittsburgh, on the edge of Amish country, and calls it a wonderful place to grow up.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="03:41" /><Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="56:45" />

## What happened to it

His account of the town today is blunt and demographic. It had about 50,000 people when he was growing up and has about 18,000 now, and *"it's not such a nice place anymore."* His measure of that is not a statistic but an institution: *"when DEA and ATF open a field office in your hometown of 18,000 people, you got a problem."*<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="03:41" />

The town also anchors his politics on incarceration and industry. Discussing private prisons he places himself geographically — Youngstown within reach, New Castle, Pennsylvania — as somebody speaking about his own region rather than an abstraction.<Cite s="2015-05-21-the-scholars-john-kiriakou" t="20:13" />

## The family business

When Kiriakou was seventeen his parents bought a restaurant in Sharon, Pennsylvania, fifteen or twenty miles away, and he and his father worked the eleven-to-seven shift there on Friday nights — the drive through the unlit Amish country between the two towns being the setting of [his UFO sighting](/wiki/kiriakou-ufo-sighting).<Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="28:19" /><Cite s="2025-03-26-podcast-ufo-live-shows" t="11:26" /><Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="04:11" /> Restaurants ran in the family: an uncle owned one in Old Town Alexandria, Virginia.<Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="31:31" />

A trip into the city was an event. Going to a Pirates game or a Steelers game was, he says, always a big deal, because Pittsburgh really was a big city — it had a zoo.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="05:19" />
