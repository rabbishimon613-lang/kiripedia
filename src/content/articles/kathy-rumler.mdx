**Kathy Rumler** was the White House counsel — the seniormost attorney in the White House — under Barack Obama. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) highlights the Epstein-file emails in which she repeatedly pushed to introduce [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) to CIA director [John Brennan](/wiki/john-brennan): *"you're gonna love John Brennan… let me introduce you."*<Cite s="2026-02-19-american-conservative-cia-propaganda-going-viral" t="05:46" /> He finds it notable that a civilian received a CIA award from Brennan of a kind normally given to those killed in the line of duty, and speculates the bond formed because, as White House counsel, Rumler would have been the final legal authority approving the assassinations on the [Tuesday-morning kill list](/wiki/tuesday-morning-kill-list) — *"I think she was into it."*<Cite s="2026-02-19-american-conservative-cia-propaganda-going-viral" t="04:41" /><Cite s="2026-02-19-american-conservative-cia-propaganda-going-viral" t="06:18" /><Cite s="2026-02-19-american-conservative-cia-propaganda-going-viral" t="07:18" />
