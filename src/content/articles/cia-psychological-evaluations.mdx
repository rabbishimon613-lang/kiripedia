**CIA psychological evaluations** are the recurring psychiatric screenings every officer of the [CIA](/wiki/cia) undergoes. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) treats the requirement as unremarkable and its rationale as obvious: *"You have to be sane in order to go overseas and carry out these operations."*<Cite s="2026-05-19-gq-ex-cia-officer-john-kiriakou-breaks-down-spy" t="05:45" />

## The schedule

The cadence is fixed. There is a pre-employment evaluation; another three years after hiring; and then one every five years *"for the rest of your life."*<Cite s="2026-05-19-gq-ex-cia-officer-john-kiriakou-breaks-down-spy" t="06:46" />

Extra sessions are triggered by conduct rather than by the calendar — *"unless you do something overseas that requires maybe a little bit of intermediate intervention. Like maybe you beat the hell out of somebody at a traffic stop or make a particularly inexplicable stupid decision in an operation. You're going to have to get with the shrinks and work it out with them."*<Cite s="2026-05-19-gq-ex-cia-officer-john-kiriakou-breaks-down-spy" t="06:46" />

## 'Sorry to hear you went nuts'

Kiriakou's illustration of the consequence is the reason he was given a short-term overseas mission just after the 11 September attacks. A branch chief in the [Counterterrorism Center](/wiki/counterterrorism-center) approached him and asked whether he would go. Kiriakou agreed, then asked the obvious question — why not send Dave, who covered the issue?<Cite s="2026-05-19-gq-ex-cia-officer-john-kiriakou-breaks-down-spy" t="05:45" />

The chief's answer was that *"Dave went nuts. He didn't pass the psych evaluation."* Kiriakou then went to find Dave to be briefed on the operation anyway, and opened with *"Sorry to hear you went nuts."* Dave's reply was equally matter-of-fact: *"Yeah, what are you going to do? It'll pass. I'll be okay. I'll be back up and at them in six months."* Kiriakou's own assessment: *"I don't know how nuts he was. He was clearly too nuts to go overseas."*<Cite s="2026-05-19-gq-ex-cia-officer-john-kiriakou-breaks-down-spy" t="06:16" />
