There is no written-down [CIA](/wiki/cia) budget available to the public; the budget is highly classified, and [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says that *"really nobody knows what the budget is."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="54:52" />

The mechanism he describes is dispersal. *"What the CIA does is it takes each line item and it hides it all over the federal budget. It might be in the Department of Agriculture, the Department of Defense, State Department, you know, the EPA — they're all over the place."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="55:24" />

Because the concealment requires an innocuous-sounding cover programme for each hidden item, the disguises can read as waste. Kiriakou's example is a line item of *"$12 million to study gay monkeys in Guatemala"* — a programme that did not exist, standing in for $12 million of agency money embedded in another department's accounts.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="55:55" /> The side effect was that Senator [William Proxmire](/wiki/william-proxmire) would target such items with his Golden Fleece Award, and the agency *"was just apoplectic every month."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="56:27" />

Kiriakou states the practice is unchanged: *"they're still lying about the budget, they're still embedding covert action programs all over government, they're still using USAID as the CIA beachhead at the State Department, and they're still using the American taxpayers' money to try to overthrow governments that they deem are not friendly enough to the United States."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="56:27" /> See [USAID as a CIA cover vehicle](/wiki/usaid-cia-front-operations).
