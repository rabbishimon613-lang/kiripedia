Attorney Bob Trout strongly advised John Kiriakou to accept a plea deal, stating, "If you were my own brother, I would beg you to take the deal." <Cite s="2026-05-25-john-kiriakou-podcast-cia-john-kiriakou-new-posdcast-2" t="40:09:00" />

## 'If you were my own brother' (Tegan Broadwater Pt 2)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recalls his attorney [Bob Trout](/wiki/bob-trout), a *"lovely southern gentleman"* and [Plato Cacheris](/wiki/plato-cacheris)'s partner, coming to his house to urge him to take the plea: *"If you were my own brother, I would beg you to take this deal."*<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="24:26" /> In a separate telling, Kiriakou describes Trout as one of the loveliest men he has ever met, known for his beautiful suits, who pulled him aside to deliver the same line.<Cite s="2026-04-12-matthew-cox-inside-true-crime" t="1:00:57" />
