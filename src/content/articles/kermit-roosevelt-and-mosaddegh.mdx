Asked whether the intelligence agencies were asleep at the switch and acting as promoters of American imperialism, Kiriakou said the questioner was exactly right, and offered his firsthand experience as support.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="47:16" />

## The record of misses

The CIA, which is supposed to be the best and the brightest, has in his account missed literally every major international development since the creation of the agency in 1947. His list runs from the building of the Berlin Wall to the fall of the Berlin Wall and everything in between; it includes the collapse of the Soviet Union and the collapse of the Shah's regime in Iran.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="47:16" /><Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="47:50" />

## The operation

Then he moved from failures of prediction to a failure of action. The Directorate of Operations, he said, completely destroyed democracy in Iran by overthrowing Prime Minister Mosaddegh on behalf of British Petroleum.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="47:50" />

The officer in charge of that operation for the CIA was Kermit Roosevelt, whom Kiriakou describes as Theodore Roosevelt's son. Roosevelt, he says, later called it the gravest mistake he had made in his life.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="47:50" /><Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="48:20" />

## The analytic failures

Kiriakou's larger point is that the operational failures are the ones everybody knows about, and the wrong ones to worry about most. He mentioned that his friend and attorney Bruce Fein — whom he calls one of the most important constitutional scholars in America — had been talking about writing a book on the CIA's analytic failures.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="48:20" />

Those failures, Kiriakou argued, have been more dangerous through history than the operational ones, because it is that analysis on which policymakers rely to make foreign policy. Frequently, he said, the CIA has been so wrong that the resulting foreign policy has been a disaster.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="48:56" />

The sequence matters to his conclusion. An agency that is bad at operations produces scandals; an agency that is bad at analysis produces wars, and does so through officials who were never told they were relying on anything uncertain.
