The **private prison system** is, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, an industry whose largest operator, GEO Group, is paid to keep every bed occupied and maximizes profit by cutting medication and food.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="12:06" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="12:37" /> He describes the fish inmates called *"sewer trout,"* served from boxes marked *"Alaskan cod, product of China"* and *"feed use only, not for human consumption"*; a dead rat found in the communal Kool-Aid; and an all-inmate email in which the warden apologized for accidentally serving dog food on taco night — the shame being, Kiriakou wrote, that no one noticed, because it tasted exactly like the daily ground beef.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="13:07" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="13:37" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="14:10" />

## Employment agencies for rural men (Tegan Broadwater Pt 2)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) cites former Baltimore detective Peter Moskos, author of *In Defense of Flogging*, who argues that American prisons are *"nothing more than employment agencies for otherwise unemployable rural white men"* — sited in farmland where the prison is the only industry.<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:17:35" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:18:08" /> The only qualification to be a guard, Kiriakou notes, is a GED and no felonies, and prisoners often ran mail call *"because the guard who was supposed to do mail call can't read."*<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:18:41" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:19:14" /> He adds that educational programs were phased out between the Carter and Reagan years, leaving only a GED course, and that the Bureau of Prisons has the largest budget in the Justice Department while recidivism tops 50%.<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:16:03" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:20:14" />
