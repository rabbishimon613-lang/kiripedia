The **Taliban embassy raid** was, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s telling, the middle-of-the-night raid on the last functioning Taliban embassy in the world, in Peshawar, Pakistan, after 9/11. Driving past it one night with Tommy McHale — a Port Authority detective on loan to the FBI — Kiriakou joked they should steal everything not bolted down, then cabled headquarters, got permission, and did exactly that at 2 a.m., filling three embassy vans with computers, phones and files.<Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="21:37" /><Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="22:43" /> Days later, McHale found a folder of telephone bills, in English, showing dozens of calls to Buffalo, Bethesda, Kansas City, Estes Park and other U.S. cities — calls that abruptly stopped on September 10 and slowly resumed on September 16.<Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="23:46" /> Kiriakou sent copies to the FBI as possible evidence of sleeper cells; two years later an agent told him they never opened the box, claiming they *"couldn't find a Pashto translator"* — though Kiriakou notes the bills were in English.<Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="24:18" /><Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="24:50" /> He pairs the story with an FBI agent pointing out a Manhattan storefront as *"the headquarters of Hezbollah in New York"* that was never raided.<Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="25:20" />

In a separate telling, Kiriakou dates the raid to about three weeks after the [Abu Zubaydah capture](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) and names Buffalo, Kansas City, and Boston among the U.S. cities the seized phone bills showed calls to.<Cite s="2023-01-20-useful-idiots-gop-intel-oversight" t="11:32" /><Cite s="2023-01-20-useful-idiots-gop-intel-oversight" t="12:34" /> He says he came to believe the FBI's failure to investigate the phone bills was a deliberate cover-up rather than incompetence, since cable traffic he later obtained via FOIA showed the bureau knew exactly what it had received: *"I think somebody made a decision that they're just not going to touch this."*<Cite s="2023-01-20-useful-idiots-gop-intel-oversight" t="16:12" />
