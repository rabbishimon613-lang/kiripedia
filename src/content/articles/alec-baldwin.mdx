**Alec Baldwin** is the actor with whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts a bitter feud. An Academy Award-winning writer had wanted to build an off-Broadway play around Kiriakou's case with Baldwin; at their first lunch in New York, Baldwin listened to Kiriakou's stories and said, *"I think you're full of shit,"* and the meeting ended with mutual curses and Kiriakou walking out.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="33:21" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="33:52" /><Cite s="2026-07-02-jack-neel" t="[32:18]" /> Baldwin later warmed to the pitch and attached himself to a TV project based on Kiriakou's story that had been picked up by AMC — but then dropped out to take an HBO role playing a CIA director, killing the deal, without a call to say he was leaving.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="35:25" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="36:28" /><Cite s="2026-07-02-jack-neel" t="[33:52]" /> A final phone exchange of *"fuck you"* followed.<Cite s="2026-07-02-jack-neel" t="[33:52]" /> After Baldwin's arrest over the *Rust* shooting, Kiriakou declined to console him: *"He killed that woman. Somebody's got to pay for this. Even if it was an accident, the buck has to stop somewhere."*<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="38:03" /><Cite s="2026-07-02-jack-neel" t="[35:23]" />
