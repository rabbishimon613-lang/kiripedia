**Alec Baldwin** is the actor with whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts a bitter feud. An Academy Award-winning writer had wanted to build an off-Broadway play around Kiriakou's case with Baldwin; at their first lunch in New York, Baldwin listened to Kiriakou's stories and said, *"I think you're full of shit,"* and the meeting ended with mutual curses and Kiriakou walking out.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="33:21" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="33:52" /> Baldwin later warmed to the pitch and attached himself, but dropped out without a call to take an HBO role, prompting a final phone exchange of *"fuck you."*<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="35:25" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="36:28" /> After Baldwin's arrest over the *Rust* shooting, Kiriakou declined to console him: *"He killed that woman. Somebody's got to pay for this."*<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="38:03" />
