**Radio and TV Martí** are U.S. government broadcasters created to beam anti-communist programming at Cuba. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) rode the plane that transmits Radio Martí from international airspace in 2011 while with the [Senate Foreign Relations Committee](/wiki/senate-foreign-relations-committee), and says its content is mostly Spanish-language baseball, which Cubans tune in for while ignoring the jammed, propaganda-heavy TV Martí.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="1:06:20" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="1:06:52" /> Because a sliver of Florida's southwest coast could pick up the Dish Network feed of TV Martí, the Obama administration — rather than change the frequency — made it legal to propagandize Americans in the National Defense Authorization Act of 2016, which Kiriakou says also reopened the door to [recruiting American journalists](/wiki/operation-mockingbird).<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="1:07:52" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="1:08:23" /> Kiriakou visited Cuba itself years later; see [Kiriakou's Cuba trip](/wiki/kiriakou-cuba-trip).
