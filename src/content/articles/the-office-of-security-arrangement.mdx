The **Office of Security arrangement** is John Kiriakou's account of a disclosure made at a custody hearing in January 2020 concerning his second wife, herself a senior CIA officer.

## The 2007 interview

Kiriakou has consistently described his wife as having accompanied him to the ABC News interview of December 2007 and sat just off camera. He has said that when the interview ended he asked her how he had done, and whether he had said anything classified; she told him he had done great and that he had not. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="54:52" />

## The hearing

By Kiriakou's account, in January 2020 — the marriage having ended and a custody dispute being under way — he was on the stand and his lawyer asked him whether she had been a good wife. He answered that she had been an amazing wife, that she had been so supportive when he blew the whistle on the torture programme, and told the story of her sitting off camera. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="55:25" />

She then took the stand. Her lawyer asked whether it was true that she had gone with him to ABC News for the whistleblowing interview. She said yes. Asked why she had done it, she answered that she had been instructed to do so by the CIA's Office of Security, and that she had continued to report back to them on his activities. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="55:55" />

## Kiriakou's reaction

Kiriakou has spoken about the disclosure in terms of trust rather than tradecraft. Asked in the same conversation whether he trusts anyone now, he answered that he does not, and that he cannot imagine ever marrying again. He has said that she was the love of his life, that he had never been in love with anybody as he was with her, and that the betrayal is something one cannot get past. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="56:29" />

He places the episode alongside that of the NSA whistleblower Thomas Drake, whose wife, also an NSA officer, was approached by the FBI on the day of Drake's arrest and told she was either with them or with him. In Kiriakou's account she answered that she was with them. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="1:28:08" /> Drake's charges were subsequently all dropped, which Kiriakou offers as the point: the marriage could not be recovered either way. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="1:28:41" />
