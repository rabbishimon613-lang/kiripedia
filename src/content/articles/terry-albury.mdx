**Terry Albury** was a special agent in the FBI's Minneapolis field office whom Kiriakou describes as *"a courageous FBI agent."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="23:20" /> Albury had filed a racial discrimination case against the Bureau, which Kiriakou calls *"notoriously racist"*, after being passed over for promotion despite being, in Kiriakou's assessment, more competitive than white agents promoted ahead of him.<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="23:51" />

An internal study of racism at the FBI was subsequently carried out. Albury photographed the document on his screen with his phone and sent an encrypted copy to [The Intercept](/wiki/the-intercept), where it was received by [Matthew Cole](/wiki/matthew-cole).<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="23:51" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="24:22" /> *"And Matthew Cole outed him to the FBI. And Terry Albury was arrested and charged with espionage and convicted and sent to prison."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="24:22" />

Kiriakou's response at the time was public. He tweeted at the publication directly: *"Hey Intercept, serious question. You guys aren't really that stupid, are you? You aren't really that bad at journalism, are you? You're really just a front for the FBI, right?"*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="24:22" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="24:54" /> He now counts Albury and [Reality Winner](/wiki/reality-winner) among his friends.<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="23:20" />
