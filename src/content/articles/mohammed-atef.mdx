**Mohammed Atef** was the actual number three in al-Qaeda's leadership — a position the CIA, in part because of confusion between two unrelated individuals using the kunya Abu Zubaydah, would later misattribute to [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah). Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou): *"The actual number three was Mohammed Atef. But we killed Mohammed Atef in October of 2001 in Tora Bora. As soon as we started hitting Tora Bora, we hit the house that Mohammed Atef was in, and it turned out that when the rocket came through the roof it blew up the kitchen table, and a shard of wood from the table hit him in the neck and killed him."*<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="1:57:45" /><Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="1:58:16" /> In a separate retelling, Kiriakou describes Atef taking cover under the kitchen table during the strike, with a splinter from the shattered table piercing his heart rather than his neck.<Cite s="2026-03-23-dead-drop-s1e20-changes" t="13:26" />

## How he died

Atef held al-Qaeda's third position until October, when the CIA bombed the house he was hiding in at [Tora Bora](/wiki/tora-bora). Kiriakou's account of the manner of his death is specific: the blast splintered the dining room table, and a chunk of wood went through his neck and killed him.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="17:07" /> The vacancy that left is why the CIA was prepared to believe that [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah), taken the following spring, was the new number three — an inference Kiriakou notes would have made him only a few months old in the job, and therefore no use as a source on the planning of the September 11 attacks.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="17:07" /><Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="17:37" />
