**Kissinger and the petrodollar** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s endorsement of the theory that Henry Kissinger restructured the 1970s petrodollar system to make the United States reliant on Middle Eastern oil — creating a standing excuse for *"future world police."*<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="27:54" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="28:25" /> He illustrates it with a former CIA colleague turned oil-industry engineer: fracking is only profitable above 60 dollars a barrel, and with oil over 110, the U.S. does frack — but exports that oil to Japan while importing Middle Eastern crude, purely to maintain the tie.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="29:27" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="29:57" /> As much as he hates Kissinger — *"the butcher of Cyprus, of Chile, of Cambodia"* — Kiriakou says on this Kissinger *"was telling us the truth."*<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="30:30" />
