**Mormons in intelligence** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s observation that Mormons are heavily overrepresented in the CIA and FBI, and to a slightly lesser extent the NSA. The first reason, he says, is the polygraph: they *"don't swear, don't drink, don't smoke, don't gamble"* — having *"never done anything,"* they pass easily.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:39:18" /> More importantly, their missionary years at Brigham Young leave many fluent in *"funky languages that nobody else speaks"* — Uzbek, Tajik, Pashto and various African languages — so they arrive already able to be sent operationally overseas.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:39:49" />
