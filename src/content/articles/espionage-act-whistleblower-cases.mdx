## Brennan's Nixonian obsession

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says the Obama administration prosecuted three times as many whistleblowers under the [Espionage Act](/wiki/espionage-act-whistleblower-cases) as all previous presidents combined because [John Brennan](/wiki/john-brennan) instilled in Obama a *"Nixonian obsession"* with leaks and drove the cases with the full force of the Justice Department.<Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="54:13" /><Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="54:44" />
