**Robert "Bob" Baer** is a former CIA case officer. Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou): *"Bob Baer is a pretty famous former CIA officer. He's written a bunch of books — one was made into the movie Syriana that starred George Clooney."*<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:17:10" /> Kiriakou also confirms Baer worked with him in Kurdistan and calls him a good friend who was helpful with one of his own books.<Cite s="2019-06-19-soundwaves-2000" t="10:03" />

## The northern Iraq Saddam-assassination episode

Per Kiriakou, during the Clinton administration Baer *"took it on the chin"* after developing *"a network of sources in northern Iraq and was making plans to assassinate [Saddam Hussein](/wiki/saddam-hussein), which he believed to be in the national interest."* NSA intercepted one of his communications and forwarded it to [Sandy Berger](/wiki/sandy-berger), the National Security Adviser, who called the CIA: *"Your guy is going to do an extra-constitutional assassination. Either you pull him back, or he's under arrest for conspiracy."* The CIA pulled Baer out of Iraq.<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:17:42" /><Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:18:12" />

Kiriakou: *"That would have changed history."*<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="2:18:12" />

## On the Strait of Hormuz — Baer's 25-year-old forecast

Asked about Baer's writing on Iran's ability to covertly retaliate against the West and shut down the [Strait of Hormuz](/wiki/strait-of-hormuz-closure-threat), Kiriakou confirms the memory is correct — Baer wrote about exactly that some 25 years ago, and Kiriakou says he was right then and remains right today: Iran can shut the strait, and while it cannot win a sustained bombing campaign, it can inflict real long-term economic harm on the West by closing it.<Cite s="2026-03-01-deprogram-w-ted-rall" t="19:44" /><Cite s="2026-03-01-deprogram-w-ted-rall" t="20:15" />
