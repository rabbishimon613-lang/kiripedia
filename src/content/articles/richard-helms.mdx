**Richard Helms** was a director of the [CIA](/wiki/cia). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) cites him as the figure responsible for the destruction of the [MK-Ultra](/wiki/mk-ultra) record.

## Destroying the MK-Ultra files

Per [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), during the [Church Committee](/wiki/church-committee) era [Senator Frank Church](/wiki/church-committee) explicitly told Helms not to destroy the [MK-Ultra](/wiki/mk-ultra) documents. Helms returned to [CIA](/wiki/cia) headquarters from that meeting and ordered everything destroyed: *"Senator Church told him, 'Do not destroy the documents.' And he went back to headquarters from that meeting and said, 'Destroy all the documents.'"*<Cite s="2025-11-19-julian-dorey-vault-7" t="37:58" />

Helms was held in contempt of Congress for the destruction. The fine was roughly $100 — small enough, Kiriakou says, that *"everybody was chipping in a buck, two bucks in the hallway to pay the director's congressional fine."*<Cite s="2025-11-19-julian-dorey-vault-7" t="37:58" />

## What survived

Because of the destruction order, only about fifteen percent of the [MK-Ultra](/wiki/mk-ultra) record survives — a fraction that escaped destruction only because it had been misfiled in a separate financial-records warehouse. Everything publicly known about MK-Ultra, Kiriakou says, comes from this remnant, which he characterized as *"a sanitized version."*<Cite s="2025-11-19-julian-dorey-vault-7" t="37:28" />
