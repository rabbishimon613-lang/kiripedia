**The second plane** is John Kiriakou's account of the moment on 11 September 2001 when he understood that the United States was under attack.

## The errand

Kiriakou was due at the White House that morning with Cofer Black for a meeting with Condoleezza Rice. He received a call telling him their car was ready and walked over to Black's office to say it was time to go. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="22:46" />

Black's secretary had a television on her desk — a novelty at the time, in Kiriakou's telling — and one of the towers of the World Trade Center was burning. Kiriakou asked what had happened, and was told that a plane had flown into it. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="22:46" />

## Mid-sentence

His reply, which he prefaces by calling himself an idiot, was that this had happened once before — that in the 1930s a plane had flown into the Empire State Building, but that it had been rainy and foggy that day, and that on so clear a morning as this one it was hard to see how a pilot could fail to see the World Trade Center. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="23:16" />

Just as he said it, the second plane hit. The secretary turned to him and asked: *"Did you see that? Or did I imagine it?"* <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="23:16" />

Kiriakou ran back to his own office and told his colleagues that two planes had struck both towers and that they were under attack. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="23:46" /> What followed, in the Counterterrorism Center's front office, was the scene culminating in Cofer Black's speech from his desk. <Cite s="2021-09-25-abe-lincoln-s-top-hat-episode-572-blowing-the-whistle-with-john-kir" t="24:17" />
