**China as economic threat** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s framing that China endangers the West economically rather than militarily. Flush with cash, it has *"essentially been able to buy friends all around the world"* while the U.S. spends a trillion dollars a year on weapons and cannot build nice roads, bridges or bullet trains.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="30:13" /><Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="30:46" /> He quotes a Congolese diplomat: *"You promise us democracy. The Chinese promise us food. You can't eat democracy."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="31:19" /> Militarily, he notes, China has one aircraft carrier to America's 24 and one foreign base (in Djibouti), and does not invade its neighbors as the U.S. does — echoing an Onion headline he found true: *"China content to sit back and watch US destroy itself."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="31:52" /><Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="32:23" />
