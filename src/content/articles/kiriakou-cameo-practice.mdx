The **Kiriakou cameo practice** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s use of the Cameo personalised-video service to send messages to paying strangers, at a volume he describes as *"probably 25 or 30 hours a week"* — 900 messages in March 2026 alone and, on his own July 2026 count, *"about 1,950 cameos so far."*<Cite s="2026-07-08-covert-strategies-revealed-covert-strategies-revealed" t="1:07:18" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-covert-strategies-revealed-covert-strategies-revealed" t="1:07:50" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-covert-strategies-revealed-covert-strategies-revealed" t="1:08:22" />

He estimates that about half of the requests are birthday wishes, roughly ten percent are pep talks, and that of the pep talks *"three have come back to say that I somehow talked them out of committing suicide. I'm not really sure how I did that. But, you know, there are a lot of really hurting people out there."*<Cite s="2026-07-08-covert-strategies-revealed-covert-strategies-revealed" t="1:08:22" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-covert-strategies-revealed-covert-strategies-revealed" t="1:08:52" />

## The fifteen-year-old bulimic girl

One pep-talk recipient sounded, in Kiriakou's account, in such acute pain that he violated Cameo's rules on the recorded message itself: *"We're not supposed to do this, but here's my phone number — call me."* She called him within the hour, crying: fifteen years old, bulimic, parents who did not understand her, planning to kill herself. He talked her out of it. She has called him twice since, *"usually for less than a minute or two — very respectful,"* just to hear a friendly voice on a down day.<Cite s="2026-07-08-covert-strategies-revealed-covert-strategies-revealed" t="1:08:52" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-covert-strategies-revealed-covert-strategies-revealed" t="1:09:23" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-covert-strategies-revealed-covert-strategies-revealed" t="1:09:54" />
