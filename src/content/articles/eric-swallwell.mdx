John Kiriakou names Eric Swalwell only once in the corpus, in a passing aside contrasting the partisan Congress of today with the 1975 Church and Pike Committees: he says the only participants from the other party on a House "weaponization" committee were Swalwell and Cheney. <Cite s="2025-08-21-eric-hunley-john-kiriakou-cia-whistleblower-exposes-the-deep-state" t="51:58" /> In the same conversation, Kiriakou notes that a proposed agreement for [9-month work visas for Mexican workers](/wiki/mexican-9-month-work-visas) was never implemented because of the September 11 attacks.
