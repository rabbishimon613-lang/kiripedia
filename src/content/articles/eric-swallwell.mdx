The FBI was ready to release a 2015 file on Eric Swallwell ahead of his May 2 gubernatorial campaign in California. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="17:38:00" /> That file was a collection of [FBI](/wiki/fbi) documents concerning Swallwell's relationship with Christine Fang. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="17:08" />

Attorney General [Pam Bondi](/wiki/pam-bondi)'s dismissal was linked to allegedly tipping off Swallwell, with Trump believing she had informed him of an upcoming announcement. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="16:37:00" />
