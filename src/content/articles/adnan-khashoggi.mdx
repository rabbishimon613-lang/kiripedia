**Adnan Khashoggi** was a Saudi arms dealer and a middleman in the [Iran-Contra](/wiki/iran-contra) affair. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) uses him as a benchmark for what the CIA notices: Khashoggi was mentioned *"all the time"* at the agency and was *"extremely public"* throughout his life — whereas Kiriakou says he *never* heard the name [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) mentioned at the CIA, which he takes as telling about how Epstein operated.<Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="19:34" /><Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="20:05" />
