**Felix Rodriguez** is a Cuban-American former [Central Intelligence Agency](/wiki/cia) officer involved in the Bay of Pigs invasion and the Vietnam War, reported to have been present at the 1967 killing of Che Guevara in Bolivia. He is a distinct figure from [Jose Rodriguez](/wiki/jose-rodriguez), the post-9/11 CIA official who ordered the destruction of the agency's waterboarding tapes.

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts that podcast host Danny Jones and his co-host Steven interviewed Rodriguez in Miami; Kiriakou notes that the trip followed an appearance by Danny Sheehan, who told Jones he had deposed Rodriguez and alleged Rodriguez had been "on the team that killed the president" — an allegation Kiriakou attributes to Sheehan rather than endorsing himself.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="2:05:27" /><Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="2:06:29" />

## Che Guevara and denial of souvenir-taking

Asked directly whether he had kept Che Guevara's Rolex as a souvenir, Rodriguez denied it: *"No, no, no, no. I never kept his ro[lex]."*<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="2:07:00" />

## The Havana CIA museum

[Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he visited the CIA museum in Havana, which displays items confiscated from Rodriguez and other Bay of Pigs fighters; the museum is housed in a mansion across the street from the Tunisian embassy and holds CIA identification, weapons, and related material from the era.<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="2:09:04" />
