The **Geneva Conventions** govern the treatment of prisoners in war — and supply the script for one of [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s defining confrontations.

## "Take the hoods off"

In Pakistan, encountering prisoners with their heads covered, Kiriakou asked why. The answer — *"we don't want them to see our faces"* — set him off: *"Are you seriously telling me that you have never read the Geneva Convention? It is a war crime to put hoods on them. Take the hoods off."* The officer turned to his own men: don't take the hoods off — and threatened to report Kiriakou to headquarters. *"I said, oh, I'm already reporting you to headquarters for committing a war crime."*<Cite s="2019-06-27-the-bitter-truth-with-abe-the-bitter-truth-with-abe" t="21:16" /><Cite s="2023-04-12-danny-jones" t="2:59:20" /><Cite s="2024-12-21-not-a-grayman" t="2:36:15" /> They reported each other. Kiriakou got in trouble: *"They didn't give two shits about the Geneva Convention."*<Cite s="2025-03-29-lionel-nation" t="22:34" />

## The lawyers' dodge

Kiriakou lays out the Bush administration's twin arguments: that al-Qaeda fighters, being non-state actors representing no country, were somehow not entitled to the Conventions' protections — and that the techniques weren't torture anyway, merely [enhanced interrogation](/wiki/enhanced-interrogation).<Cite s="2016-04-25-fort-collins-community-act-john-kiriakou-cia-whistle-blower-part-1" t="36:11" /> He describes the plan bluntly: roughly twenty people in the administration deciding to violate the Geneva Conventions and do *"what the United States executed Japanese soldiers for doing."*<Cite s="2013-05-31-thom-hartmann-program-a-letter-from-john-kiriakou" t="00:32" /> His broader question stands unanswered: how does a government justify ignoring international treaties to which it is a signatory?<Cite s="2015-05-09-awakeradio1-awakeradio1" t="22:02" />

## Invoked, and punished

Kiriakou's own use of the conventions produced the inversion he returns to. On the night of the [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) raid he told a colleague who had hooded a group of detainees that he could not seriously never have read the Geneva Convention, that hooding them is a war crime, and to take the hoods off. Each man reported the other to headquarters. It was Kiriakou who was reprimanded.<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="16:30" /><Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="17:00" />

He accepted the summary that his interviewer drew from it — that in practice *"war crimes aren't illegal, but reporting on them is."* *"That is the sad sorry state that we find ourselves in."*<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="18:31" />
