The **Greek Intelligence Service** is Greece's national intelligence agency, officially the National Intelligence Service (EYP), with which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) worked closely during his posting to Athens.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:17:20" /> He recounts a joint operation on the island of Aegina reached by hydrofoil; arriving six hours early, he visited the house and tomb of [Saint Nectarios of Aegina](/wiki/st-nectarios-of-aegina) with the service's religious head of surveillance.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:18:52" /> When Kiriakou, lacking small change, dropped a 5,000-drachma note (about 20 dollars) as a candle offering and crossed himself, the surveillance chief was watching. Kiriakou later learned the man had ordered the service to keep its hands off him and put no surveillance on him, because he was *"a good Christian boy"* who meant them no harm.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:19:22" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:19:55" />

## The Steven Lawless case

Kiriakou has discussed the case of Steven Lawless, an American with a top-secret security clearance who was recruited as a spy by the Greek Intelligence Service in a provocative operation run by Greece's conservative government in the mid-1990s. Kiriakou says he did not personally know Lawless, but the case was significant enough within the service that Lawless's handler reported directly to the director of the service rather than through the normal chain of command, because the service feared the CIA had recruited a mole inside its own ranks and worried a leak could expose the operation.<Cite s="2026-06-28-deep-focus-global-power-struggles" t="22:31" />

Lawless was eventually caught and served around seven years in prison. On release, he was given Greek citizenship and moved to Greece. Kiriakou, asked about the case after Lawless began publicly complaining in the Greek press about poverty, expressed no sympathy: *"Yeah, maybe you shouldn't have been a spy against your country."*<Cite s="2026-06-28-deep-focus-global-power-struggles" t="23:31" />
