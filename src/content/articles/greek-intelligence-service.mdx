The **Greek Intelligence Service** is Greece's national intelligence agency, with which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) worked closely during his posting to Athens.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:17:20" /> He recounts a joint operation on the island of Aegina reached by hydrofoil; arriving six hours early, he visited the house and tomb of [Saint Nectarios of Aegina](/wiki/st-nectarios-of-aegina) with the service's religious head of surveillance.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:18:52" /> When Kiriakou, lacking small change, dropped a 5,000-drachma note (about 20 dollars) as a candle offering and crossed himself, the surveillance chief was watching. Kiriakou later learned the man had ordered the service to keep its hands off him and put no surveillance on him, because he was *"a good Christian boy"* who meant them no harm.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:19:22" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:19:55" />
