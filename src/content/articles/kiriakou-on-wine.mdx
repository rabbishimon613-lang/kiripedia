Wine is one of the few private enthusiasms [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) discusses in any detail. He has subscribed to *Wine Spectator* for around twenty-five years, and he and his wife built a climate-controlled cellar into their house when it was built.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="40:03" /><Cite s="2026-07-05-the-bad-news-program" t="55:34" />

The knowledge has mostly surfaced in his accounts as a detector of pretension. The Christmas 2009 present [John Kerry](/wiki/john-kerry) gave his Senate Foreign Relations Committee staff was a bottle of wine Kiriakou did not recognise; he looked it up and found it cost ten dollars, from a man he describes as worth close to a billion.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="40:03" /> On another occasion a host thanked for the wine at his own table claimed it was his favourite and that he kept it in his own cellar, which Kiriakou did not believe for a moment.<Cite s="2026-07-05-the-bad-news-program" t="55:34" />
