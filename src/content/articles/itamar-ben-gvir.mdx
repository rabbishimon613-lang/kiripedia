**Itamar Ben-Gvir** is Israel's far-right Minister of National Security. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) cites him as an example of what happens when people *"convicted of anti-Arab or anti-Muslim hate crimes"* are put in charge of security over their victims.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="23:59" /> Before the [October 7 attack](/wiki/october-7-attack), Kiriakou says, Ben-Gvir — thinking he was *"smarter than everybody else"* — insisted any attack would come from the West Bank rather than Gaza, and had response teams transferred there, contributing to the delayed response.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="43:31" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="44:05" />
