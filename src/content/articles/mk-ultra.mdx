**MK-Ultra** was the [Central Intelligence Agency](/wiki/cia)'s umbrella program of mind-control, behavioral-modification, and biological-warfare experimentation, conducted under various subprojects from 1952 to 1975. The program is described by [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) as the institutional response to a Chinese intelligence operation that successfully planted disinformation inside the CIA suggesting the Soviet KGB was developing mind-control techniques the United States needed to match.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:10:37" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:11:07" />

## Origin

In Kiriakou's account the originating impulse for MK-Ultra was disinformation: *"The Chinese were successful in planting disinformation in the CIA that the Russians were developing the same technology. So we were like, 'Oh my god, we recruited this Chinese intelligence guy and he said the KGB is already doing it. We have to catch up.' The KGB wasn't doing anything. They had never heard of this stuff."* The 1959 publication of the novel *The Manchurian Candidate*, and its 1962 film adaptation, then *"freaked everybody out in the mid-50s"* and accelerated the program.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:10:37" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:11:07" />

## Subprojects

The program encompassed approximately six named subprojects, including MK-Chickwit and MK-Seesaw. Practices documented across these subprojects included:

- **LSD experimentation on unwitting subjects.** The agency rented brothels (so-called *"safe houses"*) in which prostitutes were instructed to dose clients with LSD and CIA officers observed the results through one-way mirrors — *"see if we can control their minds. These are American citizens."*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:11:38" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:12:42" />
- **Aerosol pathogen release in San Francisco.** *"We created a germ in a lab and we just blew it into the atmosphere in San Francisco just to see if anybody would get sick. And lots of people got sick. 11 of them were sick enough that they happened to need hospitalization."* The release was deliberately performed in San Francisco because the city's heavy fog would keep the pathogen close to ground level where it would be inhaled.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:12:10" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:12:42" />
- **Remote viewing and ESP research.** *"Astral projection and moving items with your mind"* — investigated extensively in the 1950s within the same budgetary frame.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:10:05" />

## The Haight-Ashbury Free Clinic connection

A specific institutional outgrowth of MK-Ultra's LSD-experimentation program was the **Haight-Ashbury Free Clinic** in San Francisco, which — per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s reading of Tom O'Neill's investigative book *Chaos* — was operated by the [Central Intelligence Agency](/wiki/cia) as a covert experimental site. The clinic's tenure ended approximately one month after the publication of *Chaos* in 2019.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="14:41" />

## The Manson connection

Per the same source-of-record, the agency's San Francisco LSD operations were *"totally connected to Manson"* — Charles Manson and his commune having been participants in (or subjects of) experimental dosing under MK-Ultra sub-projects. Kiriakou's framing: *"It's so nuts. And they wouldn't have even known about that until they found a stash of documents that connected it all together."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="14:41" />

## The Pont-Saint-Esprit bakery incident

Among the most-cited MK-Ultra–era pathogen releases is the case of the French village of Pont-Saint-Esprit, where in 1951 — per the long-circulating allegation — the CIA dosed the local bread supply with LSD, producing a village-wide outbreak of psychosis in which several people died. *"We've all read the stories about this bakery in France where apparently we dosed the bread and everybody in the village went nuts."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="13:06" />

## Destruction of records

In 1975 the U.S. Senate Select Committee on Intelligence (the Church Committee), under Senator Frank Church, discovered MK-Ultra's existence and ordered the CIA not to destroy program documentation. *"And so they immediately destroyed the documents."* Approximately 20% of the documentation had been misplaced and was found later — the totality of present public knowledge about MK-Ultra is therefore derived from that 20%. *"And then they were like, 'Yeah, well, they're destroyed. What are you going to do about it?'"*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:13:12" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:13:43" />

## Kiriakou's frame: MK-Ultra and Vault 7

Kiriakou's recurring framing of MK-Ultra is as a marker for the discontinuity between the agency's twentieth-century capabilities and its contemporary technical reach as documented in the 2017 [Vault 7](/wiki/vault-7) WikiLeaks disclosure: *"Couple what the CIA was trying to do in the '50s with MK-Ultra and its subcomponents compared to what it is technologically able to do today. And it is terrifying."*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:16:52" />

## Organizational structure

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described MK-Ultra as having offices staffed entirely by operational psychiatrists and psychologists — not researchers in the academic sense but practitioners who put their findings directly into use on [CIA](/wiki/cia) operations.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:10:00" />

## Documented experiments

Kiriakou described one experiment in which a subject was hypnotized and told to hold his arm in the air. The subject held the arm raised for two hours. When brought out of the hypnotic state, he immediately vomited. Kiriakou presented this as an example of the level of behavioral control the program was attempting to develop and document.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:10:30" />

MK-Ultra had named sub-operations. Kiriakou cited MK-Chickwit and noted there were five or six distinct sub-programs operating under the umbrella.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:11:00" />

## Deaths

People died as a result of MK-Ultra experiments. Kiriakou described subjects jumping out of windows and off bridges. He also cited the death of James Forrestal — the first U.S. Secretary of Defense — as connected to the program, though he presented this as a matter of historical debate rather than established fact.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:11:30" />

## Cancer weapons and document destruction

Kiriakou stated that discussions within MK-Ultra included the development of cancer-inducing weapons. [CIA](/wiki/cia) Director Richard Helms ordered all MK-Ultra documents destroyed before they could be subpoenaed. A significant cache survived because it had been misfiled in a financial records warehouse and was not found until a FOIA request decades later.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:12:00" /><Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:12:30" />

## *"Conspiracy theory"* as a [CIA](/wiki/cia) invention

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) stated that the term *"conspiracy theory"* was invented by a former [CIA](/wiki/cia) director as a deliberate tool to discredit people who raised uncomfortable truths — by making them sound like *"crazy people."* Kiriakou presented MK-Ultra as the proof that so-called conspiracy theories are sometimes accurate: it was real, it was documented, and it was exactly the kind of program the agency dismissed as fantasy before it was exposed.<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:04:00" />

## LSD program origin — Chinese disinformation

The [CIA](/wiki/cia)'s LSD program under MK-Ultra was triggered by a deliberate disinformation operation, per Kiriakou. In approximately 1951, the Chinese told the CIA that the Russians were using LSD to develop a mind-control drug. This was false — the Chinese had the LSD program; Russia had none. The CIA panicked and by 1952 had launched MK-Ultra, beginning with LSD experiments.<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:05:30" />

## San Francisco biological test

The [CIA](/wiki/cia) waited for an unusually heavy fog day in San Francisco and released a virus into the atmosphere to test whether it would cause illness. Eleven people subsequently went to emergency rooms with a rare upper respiratory infection. Kiriakou described the internal reaction as high-fives: *"Yeah, it works."*<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:05:00" />

## Employee dosing and suicides

The first phase of LSD experiments targeted [CIA](/wiki/cia) employees, dosed without their knowledge. Several committed suicide. One jumped from a hotel window. The program then shifted to using unwitting members of the public — including the San Francisco safe-house phase known as [Operation Midnight Climax](/wiki/operation-midnight-climax).<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:06:00" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:06:30" />

## Richard Helms — document destruction and contempt of Congress

[CIA](/wiki/cia) Director Richard Helms ordered the destruction of all MK-Ultra files on the same day he testified before the [Church Committee](/wiki/church-committee), which had explicitly ordered him not to destroy any documents. He returned to the CIA that afternoon and ordered everything destroyed. He was held in contempt of Congress and fined approximately $150. Approximately fifteen percent of the documents had been misfiled in a separate location and survived; what is known publicly about MK-Ultra comes from this remnant. Kiriakou described everything known as *"a sanitized version."*<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:07:30" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:08:00" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:08:30" />

## Jason Jones deep dive — from bakeries to bio-spray

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes [MK-Ultra](/wiki/mk-ultra) as a very broad program running from 1952 to 1975 — ESP, [astral projection](/wiki/project-gateway), LSD, mind reading — launched on a fabricated claim that the Soviets were pursuing such research.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:57:47" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:58:18" /> He says the CIA dosed its own employees unwittingly, made San Francisco the headquarters of the experiments, sub-programs including the theft of the LSD formula from Switzerland and the [French village bread experiment](/wiki/french-village-lsd-bread-experiment), and even sprayed a germ into the San Francisco fog to test dispersal, sickening eleven people.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:59:20" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:00:23" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:01:28" /> Only 15% of the records survive; the rest were destroyed.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:04:01" />

## Truth Hurts Show retelling

In this account [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) again traces [MK-Ultra](/wiki/mk-ultra) (1952–1975) to a fabricator's claim that the Russians were doing mind-control and ESP research, describes dosing CIA employees with LSD until they *"start jumping out of windows,"* the San Francisco prostitute safe house, and a germ released on a foggy day that sent 11 people to emergency rooms. He says the director destroyed 80% of the records after being ordered to preserve them.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:31:17" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:32:20" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:33:23" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:33:56" />

## Manson rumors and the naming (Jack Neel)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says [MK-Ultra](/wiki/mk-ultra) ran experiments at universities across the country, especially in California, and that there are unprovable rumors Charles Manson and Sirhan Sirhan may have been involved — unprovable because the director destroyed about 85% of the records.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="22:30" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="23:00" /> He explains the name: the *"MK"* digraph marked a scientific-experimentation operation, and *"Ultra"* was simply picked at random by a computer.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="48:33" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="49:04" />
