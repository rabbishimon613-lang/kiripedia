**MK-Ultra** was the [Central Intelligence Agency](/wiki/cia)'s umbrella program of mind-control, behavioral-modification, and biological-warfare experimentation, conducted under various subprojects from 1952 to 1975. Kiriakou describes it as a very broad program encompassing ESP, [astral projection](/wiki/project-gateway), LSD experimentation, and mind reading, run out of offices staffed entirely by operational psychiatrists and psychologists — practitioners who put their findings directly into use on CIA operations, not academic researchers.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:57:47" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:58:18" /><Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:10:00" />

## Origin

In Kiriakou's account the originating impulse for MK-Ultra was Chinese disinformation. In approximately 1951, the Chinese told the CIA that the Soviets were using LSD to develop a mind-control drug: *"The Chinese were successful in planting disinformation in the CIA that the Russians were developing the same technology. So we were like, 'Oh my god, we recruited this Chinese intelligence guy and he said the KGB is already doing it. We have to catch up.' The KGB wasn't doing anything. They had never heard of this stuff."* The claim was false — it was in fact the Chinese, not the Russians, who had an LSD program. The CIA panicked, and by 1952 had launched MK-Ultra, beginning with LSD experiments.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:10:37" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:11:07" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:05:30" /> The 1959 publication of the novel *The Manchurian Candidate*, and its 1962 film adaptation, *"freaked everybody out in the mid-50s"* and accelerated the program.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:10:37" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:11:07" />

Kiriakou has also cited MK-Ultra as proof that so-called "conspiracy theories" are sometimes accurate. He stated that the term itself was invented by a former CIA director as a deliberate tool to discredit people who raised uncomfortable truths by making them sound like *"crazy people"* — and presented MK-Ultra as a program that was real, documented, and exactly the kind the agency had dismissed as fantasy before it was exposed.<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:04:00" />

## LSD experimentation

### Unwitting subjects and CIA-run brothels

The agency rented brothels — so-called *"safe houses"* — in which prostitutes were instructed to dose clients with LSD while CIA officers observed the results through one-way mirrors, in what Kiriakou described as an explicit effort to *"see if we can control their minds. These are American citizens."* The San Francisco safe-house phase later became known as [Operation Midnight Climax](/wiki/operation-midnight-climax).<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:11:38" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:12:42" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:06:30" />

### Employee dosing and suicides

The first phase of the LSD experiments targeted CIA employees themselves, dosed without their knowledge. Several committed suicide; Kiriakou described subjects jumping out of windows and off bridges, including one who jumped from a hotel window. The program then shifted to unwitting members of the public.<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:06:00" /><Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:11:30" /> Kiriakou also cited the death of James Forrestal, the first U.S. Secretary of Defense, as connected to the program by some accounts, though he presented this as a matter of historical debate rather than established fact.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:11:30" />

### The Pont-Saint-Esprit bakery incident

Among the most-cited MK-Ultra–era pathogen and drug releases is the case of the French village of Pont-Saint-Esprit, where in 1951 — per the long-circulating allegation — the CIA dosed the local bread supply with LSD, producing a village-wide outbreak of psychosis in which several people died. *"We've all read the stories about this bakery in France where apparently we dosed the bread and everybody in the village went nuts."* Kiriakou elsewhere cites this as the [French village bread experiment](/wiki/french-village-lsd-bread-experiment), listing it alongside the theft of the LSD formula from Switzerland among the program's sub-projects.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="13:06" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:59:20" />

### The Haight-Ashbury Free Clinic and the Manson connection

A specific institutional outgrowth of MK-Ultra's LSD-experimentation program was the **Haight-Ashbury Free Clinic** in San Francisco, which — per Kiriakou's reading of Tom O'Neill's investigative book *Chaos* — was operated by the CIA as a covert experimental site. The clinic's tenure ended approximately one month after the publication of *Chaos* in 2019.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="14:41" /> Per the same source, the agency's San Francisco LSD operations were *"totally connected to Manson"* — Charles Manson and his commune having been participants in, or subjects of, experimental dosing under MK-Ultra sub-projects. Kiriakou's framing: *"It's so nuts. And they wouldn't have even known about that until they found a stash of documents that connected it all together."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="14:41" /> Separately, Kiriakou has said MK-Ultra ran experiments at universities across the country, especially in California, and that there are unprovable rumors that Manson and Sirhan Sirhan may have been involved — unprovable because most of the records were destroyed.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="22:30" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="23:00" />

## Biological-warfare experiments

### San Francisco aerosol test

The CIA waited for an unusually heavy fog day in San Francisco — chosen deliberately because the fog would keep a pathogen close to ground level, where it would be inhaled — and released a virus into the atmosphere to test whether it would cause illness: *"We created a germ in a lab and we just blew it into the atmosphere in San Francisco just to see if anybody would get sick. And lots of people got sick."* Eleven people went to emergency rooms with a rare respiratory infection serious enough to require hospitalization. Kiriakou described the internal reaction as high-fives: *"Yeah, it works."*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:12:10" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:12:42" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:05:00" />

### Cancer weapons and remote viewing

Kiriakou stated that discussions within MK-Ultra also included the development of cancer-inducing weapons.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:12:00" /> The program's ESP research extended to remote viewing — *"astral projection and moving items with your mind"* — investigated extensively in the 1950s within the same budgetary frame.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:10:05" />

## Behavioral-control experiments and subprojects

The program encompassed approximately six named subprojects, including MK-Chickwit and MK-Seesaw.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:11:38" /><Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:11:00" /> Kiriakou described one documented experiment in which a subject was hypnotized and told to hold his arm in the air; the subject held the arm raised for two hours, and when brought out of the hypnotic state, immediately vomited. Kiriakou presented this as an example of the level of behavioral control the program was attempting to develop and document.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:10:30" />

## Destruction of records

In 1975 the U.S. Senate Select Committee on Intelligence (the [Church Committee](/wiki/church-committee)), under Senator Frank Church, discovered MK-Ultra's existence and explicitly ordered the CIA not to destroy program documentation. CIA Director Richard Helms ordered the destruction of all MK-Ultra files that same day — on the afternoon after he had testified before the committee and been given that order. *"And so they immediately destroyed the documents."*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:13:12" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:07:30" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:08:00" /> Helms was held in contempt of Congress and fined approximately $150.<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:08:30" />

Kiriakou has given varying estimates of how much documentation survived: approximately 20%, having been misplaced in a separate location rather than destroyed;<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:13:43" /> approximately 15%, misfiled in a financial-records warehouse and not found until a FOIA request decades later;<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:12:00" /><Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:12:30" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:08:00" /> and, in a later account, that 80% of records were destroyed after Helms was ordered to preserve them, or that Manson/Sirhan rumors are unprovable because approximately 85% of the records were destroyed.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:33:56" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:04:01" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="22:30" /> In all versions, the totality of present public knowledge about MK-Ultra derives from the surviving remnant; Kiriakou has described the widely known account as *"a sanitized version."* *"And then they were like, 'Yeah, well, they're destroyed. What are you going to do about it?'"*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:13:43" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:08:30" />

Kiriakou explains the program's name as follows: the *"MK"* digraph marked a scientific-experimentation operation, and *"Ultra"* was picked at random — by a computer, in one telling.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="48:33" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="49:04" />

## Legacy and Kiriakou's framing

Kiriakou's recurring framing of MK-Ultra is as a marker for the discontinuity between the agency's twentieth-century capabilities and its contemporary technical reach, as documented in the 2017 [Vault 7](/wiki/vault-7) WikiLeaks disclosure: *"Couple what the CIA was trying to do in the '50s with MK-Ultra and its subcomponents compared to what it is technologically able to do today. And it is terrifying."*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:16:52" />
