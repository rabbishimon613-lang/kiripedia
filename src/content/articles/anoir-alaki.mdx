**Anwar al-Awlaki** was an American-born imam and later al-Qaeda propagandist whom John Kiriakou says he personally met years before 9/11, during an Arabic-immersion class field trip to al-Awlaki's mosque in Falls Church, Virginia. <Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:26:08" /> Kiriakou says al-Awlaki was born in New Mexico to a Yemeni father who had served as Yemen's ambassador to the United States, spoke unaccented American English, and self-radicalized during the Soviet occupation of Afghanistan before joining al-Qaeda around 1998 or 1999. <Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:26:39" />

Kiriakou says that years later, while working for the Senate Foreign Relations Committee, he visited an FBI legal attaché in Yemen who told him he could locate al-Awlaki "within three feet, 24 hours a day," and that al-Awlaki was killed by a drone strike a few months after that conversation. <Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:28:46" />

Kiriakou says al-Awlaki, an American citizen, was killed by US forces without ever being charged with a crime, facing his accusers, or standing before a jury of his peers, and that a week later his 16-year-old son and 16-year-old nephew — also American citizens who had never been accused of any crime — were killed as well. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="01:04:50" />
