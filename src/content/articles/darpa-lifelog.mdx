**DARPA LifeLog** was a Pentagon research program designed to track every email, photo, location and purchase a person made, all in one place. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) notes it was killed on February 4, 2004 — one day before Mark Zuckerberg filed the LLC for Facebook — and treats the timing as more than coincidence, observing that senior [CIA](/wiki/cia), FBI and NSA officials later went to work for Facebook, Instagram and their peers. He believes someone picked up what LifeLog dropped in order to monetize it. Kiriakou separately recounts the [Mark Zuckerberg–Angela Merkel meeting](/wiki/mark-zuckerberg-angela-merkel-meeting), in which Zuckerberg flew to Germany not long afterward to help define what would count as hate speech on the platform he had just filed.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="50:37" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="51:10" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="51:41" />
