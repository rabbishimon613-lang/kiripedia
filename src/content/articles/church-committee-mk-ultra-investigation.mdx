The Church Committee investigated MK Ultra in 1975, ordering the director of central intelligence to preserve documents, but many were destroyed nonetheless. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="01:14:21" />

## Destroying the evidence

Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), when the [Church Committee](/wiki/church-committee) ordered the CIA director not to destroy the [MK-Ultra](/wiki/mk-ultra) documents, the director went straight back to headquarters and had them shredded — destroying 85% of the records, leaving only 15% from which the program is known today.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:03:30" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:04:01" />
