## The prostitute dilemma

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) poses a hypothetical he uses at colleges: a proven, life-saving terrorist penetration demands you procure him a prostitute before he will give you more intelligence. Most say they would. But then the asset asks for a child prostitute — and, Kiriakou notes, no training class ever teaches where the line is. There is no rulebook at the other end of the cable; the officer must bring his own [moral](/wiki/moral-injury) principles and hold them.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:26:50" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:27:20" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:27:52" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:28:24" />

Kiriakou says he originated the hypothetical in a speech at American University, using it to illustrate that the CIA gives no training whatsoever in ethics, leaving officers to rely on their own moral values; when he puts the prostitute question to a crowd, he says, about 90% of hands go up in favor of providing one.<Cite s="2024-10-30-bulwarg" t="09:32" />

## Torturing prisoners at a JSOC black site

Kiriakou has written about a JSOC black site where some of the contractors working there encouraged his squad to torture prisoners; he told the privates under him, unambiguously, that they were not to participate.<Cite s="2024-11-16-the-team-house" t="2:33:16" />
