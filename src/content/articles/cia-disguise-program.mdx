The **CIA disguise program** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the agency's disguise and forgery specialists. On his first day he sat between two new hires: a woman recruited from the Falls Church Academy of Beauty to be trained as a master disguise maker, and a man, Sean, recruited to be a master forger because he had drawn a two-panel cartoon in every issue of his college newspaper.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="52:09" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="52:40" /> Twelve years later the disguise maker flew to Pakistan and spent six weeks building Kiriakou a bald-head mask, matching the color to his skin tone and inserting the hairs one at a time; clear-glass spectacles hid the single seam. The station chief and the ambassador, Kiriakou says, failed to recognize him until he pulled it off.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="53:12" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="53:42" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="54:12" />
