The **Government Accountability Project** is a whistleblower-advocacy organization that John Kiriakou says he met with just a couple of days after his own arrest, through its attorney Jesselyn Raddack. <Cite s="2025-09-30-deprogram-show-with-ted-rall-and-jamarl-thomas-deprogram-with-ted-ral" t="07:47" /> Kiriakou recounts telling Raddack he was not a whistleblower, only for her to insist that he was, explaining that the legal definition of whistleblowing turns on the act of bringing wrongdoing to light rather than the discloser's own motivation or self-image. <Cite s="2025-09-30-deprogram-show-with-ted-rall-and-jamarl-thomas-deprogram-with-ted-ral" t="07:47" />
