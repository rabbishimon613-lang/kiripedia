**Iran's 2024 drone retaliation** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of Iran's response to an Israeli strike on its diplomatic facility in Syria. He identifies the precursor as Israel's bombing of the Iranian Consulate in Damascus, which killed two Iranian generals — a major violation of international law, in his assessment.<Cite s="2024-08-12-danny-jones-loyal-officer-vs-dissident-spy" t="2:38:22" /> Iran launched hundreds of slow drones that took roughly six hours to reach Israeli airspace, warning both the Americans and the Jordanians in advance — Kiriakou says Iran gave Jordan's ambassador direct notice of the roughly 600 drones' flight path, which allowed Israeli, US and British jets to scramble.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="40:21" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="40:53" /><Cite s="2024-08-12-danny-jones-loyal-officer-vs-dissident-spy" t="2:38:52" /> Of the roughly 600 drones, seven got through despite the Iron Dome, which Kiriakou says shows the system is less effective than advertised — a particular concern were any future payload chemical or biological.<Cite s="2024-08-12-danny-jones-loyal-officer-vs-dissident-spy" t="2:39:26" /> Kiriakou reads it three ways: Iran showed its public it had retaliated, avoided being drawn into a wider war, and — most importantly — learned that the Iron Dome is not flawless, since about seven cheap drones *"you can buy at a hobby shop"* got through, an *"incredible intelligence collection lesson."*<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="41:26" />

## Diego Garcia — a range test, not an attack

In a later assessment of Iranian capability, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says Iran fired two stripped-down, payload-less rockets at Diego Garcia — a rocket that normally cannot reach that far, sent with no warhead, *"just an engine and a cone,"* purely to see how far it could travel. Both were intercepted and shot down.<Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="08:28" />

## Supersonic missiles and the Qatar strike (Austin and Matt)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) adds detail to Iran's [retaliation](/wiki/iran-2024-drone-retaliation): after the telegraphed drone wave, Iran launched *supersonic* missiles — which, unlike America's still-in-testing hypersonics, several got through Israeli defenses.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="14:30" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="15:32" /> When Israel then bombed Qatar and killed Hamas's chief negotiator, Kiriakou notes Qatar is a *"major non-NATO ally"* the U.S. is treaty-bound to defend — so Washington could only give Israel *"a stern talking-to"* and *"look like fools."*<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="16:03" />
