In 1981, at the age of seventeen, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) saw an object he has never been able to explain. His parents had bought a restaurant in Sharon, Pennsylvania, about fifteen or twenty miles from the family home in New Castle, and he and his father worked the eleven-to-seven shift there on Friday nights. The drive ran through Amish country, which — the Amish using no electricity — is very dark.<Cite s="2025-03-26-podcast-ufo-live-shows" t="11:26" /><Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="28:19" />

## The sighting

Driving north around half past ten, they saw a brilliant flash of white light, then a second, then a third. As his father asked what it was, an orange trapezoid lit up — saucer-shaped, Kiriakou says, but with hard edges, with a round light in each of the four corners strong enough to illuminate the body of the craft. It hung silently, not very high: he estimates fifteen hundred to two thousand feet.<Cite s="2025-03-26-podcast-ufo-live-shows" t="11:57" /><Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="04:43" /><Cite s="2024-10-06-joecat" t="1:03:11" />

They pulled off the road and got out. A third man pulled up behind them, got out of his own car and asked the same question; Kiriakou's father said he did not know.<Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="05:14" /> Then the object left at what Kiriakou describes as a fantastic speed, unlike anything he had seen before or since, and vanished. They stood a while longer wondering whether it would come back.<Cite s="2025-02-28-epstein-bin-laden-china-israel" t="1:54:39" />

## Not reporting it

Back in the car, Kiriakou asked his father whether they should call somebody. The answer has become the fixed punchline of the story: *"And tell them what? We saw a flying saucer and it flew away? They'll think we're nuts."* They told nobody until they came off shift the next morning and told his mother, who did not believe them; his father insisted.<Cite s="2025-03-26-podcast-ufo-live-shows" t="14:01" /><Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="05:44" />

## Afterwards

Kiriakou treats the episode as unresolved rather than revelatory. He was seventeen then and says he still thinks about it all the time, while noting that other people's accounts are far more dramatic than his own — *"mine's nothing like theirs"* — and that his was nonetheless enough to burn a place in his memory.<Cite s="2025-03-26-podcast-ufo-live-shows" t="14:01" /><Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="05:44" />

The sighting is also the reason he asked the [question every new CIA officer asks](/wiki/cia-ufo-question). His boss told him it was the first question all of them have on arrival, and passed on the answer he had himself been given: that all of that material is at the Pentagon and the agency has nothing to do with it.<Cite s="2025-02-28-epstein-bin-laden-china-israel" t="1:53:01" /> He remains, on his own description, a rational man who saw something — *"I thought it was all baloney myself."*<Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="15:05" />
