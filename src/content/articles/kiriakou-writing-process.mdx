**Kiriakou's writing process** is something [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he had never been asked about until a listener put the question to him in 2026 — *"nobody's ever asked me that before."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="00:30" />

## Books, in waves

His normal pace for a book is about nine months, worked in bursts rather than daily quotas. A representative session: thinking about a particular mafia figure, finding *"a little angle I wanted to get down on paper,"* then sitting and writing for an hour — about 600 words — before moving on.<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="01:33" /><Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="02:05" /><Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="05:11" />

The exception was [*Remains of the Day*](/wiki/remains-of-the-day-book), his guide to Washington's historic cemeteries, written *"in 6 weeks during COVID"* because the subject held him. He would come home from his radio show, make dinner, sit down at seven — *"and then I look at the clock again, it's 2:00 in the morning. I'm like, oh my god, what happened?"* His conclusion about that state: *"sometimes you get on a roll and you just can't stop."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="05:11" /><Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="05:42" />

## Op-eds, in one sitting

The op-ed process is deliberately slower at the front and faster at the end. He lets an idea *"develop over the course of a couple days,"* sometimes opening a Word document to put down a couple of sentences and then leaving it *"for a day or two just to let my brain process it."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="04:40" />

Then it comes out in one go. An op-ed *"is supposed to be between 700 and 1200 words, and I can usually do that in an hour or 45 minutes if I feel strongly about it."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="04:40" />

He writes them a couple of times a week, mostly for [Consortium News](/wiki/consortium-news) and [CovertAction Magazine](/wiki/covertaction-magazine), sometimes *Prison Legal News*. Past outlets he lists include the *Washington Post*, the *LA Times*, the *New York Times*, the *Wall Street Journal*, the *Los Angeles Review of Books* and the *Guardian*.<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="02:05" />

## The two a.m. habit

Asked on Facebook whether he ever sleeps, his answer was that he does not much — *"I don't know if it's stress or anxiety or whatever, but no."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="05:42" />

What he does with the hours is work: *"it's not uncommon for me to get up at 2:00 in the morning and say, 'Well, I'm up. I might as well make $400 and write an op-ed.' So I'll just write an op-ed and just send it to somebody. See if they pay me $400."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="05:42" />

## A worked example: the gerrymandering piece

The op-ed he was planning that day concerned Republican efforts to redraw districts in Texas to take five Democratic seats — and his objection was that nobody was reporting the actual history. *"I have no idea why none of the big outlets have said what I'm going to say."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="02:35" />

His argument is that the plan is a reversion, not an innovation: it returns to how districts were divided in the 1980s. Gerrymandering as practised since the 1990s was designed to protect incumbents — a Republican who used to win with 54 or 55% would now take 90%, *"but the Democrat also gets 90%."* Redistributing those safe Democratic votes to try to flip seats only works one way: *"sure, in a Republican year — but in a Democratic year, the Republicans lose."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="03:08" />

Hence his reading of the panic: *"everybody's losing their minds over the fact that the Republicans might pick up five seats. Yeah — the Democrats might pick up 10 seats. The Republicans are playing with fire here."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="03:40" />

He also rejects the framing of a *New York Times* op-ed that day arguing the real story was Democrats gerrymandering too: *"That's not the story. Of course the Democrats are going to gerrymander. The story is this has already been tried."* The evidence he points to is wave size — where recent cycles moved about two House seats, 1974 moved roughly 64 and 1994 roughly 68. *"We're going to start seeing these big waves again."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="03:40" /><Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="04:10" />

## Reach

Asked to account for his own audience, Kiriakou credits a short run of podcast appearances rather than any of the writing. About eighteen months before November 2025 he recorded, *"one right after the other over the course of maybe two months,"* three long-form interviews — with Julian Dorey, Dalton Fischer and Danny Jones — and *"for whatever reason, the three of you together talking about these issues just allowed me to hit the YouTube algorithm sweet spot."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="04:04" /> The effect he cites is not statistical: *"I was pumping gas yesterday and two guys at different pumps asked me for selfies."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="02:04" />

The reading his interviewer offers — that the full-length format let audiences see the analysis rather than only the grievance — is one Kiriakou accepts, and it is consistent with what he says he tries to do: lay out the reasoning step by step rather than inject anger at the people who prosecuted him.<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="04:36" /><Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="05:08" />
