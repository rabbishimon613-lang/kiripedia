**Abolish the CIA** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s position that the agency should be disbanded because it does nothing the rest of the intelligence community cannot. There are already 18 U.S. intelligence agencies, he notes: the State Department's Bureau of Intelligence and Research produces analysis *"at least as good"* as the CIA's; the Pentagon's human-intelligence service already recruits spies to steal secrets; and DARPA, the NSA and the private sector — via [In-Q-Tel](/wiki/in-q-tel) creations like [Palantir](/wiki/palantir) — cover the technology.<Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="55:47" /><Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="56:18" /> Asked what the CIA does that is unique or particularly valuable, he answers, *"Nothing."* A realist, he concedes it will not be disbanded, and advises young recruits to enter with their own moral principles and try to change it from within.<Cite s="2026-01-21-scott-michael-nathan-prison-for-truth" t="56:48" />

## Torn down to the studs (Judging Freedom)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says that even Eisenhower, one of the CIA's greatest early supporters, concluded it was *"out of control,"* and that he believes — as, he says, does President Trump — the agency needs to be *"torn down to its studs and rebuilt."* He frames the failure as regulatory capture: the Capitol Hill overseers are *"little more than cheerleaders"* for the CIA.<Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="33:52" /><Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="34:22" /><Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="34:54" /> Asked in the same interview what he would do to reform the CIA, Kiriakou first proposed closing the [CIA media revolving door](/wiki/cia-media-revolving-door) — a cooling-off period before officers can become paid media personalities, and revoking retirees' security clearances.<Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="27:35" /><Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="28:39" />
