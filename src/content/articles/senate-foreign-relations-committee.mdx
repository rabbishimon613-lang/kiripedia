## AIPAC's welcome and the Holy Land offer

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) served as senior investigator on the [Senate Foreign Relations Committee](/wiki/senate-foreign-relations-committee) from 2009 to mid-2011 under chairman John Kerry. He says two [AIPAC](/wiki/aipac) representatives walked unannounced into his office on his first day and offered an all-expenses-paid trip to the Holy Land, name-dropping seven colleagues already going; he refused repeatedly and finally told them to get out of his office.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="57:37" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="58:08" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="58:38" /> As chief investigator for the committee, he also ran a study of the [hawala system](/wiki/hawala-system), the informal money-transfer network he identified as one of the principal channels through which al-Qaeda moved operational money in the post-9/11 period.<Cite s="2024-11-13-julian-dorey-250" t="1:13:30" />
