The **magic box** was an improvised cell-phone locator that two CIA Directorate of Science and Technology officers built during the 2002 hunt for [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts that, given the target's phone number, the two officers went into a Pakistani bazaar, bought *"a couple of pieces of wood and some wires and a soldering iron,"* and returned with a box fitted with a telephone keypad and a small speaker.<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="14:12" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="15:16" /> After punching in the number, the team waited twelve hours; when the phone came on and made a call, the box pinged a direction — but by the time they grabbed their guns, he had switched it off, far too fast to follow through a city of 18 million.<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="15:48" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="16:19" />
