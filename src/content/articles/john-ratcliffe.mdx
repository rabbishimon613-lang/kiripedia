**John Ratcliffe** is the CIA director under Donald Trump's second term. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), a self-described *"lifelong leftist,"* says he probably disagrees with Ratcliffe on every issue but supports him anyway as a needed *"bombthrower"* to shake up the agency.<Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="14:47" /> Kiriakou had predicted Ratcliffe would be confirmed as CIA director almost unanimously, noting Ratcliffe had briefly served as acting Director of National Intelligence in Trump's first term and had previously been a Texas congressman on the armed services and intelligence committees, with no indication he intended to reform the CIA.<Cite s="2025-06-25-harrison-berger" t="35:16" />

Despite respecting Ratcliffe, Kiriakou doubts he has the wherewithal to meaningfully change the CIA from within — reasoning that if former director [Bill Burns](/wiki/bill-burns) couldn't do it, Ratcliffe likely can't either.<Cite s="2026-01-01-fair-observer" t="16:43" />

## Kiriakou's standing request

In 2026 Kiriakou disclosed that, having written a paper at a cabinet member's request in connection with his [pardon](/wiki/kiriakou-pardon-request), he had attached a condition of his own: if Ratcliffe were to resign as director of the [CIA](/wiki/cia) — *"even if it's on the last day of the Trump presidency"* — he wanted the job, for however briefly. *"I want to be the one to walk in that hallway and say, 'Guess who's back?' Even if it's for one single day. But I'm not bitter."*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="13:01" />
