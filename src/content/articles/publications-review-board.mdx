The **Publications Review Board** is the CIA office that must clear anything a current or former officer publishes. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes the obligation as total: *"Anytime I write something, whether it's a book or an article or a letter to the editor of Home and Garden magazine, I have to send that to the CIA's Publications Review Board, and they have to clear it."*<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="22:29" />

## Turnaround as a measure of standing

In the ordinary case the process is fast — *"90% of the time, they'll clear it in a couple of hours,"* and a longer article might take 24 hours.<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="22:29" />

Books are where the difference showed. Of his first: *"when I was on the outs and everybody at the CIA in the leadership hated me, it took me nine months to write my first book and 22 months to get it cleared."* By contrast, his most recent six books *"cleared in a matter of days."*<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="23:01" />

## Why they cannot stop him now

Kiriakou gives two reasons the board has little purchase on his current work. The first is age and access: much of what he discusses *"is either ancient history, because I left the CIA 21 years ago, or it is based on open-source information."* He is entitled to his own positions and analysis, and *"I don't have access to classified information. And so they can't stop me from saying something."*<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="23:01" /><Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="23:32" />

The second is statutory. There is a mandatory declassification rule — *"it's 35 years that something has to be declassified after 35 years"* — unless a compelling national security reason applies. The exceptions he lists are material revealing sources and methods, material identifying a source, or anything touching the NSA. *"Otherwise, we can do what we want."*<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="23:32" /><Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="24:04" />
