**Marjorie Taylor Greene** is an American congresswoman who appears in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s commentary as a case of a politician he disagrees with almost entirely but credits on specific questions.

## Kiriakou's assessment

Kiriakou has said he finds Greene *"impressive in some ways"*, arguing that being able to say something favourable about a person does not mean agreeing with them, and adding that she *"is kind of impressive considering where she came from"*.<Cite s="2025-05-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="58:36" /><Cite s="2025-05-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="59:07" />

## The Epstein files

Greene was among the Republicans — with [Thomas Massie](/wiki/thomas-massie) and members of the House Freedom Caucus — who joined Democrats to force a vote on releasing the [Epstein files](/wiki/epstein-files-transparency-act), against the wishes of Speaker [Mike Johnson](/wiki/mike-johnson).<Cite s="2025-07-23-the-deep-focus-show-epstein-new-bombshell-developments" t="08:53" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="1:03:31" /> She has also said publicly that the GOP is betraying conservative values and that neoconservatives have recaptured [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) from his own movement.<Cite s="2025-08-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:48" />

Kiriakou has recounted her account of a conversation with Trump, in which she asked why he was angry at her for wanting the documents released unredacted when she was not in them; Trump's answer was *"yeah, but many of my friends are."*<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="16:38" /> Kiriakou calls her willingness to stand up on this *"courageous"*, but says it was ultimately too much even for her and that *"they forced her all the way out of Washington"* — an outcome he attributes to the number of very senior figures of both parties, including presidents of both parties, who are at least implicated.<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="17:09" />

## Israel and Gaza

Greene also appears in Kiriakou's account of the American right's drift on Israel. Asked about her description of the Gaza war as a genocide, he answered that *"she's right, of course, but it's amazing to hear her and people like her saying anything like that."*<Cite s="2025-07-30-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra.sponsors" t="00:06" /> She was one of six votes in a House measure to cut off offensive military aid — the members of the Squad plus Greene — a tally Kiriakou contrasted with the 27 senators who backed an identical measure days later.<Cite s="2025-08-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:40:33" />
