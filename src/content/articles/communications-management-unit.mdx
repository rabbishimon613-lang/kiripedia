A **communications management unit** is a restricted-contact housing unit inside an American federal prison. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) names them in a short list of practices he classifies as torture.

Speaking about [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange)'s treatment in 2021, he first invoked the United Nations special rapporteur on torture — *"nils melser has said repeatedly nils mills are the the un special rapporteur on torture has said repeatedly that what julian has been uh experiencing is a form of torture"* — and then turned the same standard on the United States: *"we all know that that the way the united states practices [solitary confinement](/wiki/solitary-confinement) is a form of torture we know that these cmus these communications management units that we have in our in our medium and maximum security prisons is a form of of torture."*<Cite s="2021-11-27-live-on-the-fly-randy-credico-ellsberg-kiriakou-rowley-hail-to-the-whistleb" t="14:11" /><Cite s="2021-11-27-live-on-the-fly-randy-credico-ellsberg-kiriakou-rowley-hail-to-the-whistleb" t="14:46" />
