## From 2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange

Project Stargate was a legitimate United States government intelligence program that operated from the early 1970s through 1995, originally as an Army intelligence effort. It was funded and operational for over two decades. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="01:01:09" />

## 'I don't have time for this'

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he never sat in a [Project Stargate](/wiki/project-stargate) briefing and dismissed the psychic-spying effort as *"so out there"* — telling a colleague he needed intelligence *"from the source's mouth,"* not from *"some guy going into a trance."* He drew a sharp line between Stargate and clinical [hypnosis](/wiki/hypnosis-operation-walk-in), which he found genuinely effective.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="39:05" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="39:36" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="40:07" />
