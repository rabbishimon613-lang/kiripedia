[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described a previously unpublicized meeting at the Trump International Hotel in Washington in which Rudy Giuliani's associate solicited $2 million in exchange for a presidential pardon — a story that subsequently appeared as a front-page article in the *New York Times*.<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:02:00" />

## Background

[Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) had been seeking a pardon from President Trump. A friend who had a relationship with Giuliani put him in touch with Giuliani's office. An associate told him Giuliani would be in Washington and invited him to meet at the Trump Hotel. Kiriakou brought his attorney, [Bruce Fein](/wiki/bruce-fine) — a former deputy attorney general under President Reagan and a constitutional scholar.<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:02:00" /><Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:02:30" />

## The meeting

Giuliani arrived with two other people. The conversation began with sports — the Jets, the Mets. When Kiriakou attempted to raise the subject of a pardon, Giuliani said: *"Anybody know where the pisser is?"* and stood up and walked away.<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:03:00" /><Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:03:30" />

Giuliani's associate then said: *"You never talk to Rudy about a pardon. You talk to me about a pardon."* He then stated: *"Rudy's going to want $2 million for the pardon."*<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:04:00" />

Kiriakou's response: *"First of all, I don't have $2 million. I'll never have $2 million. But even if I did, why would I spend $2 million to recover a $770,000 pension?"* He looked at his attorney; [Fein](/wiki/bruce-fine) said: *"We got up."* They left.<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:04:00" /><Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:04:30" />

## The photograph

That evening, Kiriakou attended a book-release party at the Republican National Committee, where he ran into Robert MacLean, a TSA whistleblower and friend. When Kiriakou told him what had happened, MacLean said: *"That's a crime. That's a felony."* Kiriakou replied: *"Yeah, but what am I going to do? I don't have $2 million."*<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:04:30" /><Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:05:00" />

Several days later, Mike Schmidt of the *New York Times* called and said he had heard that Giuliani had tried to get $2 million from Kiriakou for a pardon. He said the *Times* was doing a story because Giuliani was reportedly seeking the same amount from multiple pardon applicants. He asked Kiriakou to go on the record. After consulting his attorney — who told him *"Go on the record. You have nothing to hide"* — Kiriakou did.<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:05:00" /><Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:05:30" />

## Publication and aftermath

The story ran on the front page of the *New York Times*. Giuliani's public response: *"I never met this man. I've never heard of this man. None of this is true."*<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:06:00" />

The *Times* called Kiriakou for a response. He sent them the photograph taken at the meeting that morning. The paper published the story with the note: *"The New York Times has seen documentary proof that the meeting took place."*<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:06:00" /><Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:06:30" />

[Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) later learned through Giuliani's former business development director, Noelle Dunphy, that when the article appeared, Giuliani told her: *"That damn [CIA](/wiki/cia) guy ratted me out to the New York Times."*<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="2:06:30" />
