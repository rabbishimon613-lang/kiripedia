**Ray McGovern** is a former senior [CIA](/wiki/cia) analyst turned antiwar dissident and one of [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s closest friends and constant public allies.

## "Like a brother to me"

Kiriakou describes McGovern as *"a really great friend, a former very senior CIA officer... like a brother to me."*<Cite s="2017-03-04-allen-richie-allen-richie" t="21:00" /> Their agency careers never overlapped: Kiriakou began working for the CIA on the Monday after the Friday that McGovern retired, in January 1990.<Cite s="2017-09-23-worldbeyondwar-org-nowar2017-pt-4-sam-adams-associates-drake-mcg" t="13:35" /> The friendship was forged instead in the dissident world — Assange vigils, panels, and the Sam Adams Associates circle of intelligence veterans.<Cite s="2018-12-16-unity4j-unity4j" t="03:05" /><Cite s="2019-06-18-unity4j-unity4j" t="04:17" />

Kiriakou lists McGovern among the ex-intelligence people who could genuinely reform the agency — *"can you imagine Ray McGovern back at the CIA?"* — though he concedes Ray is a little long in the tooth and would probably decline.<Cite s="2020-12-23-codepink" t="35:00" /> He also cites McGovern when rebutting the accusation that prominent ex-CIA dissidents are still secretly agency: people say it of Snowden, of Philip Agee — whom the CIA hunted for decades — and of McGovern.<Cite s="2024-10-30-bulwarg" t="42:50" /><Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="47:43" />

## MICIMATT

The phrase Kiriakou often reaches for about Washington's permanent economy — that the business of Washington is government, and behind it a military-industrial-congressional-intelligence-media-academia-think-tank complex — is, he notes, McGovern's coinage: the "MICIMATT."<Cite s="2023-08-09-london-real" t="36:40" />

## The Kremlin meeting

Kiriakou recounts, with delight, that McGovern traveled to Russia with Oliver Stone — without ever mentioning the trip to him — and that at the Kremlin, [Vladimir Putin](/wiki/vladimir-putin) said, *"Oh yeah, let me meet with Oliver and Ray,"* receiving the two in his office for 40 minutes. *"I don't know what was said, but I'm going to call Ray tonight and ask for a readout."*<Cite s="2025-05-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:34" /><Cite s="2025-05-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:06" />

## Where they disagree

Kiriakou is open about the gap between them on September 11: McGovern, he says, is convinced it was a conspiracy — that if [bin Laden](/wiki/osama-bin-laden) was involved, he was used as a stooge — a position Kiriakou does not share, though he treats it with respect rather than ridicule.<Cite s="2017-03-04-allen-richie-allen-richie" t="21:31" />

## In Kiriakou's trusted-feed cohort (2025)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) named [Ray McGovern](/wiki/ray-mcgovern) at the [Consortium News](/wiki/consortium-news) 30th-anniversary panel in November 2025 as one of the small handful of writers whose feeds he checks daily on X and Blue Sky for news — the same short list on which he placed Patrick Lawrence, Mary Kostakidis and Joe Lauria — as the mechanism by which he now escapes what he calls the intolerable ecosystem of *"any slob"* rolling out of bed to make a YouTube show.<Cite s="2025-11-16-consortium-news-consortium-news" t="1:27:36" /><Cite s="2025-11-16-consortium-news-consortium-news" t="1:28:09" />
