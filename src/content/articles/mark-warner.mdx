**Mark Warner** is the vice chairman of the Senate Intelligence Committee and a former governor of Virginia.<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="46:19" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="46:50" /> Kiriakou states the conflict before giving the assessment: *"Mark Warner I'm told does not like me at all and doesn't think I should be pardoned. I'm going to put that aside. I've never met the man. We've never exchanged a harsh word. But I'm told he doesn't like me, and that's fine."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="46:50" />

His judgement is then unreservedly favourable — *"very very smart… a terrific manager… an accomplished leader, not just in government, but in private industry"* — and he places Warner at the top of the [rankings](/wiki/kiriakou-case-officer-rankings) as a prospective director rather than a case officer: *"I think he would actually be quite an excellent CIA director."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="46:50" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="47:21" /> The honesty finding rests on longevity: *"we know that because he's been in public life for decades and he's honest."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="47:21" />
