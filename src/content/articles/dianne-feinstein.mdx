**Dianne Feinstein** was a longtime Democratic U.S. senator from California who chaired the Senate Intelligence Committee. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) calls her *"one of the greatest disappointments on Capitol Hill,"* saying she remained completely and totally bought in to the intelligence community even after CIA director [John Brennan](/wiki/john-brennan) — whom she had long supported — disrespected her by having her committee staff's computers hacked.<Cite s="2020-12-23-codepink" t="37:34" />

Kiriakou says Brennan called Feinstein a liar over her committee's finding that the CIA had been monitoring the Senate investigators while they were investigating the agency's torture program — a claim that subsequently proved to be true.<Cite s="2015-11-14-robin-hensel" t="32:43" />
