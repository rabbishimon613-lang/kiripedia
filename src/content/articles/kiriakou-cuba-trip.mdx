**Kiriakou's Cuba trip** was his first visit to the island, prompted by the Cuban government translating his first two books into Spanish and adding them to the permanent collection of the national library. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he was treated *"like a king"* and that Cubans, while disliking the U.S. government, expressed admiration for the American people and puzzlement over why Washington *"hates them so much."*<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="29:11" /><Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="29:43" /> One of Cuba's three vice presidents was more frustrated, telling him *"all your presidents are the same"* — Cuba cannot *"keep the lights on,"* its children lack shoes and it lacks medical supplies, yet it is cast as a threat while the U.S. keeps relations with far worse actors.<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="30:14" /><Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="30:45" />
