**Citizens United** is the Supreme Court decision [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) identifies as finalizing *"official corruption"* by holding that corporations are people, allowing unlimited money into political campaigns through political action committees.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="16:14" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="16:45" /><Cite s="2026-07-02-jack-neel" t="[15:12]" /> He points to a [Thomas Massie](/wiki/thomas-massie) primary in which the two sides spent 35 million dollars — almost entirely outside money — on a single House seat that pays 180,000 dollars a year, money he says [AIPAC](/wiki/aipac) spent because Massie does not support Israel's agenda.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="17:18" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="17:49" />
