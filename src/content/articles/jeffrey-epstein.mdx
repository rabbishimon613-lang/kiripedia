## "An Israeli access agent"

Asked whether Epstein worked for the CIA: *"No way. … To spy on whom? The CIA's job is to collect foreign intelligence. What foreign intelligence did he have? ## Suicide

Kiriakou believes Epstein died by suicide. His reasoning, drawn from his own time in the Bureau of Prisons: *"In the Bureau of Prisons everybody has his head up his ass. The cameras never work. The guards are always sound asleep. This happens all the time. These suicides — they happen literally every day in American prisons. Every day. When the only qualifications to be a prison guard are you have to be working on a GED or better and no felony convictions, you get what you pay for."*<Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="49:21" /><Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="49:51" />

## The Mossad access-operation thesis

Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou): *"I've maintained from the very beginning that this has [MOSSAD](/wiki/mossad) written all over it. Mossad is incredibly active on U.S. soil — hundreds and hundreds of Israeli intelligence agents active across the United States at any given time. One of the things the Israelis are very good at is the cultivation and recruitment of what are called access agents. Say you want information from Bill Clinton and Hillary Clinton and Donald Trump and Prince Andrew — well, you're not going to recruit any of those people. So you do the next best thing: you recruit somebody who has access to all those people. Well, Jeffrey Epstein bore all the hallmarks of an Israeli access operation."*<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="2:47:15" /><Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="2:47:45" />

Kiriakou's case for the Israeli connection extends to Epstein's principal lieutenant: *"His relationship with Ghislaine Maxwell, whose father was also a Mossad agent — a known Mossad agent — leads me to believe that they kept it all in the family. ## Bureau of Prisons camera reset

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), drawing on his own experience as a federal prisoner at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto), has explained the technical cause of a widely discussed anomaly in surveillance footage from the night of Epstein's death. The video showed a brief interruption — a *"blip"* — which some observers characterized as evidence of editing. Kiriakou stated that all cameras in Bureau of Prisons facilities reset themselves on a twenty-four-hour cycle, and that the blip was the routine reset.<Cite s="2026-05-19-ffn-jeff-dornik-swamps-holy-mess" t="13:02" /> *"All the cameras in the Bureau of Prisons reset themselves every 24 hours. That's what the blip was."* He added that the Bureau of Prisons should have disclosed the camera cycle in advance to preempt the speculation.<Cite s="2026-05-19-ffn-jeff-dornik-swamps-holy-mess" t="13:33" />

## The [access agent](/wiki/access-agent) framing (Diary of a CEO, January 2026)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) reframed Epstein's operation in [CIA](/wiki/cia) terminology: a textbook [access agent](/wiki/access-agent), recruited not for secrets he held himself but for his ability to keep powerful, close-in targets in a controlled environment. You cannot recruit a former president or a major CEO directly — so you recruit someone who has regular access to them.<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:22:00" />

The access agent needs money to maintain the environment — an island, a mansion — and a temperament that makes high-status people feel comfortable and valued. The secondary purpose is kompromat: hidden cameras throughout the property record behavior that can be used as leverage if needed. Kiriakou noted that Epstein's island had video cameras in every room, including the bathrooms: *"Why would he care what was going on unless it was to use that information against people?"*<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:22:30" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:23:00" />

On the funding question — how a person like Epstein becomes independently wealthy enough to operate at this level — Kiriakou noted that governments are uniquely positioned to launder money undetected, and identified real estate, fine art, and racehorses as the three easiest current vehicles for doing so.<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:23:30" />

Kiriakou cited Alex Acosta — the prosecuting attorney at the time and later Secretary of Labor under Trump — as having stated that he was ordered by the attorney general to offer Epstein the deal. Kiriakou's observation: only the president can order the attorney general to do something.<Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:24:00" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:24:30" />

## The access-agent theory (Truth Hurts Show)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says that while he has no proof, he is confident [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) was a [Mossad](/wiki/mossad) [access agent](/wiki/access-agent) — someone who supplies an intelligence service access to targets it could never recruit directly, such as Bill Clinton, Prince Andrew or Bill Gates, with blackmail improving the product. He points to the audio and video Epstein kept in *"literally every single room of his house, including the bathrooms,"* and to the unexplained millions [Les Wexner](/wiki/les-wexner) gave him, as evidence.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="01:32" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="02:03" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="02:34" /> Kiriakou believes Epstein is dead and doubts the client list will surface soon, noting one black book failed to meet its 100,000-dollar reserve at a Maryland auction house.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="03:05" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="03:36" />

## Intelligence broker, not just access agent (Jack Neel)

Asked whether [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) was an asset or whether the agencies worked for him, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) revised his view: the latest documents solidified his belief that Epstein was less an [access agent](/wiki/access-agent) than an *"intelligence broker"* who collected intelligence and sold it to multiple services — [Mossad](/wiki/mossad), and probably the CIA, FBI, MI5, MI6 and the Germans. He notes Epstein tried repeatedly to meet Vladimir Putin, refusing the Russians' offer of a group meeting.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="1:16:14" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="1:18:48" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="1:19:20" /> Kiriakou stresses no one knows the source of Epstein's money, recounting a New York Post reporter who, in the 1990s, could find nothing on the *"financial mystery man"* backing the New York Sun.<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="1:17:45" />

## Suicide, and why (The Inquiry)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has been *"adamant from the beginning"* that [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) died by suicide, not murder: having served time, he knows prison guards do not make their rounds — two hours passed rather than eight minutes — and the cameras *"reset every 24 hours"* and tape over themselves. Facing life on a *"steel prison bunk,"* Epstein *"would rather be dead."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="44:28" /><Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="48:04" /><Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="49:05" /> Epstein's brother, he adds, identified the body and accompanied it to the crematorium, debunking an AI-generated photo of Epstein *"walking down the street in Tel Aviv."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="49:36" /> Kiriakou maintains Epstein was an Israeli access agent and *"intelligence broker"* who volunteered to the CIA, FBI, MI5, MI6 and Germans — though there is no evidence the CIA ever responded.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="44:59" /><Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="47:04" />

## Never heard at the CIA (JK Podcast)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) reiterates his belief that [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) died by suicide — prison guards *"have their heads up their asses,"* the cameras *"reset themselves,"* and rounds meant for every 15 minutes took two hours.<Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="00:30" /> Notably, he says, he *never* heard Epstein's name at the CIA — unlike [Adnan Khashoggi](/wiki/adnan-khashoggi) — and explains that had an officer encountered an unknown American in the middle of a compartmented operation, standard practice would be to alert the White House and have the FBI *"start a file"* on him as a potential counterintelligence problem.<Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="19:04" /><Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="20:36" />
