**Greater Israel** is the name [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) gives to what he takes to be the Israeli government's actual territorial objective, and he is explicit that he reads it as sincere rather than rhetorical.

*"I think the Israeli government really believes that it can expel every Palestinian, starting in Gaza — and once Gaza is pacified, they can move to the West Bank, where these operations are still ongoing,"* in places such as Jenin and outside Bethlehem.<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="27:43" />

The timescale he attributes to it is what distinguishes his reading from a claim about the current war: *"even if it takes another hundred years or 200 years, I think they really believe that they can expel every single Palestinian and take all the land."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="27:43" />

The condition that makes it viable, on his account, is political rather than military: *"because they know there's not going to be pushback from the United States. They don't care about what any other country says — but I think that they really believe that there won't be pushback from the United States, and they're going to continue on with this policy."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="28:14" />

## The domestic constraint he does see

Against that he sets the one pressure he thinks American coverage underrates: *"the degree to which Israeli citizens are now objecting to ongoing military operations."* He points to a demonstration in Tel Aviv of several hundred thousand people — *"it was gigantic. Israel doesn't have that many people"* — and to reservists *"tired of being called up constantly to go to Gaza and fight … tired of the expense … tired of the deprivation,"* and *"embarrassed by the crimes against humanity and war crimes that the Israelis are committing."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="25:36" /><Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="26:07" />

He qualifies it immediately: *"I will admit to you, unfortunately, that that's still a minority view in Israel — but it's growing."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="26:07" />
