**Death threats against Kiriakou** followed his public disclosures about the [CIA torture program](/wiki/cia-torture-program-whistleblowing).

Recalling the period in 2021, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) said: *"after i made my my revelations i got so many death threats that i actually had to had to leave the country for for a couple of weeks my wife and i uh took our kids to mexico just you know the [fbi](/wiki/fbi) recommended that we go on vacation somewhere that at least two of these threats were credible and uh we should just make ourselves um scarce."*<Cite s="2021-11-27-live-on-the-fly-randy-credico-ellsberg-kiriakou-rowley-hail-to-the-whistleb" t="34:37" /><Cite s="2021-11-27-live-on-the-fly-randy-credico-ellsberg-kiriakou-rowley-hail-to-the-whistleb" t="35:11" />

He raises it in the same breath as the absence of any public defence. The one message of support he singles out came privately and after the fact, from a retired deputy director of the [CIA](/wiki/cia), and he says he kept it: *"you've chosen a difficult road but i'm glad somebody did i just wish i had had the courage to do it myself."* His verdict on what that amounted to: *"that translated into nothing for me no support at all no public support i was ostracized by my cia colleagues."*<Cite s="2021-11-27-live-on-the-fly-randy-credico-ellsberg-kiriakou-rowley-hail-to-the-whistleb" t="35:11" /><Cite s="2021-11-27-live-on-the-fly-randy-credico-ellsberg-kiriakou-rowley-hail-to-the-whistleb" t="35:42" />
