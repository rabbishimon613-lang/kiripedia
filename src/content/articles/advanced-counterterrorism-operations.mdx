**Advanced Counterterrorism Operations** was a six-week specialist course at [CIA](/wiki/cia) headquarters that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was recalled from the field to attend well over a year before the September 11 attacks. He was one of only fourteen officers chosen for it worldwide, selected, in his account, because they were the ones moving up quickly in counterterrorism.<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="10:52" />

The syllabus was broad rather than deep: weapons and explosives training, advanced counterterrorism driving, desert driving, and the technical side — *"bugs and cameras and satellites and drones and all kinds of things like that."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="11:24" />

Its significance in his telling is entirely one of timing. The course ran about eighteen months before the attacks; when 9/11 came, the fourteen were trained and *"we were ready to deploy immediately to the field."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="11:24" /> He pairs it with a standing warning that the surveillance and counter-surveillance element is not a classroom exercise — an officer has to *"always always be on the lookout for for somebody following you, which unfortunately happens."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="11:24" />
