**Kiriakou's titanium knee** is the lasting physical consequence of his CIA training.

During the roadblock element of [counterterrorist driving](/wiki/counterterrorist-driving), [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was given one of the junkyard beaters and told to crash through. About a hundred feet out he saw the roadblock and men dressed as irregular militia, one with his hand up telling him to halt. He accelerated straight at him; the man jumped clear; Kiriakou struck the rear of the blocking car and got through. But because the car was, in his words, a beater piece of junk, his seat detached from the floor and he smashed his knees into the dashboard. He went to the emergency room and wore a knee brace for months.<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="35:51" />

## Sixteen years later

The injury led to a condition called spontaneous osteonecrosis — the death of the bone in the joint. On an MRI, Kiriakou says, his knee looked like *"a sponge with a thousand little holes in it."* The doctor told him he must have been in a serious car accident; Kiriakou said he had been, a long time ago. Told that such a thing takes ten or twenty years to develop, he answered that it had been sixteen. The surgeon's verdict: *"that knee's gotta come out right now."*<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="36:24" /><Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="36:55" />

He has a titanium knee now, he says, *"and a nasty wicked scar to prove it."* His assessment of the course that cost it him is nonetheless unambiguous: *"other than that, this was the best training course I ever had in my life."*<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="36:55" />
