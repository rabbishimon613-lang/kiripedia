**Elliott Abrams** is the American foreign policy official whose appointment to a presidential advisory board on public diplomacy in July 2023 [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) used as a compact demonstration of how little separates the two American foreign policy establishments.

## The career, as Kiriakou recites it

Kiriakou ran the record in a single sequence. Abrams *"was Ronald Reagan's Assistant Secretary of State for Latin America who was in up to his neck with Iran-Contra and ended up pleading guilty to two counts of obstruction of justice over the Iran-Contra scandal. He was pardoned by George H.W. Bush."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="21:49" />

From there, in Kiriakou's account, Abrams went to work for George W. Bush and *"helped to perpetuate the lie that Saddam Hussein had weapons of mass destruction,"* then during the Trump administration served as special envoy for Iran and afterwards for Venezuela — the tenure that withdrew the United States from the JCPOA and *"tried to install this pretender to the throne Juan Guaido in the presidency of Venezuela."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="22:21" />

## The appointment as evidence

The destination is what interested Kiriakou: *"now he's working for the Biden administration on public policy advocacy. This is just a euphemism for propaganda."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="22:21" />

His conclusion was categorical. *"It goes to show you that there is no difference between the neoliberal foreign policy and the neo-conservative foreign policy. It's exactly the same and the players are interchangeable."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="22:58" /> Offered the image of one bomber painted with a rainbow flag and a Black Lives Matter sticker and another without, Kiriakou accepted it as the description of American foreign policy.<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="22:58" />
