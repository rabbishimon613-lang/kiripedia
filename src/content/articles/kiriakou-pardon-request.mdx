The **Kiriakou pardon request** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s public effort to obtain a presidential pardon from [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) for the 2013 felony conviction under the [Intelligence Identities Protection Act](/wiki/intelligence-identities-protection-act) that stripped him of his CIA pension, his federal firearm rights and, until an unsolicited pardon from Terry McAuliffe as governor of Virginia, his Virginia voting rights.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:19:19" /><Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:20:23" />

## What he wants back

Asked what a federal pardon would actually change, Kiriakou reduced it to two items: *"It would give me my pension back … and give me my gun rights back, Pat. I want my guns back."*<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:19:19" /> He said he lost his federal voting rights for life on top of that but has already recovered them at the state level: unprompted, Terry McAuliffe sent a courier to his Virginia house at seven in the morning with a hardcover envelope containing a state pardon decree bearing the gold seal, without Kiriakou ever meeting the man.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:20:23" /><Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:20:54" /> He notes the state pardon restores his Virginia gun rights but does not shield him from the federal felon-with-a-firearm charge, which carries a five-year mandatory minimum.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:21:27" /> If pardoned, Kiriakou said, he and his children have already planned to *"pile in the car"* and drive to the gun show at the Dulles Airport Expo Center *"with wads of cash"* to buy firearms.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:19:51" />

## The Mar-a-Lago encounter

Kiriakou said he went to Mar-a-Lago with a friend for lunch about a year and a half before the interview and found himself in the same room as Trump, who was dining with Mark Zuckerberg.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:17:48" /> When Trump rose and approached his table, Kiriakou stood up and used what he called his *"go-to line"*: *"Mr President, my name's John Kiriakou. I'm the CIA torture whistleblower. [Barack Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) and [John Brennan](/wiki/john-brennan) ruined my life, and you're the only person who can give it back to me."* Trump replied *"[Tucker Carlson](/wiki/tucker-carlson) told me about you"* and instructed him to *"talk to [Pam Bondi](/wiki/pam-bondi), she'll get the paperwork done."*<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:18:18" /><Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:18:49" /> Kiriakou said he never obtained direct access to Bondi and worked instead through *"a friend of a friend of a friend"* and through the US pardon attorney, Ed Martin.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:18:49" />

## Letters and public advocates

Kiriakou credits [Tucker Carlson](/wiki/tucker-carlson), Judge Andrew Napolitano and Dr Phil with writing letters of support for the pardon petition.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:18:49" />

## The Kalshi contract

On 22 April 2026 Kiriakou discovered that the prediction market Kalshi had opened a contract on whether Trump would pardon him.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:15:45" /> He said his implied odds were about 20 percent one day, 19 percent the next, on $152,000 of trading volume, and that when he tried to place a twenty-dollar bet on himself the platform blocked the trade for *"conflict of income."*<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:16:45" /><Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="1:17:16" />

## The CIA's veto (2026)

By the summer of 2026 Kiriakou described the campaign as having exhausted its political options and run into institutional opposition. He had, he said, *"enlisted the help of every Republican in Washington who has the ear of the president."* One congressman volunteered to help — *"only out of the goodness of his own heart. He's not asking anything of me"* — and, on being shown everything Kiriakou had, drew the obvious inference: *"My God, if you've got all this support and you're not pardoned yet, you're probably not getting pardoned."* Kiriakou's answer was that this was roughly what he had assumed.<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="01:03" />

The congressman then investigated and came back with a specific finding: *"the CIA absolutely positively does not want you to get pardoned."* It remained possible, he said, but *"we're going to have to go head-to-head with the CIA on this."* Kiriakou's response was to lengthen his own timeline rather than abandon it — [Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) would be president for another two and a half years, *"And so I don't feel any particular rush. I'm getting all my ducks in order and I'm hoping for the best."*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="01:33" />

## The paper, and the price

Asked whether he would ever go back to the agency for one last mission, Kiriakou admitted he would consider it, and then volunteered something he said he had not made public before. About six months earlier he had met a cabinet member about the pardon, and had been asked to write something very specific; he did the research, took it seriously, wrote it and submitted it. Word came back that *"the person that I was trying to impress appreciated the paper."*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="12:30" />

Kiriakou then named his condition. He had been happy to write the paper, he said, but he had a request: if [John Ratcliffe](/wiki/john-ratcliffe) were ever to resign as director of the [CIA](/wiki/cia) — *"even if it's on the last day of the Trump presidency"* — he wanted the job. *"I want to be the one to walk in that hallway and say, 'Guess who's back?' Even if it's for one single day."* His own gloss on the request: *"But I'm not bitter."*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="13:01" />

See [Mar-a-Lago pardon pitch](/wiki/mar-a-lago-pardon-pitch) for his direct approach to the president.

## 'Never getting a pardon'

In an episode of *Dead Drop* released on 27 July 2026, Kiriakou reported a message relayed to him that day. A colleague at Bison Media who is close to the White House Chief of Staff called to tell him that the Chief of Staff had said he is never getting a pardon.<Cite s="2026-07-27-dead-drop-s2e11-unfinished-business" t="06:12" />

The reason given was not personal. In Kiriakou's account of the message, the only thing standing in the way is not a person at all but an organisation — the [CIA](/wiki/cia). The agency was reported as saying that it does not care about Kiriakou himself, but that pardoning a CIA officer convicted of a crime would set a precedent it does not want set.<Cite s="2026-07-27-dead-drop-s2e11-unfinished-business" t="06:12" />

His response, in the same passage, was to ask what else he could do but keep working — the remark introduces a discussion of why he could not simply keep his head down in prison either.<Cite s="2026-07-27-dead-drop-s2e11-unfinished-business" t="06:12" />
