**The Italians** were the mafia contingent at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto) — and, after the [Aryans](/wiki/aryan-brotherhood), the second and far warmer alliance of [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s prison years.

## The five families at Loretto

When Kiriakou says Italians, he means it literally: men named Gambino, Bonanno, Lucchese, Genovese — *"you get the idea."*<Cite s="2015-11-12-quakerhouse" t="59:12" /><Cite s="2023-04-29-katie-halper-formerly-incarcerated-cia-agent-john-kiriakou" t="06:39" /> Loretto held the boss of the Gambino family, the underboss of the DeCavalcantes, and the Genovese boss of the Port of Newark — *"serious hardcore made guys from the five families."*<Cite s="2017-05-26-nicole-sandler" t="55:53" /> Before he self-surrendered, Kiriakou's brother — who had rented an apartment from Carlo Gambino's sister in Brooklyn for nine years — called with research: stay away from the Gambino boss, the man who replaced John Gotti, in on a stack of murder counts. Kiriakou did.<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="2:31:33" /><Cite s="2017-06-29-rob-kall-bottom-up-show" t="20:31" /><Cite s="2022-05-23-how-cia-s-rogue-agent-survived-prison-probe-v" t="29:05" />

## Pete across the hall

A senior captain in the Bonanno family lived directly across the hall. Kiriakou passed him every finished book and newspaper — good for relations — until Pete finally asked: *"Why do you sit with those Nazi retards in the cafeteria?"*<Cite s="2015-12-16-human-rights-defense-cente-human-rights-defense-cente" t="21:45" /> Pete's sentencing advice became one of Kiriakou's staples: told Kiriakou had thirty months, he said, *"Oh, you could do that standing on your head"* — and not to tell anybody.<Cite s="2019-03-04-a-good-place-with-ella-a-a-good-place-with-ella-a" t="1:07:06" /> It was Pete who tugged his sleeve out of the cafeteria staredown — *"are you crazy?"*<Cite s="2017-06-06-disruption-network-lab" t="53:57" />

A later telling gives the exchange in full and supplies Kiriakou's own answer, which was that it had never been a choice: on his first day the [Aryans](/wiki/aryan-brotherhood) had simply told him where to sit and he had done as instructed. Pete's response was the whole of the transfer — *"from today, you are with the Italians"* — to which Kiriakou's reply was *"it's about time."* He describes the Italians as the smallest of the prison's factions and the one commanding the highest respect, more than the Black or Latino groupings, and dates the invitation to a couple of months into his sentence rather than his second year; the two accounts differ on the timing.<Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:57:10" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:57:41" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="1:54:05" />

In his second year Kiriakou was formally invited over: *"From today, you're with the Italians."* They treated him very well — including via a crooked guard on the payroll who brought in pork loin, fresh mozzarella, tomatoes, and mushrooms.<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="2:27:51" /> The bond, he explains, was two-part: being Greek was half the battle; hating the FBI as much as they did was the other half.<Cite s="2021-03-10-heidi-weber-no-stop-heidi" t="17:21" /><Cite s="2019-04-20-david-gornoski-on-torture-prison-ptsd" t="41:15" />

## "Get off my property"

The friendships outlived the sentence — and drew the FBI to his door: agents suggested Pete, released, had taken over leadership of the Gambino family, and wanted Kiriakou's help. *"You want me to rat out a five-families boss? Get off my property."* He closed the door and called his attorney.<Cite s="2022-05-23-how-cia-s-rogue-agent-survived-prison-probe-v" t="34:15" /> One of his best friends in the world remains Mark, an Italian from that circle.<Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="18:32" /> Even his protection radiated outward: an Italian told a tough prisoner named Blue that his friend John lived in Central One — Blue's answer was, *"the CIA guy? I never mess with that guy."*<Cite s="2015-12-16-human-rights-defense-cente-human-rights-defense-cente" t="18:04" />
