**Sergey Lavrov** is the Russian foreign minister, and was Russian ambassador to the United Nations during the period [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was assigned there.

## The assessment

Kiriakou raised Lavrov in conversation with the Russian ambassador in Washington, and describes the view from inside the American mission at the time: *"All of us admired him because he was such a good diplomat, and he was working with so much less than what the other major Western ambassadors were working with."*<Cite s="2025-08-07-militaire-news-cia" t="27:33" />

The comparison he draws is resource for resource. The American ambassador then was Negroponte, who Kiriakou says had a billion-dollar budget and the might of the United States behind him; the British, French and Chinese ambassadors all likewise had more to work with than Lavrov. *"Yet he was seen as the most powerful, the most important, the most strategic ambassador at the United Nations."*<Cite s="2025-08-07-militaire-news-cia" t="28:06" />

## Kiriakou's explanation

He puts the gap down to what the American mission was actually doing. *"Why wasn't it the American ambassador? Because the American ambassador wasn't focused on diplomacy"* — he was focused on America's many wars around the world.<Cite s="2025-08-07-militaire-news-cia" t="28:37" />

Kiriakou treats this as a pattern rather than a personal failing, and part of a wider argument that other countries overtake the United States economically while it attends to its wars.<Cite s="2025-08-07-militaire-news-cia" t="28:37" />

## Cooperation despite hostility

In the same conversation with the Russian ambassador, Kiriakou set out a position he says he holds regardless of how bad relations become: *"I don't care how hot the war gets, we can still cooperate on counterterrorism, counter narcotics, and counter proliferation"* — differences that should be set aside on questions important not just to the two countries but to everybody.<Cite s="2025-08-07-militaire-news-cia" t="30:09" /><Cite s="2025-08-07-militaire-news-cia" t="30:40" />
