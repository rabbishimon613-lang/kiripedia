The **Muslim Brotherhood terrorist designation** is a decision [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has questioned on the ground that nothing about the organisation has changed to justify it.

## The designation

Kiriakou noticed the subject rising on Fox News and elsewhere in right-wing print media, and believed that the State Department had just declared the Muslim Brotherhood, or elements of it, a terrorist organisation. He is explicit that he is not certain of the fact — *"I think, I'm not positive."*<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="50:12" />

## The objection

His argument is from the record. The organisation is 105 years old; it has never carried out an anti-American terrorist operation; and it has never been called a terrorist organisation by any American administration.<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="50:12" />

He separates the group's character from the label: *"It's fundamentalist. It doesn't like us, but that doesn't mean it's a terrorist organization."*<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="50:43" />

What he presses on is the purpose. If it is now a terrorist organisation, *"does that mean we attack it?"* — what is the reason for making the declaration at all?<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="50:43" />

Kiriakou raises the case immediately alongside *"narco terrorist"* and [Islamofascism](/wiki/islamofascism-term), as another instance of a label arriving before any conduct that would warrant it.<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="51:13" />
