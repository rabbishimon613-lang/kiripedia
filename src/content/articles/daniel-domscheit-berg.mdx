**Daniel Domscheit-Berg**, an early WikiLeaks associate who later left the project, is mentioned in a 2026 Berlin panel discussion that included [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou). The anecdote about meeting Domscheit-Berg through the [Chaos Computer Club](/wiki/chaos-computer-club-wikileaks) — in which he is introduced as "Daniel Schmitt" before being identified as Domscheit-Berg, and described as having "chickened out" of WikiLeaks soon after — was told by a different panelist, Der Spiegel journalist John Goetz, not by Kiriakou.<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="16:53" /> Goetz was the same panelist who described being in the Guardian's London "bunker" working with Der Spiegel colleague Marcel Rosenbach on the [Afghan War Logs](/wiki/afghan-war-logs) as they were first released.<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="09:30" />

No source in the corpus captures Kiriakou himself speaking about Daniel Domscheit-Berg.
