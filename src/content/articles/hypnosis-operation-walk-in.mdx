[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described a [CIA](/wiki/cia) operation in which a walk-in witness to a terrorist assassination was hypnotized in a London hotel room, producing an actionable license plate number that verified the witness was genuine and the attack was real.

## The walk-in

The man walked into an American embassy and said he had witnessed an assassination. His account: he had pulled off a highway to urinate, parked behind a banyan tree at an abandoned church, and while relieving himself watched a van pull up. The back doors opened. He saw two motorcycles and two men inside. He heard one man ask: *"Does he still live?"* The other replied: *"He should be dead by now."* After the van left, the men saw him and one punched him in the face. He told them he had seen nothing.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="1:57:00" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="1:57:30" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="1:58:00" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="1:58:30" />

He sat with a sketch artist; his drawings were close to photographs the [CIA](/wiki/cia) had of suspected members of the target terrorist group. The FBI interviewed him and dismissed him as a fraud. The CIA took him back.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="1:58:30" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="1:59:00" />

## London — the hypnosis

Kiriakou flew to London with two CIA psychologists, one of whom was a licensed hypnotist, and a second case officer. He had been skeptical of hypnosis; the psychologist told him that intelligent people go under much more quickly than others — a fact Kiriakou says he still doesn't fully understand. The CIA bought the witness a coat because he was too poor to own one in the London cold.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="1:59:30" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:00:00" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:00:30" />

In a darkened hotel room, Kiriakou counted backwards in the witness's language — a demanding job for a translator, requiring absolute calm and very slow deliberate speech. The man went under before the count reached zero. The hypnotist had told him to raise his arm and not lower it until instructed. The arm remained in the air for three hours — in Kiriakou's characterization, *"physically impossible for a conscious person."*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:01:00" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:01:30" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:02:00" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:02:30" />

## The license plate

Kiriakou asked the hypnotized witness to walk back through the scene: the exit ramp, the church, the banyan tree. Then: *"Can you see a license plate on the van?"* Eyes closed, the man said yes. *"What does it say?"* He read off the plate. Kiriakou wrote it down, handed it to his colleague, who went to the other room and called headquarters: *"Run this plate ASAP."* Headquarters immediately cabled the field. The plate came back stolen.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:03:00" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:03:30" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:04:00" />

The witness then said under hypnosis: *"They see me."* He described being punched and went through the full three-hour account — details about the van's rust spots over the rear wheels, the motorcycles, the conversation between the men — filling in details he had not consciously recalled in his unassisted account.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:04:00" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="2:04:30" />
