**FISA court** is the secret Foreign Intelligence Surveillance court that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) explains has existed since the Carter administration, championed by Ted Kennedy after Watergate.<Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="1:00:34" /> It sits on the top floor of the Justice Department, staffed by federal judges rotating in and out, and issues warrants to wiretap targets — *"nothing in writing that can ever be released,"* so an American never knows whether one exists on him.<Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="1:01:06" /><Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="1:01:37" /> It approves nearly everything (rejecting a fraction of a percent), with no real oversight; Section 702, up for reauthorization, extends warrantless wiretaps on undisclosed targets.<Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="1:02:08" /> Kiriakou cites Carter Page — surveilled and repeatedly re-warranted with no evidence of a crime — as the abuse, and believes it was done at [John Brennan](/wiki/john-brennan)'s behest to deny Trump the presidency.<Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="1:02:39" /><Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="1:03:10" />
