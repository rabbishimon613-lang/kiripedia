***The CIA Insider's Guide to Surveillance and Surveillance Detection*** is a book by [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) drawing on his experience as a surveillance-detection instructor at the [CIA](/wiki/cia) and as a private surveillance-training consultant.<Cite s="2026-04-20-tommy-g-israel-double-agents-directed-energy" t="26:00" />

The book covers the techniques used by intelligence professionals to identify whether they are being followed, including the four-phase [surveillance detection route](/wiki/surveillance-detection-route), concealment-device placement and retrieval, and counter-surveillance strategy for private individuals including corporate executives and journalists. Kiriakou described the audience as anyone who wants to know whether they are being followed — whether by carjackers, terrorists, or federal law enforcement.<Cite s="2026-04-20-tommy-g-israel-double-agents-directed-energy" t="26:30" />

Kiriakou also operates a private surveillance-training business drawing on the same curriculum.<Cite s="2026-04-20-tommy-g-israel-double-agents-directed-energy" t="26:00" />

## Surveillance-detection instructor — private training

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) disclosed that in addition to the book, he worked as a surveillance and surveillance-detection instructor at the [CIA](/wiki/cia) and has taught the subject at Liberty University, George Washington University, and privately through a company called IV Cyber. He described giving a private class through IV Cyber the night before the interview.<Cite s="2026-02-24-deep-focus-arrested-as-spy-9-11-cia-stores-everything" t="10:00" /><Cite s="2026-02-24-deep-focus-arrested-as-spy-9-11-cia-stores-everything" t="10:30" />
