**Operation Gladio** was, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, an operation hatched by the CIA, MI6, the Vatican and NATO at the end of World War II to leave *"stay-behind"* sleeper agents across Western Europe in case of a Soviet invasion.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="14:23" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="14:56" /> He says everything he knows about it he learned after leaving the CIA, and that it became public only when Italian Prime Minister Andreotti disclosed it to parliament in 1990. It intersected, he notes, with Italy's P2 Masonic lodge and figures like Michele Sindona and Licio Gelli.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="13:21" /> Some analysts, he adds, treat Gladio as a template for later programs — the [Iran-Contra](/wiki/iran-contra) death squads and the arming of anti-Soviet fighters in Afghanistan.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="18:33" />
