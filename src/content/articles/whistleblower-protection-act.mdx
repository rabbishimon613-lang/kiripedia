The **Whistleblower Protection Act** is a United States statute that shields federal government employees who report waste, fraud, or abuse from retaliation by their employers. U.S. whistleblower protection statutes contain a specific exemption that limits protections for disclosures related to national security and intelligence matters.<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="34:05" />

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has described the law as containing a critical gap: national security employees are exempt from its protections. As Kiriakou explained, employees of agencies such as the [CIA](/wiki/cia), the Pentagon, the FBI, the DEA, and congressional intelligence oversight committees have no legally protected path to disclose wrongdoing.<Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="3:37" /> *"If you want to make a revelation, there is no way to make a protected revelation,"* Kiriakou said.<Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="4:11" />

Kiriakou noted that employees are officially directed to use their chain of command, but that avenue fails when the chain of command is itself the source of the illegal conduct. The only remaining option — going to the press — exposes the employee to criminal prosecution, financial ruin, and the destruction of their career.<Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="4:42" />

The case of [Thomas Drake](/wiki/tom-drake), a senior official at the NSA, illustrated the dysfunction of existing internal mechanisms. Drake exhausted every formal channel — the NSA inspector general, the general counsel, the Pentagon inspector general, and the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence — before being charged with nine felonies including seven counts of espionage.<Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="5:13" />

Kiriakou has called for a legislative fix that would extend meaningful protections to national security whistleblowers.<Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="4:42" /> Kiriakou says he is separately working with the [AI Whistleblower Initiative](/wiki/ai-whistleblower-initiative) on protections for the growing number of whistleblowers emerging from the AI industry.<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="1:35:20" />

## National security carve-out (News Beat)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) stresses that the [Whistleblower Protection Act](/wiki/whistleblower-protection-act) specifically exempts national-security whistleblowers: report waste, fraud, abuse, illegality or threats to public safety while working for the CIA, FBI, NSA, DOD, White House or Homeland Security, and *"you're probably going to go to prison"* — likely under the Espionage Act.<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="03:02" /><Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="03:35" />
