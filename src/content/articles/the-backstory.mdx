**The Backstory** is the daily radio programme [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) co-hosted with Lisa Stranahan, carried on 105.5 FM and AM 1390 alongside its online audience, and structured around guest segments with live call-ins.<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="00:11" />

Kiriakou opens editions in his own name and with his own credential: *"welcome back to the Backstory — I'm former CIA officer John Kiriakou."*<Cite s="2021-03-13-caleb-maupin-sanctions-us-efforts-in-eurasia" t="00:12" /><Cite s="2021-03-13-caleb-maupin-sanctions-us-efforts-in-eurasia" t="12:08" />

## On radio as a medium

When his co-host teased the format as old-fashioned, Kiriakou defended it with evidence from his previous programme: *"when Brian and I were doing Loud and Clear, he mentioned to me several times how surprised he was that taxi drivers would tell him that they listened to our show. So a lot of people do listen on terrestrial radio."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="00:44" />

The show's recurring guests include the journalist [Caleb Maupin](/wiki/caleb-maupin), whom Kiriakou introduces as *"one of the best informed people out there."*<Cite s="2021-03-13-caleb-maupin-sanctions-us-efforts-in-eurasia" t="00:12" />
