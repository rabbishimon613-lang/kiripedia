**The density of spies in Washington** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s answer to a viewer who asked whether anyone is still working on tracking and removing spies in the United States, whether it can be achieved, and whether the country will ever be free of foreign control.

## Who does the work

Kiriakou identifies the responsible organisation and the exclusivity of its mandate. *"So, the the FBI's counterintelligence division, that's literally all they do is to try to root out spies and to remove them or prosecute them."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="09:52" />

## Why it does not end

The task is described as structurally unfinishable. *"Um, it's an ongoing never-ending job because more and more spies are coming here every day."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="09:52" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="10:22" />

The reason is the concentration of targets. *"There are more spies in Washington DC than anywhere else in the world."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="10:22" />

## The limit

Kiriakou's answer to the second half of the question is negative, and the qualification he attaches is the whole argument. *"And um it's not possible to to win to root them all out. Unless you want to be North Korea."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="10:22" />

The implication is that a society that could actually clear itself of foreign intelligence officers would have to accept a level of internal control that would cost more than the espionage does. Asked to characterise the effort, Kiriakou accepted the description offered on the programme and repeated it: *"It's whack-a-ole. Yeah."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="10:22" />

## Working alongside allies

The same exchange turned to the Australian service. Kiriakou said he had never done anything operationally with the Australians, though he had briefed them a number of times — noting that as a [Five Eyes](/wiki/five-eyes) partner *"they're literally sitting there in the building."* Their focus, as he expected, was China, *"and that was never my thing."* His verdict on them was warm: *"They're quite good. They're quite a good service. Very good service."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="10:53" />
