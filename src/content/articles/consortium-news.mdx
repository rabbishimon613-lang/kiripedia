**Consortium News** is an independent investigative news outlet [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has written for. He says he went to work for the outlet out of respect for its editor, Joe Lauria — formerly of the Christian Science Monitor and, for roughly a decade, the United Nations correspondent for the Wall Street Journal — and for its founder, Robert Parry, a Polk Award-winning journalist who was one of the major forces behind breaking the [Iran-Contra](/wiki/iran-contra) story.<Cite s="2022-05-06-scott-horton-ep-5709" t="13:07" />

## The PayPal dispute

Kiriakou describes PayPal permanently shuttering Consortium News's account, initially seizing the $9,350 held in it. The company only released the funds after public complaints — and, even then, continued to refuse to reinstate the account.<Cite s="2022-05-06-scott-horton-ep-5709" t="14:43" />
