**Jim Moran**, a Democratic congressman from Virginia, intervened with the [Bureau of Prisons](/wiki/bureau-of-prisons) on behalf of his constituent [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) while Kiriakou was imprisoned at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto).

Kiriakou added a postscript to one [letter from Loretto](/wiki/letter-from-loretto) thanking Moran, who had sent a second letter to Bureau director [Charles Samuels](/wiki/charles-samuels) the previous week. Moran wrote that he had written the previous September asking that the Justice Department grant Kiriakou at least nine months of halfway-house time; that twelve months was well within Bureau guidelines; that he was disappointed to learn Kiriakou's return to society might be unnecessarily delayed; and that he was writing again to reiterate the importance of release to a halfway house as soon as possible, so that Kiriakou could be a father to his five children and resume productive contributions to society.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="36:24" />

Kiriakou notes the weight behind the request: Moran was a twelve-term congressman and a member of the House Appropriations Committee, which controls the Bureau of Prisons budget.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="36:58" />

The intervention did not succeed. Kiriakou records that he ultimately received no halfway-house time at all, not a single day.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="17:07" />
