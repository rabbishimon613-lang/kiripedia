**George J. Tenet** served as Director of Central Intelligence from 1997 to 2004, the longest serving DCI since [William J. Casey](/wiki/bill-casey). His tenure spanned the September 11 attacks and the launch of the [enhanced interrogation techniques](/wiki/enhanced-interrogation) program; in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account he is the figure most directly responsible for the agency's early-2000s institutional posture, and the patron — the *"rabbi"* in agency parlance — of [John Brennan](/wiki/john-brennan)'s subsequent rise.

## First meeting and the island-versus-mountain grudge

Kiriakou first met Tenet, then CIA deputy director, by chance in a dessert line at the Greek Festival at St. George Greek Orthodox Church in Bethesda, Maryland, where the two men discovered a shared love of *loukoumades* — fried dough balls slathered in honey and cinnamon — from the Greek island of Chios.<Cite s="2026-03-23-dead-drop-s1e20-changes" t="00:30" /> On learning that Kiriakou's family was from Rhodes while his own was from a mountain area of Greece now part of Albania, Tenet told the group around them, half-joking: *"He thinks he's better than I am. Because his people are from the islands and mine are from the mountains."*<Cite s="2026-03-23-dead-drop-s1e20-changes" t="02:39" /> The line was not a one-off. Over more than a decade — including years after Kiriakou had left the CIA — Tenet repeated the same grudge to colleagues and senior officials in Kiriakou's presence, among them Deputy National Security Advisor Stephen Hadley during an Iraq War–era briefing, and a friend named Dean at a black-tie Greek-American gala in New York years after Kiriakou's whistleblowing.<Cite s="2026-03-23-dead-drop-s1e20-changes" t="10:19" /><Cite s="2026-03-23-dead-drop-s1e20-changes" t="10:51" /> Tenet wrote in his own memoir — which Kiriakou says he has read twice — that he struggled with impostor syndrome, having grown up in an apartment above a grocery store before becoming CIA director; his brother, by contrast, became a prominent New York City cardiac surgeon and a major donor to the Greek Orthodox archdiocese.<Cite s="2026-03-23-dead-drop-s1e20-changes" t="08:42" />

## The toenail-picking briefing

Kiriakou recalls briefing Tenet on a Sunday during a period of intermittent U.S. bombing of Iraq over no-fly-zone and sanctions violations, alongside Deputy Director John McLaughlin — who, Kiriakou notes, always wore $2,000 suits — and Lieutenant General *"Soup"* Campbell. While Kiriakou gave the briefing, Tenet, dressed in a lumberjack shirt, torn jeans, and Timberland boots, removed his boots and socks and picked his toenails.<Cite s="2026-03-23-dead-drop-s1e20-changes" t="04:45" />

## The Brennan rabbi relationship

Per Kiriakou — who has known Brennan personally since 1990 — Brennan's institutional advancement inside the CIA was structurally dependent on Tenet's patronage. *"He had a rabbi in George Tenet. And the next thing you know, he's at the top of the heap."* The relationship is the institutional explanation Kiriakou offers for Brennan's rise despite what Kiriakou characterizes as a clear personality profile — *"the archetypal sociopath"* — that the agency's selection process should otherwise have flagged.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="47:36" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="48:07" />

Tenet also gave [Bob Grenier](/wiki/bob-grenier), his Iraq Mission Manager from 2002, direct personal access to the Director of Central Intelligence — one of the perquisites that came with the newly created position.<Cite s="2026-03-16-dead-drop-s1e19-torture" t="28:00" />

## EIT acceptance

In late October 2001 — at a cocktail party in the days following the September 11 attacks — Tenet personally accepted the pitch from contract psychologists [Mitchell and Jessen](/wiki/mitchell-and-jessen) for what became the [enhanced interrogation techniques](/wiki/enhanced-interrogation) program. The agency signed the Mitchell and Jessen contract in January 2002. The first detainee on whom the program was applied was [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah), captured by [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s team in Pakistan in March 2002.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="55:34" /><Cite s="2026-01-16-julian-dorey-french-intelligence" t="56:05" />

## The Five Eyes file-opening

In the early-2000s reorganization of relationships among the Anglosphere intelligence services after September 11, Tenet personally ordered that the [Five Eyes](/wiki/five-eyes) — the U.S., U.K., Canadian, Australian, and New Zealand services — *"literally open the files"* to each other. The directive established the contemporary practice of Five Eyes liaison officers sitting at each other's headquarters.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:12:35" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:13:05" />

## The "prism of smoke" quote

Tenet's memoir contains the paraphrased line cited repeatedly by Kiriakou in his characterization of the post-9/11 U.S. national-security mood: *"All major foreign policy decisions after 9/11 were viewed through the prism of smoke from the World Trade Center and the Pentagon."* Kiriakou treats the line as accurate description — *"absolutely true"* — and applies it to his own framing of the moral conduct of senior CIA personnel of the period, including [Jose Rodriguez](/wiki/jose-rodriguez), [James Mitchell](/wiki/mitchell-and-jessen), and [Bruce Jessen](/wiki/mitchell-and-jessen).<Cite s="2023-11-26-dalton-fischer-enhanced-interrogation" t="10:30" />

## Kiriakou's broader assessment

In the Kiriakou framing, Tenet belongs to the senior-CIA-figures-of-the-post-9/11-period cohort — alongside [John McLaughlin](#), [Michael Morell](#), [Jose Rodriguez](/wiki/jose-rodriguez), and Rick Pavitt — whom Kiriakou holds responsible for *"a lot of crimes that were committed in the name of national security."* Kiriakou treats Tenet as categorically distinct from [John Brennan](/wiki/john-brennan), however: Brennan, in Kiriakou's framework, *"plotted against an elected president of the United States,"* which is a different and more dangerous category of conduct than the EIT-era senior leadership's failures.<Cite s="2026-01-16-julian-dorey-french-intelligence" t="33:50" />

## *"Have they lost their minds?"*

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) served as [George Tenet](/wiki/george-tenet)'s personal notetaker at the [Principals Committee meeting](/wiki/principals-committee-meeting) on the eve of the Iraq invasion, February 2003. Tenet was the sole participant seated at the conference-room table; Kiriakou sat directly behind him.<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="39:00" />

When General Tommy Franks concluded his order-of-battle briefing with the statement *"If all goes as planned, we can be in Tehran by August,"* Tenet discreetly reached forward and turned off his microphone. He turned to Kiriakou and said: *"Did he say Tehran or did he say Baghdad?"* Kiriakou confirmed: *"He said Tehran."* Tenet said: *"Have they lost their minds?"* He then turned the microphone back on and sat quietly for the remainder of the briefing. In a separate telling, Kiriakou attributes the same reaction — *"Have these people lost their minds?"* — to Tenet on hearing the CENTCOM commander's Tehran remark, with Deputy Director [Jim Pavitt](/wiki/jim-pavitt) separately waving off the idea back at the office: *"They're not going to invade Iran — these people don't understand anything about the region."*<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="40:00" /><Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="40:30" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:22:16" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:22:47" />

## The Pollard ultimatum to Clinton

When President Bill Clinton came close to releasing [Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard) at Israel's repeated urging, [George Tenet](/wiki/george-tenet) delivered an ultimatum: *"If you release Pollard, I will resign, and the head of every intelligence agency in the American government will resign."* Clinton did not release Pollard.<Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:02:30" /><Cite s="2025-12-04-dave-smith-part-of-the-problem-1335" t="1:03:00" />

## Handing Abu Zubaydah to the CIA

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says CIA director [George Tenet](/wiki/george-tenet) went to President Bush on July 31, 2002 and asked him to turn [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) — then cooperating with the FBI's [Ali Soufan](/wiki/ali-soufan) — over to the CIA, which Bush, *"because he didn't know what was going on,"* did.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:28:42" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:29:13" />

## 'Slam dunk' and 'have these people lost their minds'

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says [George Tenet](/wiki/george-tenet) told President Bush the Iraq WMD case was a *"slam dunk"* with no supporting analysis behind it.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="12:02" /> As Tenet's notetaker in a pre-invasion secure teleconference chaired by [Dick Cheney](/wiki/dick-cheney), Kiriakou heard the CENTCOM commander say forces could be *"in Tehran by August"*; Tenet muted his microphone, asked Kiriakou *"did he say Tehran or Baghdad,"* and muttered, *"Have these people lost their minds?"*<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="17:42" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="18:13" />

## 'A war criminal' with a book advance (News Beat)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) calls [George Tenet](/wiki/george-tenet) — *"a nice guy"* and hero of the Greek-American community — a war criminal who, rather than face accountability, received a $6 million book advance and a seat on the board of a New York vulture fund.<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="22:29" />
