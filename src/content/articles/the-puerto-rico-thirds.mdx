**The Puerto Rico thirds** is the account of the island's politics [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) reports having been given on the ground, offered during a discussion of a Puerto Rican independence flag carried at an American sporting event.

## The conversation

*"the last time I went to Puerto Rico, um, I was talking with a, um, with a guy, he's kind of mid-level Puerto Rican government official. And I asked him to explain to me what people's positions were on, um, on independence."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-deprogram-ted-rall-bad-bunny-epstein-files-and-more" t="02:38" />

The answer was a clean split. *"he said, 'Oh, we are very evenly divided.' He said, 'Fully a third of Puerto Ricans want independence. A third of Puerto Ricans want statehood. Uh, to be to be a member of the United States as a state. And a third of Puerto Ricans want to just leave things exactly the way they are right now.'"*<Cite s="2026-02-11-deprogram-ted-rall-bad-bunny-epstein-files-and-more" t="03:09" />

The official's own conclusion followed: *"And because of that, nothing's ever going to change."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-deprogram-ted-rall-bad-bunny-epstein-files-and-more" t="03:09" />

## Kiriakou's own position

He volunteers his preference without claiming standing for it — *"I mean, yeah, uh, personally, if I were Puerto Rican, I think I'd want independence"* — and agrees with his interlocutor's observation that the flag at the event sat awkwardly beside a celebration of Puerto Rican culture as part of the United States.<Cite s="2026-02-11-deprogram-ted-rall-bad-bunny-epstein-files-and-more" t="03:09" />

He then draws the comparison that appears to interest him more. *"I never understood why the Scots voted not to be independent from from the Brits"* — a result that, he says, stunned him: *"I really believed that was going to be it. It was going to be I thought it wasn't even going to be close."* His grounds are economic: *"not like the Scots couldn't handle themselves economically. They have a viable economy."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-deprogram-ted-rall-bad-bunny-epstein-files-and-more" t="03:39" />
