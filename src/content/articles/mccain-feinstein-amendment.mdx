The McCain-Feinstein amendment, sponsored by Senator John McCain, formally and permanently banned torture, making it illegal under any circumstances. <Cite s="2026-02-18-barracks-media-inc-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-inside-the-cia-s-tortu" t="52:59:00" />

## A role from prison (Judging Freedom)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he was *"very proud"* to play a role in passage of the [McCain-Feinstein Amendment](/wiki/mccain-feinstein-amendment), and recalls learning from prison — six weeks before release — that the [Senate torture report](/wiki/senate-torture-report) had vindicated him and that [John McCain](/wiki/john-mccain) had called him out on the Senate floor.<Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="11:27" /><Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="11:59" />
