Chelsea Manning is a crucial source for WikiLeaks' 2010 releases, providing significant information about US military activities. <Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="01:07:00" />

After Chelsea Manning's document release, John Kiriakou personally searched for his social security number in the leaked documents. This was a direct result of Manning's actions as a whistleblower. <Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="24:45:00" />

## Emergence as a whistleblower

Manning's emergence as a whistleblower was a significant topic of discussion within secret spaces, and her actions were part of an ongoing campaign to smear [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange). <Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="15:49:00" />

## Declining to take it to the Supreme Court (Scott Horton, 2021)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says [Chelsea Manning](/wiki/chelsea-manning) was one of only two people with standing — the other being [Jeffrey Sterling](/wiki/jeffrey-sterling) — to appeal an [Espionage Act](/wiki/espionage-act) conviction to the Supreme Court and try to have the statute ruled unconstitutional. Manning, he says, *"just couldn't do it… she just didn't have it in her to go all the way to the Supreme Court."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="37:32" />
