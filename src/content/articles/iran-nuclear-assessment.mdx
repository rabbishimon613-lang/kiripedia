[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has stated his assessment that the US-Israeli military campaign against Iran was a war of choice driven by Israeli political pressure rather than by genuine intelligence indicating an Iranian nuclear weapons threat. He has described two National Intelligence Estimates contradicting that threat assessment, the [CIA](/wiki/cia)'s structural intelligence gap on Iran, and what he characterized as the nuclear-deterrence paradox underlying the conflict.

## Two National Intelligence Estimates — no nuclear weapons program

Kiriakou stated that the [CIA](/wiki/cia) produced two National Intelligence Estimates — the highest-level publication of the U.S. intelligence community — both concluding that Iran did not have a nuclear weapons program. He also noted that Supreme Leader Ayatollah Khamenei had issued a fatwa in the 1980s forbidding the creation of such a program. Iran was enriching uranium, in his telling, but not to anything approaching weapons-grade levels.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="39:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="40:00" />

## The war of choice

Kiriakou characterized the U.S. entry into conflict with Iran as the result of decades of pressure from Israeli Prime Ministers. In his description, every Israeli Prime Minister since the 1980s had come to Washington and, regardless of which president was in office, made the same request: *"Please bomb Iran. Please bomb Iran. Please bomb Iran."* Every president refused — until, in Kiriakou's assessment, the current one was susceptible to that pressure.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="38:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="39:00" />

He argued that Israeli national interests do not necessarily coincide with American national interests, and that if Israel has a problem with Iran, that is for Israel to resolve: *"I think it's high time that we start acting in the best interests of the United States, rather than in the best interests of Israel."*<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="40:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="40:30" />

## CIA intelligence gap on Iran

Kiriakou described the [CIA](/wiki/cia) as having a demonstrably weak human-intelligence posture inside Iran — what he called a *"denied area."* The absence of an American Embassy in Tehran since 1979 means CIA officers cannot operate under diplomatic cover, and cannot approach Iranian government officials for recruitment in the conventional way. The agency has consequently become dependent on liaison intelligence — which in practice means [Mossad](/wiki/mossad).<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="43:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="44:00" />

In his description, [Mossad](/wiki/mossad) excels at targeting intelligence: they recruit Afghan refugees inside Iran for approximately ten dollars a month to report on the locations of generals and nuclear scientists, providing the coordinates needed for cruise-missile targeting. However, the day-to-day operational and policy intelligence on the Iranian government comes from Iranian Jews living in the West and from the Mujahedin-e Khalq (MEK).<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="44:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="44:30" />

## MEK — former terrorist group

Kiriakou described the MEK as having been listed on the State Department's international terrorist organizations list through the Obama administration. The group had murdered an American ambassador in Tehran in the 1970s and attempted to murder an American general in the same decade. It was removed from the terrorist list and is now receiving Israeli funding. Kiriakou's characterization: the MEK has a direct vested interest in the United States going to war with Iran because they want to take power there. Their intelligence therefore cannot be treated as disinterested.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="45:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="45:30" />

## Trump fired the Joint Chiefs

Kiriakou stated that, in his experience, the people most opposed to war are the members of the Joint Chiefs of Staff — those who have seen combat are the least willing to enter it again. He stated that President Trump fired all the generals on the Joint Chiefs and replaced them with hand-picked individuals willing to accept Israeli intelligence and proceed to war with Iran.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="45:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="46:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="46:30" />

He also cited a *Wall Street Journal* reporter who asked Trump who his closest advisor was on politico-military issues. Trump's answer: *"I don't have an advisor. I just trust my gut."* Kiriakou called this *"very, very dangerous."*<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="43:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="43:30" />

## Dimona bombed — casualties classified

Kiriakou described the Iranians as having proven *"far tougher than anybody expected"* and noted that the Iron Dome had proven less effective than anticipated. He reported that Iran had bombed Dimona — the Israeli facility where uranium is enriched for [Israel's nuclear weapons program](/wiki/israeli-nuclear-program). He also stated that it was a crime in Israel, punishable by up to ten years in prison, to reveal any bomb damage assessment information, including the number of Israeli military and civilian casualties.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="47:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="48:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="48:30" />

## The nuclear-deterrence paradox

Kiriakou described a dinner conversation with several full professors who serve as paid Pentagon advisors. These individuals expressed genuine concern that Israel might use a nuclear weapon as a last resort if the United States withdrew from the conflict, leaving Israel alone against Iran.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="47:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="47:30" />

One of the professors articulated what Kiriakou called a fascinating response to the broader deterrence question. He noted that North Korea does not appear in these conversations — because North Korea has a nuclear weapon and therefore no one will attack it. His conclusion: *"The only reason that the United States and Israel attacked Iran is because they didn't yet have a nuclear weapon. If they had, there would have been no war, because that would have been the deterrent."*<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="50:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="50:30" />

Kiriakou relayed this without endorsement but noted the internal logic: the very absence of a weapon was the condition that made the attack possible.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="50:30" /><Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="51:00" />

## *"The end of the American empire"*

Kiriakou stated his view that if the United States allowed Israel to use a nuclear weapon, or used one itself, *"that's going to be the end of the American Empire."* He cited a satirical *Onion* headline: *"Chinese happy to sit back and watch US destroy itself."* He characterized this as capturing what he believed was actually occurring.<Cite s="2026-03-25-mark-bouris-cia-whistleblower-us-israel-invaded-iran" t="51:30" />
