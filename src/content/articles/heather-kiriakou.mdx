Heather Kiriakou is John Kiriakou's wife and was herself a career CIA officer. He has described her as a senior officer and really one of the best analysts in the entire intelligence community. <Cite s="2013-02-07-government-accountability-full-video-kiriakou-portrait-unveiling-busboy" t="32:13" />

## The ABC News interview

She was present for the moment that ended both their careers. Asked whether he had understood at the time that his December 2007 interview with [Brian Ross](/wiki/brian-ross) would change his life, Kiriakou said he really did not think he had done anything wrong, and that his wife had gone with him that day to ABC News and sat just off camera. When he finished, he asked her how he had done. She said great. He asked whether he had said anything classified. She said no, you did great. <Cite s="2023-03-20-trt-world-the-innerview-the-cia-spy-who-blew-the-whistle-on-torture" t="04:08" /> He had no idea, he says, that it was going to become a major international story within a day or so — or that the CIA would report him to the [FBI](/wiki/fbi) the very next day. <Cite s="2023-03-20-trt-world-the-innerview-the-cia-spy-who-blew-the-whistle-on-torture" t="04:08" />

## Losing the job

At the February 2013 unveiling of his [portrait](/wiki/americans-who-tell-the-truth-portrait), Kiriakou told the room that Heather had lost her job at the CIA just because she was married to him. He said he was sorry about that and that it was really the wrong thing for them to have done, adding — with a qualification he has repeated elsewhere — that their lives were better now that neither of them was connected to the agency. She had been, he said, completely rock solid in support of him, and he could not possibly have got through it without her. She could not attend the unveiling; she had the flu. <Cite s="2013-02-07-government-accountability-full-video-kiriakou-portrait-unveiling-busboy" t="32:13" /> <Cite s="2013-02-07-government-accountability-full-video-kiriakou-portrait-unveiling-busboy" t="32:45" />

## On September 11

The two were already together on the day of the September 11 attacks, when she was his girlfriend and also a CIA officer. Kiriakou has described abandoning his car in traffic on the George Washington Parkway, meeting her at his apartment, watching the Pentagon burn from the roof, and going out to try to give blood before the queues defeated them — at which point he said this was ridiculous and they needed to go back to work, and they walked back to the car and returned to headquarters together. <Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-real-news-reality-asserts-itself" t="10:55" />

## Accused of leaking to her husband

Before the prosecution, the pressure on Kiriakou reached his wife directly. She was then a senior CIA officer, and the agency accused her of passing classified information to him for the purpose of an op-ed he had written. His answer was documentary: the material in the piece had come from two United Press International reports and from a South American foreign ministry''s website. *"And they would back off."* He describes the episode as part of a settled routine rather than an isolated incident — *"this sort of became our life"* — one that also involved [FBI](/wiki/fbi) surveillance of the household and her being summoned to the agency''s office of security.<Cite s="2015-01-09-terrorpedia-video-center-aggregator-channel-likely-re-upload" t="32:06" /><Cite s="2015-01-09-terrorpedia-video-center-aggregator-channel-likely-re-upload" t="32:37" />
