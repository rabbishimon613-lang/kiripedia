[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was posted to **Bahrain** from August 1, 1994 to 1996, arriving with his wife Joanne and their 16-month-old son Chris. He served under U.S. State Department cover as second secretary for economic affairs, not declared as [CIA](/wiki/cia) — his language skills and the sudden centrality of the Iraq-Kuwait border to world events, following [Saddam Hussein](/wiki/saddam-hussein)'s 1990 invasion of [Kuwait](/wiki/kuwait-invasion-intelligence), had shaped his early CIA career and put him in the Gulf.<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="01:35" /><Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="02:39" />

## A small, water-rich oasis

Kiriakou describes 1990s Bahrain as fed by an ancient, prehistoric water table running underground from the Atlantic Ocean beneath the whole of Africa before surfacing in Bahrain; as recently as the 1970s, natural springs gushed water like oil. By his posting, overdevelopment had dropped the water table 35 feet and left the water brackish.<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="03:11" /> Attractions were sparse — a history museum, a Quran museum, and the roughly 3,000-year-old "Tree of Life" — reflecting a country Kiriakou says was, in 1996, the same size and population as Pittsburgh.<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="03:45" />

## Diplomatic-corps life

Kiriakou played right field in the American diplomatic corps softball league, noting the Marine Security Guards were the league's power hitters while he "hit for average, not power."<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="04:51" /> Despite his junior rank, he was invited to nearly every dinner hosted by Bahrain's newly arrived British ambassador — previously deputy chief of mission in Ankara — while American Ambassador David Ransom often was not; asked why, the ambassador told him, *"I like you. You're fun. You tell interesting stories."*<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="05:24" /> After returning from Bahrain in 1996, other U.S. agencies — DIA, NSA, and the FBI — sent officers bound for Bahrain to consult Kiriakou because of his granular knowledge of the country.<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="06:58" />

Bahrain has hosted the U.S. Navy's Fifth Fleet since 1976, the naval component of Central Command, headquartered at MacDill Air Force Base outside Tampa.<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="07:30" />

## Sectarian tension

Kiriakou describes Bahrain's underlying tension in the mid-1990s: a Sunni royal family, the [Al Khalifa](/wiki/isa-bin-salman-al-khalifa), ruling a population roughly 75% Shia. The royal family's policy of naturalizing Sunni Muslims from Syria, India, Bangladesh, and Pakistan fueled Shia resentment at being demographically diluted — tension that fed directly into the [first Intifada](/wiki/bahrain-intifada-1994) that began two months into his posting.<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="08:02" />

## Friendship with the future Crown Prince

Kiriakou became close friends with Salman bin Hamad Al Khalifa — later Bahrain's Crown Prince, at the time heir presumptive, holding ceremonial posts as president of the Bahrain Center for Studies and Research and patron of the Bahrain Environmental Protection Association — after the two overlapped studying in Washington.<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="30:49" /> The two once went, Salman in Western disguise he believed made him unrecognizable, to a low-grade nightclub near the Navy base featuring Polish dancing girls and a Filipino cover band, where patrons paid a woman at the foot of the stage in dinars for flower garlands to signal interest in a performer.<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="32:27" /> Salman grew infatuated with one Polish dancer and asked Kiriakou if he thought she'd sleep with him — *"I don't have the guts"* — despite already having two wives; nothing came of it.<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="34:03" /> Salman attended Kiriakou's going-away party at the ambassador's residence with his first wife, and the two still exchange text messages and jokes decades later.<Cite s="2025-11-24-dead-drop-s1e5-singin-in-bahrain" t="34:33" />
