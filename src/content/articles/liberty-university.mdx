**Liberty University** is an evangelical university in Lynchburg, Virginia, which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes as *"the largest evangelical university in the world."* He taught there for several years as an adjunct professor of intelligence studies in the Jesse Helms School of Government.<Cite s="2026-07-01-deep-focus-network-deep-focus-network" t="7:49" />

## The hiring

Kiriakou says he raised the obvious objection himself when the job was offered: *"Are you sure you want me? You and I probably disagree on 95% of the issues. Why would you want me to be a professor?"* The answer came *"without missing a beat"* from the dean of the Jesse Helms School of Government — *"because torture's not Christian."* Kiriakou's reply: *"You know what? I'll take the job."*<Cite s="2026-07-01-deep-focus-network-deep-focus-network" t="7:49" />

## The standing argument

He says he *"really really enjoyed"* the post, and that it came with a permanent disagreement: *"we clashed constantly over this issue of Middle Eastern Christians."* He took the institution to task whenever he heard talk of sending a delegation to convert people to Christianity in Palestine, on the ground that the mission inverts its own premise — *"This is where Christianity began. You guys are snuffing out Palestinian Christians."*<Cite s="2026-07-01-deep-focus-network-deep-focus-network" t="8:19" />

The exchange he offers as the clearest illustration is with a student bound for a mission. Asked what she would do, she said she would bring people to Christ; asked where, she said Macedonia. His response: *"But they're already Christians in Macedonia. They're Orthodox like me."* The premise he reads out of the answer is that the tradition does not count its own predecessors as Christians at all — that they *"don't consider it their type of Christian."*<Cite s="2026-07-01-deep-focus-network-deep-focus-network" t="8:50" /><Cite s="2026-07-01-deep-focus-network-deep-focus-network" t="9:21" />

He also identifies a contradiction he considers structural to the alliance the university's constituency sustains: evangelicals *"want eventually for all the Jews that they support to convert to Christianity. The Jews absolutely do not want that."* The two ends are incompatible and, in his description, simply deferred — *"Let's not talk about what happens after the rapture."*<Cite s="2026-07-01-deep-focus-network-deep-focus-network" t="9:21" />
