John Kiriakou says Mark Zuckerberg was one of the first tech figures to fly to Germany and meet with then-Chancellor Angela Merkel, around the time of Barack Obama's election, to discuss hate-speech policy and help define what would count as hate speech on Facebook. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="46:08" />

Kiriakou raises the meeting while objecting that it should not be up to Zuckerberg to decide a private citizen's position on Israel, though he agrees free speech should not extend to posting content like child pornography or threats on someone's life. <Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="46:08" />
