**Jake Tapper** is the CNN anchor who became, improbably, part of [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s prison survival strategy.

## The Loretto interview

Kiriakou's policy inside was to say yes to every interview request — and Tapper drove to [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto) to interview him for CNN, part of a media wave that included NPR (three times), Playboy, The Economist, the Atlantic Monthly, and the broadcast networks.<Cite s="2023-02-08-fortress-on-a-hill-john-kiriakou-ep-133-probe-dup-vs-6kmi7tkpf7q" t="30:46" /><Cite s="2021-10-13-sharon-kyle-la-progressive-interview-with-john-kiriakou" t="26:33" /><Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="33:34" /> [Arianna Huffington](/wiki/arianna-huffington) came too, with two young journalists.<Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="36:49" />

That access became armor. When guards threatened him, Kiriakou's standing reply was: *"Yeah? Well, let's see what Jake Tapper has to say about it. How about that — why don't we give him a call and see what he has to say?"* And they would back off.<Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="35:09" /><Cite s="2018-06-26-rdap-dan-rdap-dan" t="02:16" />

## "Jake was different"

Kiriakou generally files CNN under the go-along-to-get-along mainstream.<Cite s="2022-04-11-katie-halper-katie-halper" t="32:34" /> But Tapper, he says, was different: Tapper asked President [Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) at a press conference why he had insisted on prosecuting Kiriakou when Kiriakou had exposed a crime — *"and Obama just walked away from the podium."* The two have stayed in touch since Kiriakou came home.<Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="37:20" />
