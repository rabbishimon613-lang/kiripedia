**Code Pink** is the women-led peace group co-founded by Medea Benjamin — first an oddity in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s establishment career, then one of the pillars of his defense committee, and finally an employer of sorts.

## First contact

Working for [Senator Kerry](/wiki/senate-foreign-relations-committee), Kiriakou produced a hearing and noticed the audience filling with women in pink t-shirts. *"Who are all these women?"* he asked; a neighbor explained it was Code Pink, a peace group — Kiriakou, sequestered at the CIA for years, had never heard of them. When the chanting started he realized he was smiling, and that nobody else was. Kerry turned around: *"You think this is funny?"* — and told him to keep his opinions to himself. That was the first time he ever heard of Medea Benjamin.<Cite s="2015-11-12-quakerhouse" t="51:25" /><Cite s="2015-11-12-quakerhouse" t="51:56" /><Cite s="2015-11-12-quakerhouse" t="52:59" /><Cite s="2015-11-12-quakerhouse" t="53:30" />

## The defense committee years

When Kiriakou was arrested, *"Code Pink was right there next to me"* — and before he reported to prison they threw his going-away party at the Hay-Adams, Washington's finest hotel, gathering the city's hard left in one room.<Cite s="2015-11-12-quakerhouse" t="53:30" /><Cite s="2015-11-12-quakerhouse" t="54:03" /> When the [Bureau of Prisons](/wiki/bureau-of-prisons) moved to silence his letters, Code Pink asked all its members to call the director's office — part of the campaign, alongside Oliver Stone's and John Cusack's *"hands off John Kiriakou"* tweets, that logged 1,600 calls and backed the Bureau off.<Cite s="2018-06-26-rdap-dan-rdap-dan" t="03:49" /><Cite s="2017-05-17-strand-book-store" t="22:57" />

He has since pointed to Code Pink as a model of activism that persists past defeat — they kept up the Kavanaugh fight after the vote was lost, *"and it made the news... you reach more people"*<Cite s="2018-10-22-unity4j-john-kiriakou-and-lee-stranahan-discuss" t="28:19" /> — and credits Medea Benjamin and Code Pink with forcing the drone war into public attention.<Cite s="2020-12-23-codepink" t="41:41" /> He stood with them at the 2019 [Venezuelan embassy](/wiki/venezuelan-embassy-2019) defense.<Cite s="2019-04-25-david-hartful-john-kiriakou-speaks-at-venezuelan-embassy-04" t="23:32" />

## Code Pink Congress

By late 2020 the relationship had formalized: Kiriakou volunteered as coordinator of Code Pink Congress, tracking five to fifteen pieces of legislation, organizing Capitol Hill call campaigns, and joining the group's fights against hawkish appointments.<Cite s="2020-12-23-codepink" t="04:11" /><Cite s="2020-12-23-codepink" t="07:18" />
