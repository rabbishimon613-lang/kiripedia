[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described a double agent operation in which he posed as a CIA station chief in a Middle East posting, handled the target across eight months, and executed an arrest inside a Marriott Hotel room — then used a hand-drawn map to locate a hidden weapons cache that allowed the [CIA](/wiki/cia) to destroy an entire terrorist network.

## The setup

A colleague who was the actual station chief had a problematic asset: a double agent employed by an American defense contractor as an engineer, secretly tasked by a hostile intelligence service to identify the station chief for assassination. The colleague knew the agent was a double; the agent did not know that the [CIA](/wiki/cia) knew. He needed someone from outside the country — with no presence in-country — to pose as the station chief, handle the target, and force a confrontation. He called Kiriakou.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:34:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:34:30" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:35:00" />

Kiriakou operated under the alias *"Nick."* He gave the agent a triband cell phone that rang with a local number regardless of where Kiriakou actually was in the world — meaning the agent always believed Kiriakou was in-country. He also required the agent to rent a PO box for emergency communications.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:36:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:36:30" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:37:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:37:30" />

The agent — who had driven directly to the enemy embassy after their very first meeting, rather than running a [surveillance detection route](/wiki/surveillance-detection-route) — had been under CIA surveillance the whole time, confirming his double status.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:37:30" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:38:00" />

## The order to kill

After eight months, the actual chief called: the hostile service had given the double agent orders to kill Kiriakou in their next meeting. Kiriakou proposed the trap: use the Marriott Hotel, where all Marriotts worldwide share the same floor plan, with adjacent rooms connected by a pass-through door. Kiriakou would be in the bathroom. The double would knock, enter, and be taken down simultaneously by the room-service team and a liaison entry from the connecting room.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:39:30" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:40:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:40:30" />

The enemy service anticipated the move: when Kiriakou arrived at the hotel, surveillance reported three enemy teams already in the lobby. If the double failed, the teams outside would shoot Kiriakou as he tried to leave through the front entrance. The CIA team shifted to an exit through the back of the building.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:41:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:41:30" />

## The arrest

When the double called from downstairs, Kiriakou told him to come to the eighth floor — the top floor — and then to the roof. The double refused, repeatedly: *"I don't want to go up to the roof. I don't like this."* Kiriakou kept insisting. The double finally followed him to the roof. Kiriakou said: *"Stay here five minutes and meet me in room 515."* Five minutes later the double knocked. Kiriakou emerged from the bathroom and tackled him. Liaison partners and security personnel entered from the connecting room simultaneously. The double shouted *"Allahu akbar"* and *"I'll kill you."* Kiriakou's response: *"Did you think I didn't know you were a double agent? Did you think I'm so unprofessional that I didn't know you came here to kill me tonight?"*<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:42:30" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:43:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:43:30" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:44:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:44:30" />

## Demerol, the ambulance, and the children's map

The double was given a Demerol injection, placed on a gurney under a sheet — hotels have deaths regularly — and removed via the service entrance to an ambulance waiting at the cargo door. The enemy service teams in the lobby waited for a result that never came. He was delivered to the host intelligence service's headquarters and placed in a cell.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:44:30" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:45:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:45:30" />

He refused to speak. The CIA searched his home after staging a propane spill to evacuate the cul-de-sac: fire department called, all eight houses cleared, team broke in. The safe contained only a hand-drawn map — *"a crude children's drawing with an X."* They drove an hour south, went off-road, followed the paces, and found an abandoned bunker containing the full weapons cache: mines, grenades, rocket launchers. The terrorist network was destroyed that night. The double agent received life without parole.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:46:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:46:30" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:47:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:47:30" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:48:00" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:48:30" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="2:49:00" />

## The monthly sting and the weapons cache

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes flying to the Middle East monthly to impersonate a station chief and run a [double agent](/wiki/double-agent-marriott-trap) who did not know he was blown. When a sister agency warned the man had been ordered to kill Kiriakou, he proposed the trap: adjoining Marriott rooms, the door propped, a takedown when the agent knocked — with fourteen armed intelligence officers filling the lobby against seven of the enemy's.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="26:29" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="27:31" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="28:34" /> After the capture, a liaison partner sedated the man with a syringe of Demerol and they wheeled him out in a freight elevator as a supposed corpse. A map recovered from his safe — *"like a cartoon,"* with an X — led to an abandoned bunker packed to the roof with the group's entire weapons cache.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="29:37" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="30:09" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="33:52" />
