## Car hacking, smart TVs, and Marble

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) cites the [Vault 7](/wiki/vault-7) disclosures via [Joshua Schulte](/wiki/joshua-schulte): the CIA can remotely take control of any computerized car and drive it off a bridge, turn any smart TV's speaker into a live microphone even while off, and — through the [Marble Framework](/wiki/marble-framework) — plant Farsi or Cyrillic code to blame Iran or Russia for its own hacks.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:34:26" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:34:57" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:35:29" />

## CIA spying on NSA (HOPE 2025)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says the [Vault 7](/wiki/vault-7) documents revealed not only car-hacking and smart-TV bugging but that the CIA was electronically spying on the NSA — something he *"had to read twice."* The agency's spokesmen, he says, dismissed it on MSNBC as a *"training exercise."*<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="41:24" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="41:57" />
