The **Office of Leadership Analysis** — internally known as LDA, for Leadership Development Analysis — was the [CIA](/wiki/cia) analytic office founded by psychiatrist [Jerome Post](/wiki/jerome-post), who pushed for [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) to be placed there at the start of his agency career.<Cite s="2025-11-03-dead-drop-s1e3-golden-boy" t="02:41" />

## A quiet first assignment

On his first day, Kiriakou's boss assigned him to cover Iraq and Kuwait — a portfolio the boss described as so obscure that the office sometimes went days without a single incoming cable.<Cite s="2025-11-03-dead-drop-s1e3-golden-boy" t="02:41" />
