[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described the physical evidence in the RFK assassination as internally contradictory. Sirhan Sirhan's gun held eight rounds. The crime scene contained more bullets than eight — a count Kiriakou cited as decisive proof that a second shooter was present.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:05:00" /> In a separate telling, he put it as investigators recovering one more bullet than Sirhan's gun could hold.<Cite s="2025-03-29-lionel-nation" t="29:25" />

Los Angeles County coroner Thomas Noguchi, who conducted the autopsy, found that the shot that killed Kennedy entered from behind and below his right ear at a range of one to two inches. Witnesses and photographs, however, placed Sirhan in front of Kennedy, and contemporaneous accounts established that Sirhan never got within several feet of him.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:05:30" /> Kiriakou has also described the finding as powder deposition at the back of Kennedy's head, despite Sirhan firing from across the room.<Cite s="2025-03-29-lionel-nation" t="29:25" />

Kiriakou noted that a hotel security guard named *"Caesar"* appears on video standing directly behind Kennedy at the moment of the shooting. National Geographic tracked the man down years later.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:06:00" />

Sirhan Sirhan remains imprisoned. Kiriakou described his continued incarceration as indefensible given the physical evidence. Kiriakou also noted a possible connection to [MK-Ultra](/wiki/mk-ultra): Sirhan has said he has no memory of the shooting, and Kiriakou cited this in the context of [CIA](/wiki/cia) behavioral-control research.<Cite s="2025-06-04-tucker-carlson-cia-torture-mk-ultra-911" t="1:06:30" />

## A formative memory

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recalls his mother crying all day when Robert Kennedy was shot and killed, part of the 1968–69 upheaval — with the King assassination and the [Kent State shooting](/wiki/kent-state) — that he says formed his political conscience as a boy.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:44:45" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:45:16" /> In a separate telling, he traces the same memory to a couple of months after the King assassination: his mother crying as she told him Robert Kennedy had been killed, part of what he calls a *"nightmarish"* 1968.<Cite s="2026-05-20-covert-operations-insight" t="42:55" />

Kiriakou has also said, separately, that he believes the FBI — not James Earl Ray alone — killed Martin Luther King Jr.<Cite s="2025-03-29-lionel-nation" t="29:25" />
