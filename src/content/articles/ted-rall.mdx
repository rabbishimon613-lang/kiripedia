**Ted Rall** is an American editorial cartoonist and a two-time Pulitzer Prize finalist.<Cite s="2026-05-25-john-kiriakou-podcast-reveals-terrifying-cia-technology" t="1:01:06" /> He is the co-host with [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) of *Deprogram*, a daily news-commentary podcast launched in September 2025 — *"going every day starting next week at five o'clock on YouTube … far more of a news show, but it's light at the same time."*<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="4:11:03" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="4:11:34" /><Cite s="2026-05-25-john-kiriakou-podcast-reveals-terrifying-cia-technology" t="1:00:36" />

## Deprogram

*Deprogram* is one of two YouTube channels Kiriakou hosts. Asked in October 2025 to name them, he described the larger of the two as *Deep Focus with John Kiriakou* — long-form interview conversations — and the other as *"The DeProgram Show. That's with John Kiriakou and Ted Rall. We do that every morning and we talk about the day's news."*<Cite s="2025-10-25-dialogue-works-putin-warns-staggering-response-to-nato-iran" t="00:35" /> The five-o'clock evening slot announced at launch had by then moved to a weekday-morning broadcast.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="4:11:03" /><Cite s="2025-10-25-dialogue-works-putin-warns-staggering-response-to-nato-iran" t="00:35" />

The show's range is the day's news rather than intelligence subjects alone. Discussing the possibility of a modern gold confiscation, Kiriakou cited a conversation with Rall the previous day about the fixed $35-an-ounce gold price of their childhoods under Richard Nixon, and the price's float after Nixon lifted the peg.<Cite s="2026-02-09-epic-real-estate" t="30:06" /><Cite s="2026-02-09-epic-real-estate" t="30:37" />

## Fishback endorsement

A February 2026 post on Kiriakou's account urging Florida Republicans to *"consider [James Fishback](/wiki/james-fishback)"* for governor originated in a segment done live with Rall. Pressed on it, Kiriakou said the post was made by his assistant, that he deleted it, and that his on-air remark had been *"a stupid thing to say"* — he had seen only a clip of Fishback attacking AIPAC and knew nothing else about him.<Cite s="2026-06-10-zeteo-abolish-the-cia" t="42:17" /><Cite s="2026-06-10-zeteo-abolish-the-cia" t="42:47" /><Cite s="2026-06-11-eb-flow-mehdi-hasan-presses-john-kiriakou-about-his-p" t="16:34" />

## Central Asia trip

Kiriakou has described plans to travel with Rall to Central Asia, telling a viewer from Kyrgyzstan: *"Ted Rall and I plan to visit Kyrgyzstan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, and uh I think Kazakhstan."*<Cite s="2026-03-17-deep-focus-irans-future-cia-background-checks-us-revolution" t="14:30" />

## The shows, as he lists them

Asked in June 2025 where people could follow his work, Kiriakou named four outlets: the podcast *Deep Focus with John Kiriakou* on YouTube; the podcast he co-hosts with Ted Rall, *Deprogram*, also on YouTube; a television programme on the online platform Unify TV called *CIA Declassified*, in which they *"take declassified documents and tell the historic background stories"*; and his Substack, where everything he writes and every appearance he makes is reposted.<Cite s="2025-06-08-american-exception-late-imperial-blues-john-kiriakou-dcc85" t="22:34" /><Cite s="2025-06-08-american-exception-late-imperial-blues-john-kiriakou-dcc85" t="23:05" />
