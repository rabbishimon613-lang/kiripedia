**Angry Birds** is the mobile puzzle game that former CIA officer and whistleblower [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has played every single day for nearly nine years without missing a day — a habit he discusses openly and often in interviews, and which, alongside the [hummus](/wiki/hummus) rectal-feeding commentary that made him "the hummus guy," has become a defining feature of his internet-culture persona. Interviewers now introduce him as *"an Angry Birds legend,"* to which he replies simply, *"I live Angry Birds."*<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:13:56" />

## The streak

Kiriakou says he has played Angry Birds *"literally every day without missing a single day"* for roughly nine years. In a May 2026 appearance he put the run at *"[nine years] and 10 months,"*<Cite s="2026-05-26-inside-cia-lizzie-israel-deep-state" t="1:04:00" /> and by mid-2026 he described himself as *"coming right up on nine years of Angry Birds every single day,"* estimating his consecutive-day count at around 2,879.<Cite s="2026-06-30-jack-neel-palantir-should-terrify-you" t="36:19" /> He specifies that he plays **Angry Birds 2** — *"I love the original,"* he says, *"[but] Angry Birds 2 is better"*<Cite s="2026-06-30-jack-neel-palantir-should-terrify-you" t="36:19" /> — and that he has advanced past the ordinary star ranking into what he calls the *"senior global senior league."*<Cite s="2026-06-30-jack-neel-palantir-should-terrify-you" t="37:22" />

He frames the habit as his single indulgence: *"It's my only vice. I don't drink. I don't smoke. I don't do drugs."*<Cite s="2026-05-26-inside-cia-lizzie-israel-deep-state" t="1:04:00" />

## The morning ritual

Kiriakou describes Angry Birds as a fixed part of his morning routine. He wakes up *"an hour earlier than I need to every day just so I can play Angry Birds for the first hour,"* and does not get out of bed to do it: *"As soon as I wake up, I put my glasses on, I pick up my phone, I go to Angry Birds, and it takes me an hour to do all the daily games."*<Cite s="2026-06-30-jack-neel-palantir-should-terrify-you" t="37:53" /> Elsewhere he estimates the daily games take *"about an hour, 50 minutes,"* after which he moves on to the New York Times, Washington Post, and USA Today crosswords — which his doctor recommended to *"fend off… the degeneration of the brain"* — and a game of solitaire to preserve a streak.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:14:26" /><Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="2:14:58" />

## Favorite birds

Asked to name his favorite bird, Kiriakou consistently returns to the black bird — *"I always love the black one cuz he explodes"* — while noting a rotating cast of newer favorites: one *"that sucks everything up and then spits it out,"* and a newer white bird *"that spins around and knocks everything down,"* which he considers *"very powerful"* but weaker until leveled up.<Cite s="2026-06-30-jack-neel-palantir-should-terrify-you" t="36:19" /><Cite s="2026-05-26-inside-cia-lizzie-israel-deep-state" t="1:03:00" /> He mentioned in one stream that he had spent *"35,000 feathers"* in a single day building up the spinning bird — clarifying, *"not with money… I used my coins."*<Cite s="2026-05-26-inside-cia-lizzie-israel-deep-state" t="1:03:30" />

## Angry Birds and the divorce

When an interviewer asked whether Angry Birds had led to his divorce, Kiriakou pushed back on the framing: *"No. I think Angry Birds was a convenient excuse for divorce."* He defended the habit — *"in defense of Angry Birds and my obsession with Angry Birds, I'm proud to say I have not missed a single day in almost nine years"* — and said the game *"did not lead to my divorce,"* characterizing the accusation as *"just an excuse that a former spouse used to try to justify… the current situation."*<Cite s="2026-06-30-jack-neel-palantir-should-terrify-you" t="35:48" /><Cite s="2026-06-30-jack-neel-palantir-should-terrify-you" t="37:53" />

## A surveillance-evasion tool

Kiriakou has also cited Angry Birds in the context of his post-CIA experience with government surveillance. Describing how he would deliberately waste the time of a surveillance team, he said he would *"go to Starbucks… [and make] them sit there for 45 minutes drinking a coffee and playing Angry Birds on my phone,"* wasting *"your entire day"* — behavior he credits with eventually getting the surveillance called off.<Cite s="2026-02-24-deep-focus-arrested-as-spy-9-11-cia-stores-everything" t="12:30" />
