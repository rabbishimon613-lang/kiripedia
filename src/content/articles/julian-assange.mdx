Some media outlets have attempted to label Julian Assange as an activist rather than a journalist or publisher, reflecting a broader debate about the role of whistleblowers and the media. <Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="50:23:00" />

## Transparency absolutist (HOPE 2025)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) — a 15-year friend of [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange) and a former WikiLeaks board member — rejects claims Assange works for the Russians: *"He doesn't like the Russians any more than he likes the Americans."* He calls Assange a *"transparency absolutist"* needed to balance government *"secrecy absolutists,"* and recalls WikiLeaks meetings where members put phones in a microwave — only to find they were tracked anyway.<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="41:24" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="44:06" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="44:40" />

## The DNC emails were authentic (Reason2Resist)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) stresses that the DNC emails [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange) published were not disinformation but *"authentic"* primary-source documents — and that they revealed the DNC cheating Bernie Sanders, giving someone inside the committee a clear motive to leak.<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="07:18" /><Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="07:50" />

## An activist, not a journalist (Scott Horton, 2021)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts a Zoom call, chaired by Rob Reiner, on which a colleague asked former Knight Ridder reporters Jonathan Landay and Warren Strobel why the national-security press was not defending [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange); *"in unison they said that Julian Assange is not a journalist, he's an activist,"* which Kiriakou found flabbergasting.<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="45:18" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="45:49" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="46:21" /> He notes Assange won his extradition case — the British courts having refused three times to extradite prisoners to face U.S. long-term [solitary confinement](/wiki/solitary-confinement) — yet remains in solitary pending appeal, *"not been exposed to sunlight for something like nine years."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="47:22" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="47:53" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="48:23" /> Kiriakou says Stella Morris, Assange's partner, told him his emotional state is *"fragile"* and his health *"bad."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="48:23" />
