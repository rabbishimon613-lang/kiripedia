**Veteran Intelligence Professionals for Sanity** (VIPS) is a group of former U.S. intelligence officials — retired CIA, FBI, NSA and some DOD personnel — of which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) is a member.<Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="26:26" /><Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="20:30" /> It confers the Sam Adams Award for Integrity in Intelligence. Kiriakou recounts that when [Joe Kent](/wiki/joe-kent) went public on intelligence matters, he tracked down Kent's contact information and arranged for VIPS to give him the Sam Adams Award — though, he notes, the two had never spoken.<Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="26:57" /><Cite s="2026-05-18-covert-operations-insight" t="20:30" />

Kiriakou identifies fellow VIPS member Bill Binney as the former number-four official at the NSA, and cites Binney's study concluding that the data downloaded from the DNC server was extracted at a speed no internet connection could achieve remotely, implying a local, not Russian, leak.<Cite s="2019-04-18-scott-horton" t="28:22" />
