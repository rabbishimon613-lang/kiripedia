**Veteran Intelligence Professionals for Sanity** (VIPS) is a group of former U.S. intelligence officials of which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) is a member. It confers the Sam Adams Award for Integrity in Intelligence.<Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="26:26" /> Kiriakou recounts that when [Joe Kent](/wiki/joe-kent) went public on intelligence matters, he tracked down Kent's contact information and arranged for VIPS to give him the Sam Adams Award — though, he notes, the two had never spoken.<Cite s="2026-05-07-secret-intelligence-report-attack-iran" t="26:57" />
